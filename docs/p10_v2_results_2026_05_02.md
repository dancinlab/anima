## Architecture

- frozen + LoRA: Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct, LoRA r=16 alpha=32 on `q_proj/k_proj/v_proj/o_proj`
- trainable params: 9,175,040 / 3,221,924,864 (0.285 %)
- E_θ: 3072 → 128 → LN → GELU → 32 → LN (no Tanh)
- D_φ: 32 → 256 → LN → GELU → 4 × 3072 (soft prefix, n_prefix=4)
- loss: 1.0·CE + 0.3·AE + 0.5·InfoNCE(temp=0.1, same-bucket pos) + 0.01·KL(z‖N(0,I))
- AdamW lr=1e-4, batch=4, 5 epochs, 1000 balanced pairs (5 buckets × 200)

## Honest C3 / caveats

1. **Train-test overlap** — eval prompts are bucket seed prompts that were tiled into the 1000 training pairs. BLEU-1 inflation from rote memorization is bounded but real; v3 must hold seed prompts out.
2. **BLEU-1 vs vanilla is the wrong oracle** — vanilla Llama is flowery and verbose; the LoRA decoder is short and bucket-styled, so unigram overlap is structurally low even when bucket-quality is high. A semantic metric (BERTScore, BLEU-4 against templated reference) would likely cross PASS.
3. **z_std drift downward** — every epoch z_std falls ~1 % (1.002 → 0.989). Long-run training risks re-collapsing latent — KL coefficient (δ=0.01) may be too weak. Add a hard floor (e.g. z = LN(z) + ε·N(0,I) sample at train) for v3.
4. **n_prefix = 4 caps decoder bandwidth** — only 4 × 3072 = 12,288 floats from a 32-d code is a 384× expansion that the LoRA layers must absorb. v3 should sweep n_prefix ∈ {4, 8, 16, 32}.
