## 1. 4-cell decision matrix

The Phase 1.6 cycle confounded 4 axes: (1) data v2→v3, (2) LoRA r 64→128, (3) α-warmup 5K→3K, (4) β 0.15→0.10. Ablation A isolates (2) by reverting LoRA r. Ablation B isolates (1) by reverting data. Cells (3) and (4) remained ON in both ablations.

|  | **A: r=64 (revert LoRA)** | **A: r=128 (P1.6 r)** |
|---|---|---|
| **B: v2 (revert data)** | = P1.5 baseline (F1 0.00879) | **Ablation B → F1 0.00586** |
| **B: v3 (P1.6 data)** | **Ablation A → F1 0.00586** | P1.6 sentinel F1 0.00586 |

**4-cell read:**
- (v2, r=64) = P1.5 = 0.00879 ← only cell that beats 0.0058
- (v2, r=128) = B = 0.00586 ← reverting data alone fails
- (v3, r=64) = A = 0.00586 ← reverting LoRA r alone fails
- (v3, r=128) = P1.6 = 0.00586

**Conclusion.** F1 is constant at 0.00586 across all 3 cells that share `(α-warmup=3K, β=0.10)`. Only the P1.5 cell with `(α-warmup=5K, β=0.15)` reaches 0.00879. The data×LoRA axes are **decoupled from F1 at the 50K-step margin** under the current eval (greedy BLEU-1 on 32-prompt holdout). The α/β axes carry the entire regression signal.

**Key trajectory finding (necessary substrate, F-log read on ubu1).** P1.5 F1 sat at 0.0068 from step 10K through step 45K (10 consecutive evals identical), then jumped 0.0068 → 0.00879 at step 50K (single +29% jump in the final 5K). P1.6/A/B never produce this jump — they plateau at 0.00586 from step 30K onward. The +29% terminal jump in P1.5 is **not steady-state F1 lift** — it is a single late-window inflection that the P1.6 loss schedule (compressed warmup + halved β) suppresses. Whether the jump is genuine learning, an artefact of greedy decoding on a 32-prompt holdout (1 extra hit on 1 prompt = +0.0020), or a δ-late × β-0.15 interaction effect is **not distinguishable from current evidence**. C3 caveat #1 below.

## 4. Confound attribution (causal read)

| Hypothesis | Test | Result | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1: data-v3 (drop philos+N-22+p8 chat-style) caused regression | Ablation B reverts data | F1 still 0.00586 | **REJECTED** |
| H2: LoRA r=128 caused capacity overshoot / overfit | Ablation A reverts r | F1 still 0.00586 | **REJECTED** |
| H3: β 0.10 (was 0.15) starved tension grad in a way that disrupted joint optimization | Both ablations have β=0.10, both regressed | Consistent but not isolated from H4 | **CANDIDATE** |
| H4: α-warmup 3K (was 5K) compressed CE descent runway, missed late-window F1 jump | Both ablations have α-3K, both regressed | Consistent but not isolated from H3 | **CANDIDATE** |
| H5: P1.5's F1=0.00879 is a single-prompt holdout artefact (greedy decode + 32-prompt eval) | P1.5's F1 sat at 0.0068 for 10 evals, jumped only at step 50K | Plausible — single-eval inflection, +1 token-hit on 32 prompts ≈ +0.002 | **PLAUSIBLE NULL** |

**Strongest single statement we can make.** Across 4 runs varying data and LoRA r, F1 is **bit-identical** at 0.00586. The only run that reaches 0.00879 differs in **both α and β**. Therefore the regression cause is one of: (a) the α schedule, (b) the β level, (c) a compound interaction of both, or (d) noise floor under the eval (H5). Phase 1.7 reverts both α and β to disambiguate (a)+(b) from (c) and (d).

## 6. C3 caveats (top 3, honest)

1. **F1=0.00586 may be the eval noise floor, not the model floor.** 4 runs land bit-identical at 0.00586 = 3/512 hit rate on a 32-prompt × 32-token greedy eval. A tighter eval (200 prompts, length-matched decode, top-k sampling) is required to confirm that the F1 plateau reflects model behavior rather than discretization at the 1/512 step of the BLEU-1 statistic. P1.5's 0.00879 (= 4.5/512) might be one extra correct unigram on one prompt, well within Bernoulli noise on n=32.

2. **α and β are not isolated by Phase 1.7.** If P1.7 recovers F1=0.0088, we still cannot say whether α-5K, β-0.15, or both caused the lift. Phase 1.8a would need to run α-5K + β-0.10 OR α-3K + β-0.15 (one more 50K run, ~1hr) to separate. We accept this — the engineering goal is "recover P1.5 F1 with P1.6's data+LoRA bonuses retained," not full causal isolation.

3. **Phase 1.6's HF artefacts (`need-singularity/clm-v4-sft-1-6-step-*` and `clm-v4-sft-1-6-stage1`) are now mislabeled.** They were pushed under the assumption that Phase 1.6 was an improvement; the F1 regression makes them a regression artefact under the same name. Recommend either (a) HF metadata patch (model card warning), or (b) explicit deprecation in `state/markers/` once Phase 1.7 lands a non-regression. Do not delete — they are a reproducible regression substrate.
