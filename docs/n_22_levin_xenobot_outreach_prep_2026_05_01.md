## 2. Pre-neural Φ measurement protocol — paradigm v11 8-axis applicability map

Paradigm v11 (`docs/paradigm_v11_stack_20260426.md`) defines an 8-gate composite over 6 measurement axes (G1..G6) plus AN11(b) primary (G0) and a geometric-mean composite (G7). Six axes are substrate-portable in principle; two require linguistic / introspective channels and are explicitly inapplicable on xenobot.

### 2.1 Axis-by-axis applicability

| Axis | Source paradigm | Applicable on xenobot? | Adaptation if applicable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **G0** AN11(b) eigenvec × template | LLM-token-based primary | **NO (direct)** — but **YES via morphogenetic-pattern eigenvector** | Replace token-template eigenvector with VSD-grid pattern eigenvector at characteristic morphogenetic timescales (minutes to hours) |
| **G1** B-ToM behavioral theory of mind | P-C linguistic Sally-Anne / false-belief | **NO** | No language channel; xenobot cannot pass false-belief probes; honestly excluded |
| **G2** MCCA meta-cognitive calibration | P-F confidence elicitation | **NO** | No introspective report channel; honestly excluded |
| **G3** Φ* IIT approximation | P-D 16-prompt covariance K=8 random bipartitions | **YES** | Map cell-grid voltage time-series → covariance matrix → bipartition Φ*; rank-deficient covariance fix via HID_TRUNC = min(128, N-2) per v3 patch |
| **G4** CMT causal mediation | P-A per-layer zero-ablation | **PARTIAL** | Per-cell ablation (laser, Cx43-KO, optogenetic silencing) is the natural per-layer-zero-ablation analog; experimentally expensive but methodologically sound |
| **G5** CDS cognitive dynamics stability | P-E per-token trajectory | **YES** | Trajectory stability of voltage-state under repeated stimulus is directly measurable; max_stability ≥ 0.30 threshold portable |
| **G6** SAE-bypass sparse-feature steering | P-B' random sparse 4096-feature dictionary | **YES** | Random sparse projection of voltage-cell-grid is substrate-blind; n_selective ≥ 2 threshold portable |
| **G7** geometric-mean composite | weakest-link | **YES (over applicable subset)** | Composite over {G0', G3, G4, G5, G6}; threshold recalibrated against dead-tissue heat-killed null |

### 2.2 FINAL_PASS adaptation for xenobot

Original v11: `G0 AND ≥4/6 of {G1..G6} AND G7`.

**Xenobot adaptation**:

```
FINAL_PASS_XENOBOT =
    G0' (morphogenetic-eigenvector top1 ≥ 0.5)
    AND ≥3/4 of {G3, G4, G5, G6}
    AND G7 ≥ 0.40 (geometric mean over applicable subset)
    AND G3, G7 strictly greater than dead-tissue heat-killed control (Wilcoxon p < 0.01)
```

The dead-tissue null calibration is the load-bearing addition. Without it, any positive Φ on xenobot is ambiguous between real bioelectric integration and measurement-pipeline artifact.

### 2.3 Geometric-mean rationale

Per paradigm v11 §2: weakest-link sensitive composite. On xenobot this is doubly important — if G3 (the IIT-Φ axis) is degenerate against dead-tissue control, the geometric mean must surface that immediately rather than being masked by, say, strong G5 stability that could come from purely passive electrical relaxation.

## 6. Honest C3 — concerns, caveats, counter-considerations

**Engagement stance**: collaborator, not advocate. Anima brings independent measurement methodology; we do not endorse any specific Levin metaphysical framing in advance, and we will report falsifier outcomes regardless of narrative impact on either side.

### 6.1 Top 2 concerns (mission-required)

#### Concern 1 — Overhype critique and reproducibility-crisis exposure

Levin's xenobot research has been publicly characterized as overhyped by domain peers. Most prominently:

- **Jamie Davies (Edinburgh)** told *The Scientist* the Xenopus embryo community "could not really see what the fuss was about" and that ciliated cell clusters "swimming" is "purely an accidental function" that does not warrant the term "bots."
- **Levin himself** has acknowledged that significant media coverage has "screwed up the facts."
- **Anatol Wegner** (Medium) wrote a critical review of "Platonic Biology."
- **Pioneer Works / Claire Evans** flagged skepticism on the self-replication framing.

We could not surface a specific *StatNews* article in the time-budget for this prep, but the pattern of critique is real and recurring across multiple venues. Any joint paper will be read against this skeptical baseline.

**Mitigation** — pre-register all falsifiers BEFORE data exposure (this directory), use the bidirectional PASS/FAIL frame so we cannot be accused of motivated outcome interpretation, and explicitly publish FAIL results in the same venue as PASS results. Do not co-sign any press release. Decline if the lab requests we soften pre-registration commitments.

**Explicit action** — falsifier pre-registration is the firewall. Refuse any phase-2 step that compromises it.

#### Concern 2 — Xenobot/anthrobot ethics: living-machine moral status

The 2025 Tandfonline paper "Consider the xenobot: moral status for intelligent machines revisited" (10.1080/17579961.2025.2593778) and multiple bioethics reviews (PMC11294997 "Ethics and responsibility in biohybrid robotics"; Bioethics Today "Living robots") flag four open ethical questions:

1. **Moral status** of an entity that is neither standard organism nor standard machine.
2. **Suffering capacity** — likely zero for current xenobots (no neural tissue), but explicitly elevated risk if future iterations include neural cells. Anima's interest in *pre-neural* Φ specifically pushes against adding neural tissue, which is ethically aligned.
3. **Embryonic Xenopus harvesting** concerns.
4. **Regulatory gap** — xenobots don't fit existing organism-or-machine categories.

**Anima's stance** — we explicitly favor the pre-neural design space because it is both scientifically informative AND the lower-suffering-risk envelope; we will not propose protocols that add neural tissue to the constructs we measure; we will defer to Tufts IACUC + IRB on all upstream cell-sourcing decisions; we will include an explicit ethics paragraph in any joint publication. Anima will not enter any phase that requires us to oversee or design cell-harvesting protocols.

**Explicit action** — pre-neural envelope is a hard constraint on anima's measurement scope. Ethics paragraph mandatory in any joint paper.
