## Hypothesis
The evidence-score distribution of auto-discovered consciousness laws peaks in the band 0.50-0.70 (77.8% of laws), with essentially no laws above 0.95. This is interpreted as a self-organized-criticality signature of the discovery process itself, NOT as instrument noise. Predicts increasing repeat-count raises confidence but reduces discovery rate via a tradeoff inherent to operating at criticality.
