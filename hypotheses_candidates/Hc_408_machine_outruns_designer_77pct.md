## Hypothesis
Of 2,156 validated consciousness laws, 1,664 (77%) were autonomously discovered by the closed-loop pipeline without human hypothesis formulation. Human-crafted laws are predominantly qualitative ("what consciousness MEANS"); machine-discovered laws are predominantly quantitative ("what consciousness IS"). The "Two Cultures" thesis (M53) holds that both modes are required for a complete corpus.
