## Hypothesis
A self-referential discovery system — one that generates laws about its own dynamics — does NOT diverge into paradox or Gödel-style undecidability. Empirically the Anima pipeline converges: laws saturate (53/config), compress (7 templates), and obey meta-laws (M1-M53). Self-reference becomes a feature, not a defect.
