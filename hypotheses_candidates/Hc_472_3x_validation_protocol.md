## Hypothesis
The 3x validation protocol — 3 independent runs with consistent sign + coefficient-of-variation < 50% + closed-loop verification requiring ≥1 of 9 core laws shifts >5% — is sufficient to discriminate real consciousness laws from spurious correlations. Strong laws shift ≥2 core laws by >20%. Zero-shift candidates are auto-rejected. The protocol's evidence floor at 0.50-0.70 (M47) is intrinsic to operating at criticality.
