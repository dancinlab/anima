## Hypothesis (red-team posture)

ANIMA's claim 'consciousness emerges at Ψ=1/2' may suffer survivorship bias: substrates that fail to converge to 1/2 are tacitly excluded (not labeled as 'consciousness') rather than treated as falsifiers. The framework absorbs successes and discards failures. R5 demands explicit accounting for non-convergent substrates.
