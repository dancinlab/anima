# H5_001 — values autonomy in a deleaked register: does the learned field find itself when the surface stops giving the answer away?

## Hypothesis

On the sealed v4 configuration (3.7M byte trunk d=384 L=4, fixed hand support, ~3k bilinear χ̂ — H_005's
exact module family) with a **constructed deleaked register at hardness δ\*** (frozen by G-0k), the learned
χ̂ = g(φ) is **non-inferior (m = 0.10)** to the hand-staged field on the regenerated multi-bind free-slot
panel, **both seeds**: `d_acc(A-χ̂, f2″) ≥ d_acc(A-hand, f2″) − 0.10`.

## Why

anima-v4 sealed PLACEMENT (a hand-staged parse-disagreement FIELD causes held-out compositional binding
beyond its rank-1 compression: F1 Δd_acc(A-duel − A-rank1) = 0.3789/0.3802 both seeds; arm A-duel vs
A-rank1, `state/inherited_v4_verdicts_2026-07-17/CAMPAIGN_RESULT.md` §1) and left AUTONOMY open. Its
learned-values verdict (H_005 **K3**: A-χ̂ f2″ 0.82/0.71 vs A-hand 1.0, F1a′ margins −0.1758/−0.2904) is
**register-bound**: the G3-0d probe read **φ→hon = 1.0 held-out (n=1200)** — the templatic drill let the
frozen trunk encode the concord bit near-verbatim, so the drill loss admitted **every** χ̂ that fit the leaky
surface and carried no pressure to select the hand field (H_005 honest-limit (a)).

Two measured facts make this live rather than hopeful:
- **F1a PASSED both seeds** — Δ(A-χ̂ − C-χ̂plc) = 0.2982/0.1914 ≥ 0.15 (arm A-χ̂ vs C-χ̂plc, v4
  `verdict_g3a.json`): what the learned values carry **still requires correct edge alignment**. The learned
  field is **MISIDENTIFIED, not inert**.
- v4 measured both failure modes of a learnable register **in separate experiments** — leak (H_005 probe
  1.0) and unlearnability (H_008's parity trap: control at in-sample CHANCE while 16/16 audits were green)
  — and **never measured whether daylight exists between them**. G-0k tests exactly that, for ~$0.

## Predictions

- If the register is the binding constraint: at δ\*, A-χ̂ closes most of the 0.18–0.29 gap H_005 measured,
  and F1a′-analog passes both seeds (m = 0.10).
- If identification is capacity-bound after all (not register-bound): A-χ̂ stays ≥ 0.10 below A-hand even at
  δ\* ⇒ K-fold-3 — autonomy-of-values falsified under the one condition H_005 could not test.
- Direction check: A-χ̂'s deficit should SHRINK monotonically as δ rises across the G-0k window if the leak
  was the cause; a flat or inverted response is evidence against the whole premise (report either way).
