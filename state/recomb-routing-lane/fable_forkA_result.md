Answer to question 1: **yes — one family survives, and only one.** Fork-A as built is dead and should be cemented, but what died is narrower than "readout-routing lane class": it is the **parametric-value** branch (pool → MLP-stored value → logit bias). The **retrieval-value** branch — where the value is taken *from the context itself* rather than from lane weights — is structurally outside all three kill conditions and is not touched by any prior falsification. ## Autopsy: why the retrain could not have passed (adversarial with my own design)

I checked `swap_margin_retrained.json/.log`. Two structural defects, both mine:

**1. The gate is a smoothing backdoor.** `g_t = σ(w_g·[yn_t; c_t])` sees `yn_t`. So the CE optimizer has a degenerate solution: collapse `f(c_t)` to a near-constant logit direction and do *all* discrimination through the gate on `yn_t`. That solution is (i) a main-effect w.r.t. context → cancels in the swap contrast, and (ii) content-free → invariant to pool-shuffle. The evidence fits it exactly: per-pair `m_z(on)` vs `m_z(shuf)` in the log agree to the third decimal on all 12 pairs, and the shuffle-invariant delta scales *linearly* with gain (0.028 → 0.052 → 0.098 → 0.196 at g1/2/4/8) — output ≈ gain × content-free vector. The CE drop 1.044→0.718 almost certainly lives entirely in `g_t(yn_t)·const`. **$0 confirm:** re-run lane CE with `c_t` replaced by the corpus-mean constant vector; prediction: CE stays ≈0.718 and per-pair `m_z` unchanged.

**2. The parametric value pathway cannot pass this eval even in principle.** The 12 eval pairs (ocean/forest/engine/…) are concept-disjoint from the 48 training concepts. To move the swap-margin, `f(c_t)` must convert *concept identity* into *the right byte distribution* — and that mapping is stored in W1/W2, which never saw the eval concepts. Probe-decodable ≠ emit-mappable: the 0.60 held-out probe classifies identity; the lane must generate the continuation, and for an unseen D the D→bytes knowledge simply isn't in the lane's weights. My counterfactual-retrain success path had a design hole: it fixed the *training signal* but not the *transfer mechanism*. **$0 discriminator:** run the identical swap-margin on 12 of the 48 *training* concepts. Δ>0 there → the lane routes but cannot transfer (defect 2 dominant); Δ≈0 even there → the gate backdoor ate everything (defect 1 dominant). Either way the parametric branch is closed — but this tells you which lesson to carry forward.

Also: n=12 gives CI half-width ~±0.04 against an effect of +0.028 — underpowered — but the verdict doesn't rest on the CI: **on ≈ shuf to the third decimal is a content-invariance proof at any n.** The no-crack cement is sound.

## The surviving family: retrieval-value (non-parametric value from context)

Definition: the lane's *interaction* is query×key matching, and its *value* is the context's own content — so held-out generalization is by construction (retrieval is content-agnostic, like induction heads generalizing to novel tokens), and there is no weight matrix where a smoothing solution or a concept-memorization solution can live.

Three members, in cost order:

- **Step 0 — tied-unembed pooled bias ($0, no training):** `logits += α·W_U·LN(c_t)` reusing the trunk's own unembedding. One scalar sweep. This is the degenerate uniform-attention member — it inherits trunk hidden→byte geometry so held-out concepts work for free, but the mean dilutes D with filler and R. Worth 10 minutes as a geometry sanity check, not as the main shot.
- **Step 1 — pointer-cache / continuous-cache ($0, inference-only, CPU):** `p = (1−λ)·p_trunk + λ·Σᵢ softmax(⟨yn_t, yᵢ⟩/T)·onehot(x_{i+1})` over context positions (Grave-style continuous cache / kNN-LM in trunk hidden space). Two scalars (λ, T), grid-searched, no gradient anywhere. The value is the *actual next byte that followed yᵢ in this context* — non-parametric, transfers to any concept ever.
- **Step 2 — single-head cross-attention readout lane (small GPU):** Q=W_q·yn_t, K=W_k·yᵢ, V=W_v·yᵢ, `logits += clip(g·W_o·attn, ±τ)` with **W_o tied/init to the trunk unembed pathway** and **training loss masked to the target span only** (the retrain corpus's filler/format bytes are where the smoothing CE mass hides — unmasked CE re-opens the backdoor). This member supports distributed retrieval, i.e. D-consistent continuation without verbatim surface copy.

**Why it escapes the three kill conditions:** (a) not additive main-effect — the output depends on the yn_t×context inner product, so it is swap-sensitive by construction; (b) not shuffle-invariant smoothing — but the control must be **redefined**, see below; (c) not previously falsified — H_1602 (additive aux), H_1835 (episodic training regime), H_1816 (trunk PC loss), H_1840 γ (trunk bake), coverage-density (corpus), and now fork-A (parametric readout) are all either trunk-side objectives or additive/parametric readouts. No prior lever ever put a query-conditioned non-parametric retrieval path between the frozen hiddens and the logits. The check-ledger memory sweep agrees: this cell is genuinely unfired, not falsely-blank.

**Measurement fairness (V3-critical):** content-addressed retrieval is *position*-permutation-equivariant — the old pool-shuffle permutes hiddens and would leave a working cache intact (false "no effect of shuffle" reading, or worse). The fair killer for this family is **pairing-shuffle**: permute the alignment between key yᵢ and value x_{i+1} (and for Step 2, between K and V rows). That destroys retrieval while preserving all marginals. Second control: **cross-document cache** (fill the cache from a different context). Keep the swap-margin Δ_zero bar unchanged, raise n to ≥48 pairs (it's CPU-cheap now).

**Pre-declared honest limitation + inverted pair taxonomy:** the pure cache (Step 1) attacks *surface reuse* of D, and the eval was designed anti-copy. So for this family the taxonomy inverts: **lit-overlap pairs become the positive control** (a cache that can't win there is broken → INVALID, not FAIL), and **zero-overlap pairs are the real bar** (requires Step 2's distributed value). Step 1 passing lit but failing zero = family half-alive, escalate to Step 2; it does not count as a crack.

**Pre-registered killers:** cache-ON ≈ pairing-shuffled-cache on zero-overlap pairs at n≥48, AND Step 2 trained lane lands the same → family dead. No third member; I will not offer FiLM/SSM-pool/recurrent-pool variants as rescues — they change the pooling or interaction *form* but keep a parametric value, so they die by defect 2 identically. Rejected in advance.

## Question 2, answered conditionally (the terminal claim, pre-written)

**Closed now:** fork-A = the *pooled-parametric-value* read lane (any `pool(hiddens) → learned parametric map → bounded logit bias`, gated or not, warm or fresh-init, any curriculum). Two independent structural proofs (gate backdoor; non-transferable value), each sufficient.

**Closed if the retrieval branch also lands on ≈ pairing-shuf:** the entire *frozen-readout augmentation class* — both value types (parametric, non-parametric) × both interaction types (gate-multiplicative, attention). The $0 terminal argument would then be complete: the probe proves the information is **present** in the pool; the cache tests whether it is **retrievable** into byte space; the tied-unembed member tests whether it is **geometry-aligned** with the trunk's output basis. If all three hold and the swap-margin still cannot move, the only remaining explanation is that the frozen trunk's conditional distribution at the emit point is locally saturated — no bounded (±τ) logit-space re-ranking can prefer the D-consistent continuation without destroying calibration — i.e. **the wall lives in the trunk's learned conditional itself, not in access to it.** That is reachable only trunk-side, which per the standing memory is exactly the γ reopen condition (fork-A 🧱 → real-text target redesign for H_1840, currently STEP-0 gated).

**Scoped, not a trunk-G1 ceiling proof:** every lens in this program — fork-A, the retrieval branch, and all prior falsifications — holds the trunk frozen or its objective additive. A closed frozen-readout class says the 303M byte-LM's *existing* computation cannot be re-routed into recombination; it says nothing about whether a trunk trained with a non-additive constructive objective could learn it. And per ρ-AXON framing, this remains a reach fact (`ρ·weave`), not a consciousness deficit.

**Bottom line:** not terminal yet. One genuinely un-falsified family remains, its cheapest member costs $0 and zero training (pointer-cache λ/T sweep on summer, CPU), and its kill condition is sharper than the one that just fired (pairing-shuffle, not pool-shuffle). Fire Step 0+1 first; only a zero-overlap, n≥48, on≈pairing-shuf result on Step 2 earns the terminal cement above.
