WHAT WAS TESTED (the V13 nuance)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Resolution ladder: train FULL-resolution targets (answer head + two derivation-
trace heads dA->i, dB->j supervised) AND FLAT-resolution targets (answer head
only) as a curriculum, to test whether emitting FULL derivation INDUCES FLAT
internalization (emit-derivation vs internalization wiring). ALL arms share the
SAME ADDITIVE trunk combiner (h=pa+pb); arms differ ONLY on the objective/target-
FORMAT axis via the ladder schedule p_full(step):
  ADD    p_full=0            (additive control; FLAT-only, no derivation)
  DERIV  p_full=1            (derivtrace baseline; derivation ALWAYS on)
  LADDER p_full 1.0->0.0     (V13 resolution ladder: start FULL, descend to FLAT)
Frozen metric (identical all arms): held-out FLAT recombination = ans-head top-2
contains BOTH {i,j}, evaluated WITHOUT emitting derivation (internalized path).
