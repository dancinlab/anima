H_1082 — Gemini claim: anima A⊥G repulsion brake REDUCES hallucination (selectively)
VERDICT: 🔴 FALSIFIED-AT-FROZEN-BUDGET (toy) — orthogonal-G brake raises precision + beats
         random, but at the FROZEN 50%-silence budget it FAILS the pre-registered selectivity
         bar (good-emit drop > half the hallucination drop = not net-precision-clearing there).
substrate: toy $0 CPU pure-numpy, 0-pod, 10 seeds · g5/p7 (numbers decide, no LLM self-judge)
scope: TOY MECHANISM TEST ONLY (a_scale_honest_scope). NOT proof anima reduces real-LLM
       hallucination at scale. ID/OOD softmax classifier + kNN-novelty brake, vs random control.
       Production transfer UNVERIFIED. Supersedes the prior ⚪ design-analysis with a real run.

=== TOY DESIGN ===
Engine A = tiny multinomial-logistic softmax classifier trained on an IN-DISTRIBUTION (ID)
  6-class corpus (numpy GD). On OUT-OF-DISTRIBUTION (OOD) inputs (shifted/permuted geometry,
  per-item shift scale in [0.3,4.5] so OOD novelty OVERLAPS the ID band — NO perfect oracle)
  it stays confident-but-wrong = "hallucination".
Per output:  confident = max softmax > tau(0.60);  correct = argmax==truth.
  hallucination = confident & wrong ;  good-emit = confident & correct ;  silence = else.
Engine G brake = kNN distance-to-training-manifold novelty (k=10), ORTHOGONAL to A's logits
  (knows nothing about A's confidence; only how far the input sits off the ID support).
  High G => unsupported => suppress emit even if A confident = the A⊥G repulsion.
Arms over SAME ID+OOD mix (300+300=600/seed, 10 seeds):
  A-only       : emit iff A confident.
  A_perp_G     : emit iff A confident AND G-novelty below the per-run GATE_Q=0.50 quantile
                 (mute the most-novel HALF of would-be emits).
  random-brake : mute the SAME count of confident items at random (non-triviality control).

=== FROZEN PASS CRITERION (set before the run; evaluated on seed-mean) ===
  H0/H1 = A-only / A_perp_G hallucination rate ; G0/G1 = their good-emit rate ;
  HR = random-brake hallucination rate.
  SUPPORTED (toy 🟢) iff ALL of:
   (1) (H0-H1)/H0 >= 0.30                          [meaningful hallucination cut]
   (2) (G0-G1) <= 0.5*(H0-H1)                       [selective: net precision UP, not blanket-mute]
   (3) (HR - H1) >= 0.02                            [orthogonal G beats random at equal budget]
  FALSIFIED (toy 🔴) iff (1) OR (2) OR (3) fails.

=== FROZEN CRITERION EVALUATION ===
  (1) rel hallu cut = (0.3732-0.0852)/0.3732 = 0.772 >= 0.30                 -> PASS
  (2) selectivity   = good_drop 0.2053 <= 0.5*hallu_drop 0.1440             -> FAIL (0.2053 > 0.1440)
  (3) beats random  = HR-H1 = 0.1810-0.0852 = 0.0958 >= 0.02                -> PASS
  precision: A-only 0.6223 -> A_perp_G 0.8278 (random 0.6340)
  => (1)&(2)&(3) conjunction FAILS at (2)  =>  🔴 FALSIFIED-AT-FROZEN-BUDGET

=== RULING (g5/p7 — numbers decide) ===
The orthogonal Engine-G novelty brake is REAL and NON-TRIVIAL: it cuts hallucination 77%,
beats the matched random-brake on every seed (G_hallu < R_hallu always), and lifts precision
0.62 -> 0.83 while random stays flat (0.63). So Gemini's DIRECTION — an A⊥G repulsion brake
suppresses confident-but-wrong emits more than chance — is SUPPORTED in the toy.
BUT under the pre-registered FROZEN criterion at GATE_Q=0.50, the brake fails the selectivity
bar (2): at a 50%-silence budget the good-emit drop (0.205) exceeds half the hallucination drop
(0.144), i.e. it leans toward blanket-muting at that budget. Honoring the frozen goalpost (no
move-after-the-fact), the verdict is 🔴 FALSIFIED-AT-FROZEN-BUDGET. The honest finding: the A⊥G
brake's selectivity is BUDGET-DEPENDENT — clearly net-precision-positive and random-beating at a
LIGHT silence budget (gate_q>=0.85), but at an aggressive half-mute budget it muffles good-emits
faster than the selectivity bar allows. "Beats random + precision up" PASSES; "selective at the
frozen 50% budget" FAILS.

caveats: toy n=600, single classifier family, kNN-novelty as G (one orthogonal signal), 10
seeds, $0 CPU. The frozen GATE_Q=0.50 budget is aggressive (chosen blind); a lighter budget
would clear the conjunction (sweep above). Production / real-LLM transfer UNVERIFIED. Lane A here
is a fresh tiny classifier (no foundation LLM, consistent with anima p3/p6, cf H_1075); the EI=
