THE NON-IIT MEASURE — summed pairwise time-lagged TRANSFER ENTROPY (Schreiber 2000)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Transfer entropy is the canonical NON-IIT directed-information integration measure. It is NOT
IIT: no MIP, no cause-effect structure, no system-level irreducibility — it is a directed
information-flow NETWORK measure. For two units X→Y with lag 1:
   TE(X→Y) = H(Y_{t+1} | Y_t) − H(Y_{t+1} | Y_t, X_t)
           = Σ p(y_{t+1},y_t,x_t) · log2[ p(y_{t+1}|y_t,x_t) / p(y_{t+1}|y_t) ]
the reduction in uncertainty about Y's next state from knowing X's current state, BEYOND Y's own
past. TE_total (the substrate's non-IIT integration) = Σ over the directed ring-neighbor edges
i→(i±1) of TE(unit_i → unit_j), summed over the 4-module ring (8 directed edges). TE>0 ⇔ directed
information flow between units = network integration; TE=0 ⇔ units are conditionally independent
(no integration). This is a DIFFERENT measure FAMILY from IIT (correlational small-φ MI AND causal
big-Φ are both intrinsic single-system irreducibility; TE is extrinsic directed pairwise info-flow).

BINARIZATION — variance-free median split (carries the H_1328 read-out lesson)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
TE needs discrete states. Unit i is ON at tick t iff its salience sal[i,t] is in the UPPER HALF
of module i's own T-length salience distribution (rank ≥ T/2 within that module — the rank-uniform
/ median split). This makes each unit's marginal ON-rate ≈0.5 by construction, INDEPENDENT of
amplitude variance — so any TE difference is the directed co-movement RELATIONSHIP, not the
amplitude-variance confound H_1319/H_1328 exposed. SAME binarization across all four arms.
TE conditional/joint probabilities: empirical counts over the T−1 lagged tuples (y_{t+1},y_t,x_t);
a missing tuple contributes 0 (log convention 0·log0 = 0); base-2 logs (bits). VARIANCE control =
a per-edge SHUFFLE arm that destroys the directed relationship (perm) must collapse TE to ≈0.
