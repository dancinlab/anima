auxiliary load-balance loss, no expert-collapse mode. We compare expert-choice
vs the landed token-choice baseline (CLM/model/array_moe.py) on the mid-rung
pluggable routing slot, measured by CODE (g5), multi-seed.

                      averaged over seeds {42,43,44}. (load auto-balance floor)
