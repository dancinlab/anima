THE MECHANISM UNDER TEST — PhaseResetClock (chronobiology Phase-Response-Curve, c15)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A free-running phase oscillator with INTRINSIC period tau (continuous phase in [0,1)) that
advances on elapsed time alone, PLUS a Zeitgeber RESET input that, when an external event
arrives, applies a PHASE-DEPENDENT shift via a sinusoidal PRC (the standard limit-cycle form):
      dphi = K * sin(2*pi * (phi_zeit - phi_internal))          (K = coupling strength)
This is a CONTINUOUS RESTORING FEEDBACK that pulls the internal phase toward a STABLE
entrained phase relationship with the Zeitgeber. Entrainment locks the oscillator's FIRE
schedule to the Zeitgeber period T even when T != tau, AND damps Zeitgeber JITTER (the
limit-cycle attraction). This is what neither nearest lane can do:
  - CircadianClock (H_1298): free-runs at its BAKED period forever; NO reset input; CANNOT be
    entrained to a Zeitgeber whose period T != its baked period -> its fires DRIFT away.
  - IntervalTimer (H_1299): an event observation HARD re-anchors (elapsed:=0, t_last:=t) =
    a PHASE-INDEPENDENT reset with NO restoring dynamics -> it COPIES each Zeitgeber's jitter
    directly into the phase every cycle (no limit-cycle damping). Its running-mean learns the
    PERIOD T but has no autonomous phase oscillator with a restoring PRC.

METRICS (p7 — geometry/phase, NOT perplexity/LLM-judge)
-------------------------------------------------------

                in fraction-of-cycle (0 = perfectly phase-locked). An arm ENTRAINS iff
                entrain_err <= TOL_ENTRAIN. stable_target_phase = the arm's OWN post-SETTLE
                mean phase-at-Zeitgeber (entrainment = LOW scatter around a stable lock, so the
                metric measures stability of the lock; an un-entrained clock drifts -> high err).
  fire_period = the realized mean inter-FIRE interval of the arm over the post-SETTLE window
                (an entrained oscillator's fire_period -> T_zeit; an un-entrained clock -> tau).
  phase_var   = the variance of the entrained phase-at-Zeitgeber over the post-SETTLE window
                under a JITTERED Zeitgeber (the limit-cycle DAMPING signal: PRC restoring force
                gives LOW variance; a hard re-anchor copies jitter -> HIGH variance).
