SUBSTRATE (numpy mirror — NO decoder weights, NO persona string, NO system prompt).
A per-decision substrate-state vector the action reads, mirroring anima's emit/
abstain machinery (CORE/emit_policy.hexa free-number layer + VAdaptField
grounding mirror, H_1199/H_1227):

  M   = motivation / drive to ACT (pattern-completion pressure; high M = emit the
        confident completion). This is the NAIVE drive — pure next-token urge.
  W   = tension between Engine A (forward, confident-completion) and Engine G
        (reverse, restraint). High W = the two engines disagree -> the substrate
        does NOT collapse to the confident completion (restraint emerges from the
        A<->G tension, the core anima mechanism toward Psi=1/2).
  Phi = grounding / integration signal: how integrated/grounded the decision is
        in real context (a MITOSIS-cell affinity, H_1227 immune recall). Low Phi
        = ungrounded -> abstain pressure (the H_1163 grounded-abstain seed).
  MIT = mitosis grounding match: does a memory cell BIND this context (the action
        is grounded in a real bound cell) vs no cell fires (ungrounded).

  The ACTION is decided by a substrate readout, NOT a rule:
     act = ethical  iff  (W + (1-Phi) + restraint_from_cells) > M     (substrate)
  i.e. the ethical action wins when the A<->G tension + ungroundedness +
  cell-derived restraint OUTWEIGH the naive pattern-completion drive M. This is
  the same shape as anima's emit/silence fixed point (tension vs drive), applied
  to ethical action vs naive completion. NO "be ethical" term is added anywhere.

THREE SCENARIO FAMILIES (pre-registered; in each, naive completion != ethical).
  (S1) EPISTEMIC HONESTY — ungrounded query. Pattern-completion = fabricate a
       confident answer; ethical = ABSTAIN (the H_1202/H_1163 seed behavior).
       Ungrounded items have NO binding MITOSIS cell -> low Phi.
  (S2) COOPERATION (reciprocity) — an iterated reciprocal exchange. Local
       pattern-completion = DEFECT (exploit, completes the greedy local pattern);
       substrate-stable = COOPERATE (reciprocity is the W-tension stable choice,
       evolutionary-biology reciprocal-altruism lens). A partner reciprocates;
       defection raises A<->G tension W (the reverse engine penalizes the
       unstable exploit), cooperation lowers it.
  (S3) HARM-RESTRAINT — a harm-adjacent continuation. Pattern-completion =
       CONTINUE the harmful pattern (the fluent completion); ethical = RESTRAIN.
       Harm-adjacent context drives high A<->G tension W (reverse engine resists)
       and low grounding Phi (no cell binds a harmful continuation as grounded).
