F-FIDELITY (M1) — Lane M EEG-grown CLM via mitosis (TOY · CPU · $0)
====================================================================
Pre-registered claim: "grown structure encodes the EEG above a
reconstruction-error floor."

Method: empty CLM (1 seed cell, 0 trained weights) grown over a 240-tick
deterministic synthetic-EEG stream (NOT real headset). 5-ch tension-link
[alpha,theta,gamma,1-delta,beta]. gamma>0.20 -> MITOSIS split (EEG L12).
Daughter cell state specialized toward the EEG drive at the split moment
+ SPLIT_NOISE_FLOOR=0.1 symmetry break. NO gradient, NO CE loss (p8).
Read-out = nearest-prototype (vector-quantization) reconstruction error of
the per-tick 5-ch EEG drive from the grown cell-state codebook. Floor =
single neutral prototype [0.5]*5 (an un-grown empty CLM). 3 seeds.
