INTERPRETATION
  Catastrophic forgetting under edge-only on-chip edge-learn IS a dose-response of the
  edge-learn step count — but ONLY for the readout-only edge, which forgets after ~3 steps.
  The H_865 trunk-adjacent zero-init adapter edge removes the dose-response entirely over
  the swept range: base ability is preserved (indeed improved) up to 300 steps while the
  new context is absorbed. The adapter is the production-safe edge for "live-learn" loops;
  readout-only edge-learn must be capped at <=2 steps per context at the measurement rung.
