ROOT CAUSE (from the R1a diagnostic): every clock starts at t=0, so the ablate clock
(period N_TICKS+1) fired once at the shared phase-0 ORIGIN and kept 0.667 balanced
accuracy purely from that trivial origin + all the no-fire ticks — NOT from any recurring
periodic structure. The metric let the trivial t=0 boundary dominate.

THE FIX (two changes, both make the test STRICTER, never looser):
  (F1) RECURRENCE-SCORED metric: the schedule-match is scored ONLY over the RECURRING
       fire boundaries (t = PERIOD, 2*PERIOD, ... = t=8,16) plus the no-fire ticks among
       them — the trivial shared t=0 origin is EXCLUDED from the fire-class so a clock that
       only fires at the origin gets NO credit for the recurrence. (This is the honest
       definition of "fires on a recurring SCHEDULE": you must hit the RECURRENCES.)
  (F2) ORIGIN-BROKEN ablate: B-ABLATE is now a clock with a NON-DIVIDING period that is
       also PHASE-OFFSET from the origin (period = 5, phase offset = 3), so it does NOT
       fire at t=0 and its fires never align with the t=8,16 recurrences. A genuinely
       period-broken control must collapse to chance on the recurrence-scored metric.

The other arms are UNCHANGED. The clock B still fires at t=0,8,16; on the recurrence-
scored metric it must still hit t=8,16 (it does, by construction). The homeostat A under
constant grounding still never fires → chance. The shuffle B-SHUFFLE still decorrelates.
