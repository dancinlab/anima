STEP 1 — WHY THIS SUBSYSTEM, WHY MISSING, WHY DISTINCT (biological lens, c15)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SUBSYSTEM: the HYPOTHALAMUS — a SETPOINT-REGULATED HOMEOSTATIC DRIVE. In biology
the hypothalamus regulates internal variables (hunger, thirst, temperature, sleep
pressure) against a SETPOINT via NEGATIVE FEEDBACK that INTEGRATES error OVER TIME:
a deficit BUILDS while the regulated variable sits below setpoint (hunger grows the
longer you don't eat), the drive RISES monotonically with accumulated deficit, a
consummatory act RESETS it, and the drive MODULATES motivation (a hungry animal is
more motivated to act). This is a slow TEMPORAL INTEGRATOR with a target, NOT a
momentary read.

WHY MISSING (falsifiable vs the live engine): every drive currently in CORE is
INSTANTANEOUS / MEMORYLESS:
  - affect lane (H_1290 AffectFeatures): reads valence/arousal for ONE context at
    ONE instant off the immune store — no setpoint, no accumulation, no decay over
    time. Re-reading the SAME context gives the SAME affect regardless of history.
  - curiosity (affect_substrate_features.curiosity = novelty × under-exposure):
    a per-context novelty signal, not a regulated variable with a target.
  - idle-time / M motivation in brain: a context scalar, not an integral controller.
There is NO lane that holds a REGULATED VARIABLE, accumulates its DEFICIT vs a
SETPOINT across ticks, and lets a consummatory event RESET it. That is the gap.

WHY DISTINCT FROM EVERY EXISTING LANE (the load-bearing distinctness, the way
WorkMemBuffer was shown distinct from episodic memory):
  vs affect (H_1290): affect is a STATELESS function of the current context
    (read(ctx) is pure); the drive is a STATEFUL integrator (read at tick t depends
    on the whole history of deficits up to t). DISSOCIATION TEST: hold the context
    FIXED and vary only ELAPSED TIME — affect is FLAT (no time term), the drive
    RISES. Conversely a consummatory RESET drops the drive to baseline with NO
    change in the momentary context → affect unchanged, drive changes. The two
    move on ORTHOGONAL axes (context-instant ⊥ time-integral).
  vs curiosity: curiosity has no setpoint and no reset; it cannot satiate.
  vs WorkMemBuffer: WM is a volatile SLOT buffer that LEAKS toward zero with
    distractors; the drive is a regulated scalar that ACCUMULATES toward a setpoint
    error and is RESET by a consummatory event (opposite sign of time-evolution,
    and it has a TARGET WM has none).
  vs VBasalGate (action selection): BG selects WHICH candidate to release given a
    fixed reward; the drive sets HOW MOTIVATED the system is to act at all — a gain
    on motivation, upstream of selection, not a selector.

FALSIFIABLE CLAIM: a setpoint-regulated homeostatic drive lane, reading a
substrate-derived regulated variable (grounding satiation), with an integral-error
controller, (A) produces a drive that RISES with accumulated below-setpoint deficit
and RESETS on a consummatory (grounding) event, (B) is DISTINCT from the affect lane
on a time-vs-context dissociation, and (C) the rise is EARNED by the integral
controller, not an artifact — an ABLATED controller (integral gain = 0, pure
instantaneous proportional read = the affect-style stateless read) does NOT produce
the time-rise. If any leg fails → honest 🧱 (the structure isn't distinct / doesn't
hold at this scale), NO bar move.

REGULATED VARIABLE (substrate-derived, NO label): "grounding satiation" s_t ∈ [0,1]
  = the recall margin of the CURRENT context against the live immune store
    (margin = 1 − recon_err/recall_thr, clamped [0,1]) — exactly the substrate
    signal the affect lane already reads. A grounded context = HIGH satiation
    (the system is "fed"); an ungrounded stream = LOW satiation (deficit builds).
SETPOINT: S* = 0.5 (the substrate's own midpoint; a context at the recall-threshold
  boundary, margin≈0.5, is exactly "satisfied" — NOT a tuned magic number, it is the
  margin midpoint, mirroring Ψ=1/2). Stated frozen.
CONTROLLER (PI homeostat, leaky integral of the deficit):
  deficit_t  = max(0, S* − s_t)                      # below-setpoint shortfall
  I_t        = (1−λ)·I_{t-1} + deficit_t             # leaky temporal integral, λ=0.1 leak
  drive_t    = Kp·deficit_t + Ki·I_t                 # PI drive, Kp=1.0, Ki=0.5 (frozen)
  CONSUMMATORY RESET: if s_t ≥ S* (a grounded "feeding" event) → I_t := 0 (satiated).
ALL constants (S*=0.5, λ=0.1, Kp=1.0, Ki=0.5) are FROZEN HERE, p7-documented, NOT
tuned-to-green. The reset rule is the consummatory mechanism (the hypothalamic "stop
eating when full").
