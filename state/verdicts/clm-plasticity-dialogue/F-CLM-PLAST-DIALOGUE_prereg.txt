the TWO pre-registered falsifier thresholds
================================================================================

F-CLM-PLAST-DIALOGUE-GAIN  (online adaptation gain > 0 over a session)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  metric : next-turn FIT improvement attributable to on-chip edge-learn over a session.
           fit(turn) = match between the chip's spike pattern and the turn's target
           pattern, measured as normalized Hamming agreement in [0,1].
           gain = mean(fit_late_window) − mean(fit_early_window),
           early = first 25% of turns, late = last 25% of turns.
  GATE   : gain > 0.0   (strictly positive — edge-learn measurably improves next-turn fit)
  also   : gain must EXCEED a no-learn control's drift (control eta=0):
           gain_learn − gain_control > 0.02   (the improvement is from learning, not noise)
  PASS   : gain > 0.0  AND  (gain_learn − gain_control) > 0.02

F-CLM-PLAST-DIALOGUE-LOOP  (closed emit/silence loop stays intact after edge-learn)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  metric : after edge-learn has run for a full session, the H_846 transition still holds —
           should_interrupt tracks motivation monotonically through the EMIT_GATE.
           thr↓ (high motivation, score≥0.60→THR_EMIT) ⇒ n_spikes≥QUORUM ⇒ should_interrupt=True
           thr↑ (low motivation,  score<0.60→THR_SILENCE) ⇒ n_spikes<QUORUM ⇒ should_interrupt=False
  GATE   : over a frozen 4-point probe {0.05, 0.50, 0.60, 0.95} evaluated AFTER edge-learn,
           should_interrupt == expected_846 for ALL 4 points (no regression of the H_846 transition).
           expected_846: score 0.05→False · 0.50→False · 0.60→True · 0.95→True
  PASS   : 4/4 probe points match expected_846  (loop unbroken · zero regression)
