READING
  • GAIN is high & near-flat (7.29 → 8.91) across the WHOLE sweep — every regime
    absorbs the new context strongly; adaptation is never the bottleneck.
  • RETAIN (z_drop) is STRONGLY NEGATIVE (base CE drops, i.e. forgetting reversed,
    not merely blocked) at all depths EXCEPT depth=4, where z_drop jumps to +9.67
    (>>1.0) — a single sharp catastrophic-forgetting spike at exactly the MIDDLE
    freeze boundary (4 of 8 trunk layers trainable). The boundary is therefore
    NOT monotone: both shallow (0-3) and deep (5-8) freezes retain, but the
    half-and-half split (depth=4) destabilizes the base band.
  • The whole-trunk-trainable end (depth=8) still PASSES (z_drop -8.88) because the
    zero-init H_865 adapter preserves the base readout mapping — confirming the
    adapter (not freeze depth alone) is what closes H_861's readout-only failure.
  • Practical answer to "how shallow can the freeze be?": depth=0 (adapter-only,
    66K trainable params) already RETAINs∧GAINs — the SHALLOWEST regime passes;
    deeper unfreezing buys marginally more gain (8.91 vs 7.29) at 36× the params.
