================================================================================
THE WALL (H_1284, 4 prior census families + 12 lenses)
================================================================================
H_1284 NEUROMODULATION is 🧱 (no-free-lunch): a state-driven controller of the
engine's LR/SPLIT_THRESH/abstain never beats a single tuned FIXED operating point.
Every prior lens (controller / capacity / geometry / interference / retrieval — 12
probes) shared ONE hidden precondition: the store ADMITTED EVERY FACT (random/full
admission, no capacity budget). When the store holds everything, selection of WHAT
to store cannot help — that is structurally why the prior probes were inert.

================================================================================
C4 ORTHOGONAL LEVER (active-sampling, Gottlieb & Oudeyer 2018 Nat Rev Neurosci)
================================================================================
Add the precondition the prior probes lacked: a FIXED STORE BUDGET N < total facts.
Build a fact stream that mixes (a) DISTINCT facts and (b) REDUNDANT near-collinear
duplicates of already-stored facts. Under budget N, a curiosity / information-gain
ADMISSION gate that ADMITS by key-novelty and SKIPS redundant near-collinear dups
spends the scarce budget on DISTINCT facts -> at recall it answers more distinct
queries than random admission, which wastes budget slots on duplicates.

If curiosity-admission only TIES random-admission at the same budget, the wall
EXTENDS to the active-sampling family (honest 🧱, c9).

================================================================================
ARMS (all share the SAME fact stream + SAME budget N per (seed))
================================================================================
RANDOM    — best fixed-budget baseline: reservoir-sample N admits uniformly at
            random from the stream (each arriving fact admitted with prob to keep
            a uniform-random N-subset; collinear dups can occupy slots).
CURIOSITY — admit a fact iff its key-novelty (recon-err = L2 dist to nearest
            stored prototype) > adaptive threshold (running EMA of seen novelty);
            collinear near-dups fall BELOW threshold -> SKIPPED. Capacity-bound:
            if full and a novel fact arrives, LRU-evict stalest. Reads ONLY the
            substrate key geometry — NO injected answer/quality label (p6).
ABL       — curiosity OFF -> reverts to RANDOM admission at SAME budget N
            (must collapse to RANDOM: isolates that the GATE, not the budget,
            is load-bearing).
SHUFFLE   — curiosity gate ON but its per-fact info-gain scores are
            phase-scrambled (permuted) before the admit decision (decouples the
            novelty signal from the fact it scores -> admits ~randomly -> must
            collapse to RANDOM). Decisive control: if SHUFFLE matches CURIOSITY,
            the lift is a budget/sampling artifact, not the curiosity signal.

================================================================================
FROZEN BARS (pre-registered — NOT moved post-hoc)
================================================================================
Metric = recall ACCURACY over a held query set of DISTINCT subjects (fraction of
queries whose true fact is in the store and correctly recalled), under budget N.
seeds = [11, 22, 33] (3 seeds). MARGIN = 0.05. Means over seeds.

  B1 LIFT     recall(CURIOSITY) - recall(RANDOM) >= +0.05  on >= 2/3 seeds
  B2 EARNED-ABL   recall(CURIOSITY) >= recall(ABL) + 0.05         (mean)
                  AND recall(ABL) within 0.03 of recall(RANDOM)   (revert check)
  B3 EARNED-SHUF  recall(CURIOSITY) >= recall(SHUFFLE) + 0.05     (mean)
                  AND recall(SHUFFLE) within 0.03 of recall(RANDOM)(collapse)
  B4 NO-FAB    fab(CURIOSITY) not worse than fab(RANDOM) by >0.05  (abstain held)

VERDICT:
