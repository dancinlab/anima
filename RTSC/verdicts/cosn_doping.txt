METHOD (rigid-band, BZ tetrahedron-grid weighted)
--------------------------------------------------
- Parse spin-up + spin-down eigenvalues (28 irreducible k-pts x 56 bands per spin) and the
  k-weights wk from scf.out (the converged 6x6x6 tetrahedron grid).
- Occupation N(E_F) = sum_{spin,k,band} wk_k * f((eps - E_F)) with Gaussian occupation
  (sigma = 0.10 eV). wk normalized to sum=1 (both spin channels carried explicitly).
- SANITY: N(E_F = 14.7132) = 93.039  (vs true 93.0)  -> integrator validated.
- Hole-doping to reach a target E_F:  Delta_n(hole) = N(14.7132) - N(E_F_target).
- DOS(E_F) = dN/dE via central difference (dE = 0.02 eV).

CORRECTION vs RTSC_21
---------------------
RTSC_21 quoted "hole-dope ~0.6-0.8 e/cell" from a crude path-sampled DOS (only the bands the
high-symmetry path happens to cross). The proper BZ-grid integral here counts ALL bands between
E_F and the flat band -> the true rigid-band hole-doping is MUCH larger, ~4.7 e/cell. The
path-sampled number was an order-of-magnitude underestimate of the electrons to remove.

HONESTY (p7)
------------
- Rigid-band = order-of-magnitude. It assumes the band structure is frozen under doping (no
  self-consistent reconstruction, no lattice/charge response). Real (non-rigid-band) doping shifts
  bands; 4.7 e/cell is a LARGE perturbation where rigid-band is least reliable -> treat x as an
  upper-bound-ish indicative figure, not a precise dopant target.
- sigma=0.10 eV Gaussian occupation smears the flat-band spike; the true N(E_F) on a sharp flat
  band could be even higher with finer k-mesh + smaller sigma.
- 6x6x6 grid (28 irred k); DOS not k-converged. Single converged SCF reused (no new SCF for the
  rigid-band part).
