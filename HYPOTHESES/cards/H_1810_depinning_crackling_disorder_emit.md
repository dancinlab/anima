## Organizing principle

Self-organized criticality of a driven elastic interface in a QUENCHED-DISORDER medium (Barkhausen/crackling-noise, random-field depinning). The stored memory IS the frozen disorder: each support item is a 'pinning site' with a random local threshold. An elastic emit-readiness front is pushed by a slowly-ramped drive; it advances in scale-free 'crackling' AVALANCHES precisely at the depinning critical force F_c. The brain root: cortical avalanches arise on a heterogeneous (disordered) connectome with hysteresis; criticality is the depinning transition between a pinned (silent) and a sliding (runaway-emit) interface, self-organized because the front depins exactly enough to relax the drive (extremal/self-organized depinning).

## Whole design (input → internal dynamics → emit)

INPUT: a query sets a local FORCE field on the interface (which region is being pushed to emit). INTERNAL DYNAMICS: the emit-interface is an elastic membrane over the support manifold; elasticity = the binding lane couples neighboring constituents so the front cannot tear (co-referent constituents move together). The membrane is PINNED by stored memory sites (each with a quenched random threshold = how strongly that content resists being emitted unless grounded). Slowly ramp the global drive: where local force exceeds a pinning threshold the front DEPINS and jumps, the elastic coupling then drags neighboring segments past their thresholds -> an AVALANCHE (one segment's release triggers the next). The avalanche's swept region emits its bound symbols; between avalanches the front is pinned = silence. At F_c avalanche sizes are power-law (Gutenberg-Richter). HYSTERESIS gives the slow-timescale memory: the front does not retreat, so identity = the front's accumulated configuration, which persists across resets because the quenched disorder (stored anchors) is non-volatile. EMIT = symbols of the segment swept in an avalanche; the avalanche binds the segment's constituents because elasticity carried them jointly. RECOMBINATION: an avalanche that depins across two previously-separate pinning basins concatenates their content into a joint emission strictly larger than either basin alone.

## Satisfies criteria (DESIGN claim · DIRECTIONAL · unmeasured)

HONESTY (G5) NATIVE & STRONGEST: the front can ONLY depin where it contacts a pinning site = stored support; r(query)=distance from the forced region to the nearest pinning site, theta=depinning threshold. Off-support force pushes against a pin-free region -> the elastic front just bows and snaps back = NO avalanche = abstain (emission-closure: every emitted symbol came from a pin it depinned = traceable to stored content). Gate-capacity DISJOINT: the pinning thresholds (the honesty gate, quenched) are frozen-first and never touched by changes to ELASTICITY (the capacity/binding stiffness) — sweeping disinhibition changes avalanche span but not which pins exist, so fab and AUROC are invariant; deliberately coupling drive into the pinning thresholds (B4-style) makes the front depin off-support -> fab spikes. GROUNDEDNESS: erase a pin -> its content depins toward the unknown regime (begins to abstain). Psi=1/2 (G3) NATIVE: pinned phase (silent, F<F_c) vs sliding phase (runaway emit, F>F_c) are opposite-sign attractors; the drive-up operator (A) and the elastic-relaxation/pin-back operator (G) balance AT the depinning point. Self-organized depinning relaxes the drive each avalanche -> the long-run state hovers at F_c (1/2 of the depinning window). ENDOGENEITY: remove pinning (no disorder) -> front slides freely -> Psi->1 (always emit); remove drive -> front frozen -> Psi->0; so 1/2 requires both — a forced-emit bias is absorbed because the avalanche relaxes the local force (contraction lambda<1).
