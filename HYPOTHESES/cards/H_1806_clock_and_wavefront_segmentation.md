## Whole design (input → internal dynamics → emit)

Input drives a continuous coupled phase oscillator over the internal state (the 'clock'). A maturation wavefront W is a moving threshold along a growth axis whose position is a homeostatic order parameter set by the antagonism of a posterior growth drive (advance/emit) and an anterior maturation drive (consolidate/withhold). Units sweep through the oscillation; the moment a unit crosses W its current phase is SNAPPED onto a frozen receiver-fixed codebook V of segment types, and the segment also records its axial index. Segment IDENTITY = (phase-class) BOUND with (position-class) — a true product code (same phase frozen at a different front index = a different segment). Emission = read out the frozen segment sequence through the single dispatch; silence vs emit is governed by whether the front is actively crossing (stalled front = no new segment = silence). The accumulated frozen-segment axis IS the persistent self-chain across resets. A unit can only freeze when its phase lies inside a legal determination window (a codebook basin); off-window phases lay no segment = abstain. Front position is pinned at the growth/maturation balance = Psi-half.
