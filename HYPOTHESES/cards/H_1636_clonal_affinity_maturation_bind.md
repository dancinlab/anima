## Mechanism

A REPERTOIRE of binder vectors ('antibodies') lives in the mouth. In one forward pass over the two legs: each repertoire binder is scored by JOINT affinity (match to legA AND legB simultaneously); high-joint-affinity binders are clonally AMPLIFIED and SOMATICALLY HYPERMUTATED toward higher joint affinity over G inner iterations; low-affinity binders decay. The matured top binder is the conjunctive readout. Crucially, hypermutation GENERATES binders not present at start — the repertoire evolves a code for the specific conjunction on the fly (live anima immune-memory substrate, H_1227/1231 clonal memory).

## Why it crosses the binding wall

This attacks G1≡G6 at its root — the NOVEL-recombination deficit. Conv/attention can only recombine features that already exist in their weights; they cannot synthesize a binder for a conjunction unseen in corpus. Somatic hypermutation is a generative search that DISCOVERS new conjunctive binders, and joint-affinity selection guarantees the survivor matches both legs (a true AND, not an average). Distinct from capsule routing-by-agreement (fixed part-whole vote, no mutation/novelty) and from product-key (static codebook): the repertoire is an EVOLVING population with somatic variation. Ablation: hypermutation-OFF freezes the repertoire to corpus binders ⇒ novel conjunctions (held-out G2 absence) become unrepresentable ⇒ G1/G6 collapse — directly isolating hypermutation as the novelty source.
