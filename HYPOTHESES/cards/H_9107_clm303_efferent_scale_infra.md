## 정직 판정 (c9 — BLOCKED-INFRA, not a science tier; bars NOT moved)
**Neither 🟢 nor 🔴.** Zero of the frozen D1-D5 bars were measured on clm303_clean.clm — not one of the 20 HIGH seeds (let alone the 20 LOW + D3's 24-tick decision-Ψ guard) completed on either pod. This is an **infrastructure/resource wall**, not a science result, and per `a_break_the_wall` type-c / `HYPOTHESES/CLAUDE.md` lesson 2 ("인프라벽 ≠ 과학천장... pool reboot·OOM·SSH-refused = ⏳BLOCKED-INFRA, verdict 아님") it must NOT be recorded as RED or tuned/forced into any D1-D5 outcome.

**Root-cause diagnosis (informative, not itself a verdict):** both independent pods (different vCPU/RAM/provider offer, same unmodified code+command) show the SAME sustained ~4-5GB/min RSS growth throughout a single HIGH seed's deliberation — consistent with `generate_deliberate`'s best-of-K candidate loop (K∈{1..4}) *plus* the harness's own D4 shuffle-control loop independently re-generating up to K-1 more candidates, i.e. up to ~6-8 separate `gen_auto_ideate(ckpt, ...)` calls per HIGH seed, each apparently re-materializing the clm303_clean mouth from its on-disk path rather than reusing the `backend` object already loaded once at harness start. On the tiny d768.clm (4.46MB) this reload cost was invisible (H_9106's aiden/summer pool runs completed a full 20+11 seeds); on the ~34x larger production ckpt (168MB, ~303M params) it compounds far faster than any single seed's compute can finish, exhausting even a 176-220GB pod before HI[0] completes. This is a property of the (frozen, unmodified) `gen_auto_ideate`/mouth-loading path at production scale, not a bug introduced by this measurement attempt, and **fixing it would require touching production `core/generator.hexa`/`core/decode.hexa` code — out of scope for this execution-only task** ("설계 변경 금지, frozen PREREG 그대로").

**Answer to the motivating question is therefore still open**: whether H_9106's "unconditional byte change" (HIGH≡LOW, c0 context/anchor-invariant) is a d768-tiny-ckpt artifact or a production-scale-persistent finding **cannot be determined from this attempt** — the wall is hit before any byte-Hamming comparison is even possible at 303M scale via this harness/path.
