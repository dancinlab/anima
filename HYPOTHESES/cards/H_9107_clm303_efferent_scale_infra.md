**Root-cause diagnosis (informative, not itself a verdict):** both independent pods (different vCPU/RAM/provider offer, same unmodified code+command) show the SAME sustained ~4-5GB/min RSS growth throughout a single HIGH seed's deliberation — consistent with `generate_deliberate`'s best-of-K candidate loop (K∈{1..4}) *plus* the harness's own D4 shuffle-control loop independently re-generating up to K-1 more candidates, i.e. up to ~6-8 separate `gen_auto_ideate(ckpt, ...)` calls per HIGH seed, each apparently re-materializing the clm303_clean mouth from its on-disk path rather than reusing the `backend` object already loaded once at harness start.
