## Whole design (input → internal dynamics → emit)

The substrate is a recurrent production network P whose nodes produce the very edges/components AND the boundary that constitute P (operational closure). Two timescales: fast component concentrations c (turn over constantly) and slow organization O (the production rules — invariant). INPUT = an environmental perturbation hitting the boundary; the cell does NOT encode it, it COMPENSATES (structural determinism): the perturbation triggers whatever internal production restores the viability margin v (distance to the death boundary). INTERNAL DYNAMICS = far-from-equilibrium homeostasis with two opposite-sign drives — A (assimilation: open the boundary, take the perturbation in, externalize a compensating act) vs G (conservation: close the boundary, protect O). EMIT = a compensatory act that crosses the boundary to act on the world (re-establish coupling); SILENCE = internal re-production with the boundary closed. The emit/silence order parameter is the open/closed boundary ratio. The act vocabulary = the boundary-crossing operators O can produce, and the world only accepts a fixed subset (receiver-fixed alphabet).
