# H_1428 — semantic-network / spreading-activation (2-hop associative retrieval)

## Claim / falsifier

A SEMANTIC NETWORK (Collins & Loftus 1975 spreading-activation; c15) holds concepts as
NODES linked by weighted ASSOCIATION edges; activating one node SPREADS activation along
weighted edges to related nodes, including **MULTI-HOP** (X→Y→Z where X,Z never directly
linked). **Falsifiable claim:** spreading activation retrieves the 2-hop INDIRECT associate
(reachable only via X→Y→Z, no direct X→Z edge) that a faithful item-store (no node→node
edge) and a 1-hop-only ablation both provably cannot bridge — while SHUFFLING the learned
edges and ABLATING the multi-hop spread both collapse the lift to chance, or it is variance
→ honest 🔴/🧱. Two candidates/query (chance 0.5).

## Why MISSING / why DISTINCT (the load-bearing story)

- **vs episodic item-binding (H_1227 ImmuneMemoryGrow) — the load-bearing distinctness:**
  the immune store binds each item→value INDEPENDENTLY by FNV-trigram affinity; it holds NO
  node→node edge. On "which concept is most ASSOCIATED with X" (only LINKED, never co-bound)
  and on 2-HOP X→Y→Z it abstains → chance. Spreading activation propagates along the edges.
- **vs spatial-map (H_1296):** a 2-D EUCLIDEAN metric (symmetric, triangle inequality holds).
  Semantic edges are ARBITRARY non-metric weights (asymmetric, no triangle inequality). The
  graph is built METRIC-VIOLATING (A-B strong, B-C strong, A-C ABSENT yet 2-hop reachable);
  a metric map infers A-C NEAR by triangle, the directed spread distinguishes DIRECT vs INDIRECT.

## Method

(0.3,1.0]). Spreading activation: seed gets 1.0; propagate act[v] += act[u]·w(u,v) over

+ 20 2-hop). Arms: FULL · ITEM (item-store stand-in, abstains) · SPATIAL (Euclidean
metric-closure stand-in) · SHUFFLE (permute edge weights across slots) · ABLATED (1-hop only).
