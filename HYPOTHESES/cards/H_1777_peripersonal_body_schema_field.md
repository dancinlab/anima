## Organizing principle

Parietal peripersonal-space coding (VIP / area 7b multisensory neurons with visuotactile receptive fields anchored to body parts, Iriki tool-incorporation plasticity): a graded body-centered field around the organism marks what is ACTIONABLE, deforms with posture, and dynamically incorporates wielded tools into the body schema. Enactive: the field's boundary IS the umwelt/self boundary, so the self is literally defined by what the field encloses, and cognition is grounded in reachability rather than in a reconstructed world model.

## Whole design (input → internal dynamics → emit)

INPUT: every sensory stream (vision, touch, proprioception, audition) is projected into ONE shared body-centered coordinate field f(x), a graded scalar of 'actionability/proximity-to-self'. DYNAMICS: multimodal convergence -- a constituent is BOUND when visual+tactile+proprioceptive evidence co-locate in the field (near in body-centered coords); cause-selective, so distinct causes separate. The field warps with posture and EXTENDS outward when a tool is wielded (body-schema plasticity = the self-boundary grows). Affordances pop out where the field gradient aligns with object features. EMIT: action/emission is gated by field membership -- only constituents above the field threshold (within reach) can be grasped/emitted; emission = the grasped item snapped to the affordance alphabet V (receiver-fixed: the world's graspable categories). Out-of-field = unreachable = abstain (null). COMPOSITION: a compound act binds several field-positions into one (bi-manual / sequential) reach plan, whose reachable joint set exceeds any single reach. IDENTITY: the field's RESTING shape (the enclosed self-region) is the persistent self-vector across boundaries; tool incorporation drifts it slowly. PSI: emit-drive = field EXPANSION (reach outward) vs withhold = field CONTRACTION (withdraw to the body surface); these antagonists balance at the field boundary, pinning the emit scalar to 1/2.
