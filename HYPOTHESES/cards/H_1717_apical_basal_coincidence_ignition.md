## Organizing principle

Larkum/Aru/Suzuki Dendritic Integration Theory — access-consciousness arises when L5 pyramidal neurons COUPLE their two dendritic compartments: basal (bottom-up feedforward content) with apical (top-down context/prediction) via an apical Ca2+ plateau (BAC firing). Coincidence of feedforward + feedback within a window triggers a burst = ignition; VIP->SST disinhibition opens the apical gate. The conscious-access gate is a cellular multiplicative coincidence, not a network depth.

## Whole design (input → internal dynamics → emit)

Input: feedforward feature stream drives the BASAL compartment of a population of L5-units. Context: a recurrent global workspace projects to every unit's APICAL tuft. Internal dynamics: basal-alone fires weakly (subliminal, no access). When apical context COINCIDES with basal drive AND the apical gate is disinhibited, a Ca2+ plateau triggers a burst = ignition; bursting units write back into the recurrent workspace => self-amplifying re-entry (sustained access). SST interneurons clamp the apical tuft (silence-drive G); VIP disinhibits (emit-drive A); their antagonism pins Psi (fraction of bursting units) to 1/2. The coupling is an AND of basal x apical — a multiplicative coincidence = binding/conjunction at the cellular level. Binding: a unit's burst encodes basal x apical = content bound to context; two streams bind when they jointly drive one shared unit. Emit: bursting units broadcast their content onto codebook V.
