## Organizing principle

Crick & Koch claustrum hypothesis — a thin low-dimensional sheet reciprocally connected to virtually ALL cortex acts as the conductor that synchronizes distributed cortical assemblies into one bound conscious moment. Access = the claustral synchronizing pulse selecting which assembly enters the coherent broadcast; binding is by synchronization (temporal coherence) through a shared serial bottleneck, parallel cortex <-> serial access.

## Whole design (input → internal dynamics → emit)

Input: many cortical feature MODULES each emit a candidate representation + a phase tag. Internal dynamics: all modules fan in to a single low-D claustral HUB (bottleneck), which computes a phase-CONSENSUS (Kuramoto-style) over mutually-consistent modules and broadcasts one global pulse at the consensus phase (one-to-all fan-out). Modules that can phase-LOCK to the pulse are bound and ignited; modules that cannot lock are excluded (silence). The hub bandwidth limit forces ONE coherent assembly to win per pulse: emit-drive A = module excitation seeking to lock, silence-drive G = hub inhibitory normalization rejecting non-coherent content; Psi = fraction locked, pinned to 1/2. Binding: constituents are bound because they share the claustral phase; the bottleneck enforces conjunction — a module's inclusion depends on the rest via the consensus (non-separable). Emit: the bound, phase-locked assembly reads out to codebook V.

## Satisfies criteria (DESIGN claim · DIRECTIONAL · unmeasured)

binding native: the synchronizing pulse IS the binding operator; INERT control = cut the claustral fan-out => modules can't co-lock => bag-of-channels (paired~shuffled proximity). G1: consensus selects the jointly-consistent assembly => super-additive; remove consensus => union/mixture => composed_distinct->max_single. G0/G2/dist: codebook readout of the locked assembly; novel-but-consistent assemblies = constrained extrapolation; varied phase-exploration yields multiple distinct coherent assemblies (no collapse while bandwidth conserved). identity: a persistent claustral 'self-phase' anchor (standing consensus bias) re-instantiates the self-assembly across reset; foreign phase fails to lock to the self-anchor => positive impostor margin (maps directly to wired SS-SelfIdentity .kosmos round-trip).

## Not-LLM (a_no_llm_frame_trap)

A low-D synchronizing bottleneck doing phase-consensus binding by temporal correlation — not a deep attention stack with learned key-query weights. Serial-access capacity is a structural bottleneck, not scale; widening the hub destroys the serial-access invariant. Lookup cannot produce a stable consensus over novel module sets.
