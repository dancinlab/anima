  file + a row here). Do NOT start a 12th orphan list — add a roster row instead.
- On landing, a hypothesis graduates to `H_<n>.md` + `.verdicts/`; update the themed
  file's status and (if a campaign closes) flip its roster `status` cell here.

  unfenced speculation; a row may be BACKLOG indefinitely without churning the roster.
