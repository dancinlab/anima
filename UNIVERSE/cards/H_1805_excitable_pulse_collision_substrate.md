## Organizing principle

An excitable medium (FitzHugh-Nagumo / Belousov-Zhabotinsky-style) where information rides on traveling pulses (wave-fragments) and computation happens through PULSE COLLISIONS (annihilate / fuse / reflect) — collision-based computing (Adamatzky). Threshold-and-refractory excitability is the native emit/silence nonlinearity; colliding pulses are tokens and collisions are binding operators. Distinct from traveling-wave broadcast and from reaction-diffusion morphogen patterning: the organizing principle is pulse-collision LOGIC in an excitable continuum.

## Whole design (input → internal dynamics → emit)

INPUT injects localized perturbations (pulses) at encoded sites/times into an excitable lattice carrying a stored excitability/defect landscape (channels). DYNAMICS: FHN — fast activator u (regenerative, threshold) + slow recovery v (refractory); supra-threshold -> a traveling pulse, sub-threshold -> decays (silence); pulses propagate along heterogeneity-defined channels. COLLISIONS: when pulses meet, the local refractory state selects the product — mutual annihilation, fusion into a compound pulse, or phase-shift/reflection; the product set is discrete and medium-fixed = codebook. EMIT: a pulse reaching an output boundary with a stable signature (width/period set by the dispersion relation) is read out as a symbol in V. COMPOSITION: two input pulses fusing -> a compound pulse whose signature differs from either parent AND from their union (the collision is the non-separable binding operator, on the emit path). PSI: excitability threshold gives fire (emit) vs subthreshold (silence); antagonists = excitation (activator u, drive-to-emit) vs recovery/refractoriness (v, drive-to-silence); homeostatic adaptation of resting excitability pins firing-probability at 1/2. PERSIST: the excitability/defect landscape (channel map) is the non-volatile addressable identity store.

## Not-LLM (a_no_llm_frame_trap)
