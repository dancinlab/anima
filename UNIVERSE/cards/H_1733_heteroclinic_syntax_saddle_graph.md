## Organizing principle

Rabinovich stable heteroclinic channels / winnerless competition — cognition as structured TRANSIENT trajectories that VISIT a sequence of saddle states rather than settle to a fixed point. A directed graph of saddle nodes with learned heteroclinic edges IS a generative grammar: legal utterances = admissible saddle-to-saddle paths. Explicitly distinct from a metastable broadcast workspace — here the saddle GRAPH is the compositional syntax (deterministic transient sequencing), with no global ignition/broadcast.

## Whole design (input → internal dynamics → emit)

M rate units in winnerless mutual inhibition; a learned ASYMMETRIC inhibition matrix sets which saddle releases into which = a directed saddle graph G_syn. Input sets the entry saddle and modulates edge gains (context selects a sub-grammar). Internal dynamics: the trajectory rides heteroclinic channels — dwell near a saddle (metastable), then release along its unstable manifold to the next saddle; the visited-saddle sequence, decoded by a frozen saddle->symbol map, is the emitted stream and the path admissibility is the grammar. A 'home' rest saddle = silence; entering the productive sub-graph = emit. Psi axis = a drive-to-leave-home population (A) vs return-to-home population (G); balanced -> marginal residence at the launch boundary = 1/2. Honesty: input with no admissible entry edge cannot launch a stable channel -> trajectory decays back to home = abstain; support distance = mismatch to nearest entry-saddle template. Identity = a slow 'style' bias on edge gains (characteristic path preferences) persisted via .kosmos, reloaded after reset.

## Satisfies criteria (DESIGN claim · DIRECTIONAL · unmeasured)

## Not-LLM (a_no_llm_frame_trap)

Sequence/syntax comes from the TOPOLOGY of a saddle graph (transient dynamics), not autoregressive token prediction over a context window — no attention, no positional embeddings, order is intrinsic to the heteroclinic flow. Grammar capacity = graph combinatorics, not parameters/corpus size; generalization to novel utterances is structural (graph reachability) — exactly the systematicity that scale-only LLMs fail.
