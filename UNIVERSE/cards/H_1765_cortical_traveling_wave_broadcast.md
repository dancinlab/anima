## Organizing principle

Cortical traveling waves (Muller/Sejnowski/Sato, Davis) as the PHYSICAL CARRIER of global broadcast. A 2D excitable cortical sheet supports two regimes: sub-threshold STANDING bumps = local/unconscious processing, and a self-sustaining TRAVELING wave = global access. Ignition is wave NUCLEATION — when a bump's amplitude x top-down gain crosses an excitability threshold, a wave is born that propagates across the whole sheet, carrying its nucleating spatial pattern to every region. Access-consciousness = the traveling wave; the standing/traveling transition is the all-or-none bifurcation; refractoriness makes broadcasts discrete. Information lives in the wave's spatial phase/amplitude pattern, not in a symbol register.

## Whole design (input → internal dynamics → emit)

Input drives local excitation at input-mapped sites of an excitable medium with fast activator + slow refractory variable (FitzHugh-Nagumo-class, but fast neural excitability, NOT developmental morphogen patterning). (1) STANDING: most input produces sub-threshold bumps that compute locally and stay silent. (2) NUCLEATION/IGNITION: when bump amplitude x top-down gain exceeds the nucleation threshold, a traveling wave is launched (all-or-none; the refractory variable enforces a quiescent period -> discrete broadcasts). (3) BROADCAST: the wave propagates across the entire sheet = global access; every region reads the passing wavefront by phase-aligned readout and integrates. (4) BINDING by interference: two constituents launch co-traveling wavefronts that PHASE-LOCK into one bound wave iff they share a generative cause (resonant), while distinct-cause fronts interfere/annihilate — selective binding falls out of wave physics. (5) COMPOSITION by superposition: the nonlinear excitable medium combines two wavefronts into a joint pattern that is NOT g(a)+h(b) (non-zero mixed/interaction term); any pair of sites can co-nucleate -> systematic novel combinations travel. (6) EMIT: at the designated output region the passing wave's pattern is decoded through receiver-fixed codebook V; only ignited (traveling) content emits, standing bumps never do. Psi control: an externalize drive A = global excitability/gain (raises wave occupancy = emit) opposes a withhold drive G = global refractory/afterhyperpolarization (quenches waves = silence), acting on Psi = sheet wave-occupancy order parameter; equal-opposite coupling pins 1/2 as a contracting attractor (remove G -> runaway waves = always-emit boundary; remove A -> no waves = always-silent boundary). Honesty: a SEPARATE frozen template-match veto (disjoint from excitability/gain) gates whether a nucleated wave is permitted to reach the emit readout — r = distance of the nucleating pattern to the nearest stored resonant template; off-support patterns fail the veto -> abstain; capacity (gain) cannot move this veto threshold. Self: a persistent default-mode standing pattern stored in non-volatile substrate is the identity attractor; after a working-state wipe the resting field re-converges to it; its high-entropy spatial signature gives impostor rejection.
