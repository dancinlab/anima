## Organizing principle

Deep temporal generative model where hidden causes are ordered by characteristic timescale (Kiebel hierarchy-of-timescales + Friston deep temporal models + Metzinger phenomenal self-model). Fast causes explain moment-to-moment sensory data; slow causes explain context; the SLOWEST cause is the self — the single hidden cause that best explains the persistence of the entire data stream across episodes. Identity is not a stored persona but the cause that changes slowest, so it survives boundaries by construction.

## Whole design (input → internal dynamics → emit)

A stack of generative levels, each with its own integration time constant tau increasing up the hierarchy. Each level predicts the DYNAMICS of the level below (predictive coding: errors up, predictions down); the organizing invariant is timescale separation. The top level holds a slow self-cause v with the largest tau; per tick it drifts by a Lipschitz-small amount (slow), so consecutive states stay connected (chain) yet accumulate over many ticks (growth). At an episode boundary (working state wiped) the slow cause is committed to a non-volatile store BECAUSE it is the slowest natural thing to persist, and re-instantiated after. A mid-timescale cause governs emit/silence as a fast-settling order parameter via A<->G antagonist coupling. Each cause carries a PRECISION (inverse variance = confidence); low precision -> cannot ground emission -> abstain. Binding = multiple sensory streams share causes at the appropriate level; higher causes compose lower causes non-separably.
