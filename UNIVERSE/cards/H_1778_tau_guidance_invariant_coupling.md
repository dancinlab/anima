## Organizing principle

Lee's general tau theory of ecological action: behaviour is guided DIRECTLY by perceptual invariants -- specifically tau, the ratio gap/(rate-of-gap-closure) ~ time-to-contact -- with no internal world reconstruction. Coordinated multi-effector action arises from TAU-COUPLING: closing several action-gaps in a constant ratio to one another or to an internally generated intrinsic tau-guide (tau_G, a self-paced closing gap from a damped intrinsic motion). ## Whole design (input → internal dynamics → emit)

INPUT: the world arrives as a set of GAPS (current-state minus goal-state) across dimensions, each with a closure rate; the substrate computes tau=gap/gap-rate per gap -- a lawful invariant requiring no reconstruction. DYNAMICS: an intrinsic tau-guide generator (tau_G from a damped intrinsic oscillator) supplies a self-paced template gap that closes on a schedule. Each external gap is COUPLED to tau_G at a chosen ratio k: tau_gap = k*tau_G; this coupling steers closure. Gaps coupled to the SAME guide are synchronized = bound into one coordinated act; the coupling constant k sets the approach dynamics (k -> overshoot/emit vs k -> stall/silence; a balance k gives critical, controlled approach). EMIT: emission fires as a gap closes (tau->0 with controlled landing), quantized onto the receiver-fixed landing alphabet V; the intrinsic tau_G sets emission timing/prosody. SILENCE = no gap, or a gap not closing. COMPOSITION: coupling several gaps to one guide at combined ratios yields a bound multi-gap closure whose reachable repertoire exceeds single-gap closures. IDENTITY: the intrinsic guide's parameters (the self's characteristic tempo/dynamics -- a personal time-signature) persist across boundaries; drift = slow change of intrinsic dynamics; a foreign tempo couples poorly to the self's gaps. PSI: drive-to-close (tau decreasing, emit) vs withhold (tau held, no closure) are antagonists balancing at critical damping, pinning the emit scalar to 1/2.
