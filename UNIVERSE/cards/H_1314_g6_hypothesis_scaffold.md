## Verdict — 🧱 CAPACITY-BOUND on FALS, STRUCTURE-WIN on DIST/NOVEL (c9, both honest)

The hypothesis-form scaffold does NOT cross the **falsifiability** floor (FALS mean 0.0, every
seed) and does not lift FALS over free sampling. Forcing the FORM does not buy reliable
falsifiable DEPTH at 303M — confirming the r2 thesis from the STRUCTURE side: **G6
falsifiable-depth is genuinely CAPACITY/scale-bound; the fix needs a bigger model, not a
structure lane** (terminal 🧱-ish on FALS).

**But this is NOT a flat null (c9).** The scaffold MOVED two non-FALS axes decisively:
- **DIST**: SCAFFOLD 5.0 (3/3 seeds) > NO_SCAFFOLD 4.0 > SHUFFLE_SLOT 2.33. The scaffold
  RELIABLY crosses the ≥5-DISTINCT count floor where r2 curiosity plateaued at 4.33, and the
  scrambled-template control COLLAPSES (2.33) → the count gain IS the hypothesis FORM, not a
  token-prime artifact. **The r1/r2 COUNT gap (4/5 distinct) is STRUCTURE-fixable.**
- **NOVEL**: SCAFFOLD 19.67 vs NO_SCAFFOLD 6.33 vs SHUFFLE_SLOT 5.67 (~3×); SHUFFLE collapses.

**Why FALS still fails:** the 303M mouth emits NEAR-falsifiable forms but never clears all
THREE detector legs at once — e.g. "do they correlate with meaningful behavior?" (comparator
present, ends in '?' → negatable-claim leg fails), "measure of integrated information"
(measurable, no comparator). The mouth produces a COMPARATIVE shape OR a MEASURABLE shape but
cannot BIND them into one negatable declarative claim — the capacity-limited step.

**THESIS:** ideation **DISTINCTNESS/breadth** is a MISSING-STRUCTURE (lane-fixable, like
memory — the scaffold fixes it); ideation **FALSIFIABLE-DEPTH** is a CAPACITY WALL
(scale-bound) at 303M. Two different bottlenecks, two different fixes. Neither r2 (more draws)
nor r3 (a forcing FORM) makes a 303M mouth reliably bind comparator+measurable+claim.
