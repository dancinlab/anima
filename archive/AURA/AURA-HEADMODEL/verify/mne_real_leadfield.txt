VERDICT — 🟡 SUPPORTED-NUMERICAL (real head-model; toy DIRECTIONS survive, toy
          ABSOLUTE R2 does NOT transfer — an honest toy->real limitation found)

This is the real fsaverage 3-layer BEM forward — the ground-truth the AURA
C-axis papers left NOT-MEASURED. Findings, in order of robustness:

(1) DEEP < CORTEX (depth wall) — ✅ DIRECTION CONFIRMED on the real head model.
    The deterministic, well-conditioned measure (gain-column magnitude ||G[:,s]||,
    the physical signal a source projects to scalp) falls monotonically with
    cortical depth: 1.087e3 (shallow) -> 8.568e2 (deep), a x1.27 drop. The
    ridge-R2 measure collapses to the noise floor at depth (0.040 -> 0.000).
    Same DIRECTION as the gaussian toy (C15) and the 3-shell sphere (C18):
    deeper source -> weaker scalp signal -> worse recovery. AURA C16 (cortex
    reachable) / C17 (deep nuclei hard non-invasively) survive on real geometry.

(2) ELECTRODE SATURATION — ✅ C10 CONFIRMED on the REAL head model: NO saturation.
    Both the captured-signal magnitude and R2 keep climbing with electrode count,
    monotonically to 256 channels: R2 16->256 = 0.005,0.007,0.014,0.021,0.026
    (deltas +0.002,+0.007,+0.007,+0.004, all positive); mean||G|| captured grows
    203 -> 841. The real BEM behaves like the 3-shell physical model and C10's
    exp/power-law kernels (un-saturating), NOT like the gaussian strawman of C5
    (which flattened, delta collapsing to +0.022 @1024). The lambda sweep shows
    the M32->M256 climb stays POSITIVE for all well-conditioned lambda (1e-1:
    +0.010, 1e-2: +0.018). FINAL RULING: "electrode saturation = gaussian-only
    artifact" is confirmed by a real, physically-meshed head model.

(3) DEPTH-WALL STEEPNESS is far GENTLER on the real head (gain-magnitude axis).
    cortex shallow->deep gain-magnitude drop:
        REAL MNE cortical  ||G||  x1.27   (1.087e3 -> 8.568e2)
        gaussian toy (C15)  R2    x8.4    (0.82 -> 0.10)   [different axis]
        3-shell sphere(C18) R2    x15.4   (0.239 -> 0.016) [different axis]
    On the directly-comparable physical signal-strength axis the real wall is
    very gentle (x1.27); the toys' R2 ratios (x8.4, x15.4) reflect their smooth-
    kernel inverse behaviour, NOT the real head's signal physics. Direction is
    robust across all models; absolute steepness is model/metric-dependent.

(*) METHODOLOGY FINDING (honest, load-bearing): the toy's SIMPLE-RIDGE R2 metric
    does NOT transfer to a real BEM gain. On the real ill-conditioned 343x2052
    gain matrix, ridge-R2 sits at the noise floor (0.0-0.05) at the well-conditioned
    lambda (1e-2) and goes strongly NEGATIVE at lower lambda (1e-3 -> -0.05/-0.34;
    1e-4 -> -0.26/-0.34). The 0.2-0.8 R2 the toys reported is an artifact of their
    smooth, well-conditioned synthetic kernels — it does NOT survive on a real
    meshed head with simple ridge. (cf MEMORY: toy-scale results don't auto-transfer
    to production scale.) The robust real-head signal is the gain-column MAGNITUDE,
    not the toy R2. The MNE-native dSPM resolution-metric path (peak-localization-
    error) was attempted but hit an MNE 1.12.1 resolution_metrics indexing bug with
    loose-orientation forwards (IndexError in _localisation_error) — deferred.

VOLUME (Q1b) honest caveat: subcortical gain-magnitude was NOT monotone in depth
    here (x0.96, essentially flat) because the volume voxels span both hemispheres
    and the depth was referenced to the cortical centroid (some "deep"-binned voxels
    sit near contralateral cortex). The R2 measure did drop (0.047 -> 0.013). The
    clean subcortical depth-wall is better shown by the cortical-eccentricity axis
    (Q1); the volume result is reported as-is (no monotone subcortical claim made).

NOT-MEASURED (honest):
  - subject-specific anatomy (this is fsaverage, the FreeSurfer AVERAGE head, not
    an individual MRI; individual skull thickness/anisotropy will vary).
  - skull conductivity anisotropy (radial != tangential) — MNE BEM uses isotropic
    per-shell conductivity by default.
  - MNE-native dSPM/PSF resolution metrics (resolution_metrics bug, MNE 1.12.1).
  - measured-EEG inverse benchmark (synthetic sparse sources, as in the toys).
  - absolute R2 on real BEM (near noise floor with simple ridge — needs a proper
    sparse/Bayesian inverse, not the toy's ridge, to be meaningful).
