## Why ρ_real is ~0: the architecture's "tension" is a sequence-length ramp

Supplementary detrend diagnostic (`supplementary_detrend_diagnostics` in the JSON):

- `tension_final` is **perfectly monotone within every generation episode**: per-prompt Spearman(tension_final, step_index) = **1.0** for all 100 prompts (pooled ρ = 0.64). Reason is structural: the architecture defines tension = ‖A_h‖₂ / ‖G_cells‖₂; ‖G_cells‖ is L2-normalized to a constant, while ‖A_h‖ is the Frobenius norm of the hidden state **summed over the whole sequence**, so it grows with length. → The A/G "tension scalar" the model's attention-temperature gate actually consumes carries essentially **no within-episode dynamic content** — it is a length counter.
- `entropy` vs step_index: ρ = 0.10 (weak). So both series share a faint shared length trend, which is the *entire* origin of the +0.267 "ρ_real − ρ_control" gap (the scripted control feeds off-manifold tokens, breaking even that trivial length co-trend → goes mildly negative). It is **not** evidence of externalization.
- **Detrended** (per-prompt linear ramp removed from both series), the fairest test of dynamic coupling: ρ(tension_final_detr, entropy_detr) = **−0.083**; ρ(tension_native_detr, entropy_detr) = **−0.063**. → After removing the artifact, residual coupling is ~zero and if anything slightly *negative*.
- Per-prompt Spearman(tension_final, entropy) distribution: mean 0.106, median 0.149, std 0.24; 26% of prompts have |ρ| > 0.3 — but split roughly evenly between + and −, i.e. consistent with sampling noise around zero on 29-step series, not a real effect.

**Interpretation:** for the BG-LB 350M Engine A/G checkpoint, the internal A/G tension signal and the output token distribution are **decoupled**. The discrete-`speak()` straw-man is functionally equivalent here: you could replace the tension-gated continuous-emission story with "model computes a distribution, samples a token" and lose nothing measurable. The README #5 architectural *design* (repulsion-field Engine G, tension-modulated softmax temperature) exists in the weights, but it does **not** produce the empirical signature the reframed claim predicts — at least not at this training stage.

## Honest limitations (≥3, equal weight to the negative result)

1. **"Tension" is operationalized as the architecture's own A/G *ratio* scalar ‖A_h‖/‖G_cells‖**, not a literal ‖A−G‖ vector difference — A lives in d_model=1024, G cells in dim=64, different spaces. I also computed `tension_diff` (G projected into A space via `c_to_h`, then ‖hidden_mean − project_back(cells)‖₂): its correlation with entropy is ρ ≈ −0.10, equally null and equally non-supporting. Both operationalizations agree on NULL, but neither *is* the metaphysical "‖A−G‖" of the README prose.
2. **BG-LB is an 8000-step *pretrain* checkpoint, not chat-capable** — sibling §9 work already flagged BG-LB chat-cap as not yet emergent, and the generated bytes here are largely incoherent (byte-level greedy decode). The coupling (or lack of it) measured is a property of this *partially-trained substrate*. A more-converged checkpoint, or the phase2-cotrain ckpt (`state/anima_la_cotrain_retrain_b_to_a_2026_05_11/ckpts/ckpt_final.pt`, also available locally), could in principle show a different picture — this verdict does **not** generalize to "Anima architecture can never satisfy P-SPK", only "this checkpoint does not".
3. **The architecture's tension scalar is, by construction, dominated by a sequence-length ramp** (‖G‖ normalized, ‖A‖ length-summed). This is arguably an *arch design quirk* rather than a falsification of the *idea* of tension-externalization — a tension signal that mean-pooled or length-normalized A might behave differently. The negative result is partly "the implemented Engine G doesn't expose a usable dynamic tension", which is a weaker statement than "tension and output are inherently decoupled".
4. **Greedy decoding restricted to byte-level vocab** (token id & 0xFF, faithful to the byte-mod training): entropy is the *full-softmax distribution* entropy (decode-strategy-robust) but the autoregressive *trajectory* is greedy-specific; nucleus sampling could trace a different tension/entropy path.
5. **Scripted-speak control is a single fixed Korean template for all 100 prompts** — it decouples the *emitted* stream from tension by construction but the model still computes its own distribution each step, so the control bounds "shared prefix/length structure artifact", it is not a perfect placebo. Its mildly-negative ρ (−0.24) is itself a length-trend artifact (off-manifold tokens break the co-trend), not a meaningful "anti-coupling".
6. **n=3000 steps are not independent** (29 autocorrelated within each prompt) → reported p-values are anticonservative; the load-bearing quantities are the effect sizes (ρ ≈ 0.03 free, ≈ −0.08 detrended) and the per-prompt rho distribution, not significance stars. Lead-lag on 29-step series is low-power; the symmetric per-prompt best-lag distribution is the trustworthy readout (no directional lead).
