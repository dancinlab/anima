\section{Future work}\label{sec:future}

The \qmirror{}~2.0 axes (specified in
\texttt{anima/docs/qmirror\_2\_axes\_spec\_2026\_05\_03.md} and ranked
in \texttt{state/qmirror\_2\_axes\_2026\_05\_03/ranked\_axes.json})
extend the substrate to:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{cond.9 process tomography} (rank~1, USD~0). Reconstruct
  Choi-state $\rho$ via $4^{n}$ Pauli POVMs on Aer state-vector at
  $\le 4$ qubits; fidelity threshold $\ge 0.99$ across
  $\{$Hadamard~1q, CNOT~2q, Toffoli~3q, QFT~3q$\}$. Compressed-sensing
  variant per Flammia--Gross~\cite{Flammia-Gross-2012} optional.
\item \textbf{cond.10 GHZ-3 + Mermin witness} (rank~2, USD~0).
  Generate 3-qubit GHZ~\cite{GHZ-1989} on Aer; measure
  Mermin-3~\cite{Mermin-1990}
  $M = \langle XYY\rangle + \langle YXY\rangle + \langle YYX\rangle
   - \langle XXX\rangle$;
  classical bound $\lvert M\rvert \le 2$, quantum bound
  $\lvert M\rvert \le 4$, GHZ analytic $\lvert M\rvert = 4$.
  Falsifier band: $M \in [3.7, 4.0]$, $\min(M) \ge 3.5$ across
  30 trials.
\item \textbf{cond.11 stabilizer measurement primitive} (rank~3,
  USD~0). Non-destructive $Z\!\otimes\!Z$ and $X\!\otimes\!X$ parity
  via ancilla + CNOT gadgets on 4 qubits (2 data + 2 ancilla);
  foundational for QEC. Falsifier: syndrome-plus ratio $\ge 0.99$
  plus post-state fidelity $\ge 0.99$.
\item \textbf{cond.12 surface code distance-3 toy} (rank~4, USD~0).
  17-qubit (9 data + 8 ancilla) $3 \times 3$ lattice; weight-4 $X$-
  and $Z$-stabilizers per Kitaev~\cite{Kitaev-2003} and
  Fowler--Mariantoni--Martinis--Cleland~\cite{Surface-Code-2012};
  logical $|0_{L}\rangle$ prep + 1 round of stabilizer measurement
  + logical $Z_{L}$ destructive readout. \textbf{Not fault tolerance}:
  no decoder, no error model, no logical Cliffords (a la
  Knill--Laflamme~\cite{Knill-Laflamme-1998}).
\item \textbf{cond.13 chained sequential CHSH} (rank~5, USD~0--25).
  Two sequential Bell pairs on the same backend with controlled time
  gap; pair-pair statistical independence ($\chi^{2}$ $p$-value
  $\ge 0.05$); aggregate witness $W = (S_{1} + S_{2})/2 \ge 2.7$ with
  $\sigma \le 0.10$. Optional USD~25 IBM Heron 2-pair anchor.
\end{itemize}

Deferred axes (impact $\times$ feasibility scored low):

\begin{itemize}
\item Variational quantum classifier (VQC, $I\!\times\!F = 9$): no
  \texttt{anima}/\texttt{nexus} downstream consumer yet identified.
\item Magic-state distillation~\cite{Bravyi-Kitaev-2005}
  ($I\!\times\!F = 10$): requires $\ge 30$ qubits for non-trivial
  yield; saturates Aer ceiling.
\item Random-circuit sampling / quantum supremacy
  ($I\!\times\!F = 8$): metric meaning is INVERTED for \qmirror{},
  whose entire premise is classical simulation. Loophole-free
  Bell~\cite{Hensen-2015} is also out of scope (no spacelike
  separation in classical simulator).
\end{itemize}

Total \qmirror{}~2.0 wall: 9 days sequential or 5 days two-lane
parallel; total cost USD~0 floor, USD~25 ceiling.
