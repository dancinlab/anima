  the assumption that arXiv \texttt{quant-ph} is the natural
  category. If retargeting to \texttt{cs.LG} / \texttt{cs.AR},
  switch to \texttt{\textbackslash documentclass\{article\}} (or
  IEEEtran) and re-flow tables.
