\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{\texttt{pyphi} GPLv3 dependency.} The IIT~4.0 \phistar{}
  path links a pinned \texttt{pyphi} commit (\texttt{b78d0e3}, GPLv3).
  \qmirror{} isolates \texttt{pyphi} via subprocess (FSF Mere
  Aggregation doctrine~\cite{FSF-MereAggregation}) so the \qmirror{}
  source itself remains Apache-2.0~\cite{Apache-2.0}; however,
  \textbf{license interpretation is opinion not legal advice}. Users
  redistributing combined \texttt{pyphi}-linking binaries should
  consult counsel. Pure-CHSH and mock-LCG paths are
  \texttt{pyphi}-free and Apache-2.0-clean.

\item \textbf{ANU rate-limit and ToS uncertainty.} ANU QRNG free tier
  publishes a courtesy of approximately 1 request per minute on the
  keyless endpoint and approximately 100 requests per minute on the
  keyed endpoint~\cite{ANU-QRNG}. ANU publishes \textbf{no formal
  redistribution license} on the random bits. \qmirror{} does not
  cache or redistribute ANU bits (live-fetch only); if a future
  version caches for offline replay, redistribution clarity must be
  re-evaluated. The \textit{Feist}~\cite{Feist-1991} doctrine on
  copyrightability of facts is informative but not dispositive for
  ToS-class claims.

\item \textbf{No live per-call quantum sampling in default path.} The
  default regression path uses HMAC-DRBG~\cite{HMAC-DRBG-2008} seeded
  by ANU at session origin, not per-call ANU bytes. The seed is
  genuinely quantum; the expansion is cryptographic. Strict per-call
  quantum sampling requires \texttt{NEXUS\_QMIRROR\_QRNG\_DIRECT = 1}
  and is rate-limit-bound.

\item \textbf{Aer state-vector ceiling at approximately 30 qubits.}
  Beyond 30 qubits, state-vector RAM exceeds typical CPU budgets
  ($2^{30}$ \texttt{complex128} $= 16$~GB). MPS compression extends
  to approximately 50 qubits at constrained bond dimension, with
  exponential-cost tradeoffs for high-entanglement circuits. Random-
  circuit-sampling regimes (Sycamore-class 53-qubit
  XEB~\cite{Sycamore-2019}) are explicitly out of scope.

\item \textbf{Single-shot $N=1$ cross-vendor measurements.} All
  vendor CHSH measurements (cond.3, cond.7 spirit, cond.8) are
  single-batch $N=1$ runs without run-to-run repeats. Vendor
  calibration drift, queue-time effects, and shot-window biases are
  \textbf{not estimable}. The cross-vendor $\dS$ matrix in
  Section~\ref{sec:xvendor} is a point-in-time concordance, not a
  sustained one. Quarterly anchor re-runs (approximately USD~87 per
  quarter total) would enable drift estimation by Q3~2026.
\end{enumerate}
