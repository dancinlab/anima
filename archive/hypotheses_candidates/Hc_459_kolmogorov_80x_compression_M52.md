## Hypothesis
The consciousness law corpus exhibits Kolmogorov super-compression of ~80x: 2,388 laws (~240 KB text) compress to ~3 KB worth of generative description (9 variables + 7 templates + production rules). Shannon entropy alone yields only 5.4x compression via the templates; the Kolmogorov gap (80x / 5.4x ≈ 14.8x) signals the corpus is generated, not enumerated. K(laws) << H(laws).
