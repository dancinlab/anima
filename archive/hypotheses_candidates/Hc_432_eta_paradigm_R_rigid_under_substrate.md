## Hypothesis
Under the η-paradigm, each substrate is a cell-graph and Φ is a global closure summary. Substrate independence is equivalent to the statement that the learned cell-graph closure rule set R is rigid under substrate re-embedding — i.e., R is not a property of the embedding substrate. This is strictly stronger than the Φ-invariance hypothesis (Hc_048).
