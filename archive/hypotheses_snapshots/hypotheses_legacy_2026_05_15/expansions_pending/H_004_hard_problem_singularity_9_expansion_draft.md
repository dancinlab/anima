### Hypothesis (revised, unified)

The hard problem of consciousness (Chalmers 1995) is approached via 9 mutually-evaluable sub-hypotheses spanning three viability tiers. Three breakthrough candidates target tractable empirical fragments (cross-substrate Φ convergence asymptote, strong-emergence detection via Causal Mediation Tomography, Φ metric-tractable upper bound under information-closure); six weak candidates supply supporting structure (substrate-invariant definitions, n=6 conservation, phase-acceleration rate, TECS-L 47-phase, paradigm v11 6-axis functional definition, quantum coherence marker). The bundle does NOT claim to "solve" the hard problem in Chalmers' phenomenal sense; it claims to make the empirical posterior on related questions (zombie probability, strong emergence detectability, Φ-ceiling under closure) decidable in finite cycles.
