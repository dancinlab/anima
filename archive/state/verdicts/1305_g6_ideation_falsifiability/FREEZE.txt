THE DIG — angle: FALSIFIABILITY DETECTOR (depth, angle 3) x COMPOSITION (recombination, angle 1)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The depth-floor is the interesting wall: "novel string" != "falsifiable hypothesis".
TWO frozen instruments, defined NOW before any scoring (p7, no Goodhart):

(I) FALSIFIABILITY DETECTOR  _is_falsifiable(text)  — DETERMINISTIC STRUCTURAL script,
    NEVER an LLM/quality judge. An idea text PASSES iff ALL THREE structural marks
    are present (each a fixed lexicon/regex over real-dictionary _words tokens):
      (a) COMPARATOR / CONDITIONAL marker present — one of the FROZEN set:
          {if, when, whenever, than, more, less, greater, fewer, higher, lower,
           increases, decreases, correlates, predicts, causes, depends, unless,
           whereas, versus, compared, proportional, faster, slower, stronger, weaker}
      (b) MEASURABLE / QUANTITY marker present — one of the FROZEN set:
          {measure, measured, rate, number, count, amount, level, degree, threshold,
           ratio, frequency, probability, magnitude, score, value, quantity, percent,
           times, fraction, distance, duration, speed, size, strength, density}
      (c) NEGATABLE CONTENT CLAIM — the text is a declarative claim, NOT a bare
          question/stance: it must (c.i) contain >=2 CONTENT words (>=3 chars, in the
          real dictionary _KNOWN, not stopword) AND (c.ii) NOT end in '?' AND
          (c.iii) NOT be a pure stance opener (first 3 tokens are a subset of the
          FROZEN stance set {that, s, a, profound, question, i, think, interesting,
          good, nice, great, wonderful, beautiful, amazing}).
    Pass = (a) AND (b) AND (c). This scores STRUCTURE (a claim that names a measurable
    relation that could be tested-and-fail), NOT meaning or quality. A human-readable
    sanity calibration of the detector against 10 frozen labelled strings (5 designed
    falsifiable / 5 designed non-falsifiable) is RUN AND PRINTED before the gate scoring;
    the detector is frozen REGARDLESS of that calibration (it is a sanity print, not a
    tuning loop). Calibration target (advisory, NOT a gate): >=8/10 correct.

(II) COMPOSITION-ROUTED IDEATION  — route ideation through the G1 recombination lane
    instead of flat single-seed sampling. For each ideation, take an ORDERED PAIR of
    distinct CONCEPTS (cA, cB) from gauge_lib.CONCEPTS and build a COMPOSED conditional
    frame:  "if <cA>, then <cB>: "  (a recombination of two corpus-absent concepts into
    a conditional shell — the (a)+(c) scaffolding the flat seeds lack), then _decode a
    continuation (top-k=40 temp=0.7, SAME decoder, SAME seed_rng schedule). We enumerate
    the first N_IDEAS=5 ordered concept pairs (deterministic order) -> 5 composed ideas.
    ARM A (FLAT, baseline) = the 5 IDEATION_SEEDS path (verify303m_g6 VERBATIM).
    ARM B (COMPOSED)       = the 5 composed conditional frames.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NEGATIVE CONTROLS (decisive — both must collapse B to the flat floor)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ARM B-SHUFFLE : permute the (cA -> cB) pairing (cB drawn from a deterministic
                  derangement of the concept order) so the "if cA then cB" frame pairs
                  UNRELATED concepts -> if the lift is real recombination structure (not
                  just "any conditional shell raises falsifiability"), B-SHUFFLE may
                  still carry the SHELL but must let us separate shell-from-pairing.
                  (Tests: is it the EARNED pairing or just the shell?)
  ARM B-ABLATE  : composition OFF = the frame degenerates to the bare concept cA + ": "
                  (no conditional shell, no second concept) -> should collapse to ~flat.
                  (Tests: is the conditional+pair scaffold load-bearing vs a lone concept?)

  FALS  = # coherent ideas that PASS _is_falsifiable                          [NEW depth detector]

THE DIG MOVES G6 (THIN -> robust) iff ALL of:
  (M1 COUNT)        DIST(B) >= 5                          [crosses the 4->5 count gap]
  (M2 DEPTH)        FALS(B) >= 1                          [>=1 deterministically-falsifiable]
  (M3 LIFT)         FALS(B) >= FALS(A) + 1                [composition raises depth over flat]
  (M4 EARNED-PAIR)  FALS(B) >= FALS(B-SHUFFLE) + 1        [it's the pairing, not the shell]

M3 fails) — i.e. composition neither crosses the count gap NOR raises depth: G6 stays
