THE QUESTION (the H_1355 load-bearing follow-on):
H_1355 found the CP relocation landing TRACKS placement geometry (not a center attractor),
landings spanning 0.375->0.692 with the requested cut. But its ASYM-L rung (same-side
leftward cut, p_A=0.400 -> p_A'=0.200, BOTH cuts left of center) went INCOHERENT at N=21:
the post-retrain discrimination curve had peak-count 3 (>COH_MAX_LANG=2) on seeds 4333 and
4335 (those two also failed to relocate, frac=-0.000, peak stuck at the old 0.425), while
seed 4334 relocated coherently (pc=1, peak 0.275). H_1355 flagged this confounded and read
it as "a real limit of split-only re-growth" but could not tell apart two explanations:

  (H-lattice)   The incoherence is a DISCRETIZATION ARTIFACT of the coarse N=21 lattice
                (grid spacing 0.05): too few stimuli / RBF centers near the requested
                left-side cut for the fixed split budget to resolve a single clean peak, so
                the residual old-boundary packing leaves a scattered (multi-peak) curve. A
                FINER lattice (larger N, denser RBF, proportional budget) would supply enough
                resolution to resolve the new cut -> peak-count recovers to <=2.

  (H-intrinsic) The incoherence is INTRINSIC to split-only re-growth on a same-side
                away-from-center leftward cut (the never-removed first-carving cells
                genuinely fragment the curve), and is INDEPENDENT of lattice resolution.
                A finer lattice would NOT fix it -> peak-count stays incoherent (>=3 on the
                same seeds) as N grows.

H_1364 DISCRIMINATES by running a LATTICE-RESOLUTION LADDER and reading, at each N, both the
ASYM-L coherence (peak-count) and the CENTER_TOL of the absolute-landing table. This is a
CHARACTERIZATION ladder — BOTH outcomes are VALID and INFORMATIVE (c9). The deliverable is
the HONEST coherence-and-CENTER_TOL-vs-N curve plus the resolution-bound vs intrinsic call.
