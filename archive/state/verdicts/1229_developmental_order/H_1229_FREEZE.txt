  difficulty(line) = z(length_bytes) + z(rarity) + z(syntax_complexity), where
    length_bytes      = len(line)                                  (longer = harder)

                        (rarer characters/words = harder)
    syntax_complexity = (#non-alphanumeric-non-space bytes)/len     (punctuation/markup density =
                        compositional/structural complexity proxy)
  z() = per-feature standardize over all lines. Lines sorted ASCENDING by difficulty = easy->hard.

STAGING SCHEDULE (frozen): N_STAGES = 4 developmental stages (quartiles of the difficulty-sorted

  window: early window = easy material; harder material enters later). STEPS split evenly across the
  4 stage-windows (each window = STEPS/4 steps; window s samples uniformly from stages 0..s).
  Total examples seen, total steps, total tokens are IDENTICAL to flat across all arms.

THREE ARMS (identical model init seed, identical hyperparams, identical step/token budget):
