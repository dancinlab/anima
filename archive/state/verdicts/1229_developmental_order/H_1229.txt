MECHANISM (toy reading): a flat shuffle gives the model i.i.d. exposure to the FULL difficulty
distribution from step 0, so its weights see the rare/long/compositional bytes throughout training and
under cosine-LR decay settle on a broad next-byte distribution -> more distinct corpus-absent
continuations. The easy->hard curriculum spends its early (high-LR) budget on the easiest quartile
(short, high-frequency, low-punctuation lines); by the time the hard quartile unlocks the LR has
decayed, so the compositional/rare material is under-learned, NARROWING the generative distribution
(fewer distinct ideas, lower kwr). This is the classic curriculum-vs-i.i.d. tension surfacing at toy
scale: staged windows trade breadth for an early-easy bias that the small model never recovers from.
