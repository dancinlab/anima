dispatch-KL mechanism (NEW arm, does NOT edit array_moe / model.py):
  router emits per-token expert probs P (softmax over E logits, per (B,T)).
  let f = batch-averaged dispatch distribution = P.mean over (B,T)  (shape E).
  let u = uniform = 1/E each.
  L_kl = KL(u || f) = sum_i u_i * (log u_i - log f_i)  (balanced-target distill).
  total loss = CE + aux(baseline) + KL_COEF * L_kl.
  baseline arm = identical config with KL_COEF = 0 (the AB variant, untouched).

  dispatch_entropy = H(f) (nats) of the batch-averaged dispatch distribution f.
  held-out CE = next-byte cross-entropy on a held-out eval stream (quality).
