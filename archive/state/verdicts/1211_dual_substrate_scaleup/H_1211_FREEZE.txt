WHY DIM stays 8 (honest scope of the ladder axes)
-------------------------------------------------
DIM=8 is STRUCTURAL: H_1163 _byte_feature emits exactly 8 byte-statistics per window
(VERBATIM H_1160 feature). Scaling DIM would require a DIFFERENT feature function =
NOT a verbatim reuse + would change the mechanism, confounding the scale test. So the
ladder does NOT vary DIM (stated honestly). The arc's toy-scale concerns that ARE
testable verbatim — short stream length, one small corpus, small alphabet — are the
ones varied. (a_scale_honest_scope: the un-scaled axis is declared, not hidden.)
