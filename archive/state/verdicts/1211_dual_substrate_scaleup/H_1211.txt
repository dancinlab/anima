WHAT BREAKS (the trajectory half — TOY ARTIFACT, located + mechanism explained)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The H_1208/H_1209 TRAJECTORY gate (GATE-B transition-predictability) separation on the
ORDERED WALK stream COLLAPSES as stream length grows:
  WALK/WALK_SHUF  =  10.916 (T=2400)  ->  2.629 (T=24000)  ->  1.136 (T=240000, FAIL).
The toy 10.9x is NOT scale-stable; by 100x it is statistically a non-separation.

MECHANISM (root cause c1, why the trajectory gate decays with T):
  GATE-B fires when a realized prototype-transition (prev->cur) is PREDICTABLE: prev
  seen >= MIN_PREV=3 AND P(cur|prev) >= CONF_FLOOR=0.34 from an ONLINE count table over
  a FIXED alphabet of N_PROTO=24 prototype ids. With only 24 symbols and a stream of
  length T, the count table SATURATES: as T grows, EVEN A SHUFFLED proto-id stream
  accumulates enough mass on each (prev,*) row that some realized transition crosses
  the 0.34 confidence floor BY CHANCE. So WALK_SHUF division climbs toward WALK division
  (toy WALK_SHUF mean 91.7 -> 100x 120575), erasing the separation. The trajectory gate's
  "predictability" signal is only distinguishable from chance when the alphabet is large
  relative to the observed transition count — a finite-alphabet / finite-sample effect,
  not a property of order itself. (The per-seed variance also explodes: at T=24000 the
  WALK_SHUF seeds are [96, 6893, 7640] — a regime where some shuffles saturate and some
  don't, a classic small-alphabet saturation signature.)

AXIS-P CONFIRMS THE MECHANISM (the diagnostic that nails it):
  At fixed toy T=2400, ENLARGING the alphabet RESTORES + sharpens the separation and
  drives the sanity floor to ZERO:
    N_PROTO=24  -> WALK/WALK_SHUF  10.9   sanity 1.750
    N_PROTO=64  -> WALK/WALK_SHUF 152.5   sanity 0.000   (WALK_SHUF collapses to 2.0)
    N_PROTO=128 -> WALK/WALK_SHUF  28.5   sanity 0.000
  A richer alphabet makes chance-predictability much harder, so the shuffle stops firing.
  => the collapse on AXIS-T is alphabet-SATURATION (fixed 24 symbols vs long T), NOT a
     failure of the predictability principle. With N_PROTO scaled WITH T the trajectory
     gate would likely separate again — but at the FROZEN toy alphabet the trajectory
     gate is NOT scale-stable in stream length, which is exactly what F2 measures. (The
     N_PROTO=64/128 sanity of 0.000 also retires the H_1208/H_1209 toy ARTIFACT-WARN: at
     N_PROTO=24 the sanity 1.75 was small-int noise [2.33 vs 1.33]; richer alphabets show
     the gate genuinely does not fire on i.i.d. noise.)
