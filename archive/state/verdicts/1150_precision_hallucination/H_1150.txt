=== FINDING (closed-negative, rules out an axis) ===
Prior-precision-as-temperature does NOT drive corpus-absence hallucination at toy scale (F1 null/anti),
and the free-energy precision-balance brake (re-weight an over-sharp prior toward the corpus-bigram
likelihood term) does NOT surgically cut fabrication where the entropy-brake (H_1146) failed — it only
"cuts" by collapsing coherence (kwr -0.134, d_degr +1.22), which the frozen coherence gate correctly
rejects. RULES OUT the 'temperature = aberrant-precision => more hallucination' axis AND the
'mix-toward-bigram-evidence corrects it' axis at toy scale. Unifying lesson with H_1146/H_1148: the
byte-LM's hallucination (corpus-ABSENCE) is decoupled from BOTH its realized confidence (H_1146) AND its
prior sharpness (H_1150) — sharpening the prior commits to FREQUENT (=corpus-present) byte-strings, the
OPPOSITE of the conditioned-hallucination prediction. The H_1148 within-T confident-fabrication effect is
a different axis from the across-T precision knob; aberrant-precision-as-temperature is not the toy mechanism.
A faithful free-energy correction needs a TRAINED grounding/likelihood signal, not a raw bigram-mix at inference.
