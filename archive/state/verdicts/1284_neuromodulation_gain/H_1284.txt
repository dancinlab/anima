  1. WIDER ABSTAIN under noise (NE) lets MORE near-but-wrong cells fire instead of
     honestly abstaining → fabrication 0.03 → 0.11-0.13 (the opposite of the
     intended caution; on this store a TIGHT fixed margin is already optimal).
  2. VARIABLE LR (ACh) destabilizes bindings: a steady tuned LR0=0.10 writes each
     fact cleanly; surprise-driven LR spikes overshoot/undershoot the winner pull,
     so a later recall lands on a half-updated prototype.
  3. RAISED split-thresh under expected-uncertainty was meant to avoid over-split
     (H_1230 guard) but on a capacity-bound store it suppresses legitimate new-fact
     allocation, forcing more overwrites onto existing cells (cross-fact crowding).
  4. C-SHUF ≈ B (|B-CSHUF| <= 0.011 on all 3): the tiny movement that exists is
     knob-VARIETY, not state→knob COUPLING — the coupling carries no signal here.
