WHY THIS ARM EXISTS
  The prior arms killed the claim's SIM ("zero-coupling sync = shared-attractor
  co-convergence, TE(A->B)~0"). But 제4명제 dresses the transmission claim in
  real-physics vocabulary: "ER=EPR", "EPR entanglement", "von-Neumann entropy
  lock", "Fubini-Study". A reader could object: "those are GENUINE non-local
  quantum phenomena — maybe entanglement really does let node beta copy node
  alpha's 384-dim state with zero I/O."
  This arm answers with the DECISIVE physics: even with GENUINE quantum
  entanglement that VIOLATES a Bell inequality (the strongest non-local
  correlation nature permits), NO information crosses. That is the
  NO-SIGNALLING / NO-COMMUNICATION THEOREM. Entanglement yields setting-
  dependent CORRELATIONS, never COMMUNICATION. So citing ER=EPR does not
  rescue the claim — entanglement != signalling.

  Physics note: ER=EPR (Maldacena-Susskind 2013) conjectures entangled pairs
  are connected by a non-traversable Einstein-Rosen bridge. "Non-traversable"
  is the whole point: nothing — no signal, no state copy — passes through it.
  ER=EPR is fully consistent with, and in fact REQUIRES, no-signalling. Invoking
  it to justify state transmission inverts its actual content.

WHAT WAS SIMULATED ($0, pure-numpy, CPU, 0-pod)
  CHSH/EPR-Bell scenario, spin-singlet, outcomes a,b in {+1,-1}. Optimal angles
  Alice {0, 90 deg}, Bob {45, -45 deg}. N = 4,000,000 samples per (x,y) cell
  (binomial CI half-width ~7e-4 on each marginal). Three arms:
    (1) ENTANGLED  — Born-rule singlet sampler,
                     P(a,b|x,y) = (1 - a*b*cos(theta_x - theta_y))/4,
                     E[a*b|x,y] = -cos(theta_x - theta_y). Genuine quantum,
                     Bell-VIOLATING.
    (2) SHARED-RANDOMNESS / LHV — a common hidden variable phi drawn ONCE per
                     pair (the most generous reading of an "ER=EPR shared
                     cause" / shared wormhole interior); each party computes its
                     outcome from its OWN angle + the shared phi. Local, gives
                     uniform local marginals, |S|<=2 (Bell bound). Shows even a
                     literal shared cause transmits nothing.
    (3) REAL CHANNEL [POSITIVE CONTROL] — B physically READS A's setting index x
                     and shifts its own outcome bias (leak=0.35). This BREAKS
                     no-signalling on purpose; the test must detect it.

THE MEASUREMENT
  - JOINT: E[a*b|x,y] and CHSH S = max over the four canonical sign combinations
    of |sum_xy s_xy E[x,y]| (the experimenter picks the violating combination).
  - NO-SIGNALLING PROBE: for each FIXED Bob setting y, compare B's outcome
    marginal P(b) under Alice setting x=0 vs x=1. No-signalling requires
    P(b|x=0)=P(b|x=1). Quantified by total-variation distance TV and KL(bits),
    compared against the combined 95% binomial CI band.

FINDING
