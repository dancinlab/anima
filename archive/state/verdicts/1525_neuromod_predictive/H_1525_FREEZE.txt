WHY REACTIVE LOSES (the a-priori diagnosis, a_break_the_wall):
a reactive controller reads the CURRENT surprise and adjusts gain AFTER the
regime has already shifted — it is structurally ONE STEP BEHIND every regime
change. By the time surprise has risen, the damaging writes/splits of the
new regime have already landed. The best-FIXED point wins because a
lagging adjustment is worse than a good compromise constant.

THE NEW ANGLE (genuinely different mechanism-family, a_break_the_wall)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Make the modulator ANTICIPATORY: gate the gain by the PREDICTED next-step
surprise from a FORWARD MODEL, BEFORE the step is taken — pre-adjusting gain
ahead of the regime shift rather than reacting to it.

SUBSTRATE LENS (a_no_llm_frame_trap, c15): anima already has a cerebellar
forward-model lane — VForwardField (H_1280, core/engine_cli.hexa §2697+:
vforward_new/_predict/_err/_update/_correct). The cerebellum's biological
role is exactly forward prediction of the next sensory frame for pre-emptive
motor adjustment. Here the forward model predicts the next-step surprise
signal s_{t+1}; the neuromodulator gates LR / SPLIT_THRESH / abstain on that
PREDICTION, not on the current value.

FORWARD MODEL (pre-registered, simple AR/EMA on the surprise series):
  The forward model is an online linear AR(2) + EMA predictor of the
  per-tick surprise series s_t (recon-error of the current key off the live
  store). It predicts ŝ_{t+1} from (s_t, s_{t-1}, running EMA) with online
  least-mean-squares weight updates (η=0.05) — the cheap numpy stand-in for
  VForwardField's vforward_update tick (DIRECTIONAL; engine-native VForward
  re-test is the GREEN follow-on). The predictor sees ONLY past surprise
  values (no regime label, no future leak); ŝ_{t+1} is formed BEFORE the
  current event's knobs are set.

KNOB MAP (predictive arm gates on ŝ_{t+1}; SAME knob formulas as the wall's
Neuromod, only the input swapped current-s → predicted-next-s):
  ACh plasticity   LR(t)  = clip(LR0*(1 + kA*(ŝ_{t+1} - û)), 0.05, 0.60)
  ACh/novelty split TH(t) = clip(TH0*(1 + kT*(û - TH0)),     0.15, 0.60)
  NE exploration   abstain= clip(abstain0*(1 + kN*(û - TH0)), 0.20, 1.20)
  DA gain          LR(t) *= (1 + kD*reward_ema)
  û = running EMA of surprise (same as wall). kA=1.2 kT=0.8 kD=0.4 kN=0.6
  EMA=0.1 (VERBATIM the wall's Neuromod constants — NOT re-tuned).
The ONLY difference vs the wall's REACTIVE arm is the LR input: reactive
uses s_t (current), predictive uses ŝ_{t+1} (forward-model prediction).

ABLATION (decisive — the anticipation must be load-bearing)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  ABL-NOLOOKAHEAD : replace the forward-model prediction ŝ_{t+1} with the
       CURRENT value s_t (i.e. ŝ_{t+1} := s_t) — removes look-ahead but
       keeps everything else. If ABL-NOLOOKAHEAD ≈ P (within MARGIN), the
       anticipation is INERT and the wall HOLDS (this is exactly the
       reactive arm R by construction → P must SEPARATE from it).

       phase-scrambling the predicted series ŝ (same marginal values,
       decorrelated from the true future). P must COLLAPSE to reactive/
       fixed-level — proves the lift is the predictor's ACCURACY, not
       knob variety.
