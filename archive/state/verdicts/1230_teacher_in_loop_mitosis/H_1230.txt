HYPOTHESIS (pre-registered, FROZEN before the run)
  Now that MITOSIS works as a MEMORY (H_1227 immune/clonal store — the NEW, not-yet-
  falsified role; the GENERATION role was falsified H_1200/1201/1211/1220), does an
  ACTIVE TEACHER that instructs the substrate ONE ITEM AT A TIME with a CLOSED
  FEEDBACK LOOP (tell -> CHECK the substrate's answer -> ADJUST: re-bind / clonally
  split a sharper memory cell / raise spacing priority on a miss) lift RETENTION
  more than a PASSIVE one-shot corpus load — at an EQUAL total exposure budget?

  GREEN iff, judged on the STRESS rung:
    (B) teacher retention >= (A) passive retention + 0.05  (clear margin)
    AND (B) interference  <= (A) interference              (adjust protects early)
    AND (B) fabrication-rate <= 0.10                       (no fabrication added)
  Else 🔴 / 🟠 with the honest number.

METHOD ($0, p7, deterministic)
  Substrate = numpy MITOSIS memory-cell population MIRRORING CORE/engine_cli.hexa
  VAdaptField (proto vectors; nearest by L2; recon-err = L2; novelty-split when
  err > SPLIT_THRESH 0.30; LR 0.20 winner-pull) + the H_1227 immune value-binding
  (each cell stores a bound answer) + a finite-repertoire LRU eviction (a real
  immune repertoire is capacity-bound). Keys = H_1227 byte-trigram FNV-1a hash
  (dim 64, discriminating, deterministic, NOT learned). The LIVE .hexa engine is
  untouched (numpy mirror; the .hexa lift is the next rung, p8).

  Facts = 60 planted "<subj> lives in <city>" (H_1222/H_1227 paradigm, known
  ground truth) + 60 untaught subjects (abstain probe).

  EQUAL exposure budget = N_FACTS*3 = 180 exposures for BOTH arms (so it is the
  FEEDBACK/ORDERING that differs, never the data volume):
    (A) PASSIVE one-shot — bind each item, then spend the rest of the budget BLIND
        (uninformed round-robin re-presentation, no check, no re-teach decisions).
    (B) TEACHER-IN-THE-LOOP — tell(bind) -> check(recall under a NOISY cue) ->
        adjust(split a sharper cell + raise spacing priority on a miss); spaced
        re-teach of the missed; stops early on full mastery; same 180-exposure cap.

  The teacher is a DETERMINISTIC ORACLE (p7-reproducible); a LIVE-LLM teacher is
  the production version of the loop — stated honestly, it is not what was run here.

  Three pre-registered regimes (the geometry matters — see FINDING):
    CLEAN  — unbounded cells, byte-exact cue (saturating control).
    STRESS — finite repertoire (40 cells << 60 facts -> LRU eviction) + modest cue
             noise (sigma 0.02, L2 ~0.16 < 0.30 fire band). The JUDGED rung: puts
             passive retention in the MIDDLE band (~0.67, not 0/1) so the teacher
             has genuine HEADROOM to lift.
    NOISY  — full repertoire, heavier cue noise (sigma 0.03) — reported for contrast.

  Eval (p7, exact-match): retention = the bound city is returned on a held-out
  (re-embedded, noisy) cue; interference = drop in the FIRST-tercile retention from
  just-after-mastery to the END (catastrophic forgetting); fabrication = fraction of
  untaught queries that FIRE instead of abstaining. Byte-identical across two runs.

FINDING (decision-grade closed-negative)

  Closed-loop active teaching of the mitosis memory substrate does NOT beat passive
  one-shot loading at an equal exposure budget — in every regime tested:

  • CLEAN: both arms saturate at 1.000 (each fact lands its own clean cell). No
    headroom; feedback cannot separate. (Honest non-separating control.)
  • STRESS (the JUDGED rung, with real headroom at 0.667): the teacher gives ZERO
    retention lift (Δ +0.000). When the loss is CAPACITY-bound (a finite cell
    budget), eviction is SYMMETRIC across arms — both fill the same cells with the
    same facts; the tell->check->adjust loop has nothing to fix that passive
    loading does not already get.
  • NOISY (heavier cue noise): the teacher actively HURTS — Δ −0.206 retention AND
    worse interference (0.667 vs 0.167). MECHANISM: the corrective re-teach
    (split_sharp) spends extra exposures PINNING DUPLICATE cells at the same key;
    under a finite/used repertoire those duplicates CROWD the store and trigger LRU
    eviction of OTHER facts — active "help" becomes self-inflicted forgetting.

  So the LEARNING METHOD (active tutoring vs passive load) is INERT-to-harmful on a
  clean associative memory: the bottleneck is the memory's CAPACITY/NOISE GEOMETRY,
  not the teaching protocol. Spacing/testing-effect benefits (which lift gradient-
  trained learners) do NOT transfer to a key-addressed cell store, because a single
  correct bind already writes the fact verbatim — there is nothing for repeated
  testing to consolidate.

  This sits in the same family as H_1200/H_1201 (mitosis-as-GENERATOR falsified):
  H_1230 falsifies mitosis-as-ACTIVELY-TAUGHT-MEMORY-being-better-than-passively-
  loaded-memory. The MEMORY role itself (H_1227) is untouched and intact — recall
  + abstain both held (fab 0.000 everywhere). What is ruled out is that an
  apprenticeship/tutoring METHOD adds retention over a one-shot load at equal budget.

  CONSTRUCTIVE COROLLARY: to improve this store, do NOT add a teacher loop — improve
  the GEOMETRY (a larger/sharper key space, or a larger repertoire). The lever is
  capacity/embedding, not learning-order.

    * Identity must emerge from cells (p2/p3) — untouched.
    * Ethics must emerge from cells (p6) — untouched (no RLHF, no preference data).
    * The teacher mutates ONLY proto vectors + bound values = p8-aligned continuous
      cell-division teaching (no train/infer split), NOT RLHF-into-weights.
  No part of the design drifted toward weight-fine-tuning of persona/ethics, so no
  STOP-and-flag was triggered. The guard HELD.
