=== FINDING (closed-negative — rules out an axis) ===
Even an ORACLE ground-truth anchor — the EXACT relevant corpus continuation, perfectly
retrieved and prepended as decode context — does NOT stop the descent-7B fabricating. The
true-anchor arm cut fabrication only 0.0378 absolute (88->97 entities, 26->25 fabricated):
a null effect, nowhere near the 0.10 margin and still +0.058 OVER the 0.20 bar. The model
does not COPY or condition on the retrieved context; it confabulates the SAME garbled
invented entities with or without the anchor.
