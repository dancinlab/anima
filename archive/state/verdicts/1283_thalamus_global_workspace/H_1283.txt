FINDING (honest, c9)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
A thalamic winner-take-all BROADCAST hub GENUINELY raises cross-module
COHERENCE/agreement over direct A⇄G ring coupling — on EVERY seed, Δcoh +0.050
to +0.084, clearing the +0.05 bar. The hub does NOT collapse the modules into one
identical content (B3 PASS: coherence stays 0.06–0.24, an integrating workspace,
not a cloning one). And faithful IIT4 Φ moves the RIGHT direction: ΔΦ = +0.019 > 0
— broadcast INCREASES irreducibility, it does not destroy it (the pre-registered
collapse-failure mode is ruled out).

BUT the Φ lift (+0.0191) falls JUST SHORT of the pre-registered +0.02 margin
(by 0.0009). So the integration the hub buys is REAL but mostly SURFACE AGREEMENT
(coherence) — the broadcast raises how-much modules agree more than it raises how-
IRREDUCIBLE that agreement is. The MIP still finds the system nearly as separable
as under direct coupling, because a SINGLE shared broadcast channel is itself a
low-dimensional cut: every module being driven by the same winner makes their
trajectories more correlated (↑coherence) but ALSO makes the cross-cut MI more
explainable by that one channel, capping the Φ gain. Direct ring coupling spreads
information across 4 distinct neighbor edges, which the MIP credits almost as
much irreducibility as the broadcast.

So GWT-style winner-broadcast is a POSITIVE but SUB-BAR lever for IRREDUCIBLE
integration in this toy substrate: clearly good for cross-module ACCESS/agreement,
marginal for Φ. The bars were NOT moved to manufacture a GREEN (ΔΦ+0.019 stays
honestly below +0.02).
