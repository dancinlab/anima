=== THE NEW ANGLE (objective-modulation) ===
The one knob-axis with a GENUINELY regime-dependent optimum: when the COST TRADEOFF
(false-emit vs abstain) DIFFERS by regime, the Pareto-optimal abstain threshold actually
SHIFTS. A controller that DETECTS the regime's cost-asymmetry and MOVES the precision/
recall operating point could beat ANY single best-fixed point that must serve all regimes.

DISTINCT vs emit-gate H_1526 (HELD): H_1526 scored a SYMMETRIC calibration metric
(acc − fab, both weighted 1.0) under a FIXED cost — so the optimal margin did NOT shift
and the substrate surprise signal could not separate the two query classes. H_1535
makes the COST ASYMMETRIC and REGIME-DEPENDENT (the optimum genuinely shifts), and the
controller does NOT need to separate noisy-groundable from ghost per-query — it only
needs to DETECT the regime-level base-rate / cost-asymmetry (a coarser, achievable
signal: the running fraction of high-surprise events). If even THAT coarse regime-cost
detection cannot beat the best-fixed point, the wall extends to the objective family too.

=== DESIGN (numpy DIRECTIONAL; engine R2 deferred ING) ===
Import MemStore/regimes/seeds/key geometry from

Two cost regimes with GENUINELY DIFFERENT, PRE-REGISTERED false-emit:abstain cost ratios:
  R_PRECISION : false-emit VERY costly  → optimal point should ABSTAIN MORE (high margin)
                cost(false-emit)=5.0, cost(miss/abstain-on-groundable)=1.0
  R_RECALL    : abstain (missing a groundable answer) costly → should EMIT MORE (low margin)
                cost(false-emit)=1.0, cost(miss/abstain-on-groundable)=5.0
  R_BALANCED  : symmetric                → middle point
                cost(false-emit)=2.0, cost(miss/abstain-on-groundable)=2.0
Each regime stream has BOTH groundable (noisy) + ungroundable ghost queries
(OOS_RATE=0.30) so the abstain point is the load-bearing decision in EVERY regime.

CAPABILITY = COST-WEIGHTED UTILITY (higher=better), normalized per regime so regimes are
comparable: utility = (correct_emits − Cfe·false_emits − Cmiss·groundable_misses) / N,
then min-max normalized within each regime to [0,1] using the achievable best/worst grid
point (so +0.05 is a meaningful normalized margin). Cfe, Cmiss are the regime's frozen
cost ratios above. (Abstaining on a GHOST is CORRECT and costs 0 — that is the whole
point of an asymmetric objective: it rewards the regime-appropriate operating point.)

ARMS:
  FIXED  = single best-fixed abstain margin, grid-tuned on a DISJOINT seed to maximize
           AVERAGE normalized cost-utility ACROSS the 3 cost regimes (the fair single
           operating point — strongest honest fixed baseline).
  ADAPT  = neuromodulator detects the regime's cost-asymmetry (running surprise base-rate
           + the regime's frozen cost ratio, both substrate-/objective-derived, NO injected
           label) → shifts the abstain margin toward the regime's Pareto-optimal point.
  ABL    = ADAPT collapsed to its own per-regime MEAN margin (fixed) — kills the
           per-regime SHIFT, keeps everything else. If ADAPT≈ABL the OBJECTIVE shift
           contributes nothing.

=== FROZEN FALSIFIER (pre-registered, immutable) ===

  (1) ADAPT cost-utility − FIXED ≥ +0.05 (normalized) on ≥2/3 regimes, AND
