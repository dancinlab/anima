THE QUESTION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
H_1360 (🟢 GREEN, 1-D) proved that CARVING RELOCATION IS MOVE-THE-CELLS: at a FIXED LOW budget,
physically DRIFTING the residual phase-1 prototype POSITIONS toward the moved boundary p_A' lands a
COHERENT single CP peak AT p_A' (|peak−p_A'| 0.144→0.002, peak-count 4.3→1.0) where budget (H_1340)
and decay (H_1352) BOTH failed coherence. H_1364 showed split-only re-growth incoherence is
INTRINSIC (worse at finer lattices). BOTH were on a 1-D categorical AXIS (boundary = a POINT).

H_1369 asks: does the move-the-cells win GENERALIZE to a 2-D FEATURE SPACE where the TRUE category
partition is a FIXED, KNOWN geometry (a half-plane boundary u>p, a LINE in 2-D, not a point)? When
the category SHIFTS (p_A→p_A') the prototype cells must relocate to track it. Does coherent
single-ridge relocation SURVIVE in 2-D, or does the extra dimension REINTRODUCE the incoherence
that split-only re-growth showed (H_1364) — i.e. is move-the-cells a 1-D-only artifact?

The boundary is the VERTICAL LINE u=p (cat = u>p), shifting p_A=1/3 → p_A'=2/3. The free dimension
v is the categorically-IRRELEVANT axis — the place where 2-D fragmentation can appear (residual
phase-1 cells at the old line u=1/3 inject a SECONDARY vertical ridge at the old cut, and the v-axis
gives the discrimination ridge room to break into disconnected blobs rather than a single line).

R2 ANGLE (genuinely new, pre-registered HERE before any R2 scoring): replace the unbounded
connectivity count with a BOUNDED ridge-CONCENTRATION coherence metric (the H_1343 R3 prescription).
A coherent CP ridge is a THIN line concentrated at one u-value; a shuffle smear fills the grid.
  * RIDGE_FRAC = (#ridge nodes)/(G*G) in [0,1]  — thin line small, smear large.
  * U_CONC = 1 - min(1, ridge_u_std / U_STD_REF) in [0,1], U_STD_REF=0.20  — concentration of the
    ridge along the categorically-relevant u-axis (a single vertical line -> u_std~0 -> U_CONC~1;
    a grid-filling smear -> u_std large -> U_CONC~0). v-spread is NOT scored (v is the irrelevant
    axis; a coherent vertical ridge legitimately spans all v).
  * COH2D = U_CONC * (1 - RIDGE_FRAC) in [0,1]  — a single BOUNDED coherence score (high = thin
    concentrated line; low = grid-filling smear). BOTH factors must be high.
