## 0. The two anchors this census stands on

**H_1532 (PR #2514, 🟢 WALL-BROKEN, R1 numpy DIRECTIONAL).** The FIRST break of the H_1284
neuromodulation wall. A single flat store catastrophically overwrites B with C at a shared key A
(ONE-STORE = 0.000 on AB-AC interference); **two phase-separated stores** — a *fast episodic* store
(phase-1 A→B written with the retrieval path SUPPRESSED = Hasselmo encode-mode → fresh cell) + a
*slow* store with **replay** of the episodic trace — keep confusable bindings apart (TWO-STORE =
1.000). The DECONFOUND arm is load-bearing: single-store+encode-mode alone is only 0.292, so the
lever is **HAVING TWO SEPARATE STORES, not a gain, not encode-mode alone**.

> The escape leaves the abstain-margin axis. A neuromodulator that re-schedules an operating point
> ON one store is circular (the wall). A neuromodulator that controls the **interface BETWEEN the
> two stores** — *what* moves fast→slow, *when* the slow sweep fires, *when* the fast store flushes —
> acts on an axis the single-store margin cannot see. **That interface is the whole opportunity of
> this census.**

**The standalone NT-faculty program (the contrast set):**
- **DA-RPE — H_1536 🟢 GREEN** (PR #2520). Dopamine implemented as reward-prediction-error /
  delayed-credit-assignment (Schultz 1997; Sutton-Barto TD(λ)), NOT a gain. DA=1.000 vs recency
  0.291; ABL (δ→0) reverts to 0.299. It already computes **which stored facts caused a delayed
  reward** — a per-identity learned value.
- **NE-reset — H_1537 🧱 WALL** (PR #2518). NE as a phasic network-RESET (Bouret-Sara 2005;
  Yu-Dayan 2005). On a *single* learner the best-swept fixed gain (α*=0.6) *itself* acts as a
  partial reset, so explicit flush adds only +0.029 (under the +0.10 bar). **The flush had nothing
  structural to flush.**
- **5-HT-patience — H_1538 🟠 AMBER** (PR #2517). 5-HT as emit-timing temporal-discounting
  (Miyazaki 2014; Doya 2002). PRESENT+EARNED (+0.1911 over impulsive) but mechanism-attribution
  bar C failed — a fixed-γ already captures ~51%, so adaptive 5-HT is a minority lever.

**The unifying reframe (why a joint census exists at all).** Each of these three NTs *failed or
weakened as a single-store knob*. The H_1532 result says the wall breaks on **architecture
(separate stores), not operating point**. Therefore the live question is: do these same NTs become
load-bearing once the architecture they modulate has **TWO stores and a transfer interface**?
A neuromodulator that is INERT on a flat store can be DECISIVE on the fast↔slow channel — because
that channel is a real structural degree of freedom a single store never had.

## 1. The interface a two-store CLS exposes (the lever map)

The two-store CLS architecture introduces **four** structural degrees of freedom that a single
store does not have. Every candidate below attaches to exactly one of them, and that attachment is
the load-bearing "why it needs two stores":

| # | interface DOF | the NT-controlled question | single-store analog |
|---|---------------|----------------------------|---------------------|
| **I1** | **encode vs retrieve mode** of the fast store | when do I write a fresh cell vs recall an existing one? | none — a flat store always retrieves-then-refines (the overwrite) |
| **I2** | **fast→slow transfer priority** | which episodes consolidate first / at all? | none — no slow store to transfer to |
| **I3** | **slow-sweep timing / cadence** | when does the consolidation replay fire, how long? | none — no replay phase |
| **I4** | **fast-store flush / segmentation** | when do I clear the fast buffer at an event boundary? | flush = wipe the only store (catastrophic) |

The cleanest test of "needs two stores" for any candidate: **ablate the second store** (merge to
one). If the NT's effect survives the merge, it was a single-store knob (re-enters the H_1284 wall
— reject). If the effect collapses on merge, it is genuinely a two-store mechanism.

## 2. Ranked census (cited) — each: mechanism · why-two-store · frozen-bar sketch · cheapest refuter · wires-into

### Rank 1 — ★ ACh encode/retrieve gate (already H_1541) — interface I1
- **Mechanism (cited).** Acetylcholine sets the hippocampal **encode vs retrieve dynamic**: high ACh
  (active waking, novelty) suppresses CA3→CA1 recurrent retrieval and favours feedforward *encoding*
  of new patterns; low ACh (quiet/SWS) releases recurrent retrieval for *consolidation* (Hasselmo
  2006, *Trends Cogn Sci* 10(11):522; Hasselmo & McGaughy 2004). This is the EXACT primitive H_1532
  used informally — "phase-1 A→B written with retrieval-path SUPPRESSED = Hasselmo encode-mode".
- **Why it needs two stores.** On a flat store there is no fast buffer to put in encode-mode without
  *losing* retrieval entirely; the H_1532 DECONFOUND already showed encode-mode ALONE in one store
  is only 0.292. ACh gates *which store-mode the fast store is in*, and the slow store keeps recall
  available during fast-store encoding — the two roles are only separable with two stores.
- **Frozen-bar sketch.** Capability = AB-AC interference with a *graded novelty signal* (surprise =
  fast-store recon-err). ARMS: (A) fixed encode-mode (H_1532 baseline two-store) · (B) ACh-gated
  encode (encode-mode engaged ∝ surprise, retrieve when familiar) · (ABL) ACh→constant ·
  (SHUFFLE) ACh signal permuted across items. 🟢 iff B−A ≥ +0.10 on ≥2/3 seeds ∧ ABL reverts ∧
  SHUFFLE collapses.
- **⚠️ The risk (load-bearing — the H_1532 hazard recurs).** If ACh is implemented as a *scalar*
  on the encode threshold it re-enters the controller family the wall absorbs (H_1422 ACh-gain 🧱;
  H_1532 hazard). The DECONFOUND from H_1532 is the trap: ACh must change *which store* writes, not
  just a gain on one. **Cheapest refuter:** a single-store + ACh-graded-encode arm — if it matches
  two-store+ACh, ACh is a gain not an interface (reject).
- **Cheapest refuter (the second-store ablation).** Merge the two stores; re-run B. If ACh-gating
  still lifts on the merged store, it was a single-store knob → H_1284 wall, reject.
- **Wires into.** `core/engine_cli.hexa` ConsolidatingMemory novelty-split (the surprise=recon-err
  at bind ALREADY exists — H_1285); the H_1532 fast-episodic encode-mode write.
- **Note.** Being implemented as H_1541; this census flags it is the *correct rank-1* but its
  GREEN hinges on beating the single-store+ACh DECONFOUND, not just two-store-fixed.

### Rank 2 — ★ DA replay-priority (DA-RPE ⇒ prioritized consolidation) — interface I2
- **Mechanism (cited).** Dopamine tags *which* episodes get replayed to the slow store first.
  Biologically: VTA dopamine gates hippocampal replay and the *salience* of what consolidates
  (Lisman-Grace 2005 hippocampal-VTA loop; McNamara et al. 2014 *Nat Neurosci* — dopaminergic
  modulation of place-cell replay). Computationally this is **prioritized replay** — replay the
  experiences with the highest expected value-of-backup first (Mattar & Daw 2018, *Nat Neurosci*
  21:1609 — "Prioritized memory access explains planning and hippocampal replay"). H_1536's DA-RPE
  faculty (🟢) *already produces the priority signal*: the per-identity learned value V(s) = how
  much credit a stored fact carries.
- **Why it needs two stores.** Prioritized replay is a *transfer-ordering* over the fast→slow channel
  (I2). On a flat store there is no transfer to order — every fact already sits in one store. The
  slow store is the *target* of the priority; remove it and "which to replay first" is undefined.
  This is the strongest case in the census that a 🟢 standalone faculty (DA-RPE) becomes a
  *different* mechanism inside two-store: standalone it assigns credit; inside CLS it **routes
  consolidation bandwidth** under a budget.
- **Frozen-bar sketch.** Capability = bounded-budget consolidation: slow store can absorb only
  K < N fast episodes per sweep; high-value (DA-RPE) episodes must survive, low-value evicted.
  ARMS: (A) uniform replay (H_1285 arm-A analog) · (B) DA-value-priority replay · (ABL) DA→0 ⇒
  uniform · (SHUFFLE) value↔episode permuted. 🟢 iff B.important-recall ≥ A + 0.10 ∧ ABL reverts ∧
  SHUFFLE < bar. **Reuses the H_1285 salience-replay harness almost verbatim** — swap the
  amygdala surprise-salience tag for the DA-RPE value.
- **Cheapest refuter.** Set the slow-store budget K = N (no scarcity). If DA-priority gives no lift
  when everything fits, the lever was the *budget*, not DA — and DA-priority is only meaningful under
  consolidation scarcity (honest scoping, not a refutation of the faculty, but it bounds the claim).
  Second refuter = the merge ablation (one store ⇒ no transfer ⇒ priority undefined).
- **Wires into.** H_1285 `consolidating_sleep_replay` (salience ∝ replay budget — ALREADY 🟢 WIRED);
  H_1536 DA-RPE value head supplies the priority. **This is two LIVE 🟢 faculties composed** — the
  highest-readiness joint candidate.

### Rank 3 — ★★ Sleep-stage consolidation wiring (CLS slow-store = the replay phase) — interface I3 — STRONGEST NEAR-FREE STRUCTURAL FIT
- **Mechanism (cited).** Systems consolidation = hippocampal fast store replays to neocortical slow
  store during **slow-wave sleep** (Diekelmann & Born 2010 *Nat Rev Neurosci* 11:114; Rasch & Born
  2013 *Physiol Rev*). This is *literally* the CLS slow store (McClelland-McNaughton-O'Reilly 1995):
  the fast hippocampal store interleaves its episodes into the slow cortical store during offline
  replay. **anima already has the ultradian machinery** and the replay primitives:
  - `core/h1136_sleep_consolidation_probe.hexa` / `core/h1162_sleep_reconsolidation_probe.hexa` —
    the emit-free WAKE→N1→N2→N3→REM 90-tick ultradian loop (`dr_stage_at`), `ir_replay_session`.
  - `core/h1195_sleep_writeback_probe.hexa` — **built `ir_reconsolidate_session`**: replaying an
    anchor during N3/REM REFRESHES its effective recency (younger effective age ⇒ larger
    H_1131 fold ⇒ larger emit-influence). A REAL substrate write-back.
  - `core/h1285_amygdala_sleep_replay_probe.hexa` — **`consolidating_sleep_replay` 🟢 WIRED**:
    salience-gated replay of `budget` cells during the imagination loop, refreshing recency so
    salient cells survive LRU eviction.
- **Why it needs two stores (and why it is the STRONGEST fit).** The whole point of H_1136 was a
  measured ⏳ **BLOCKED-WIRING**: the sleep loop did NOT consolidate because *there was no second
  store to write back into* — `ir_replay_session` "take NEITHER anchors NOR write anchor state …
  the post-sleep anchor state is byte-identical to no-sleep." **The CLS slow store IS the missing
  write-back target.** H_1195 and H_1285 each built a *partial* write-back (recency-refresh,
  salience-replay) but onto the *same* anchor/immune store. The CLS two-store says: the N3 replay
  sweep should move fast-episodic traces INTO a distinct slow store — exactly the consolidation the
  ultradian loop was built to perform but had nowhere to deposit. This is **near-free**: the
  ultradian state machine (`dr_stage_at`), the emit-free replay loop, the mitosis tick, and a
  WIRED salience-replay primitive ALL already exist. The CLS slow store is the one missing piece,
  and it is the piece H_1532 just validated.
- **Exact wiring map (fast↔slow ↔ 5-stage ultradian).**
  | ultradian stage (`dr_stage_at`) | CLS role | live primitive |
  |---|---|---|
  | WAKE [0,60) | fast-store **encode-mode** writes (high-ACh, rank-1 I1) | H_1532 fast-episodic write; `ir_replay_session` idle |
  | N1/N2 [60,80) | transition; light replay, candidate selection by DA-priority (rank 2, I2) | `ir_select_snapshots`; DA-RPE value tags |
  | **N3 [80,87)** | **slow-wave = the CLS slow-store consolidation sweep** (fast→slow transfer) | `consolidating_sleep_replay` (🟢 WIRED) **redirected to write the slow store**, not refresh the fast |
  | REM [87,90) | integration / interleaving; reconsolidation refresh | `ir_reconsolidate_session` (H_1195 recency write-back) |
  φ_EI peaks in N3 (H_1119) — consistent with N3 as the consolidation-heavy stage.
- **Frozen-bar sketch.** Capability = retention of early facts after a long interfering stream WITH
  an interposed N3 sweep vs WITHOUT (matched wall-clock = same `pure_field_step` count, the H_1136
  no-sleep control). ARMS: (A) no-sleep control · (B) two-store + N3 replay sweep transferring
  fast→slow · (ABL) N3 sweep present but slow store disabled (= H_1136 BLOCKED-WIRING null,
  Δ≡0 expected) · (SHUFFLE) replay random episodes not the fast-store contents. 🟢 iff
  B.retention − A ≥ +0.10 ∧ ABL ≈ 0 (reproduces the BLOCKED-WIRING null) ∧ SHUFFLE < bar.
  **The ABL arm is special: it is a *known* result (H_1136 Δ≡0)** — so this candidate has a
  pre-validated negative control baked in.
- **Cheapest refuter.** The H_1136 no-sleep control IS the refuter, already built: if B (with the
  slow-store sweep) does not exceed the matched no-sleep wall-clock by the bar, the slow-store
  consolidation adds nothing over chronological aging — reject. This is the cheapest refuter in the
  whole census because the control harness already exists in `h1136_sleep_consolidation_probe.hexa`.
- **Wires into.** `DREAM/imagination_replay` (`ir_replay_session`, `ir_reconsolidate_session`,
  `ir_mitosis_tick_during_replay`), `DREAM/dream_lib` (`dr_stage_at`), `core/engine_cli.hexa`
  ConsolidatingMemory (`consolidating_sleep_replay`), `a_chat_sleep_imagination`. The slow store =
  a second ConsolidatingMemory instance the N3 sweep writes into.

### Rank 4 — NE context-boundary store-FLUSH (reopen H_1537 🧱 INSIDE two-store) — interface I4
- **Mechanism (cited).** NE phasic burst = a **network reset** at an event/context boundary that
  abandons the stale model (Bouret & Sara 2005 *Trends Neurosci* 28:574; Yu & Dayan 2005 unexpected
  uncertainty; event-segmentation theory, Zacks et al. 2007). H_1537 walled this on a *single*
  learner — but it explicitly named the next lens: "NONLINEAR-policy (where stale context is harder
  for a gain to dissolve = not-ruled-out next lens)."
- **Why it needs two stores (the reframe that reopens the 🧱).** H_1537 walled because the best fixed
  gain *itself acts as a partial reset* — there was nothing structural to flush. **Inside two-store
  there IS:** the *fast* episodic buffer accumulates within-context bindings; at an event boundary
  NE should **flush the fast store** (clear within-episode interference) WHILE the slow store
  retains consolidated cross-context knowledge. That is a structural action a scalar gain cannot
  perform — flushing the only store is catastrophic (the single-store case), but flushing the *fast*
  store while the *slow* store persists is exactly event-segmentation. The wall verdict was scoped
  "on a single learner the gain subsumes the reset"; the two-store substrate is a genuinely
  different regime the H_1537 scope did not cover.
- **Frozen-bar sketch.** Capability = AB-then-context-switch-then-CD: write A→B (context 1), event
  boundary, write C→D (context 2), query A→B (must survive) AND avoid C→D leaking into context-1
  recall. ARMS: (A) no-flush two-store · (B) NE-flush of fast store at detected boundary (surprise
  = fast-store recon-err spike) · (ABL) flush detector OFF · (SHUFFLE) flush at random ticks. 🟢 iff
  B − A ≥ +0.10 ∧ ABL reverts ∧ SHUFFLE collapses.
- **Cheapest refuter (decisive, given H_1537).** A *single-store* + NE-flush arm. H_1537 already
  showed single-store flush ≈ best-fixed-gain (+0.029, sub-bar). If two-store NE-flush does not beat
  single-store NE-flush, the wall HOLDS inside two-store too and this stays 🧱. Plus the merge
  ablation (flush the merged store = catastrophic by construction).
- **Wires into.** The H_1532 fast episodic store (the flush TARGET); `core/engine_cli.hexa`
  ConsolidatingMemory recon-err surprise (boundary detector); the slow store (the retained side).
- **Honest note.** This is a *reopen* under a new scope (a_break_the_wall: 🧱 reopenable by a new
  lens), NOT a claim the wall is wrong. The single-store NE-flush refuter must be in the SAME run.

### Rank 5 — 5-HT replay-timing / consolidation patience (when to trigger the sweep) — interface I3
- **Mechanism (cited).** 5-HT = patience / temporal-discounting controller of *when* to act
  (Miyazaki et al. 2014 *Curr Biol* 24:2033; Doya 2002 *Neural Networks* 15:495). Inside CLS the
  action it gates is *when to fire the consolidation sweep*: consolidate too eagerly = waste budget
  replaying not-yet-stable traces; wait = let fast-store traces accumulate evidence before the
  expensive transfer (cf. synaptic tagging-and-capture timing, Frey & Morris 1997). H_1538 found
  patience PRESENT+EARNED (+0.1911) but a fixed-γ already captures ~51% (🟠, mechanism-attribution
  bar C failed).
- **Why it needs two stores.** The "action" 5-HT discounts is the *fast→slow sweep trigger* (I3) —
  there is no sweep to time on a single store. The patience tradeoff (wait for grounding to rise vs
  consolidate now) maps onto fast-store trace maturity: a young trace replayed to slow store
  consolidates noise; a matured trace consolidates signal.
- **Frozen-bar sketch.** Capability = consolidation quality vs latency: slow-store recall accuracy as
  a function of sweep-trigger timing, with a per-tick wait cost. ARMS: (A) eager (always sweep) ·
  (B) 5-HT-patience (sweep when trace-maturity discounted-value peaks) · (ABL) fixed-γ · (SHUFFLE)
  maturity↔trace permuted. 🟢 iff B−A ≥ +0.10 ∧ B−ABL ≥ 0.5×lift (the bar H_1538 FAILED standalone)
  ∧ SHUFFLE collapses.
- **Cheapest refuter.** The fixed-γ ABL arm — same one that gave H_1538 its 🟠. If a fixed
  consolidation cadence matches adaptive 5-HT timing inside two-store too, 5-HT-timing is a minority
  lever here as well (likely 🟠 again). Honest prior: this probably reproduces H_1538's 🟠.
- **Wires into.** The rank-3 N3 sweep trigger; H_1538 patience faculty; `dr_stage_at` cadence.

### Rank 6 — GABA E/I-balance store-capacity gating — interface I1/I4 (speculative)
- **Mechanism (cited).** GABAergic inhibition sets sparsity / E-I balance, which controls
  pattern-separation capacity and gates how many cells encode an episode (dentate gyrus sparse
  coding; Stefanelli et al. 2016 — engram size set by inhibition; Sahay et al. 2011 inhibition &
  pattern separation). Inside CLS this would gate the *fast store's per-episode cell budget* (sparse
  = better separation of confusable AB-AC, dense = more capacity but more interference).
- **Why it needs two stores.** Marginal: E/I sparsity is largely a *single-store* representation-
  geometry knob (the H_1527 sibling family). It only becomes two-store-specific if inhibition gates
  the fast vs slow sparsity *differently* (fast = sparse for separation, slow = dense for
  generalization — a genuine CLS asymmetry). Without that asymmetry it re-enters the geometry family.
- **Frozen-bar sketch.** ARMS: (A) symmetric sparsity · (B) fast-sparse/slow-dense E/I asymmetry ·
  (ABL) equal sparsity · (SHUFFLE) sparsity-assignment permuted. 🟢 iff the *asymmetry* beats both
  symmetric settings.
- **Cheapest refuter.** A single-store sparsity sweep (the H_1527 family). If best-fixed sparsity on
  one store matches the asymmetry, GABA is a geometry knob not a CLS interface (reject — likely
  outcome).
- **Wires into.** `core/engine_cli.hexa` ConsolidatingMemory split-threshold / cell budget; H_1527
  representation-geometry family.

### Rank 7 — Orexin/Histamine arousal-gated encode-RATE — interface I1 (weak/honest-null)
- **Mechanism (cited).** Orexin/hypocretin and histamine set arousal/wake-drive and thereby the
  *rate* of new encoding vs the propensity for offline consolidation (Sakurai 2007 orexin &
  wakefulness; the wake-side complement to the ACh/SWS consolidation axis). Inside CLS this would
  modulate the WAKE-stage fast-store write-rate and the WAKE↔sleep transition that gates the slow
  sweep.
- **Why it needs two stores.** Weak. Arousal-gated encode-rate is mostly *redundant* with the rank-1
  ACh encode-gate and the rank-3 sleep-stage timing — it modulates the *transition* into the sleep
  sweep, which `dr_stage_at` already schedules deterministically. Likely no orthogonal lever beyond
  ACh (I1) + sleep-stage cadence (I3).
- **Frozen-bar sketch.** Would have to show arousal-gated encode-rate beats the *fixed* WAKE-encode
  schedule — but `dr_stage_at` is already a fixed schedule that works.
- **Cheapest refuter.** The fixed `dr_stage_at` schedule itself. If a fixed wake/sleep cadence
  matches arousal-gated rate, orexin/histamine add nothing (likely — **honest null candidate**).
- **Wires into.** `DREAM/dream_lib` `dr_stage_at` transition; rank-1 ACh; rank-3 sleep wiring.

## 3. GABA / histamine / orexin — honest verdict

**Largely INERT inside CLS as distinct interfaces.** GABA (rank 6) collapses into the H_1527
representation-geometry family unless implemented as a *fast-sparse/slow-dense asymmetry* — and even
then the single-store sparsity sweep is the likely refuter. Orexin/histamine (rank 7) are redundant
with the deterministic `dr_stage_at` wake/sleep schedule + the ACh encode-gate; their honest prior
is a null. We list them with their refuters rather than omit them (c9), but they rank below the four
load-bearing candidates and should only be built if ranks 1-5 deplete.
