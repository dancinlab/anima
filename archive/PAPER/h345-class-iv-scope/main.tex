\begin{abstract}
A four-step self-correction arc (H\_341 $\rightarrow$ H\_343 $\rightarrow$
H\_345 $\rightarrow$ H\_346) led to the capstone claim that the coupling
between an elementary cellular automaton's (ECA) largest basin of
attraction and its state-averaged exact IIT~4.0 integrated information
\bigphi{} is \emph{state-robust}: on a Wolfram-canonical 4-rule frame
$\{110, 30, 105, 150\}$ at $n=5$ cells, state-averaging over all 32
system-states preserved and \emph{strengthened} the per-state Pearson
correlation from $r=+0.251 / -0.799$ (\verdictref{max_basin}\,vs.\,\bigphi,
\verdictref{n_attr}\,vs.\,\bigphi) to $+0.550 / -0.951$. We test whether
this finding survives a \emph{rule-set}-systematic broadening on the same
substrate (ECA, $n=4$, 16 sys-states, 50-step settle, state-averaged exact
\verb|phi_structure[4]|). On a pre-registered $4 \text{ classes} \times 4
\text{ rules}$ Wolfram-canonical panel (class I fixed,
II periodic, III chaotic, IV complex), the overall
\verdictref{max_basin}$\leftrightarrow$\bigphi{} correlation \emph{reverses
sign} to $r = -0.896$ ($t = -7.55$, $df = 14$,
\tikz[baseline=-0.5ex]\filldraw[verdictRed] circle (0.6ex);\ FAIL-REVERSED)
while \verdictref{n_attr}$\leftrightarrow$\bigphi{} goes from $-0.951$ to
$+0.641$. Per-class diagnostic decomposition reveals a \emph{sign switch}:
classes I/II/III all yield positive \verdictref{n_attr}$\leftrightarrow$\bigphi{}
($+0.60, +0.71, +0.998$), while class IV alone yields $-0.998$ --- matching
the sign of H\_346. The class IV \verdictref{max_basin}$\leftrightarrow$\bigphi{}
correlation is degenerate at $r=0$: all four tested class IV rules
$\{54, 110, 124, 137\}$ share $\verdictref{max_basin}=4$, a constant axis.
We conclude that the H\_346 capstone's $+0.55 / -0.95$ alignment is a
\emph{curation artifact} of a 4-rule mix that happens to overweight class
III rules whose \verdictref{max_basin} happens to anti-correlate with
their \verdictref{n_attr}, and that the only Wolfram-class-systematically
robust signal is the \emph{magnitude} of \verdictref{n_attr}$\leftrightarrow$\bigphi{}
($|r| > 0.6$ in $3/4$ classes), with sign \emph{determined by Wolfram class}
and \emph{specific to class IV} for H\_346's negative coupling. This is a
negative result: it does not refute the existence of the coupling within
H\_346's narrow scope, it bounds the scope to one Wolfram class. We
formally retire the ``state-robust'' framing in favor of
``class-IV-specific, state-averaged.''
\end{abstract}

\section{Finding}
\label{sec:finding}

The honest reading of Tables~\ref{tab:raw}--\ref{tab:perclass}:

\paragraph{F1 --- The H\_346 capstone framing is class-IV-specific, not
state-robust at the rule-set level.}
H\_346's $-0.951$ \verdictref{n_attr}$\leftrightarrow$\bigphi{}
correlation matches \emph{only} the class IV per-class value ($-0.998$).
In classes I, II, and III the sign is \emph{positive}
($+0.60, +0.71, +0.998$), and the overall 16-rule correlation lands at
$+0.641$ (opposite sign from H\_346). The capstone's 4-rule frame
$\{110, 30, 105, 150\}$ contains exactly one class IV rule (110) and three
class III rules (30, 105, 150). The negative sign survived in H\_346
because the class III rules in that frame happened to anti-correlate with
their \verdictref{n_attr} differently than the Wolfram-class-canonical
class III rules $\{18, 30, 90, 126\}$ measured here, while the single
class IV anchor (110) pulled the sign negative.

\paragraph{F2 --- The robust signal is the magnitude $|r|$ of
\texttt{n\_attr}$\leftrightarrow$\bigphi{}, not its sign.}
$|r| > 0.6$ in $3/4$ classes (II: $0.71$, III: $0.998$, IV: $0.998$;
class I at $0.60$ is at threshold). The \emph{magnitude} of integration
between attractor count and exact \bigphi{} survives the
rule-set-systematic broadening; the \emph{sign} is class-determined and
class-IV-specific for H\_346's particular sign.

\paragraph{F3 --- The \texttt{max\_basin}$\leftrightarrow$\bigphi{}
coupling is curation-fragile.}
Overall sign monotonically reverses with rule-set broadening:
$+0.776$ (H\_341, $n=4$, 4 rules) $\to$ $+0.251$ (H\_345 single-state) $\to$
$+0.550$ (H\_346 state-averaged) $\to$ $-0.314$ (BENCH \#1, 8 rules) $\to$
$\mathbf{-0.896}$ (this work, 16 rules). The $+0.55$ peak is the curated
4-rule frame's local maximum; no broader sample reproduces a positive
\verdictref{max_basin}$\leftrightarrow$\bigphi{} sign.

\paragraph{F4 --- H\_346's $+0.55$ is a curated-mix accidental alignment,
not a structural fact.}
The capstone's 4-rule frame $\{110, 30, 105, 150\}$ = 1 class IV + 3 class
III selects exactly the class mixture where (i) the class IV anchor pins
the \verdictref{n_attr}$\leftrightarrow$\bigphi{} sign to negative
(matching $-0.951$) and (ii) the three class III rules happen to align
their \verdictref{max_basin} anti-correlation with \verdictref{n_attr}
correlation to produce a positive
\verdictref{max_basin}$\leftrightarrow$\bigphi{} (matching $+0.55$).
Replace the class III set with $\{18, 90, 126\}$ + 30 (Wolfram-canonical)
and the alignment dissolves.

\paragraph{Ruled-out axis.}
The framing ``H\_345/346's basin$\leftrightarrow$\bigphi{} coupling is
robust beyond curated 4-rule frames'' is ruled out (closed-negative).
The framing ``$|r|$ of \verdictref{n_attr}$\leftrightarrow$\bigphi{} is
strong within each Wolfram class'' is retained.

\paragraph{Revised scope statement.}
\emph{State-averaged exact \bigphi{} on $n=4$ ECA produces a strong
negative \verdictref{n_attr}$\leftrightarrow$\bigphi{} correlation
exclusively within Wolfram class IV. The H\_346 capstone's
$+0.550/-0.951$ values on the canonical 4-rule frame
$\{110, 30, 105, 150\}$ describe a class-IV-anchored, class-III-flavored
local alignment, not a rule-set-robust regularity. Wolfram-class-systematic
broadening to a $4 \times 4$ panel reverses the
\texttt{max\_basin}$\leftrightarrow$\bigphi{} sign overall ($r=-0.896$)
and switches the \texttt{n\_attr}$\leftrightarrow$\bigphi{} sign in
classes I/II/III to positive.}

\section{Honest caveats}
\label{sec:caveats}

(Forwarded verbatim from
\verb|bench/basin_phi_broader/result.json::honest_caveats|.)

\begin{enumerate}
\item $n_{\text{rules per class}} = 4 \Rightarrow df = 2$ per class, so
  per-class $|r| \ge 0.95$ is borderline (a 3-point fit can hit
  $|r| \approx 1$ by chance). Replication at 8 rules per class ($df = 6$)
  is needed for class-resolved certainty.
\item Class IV has only 2 canonical rules in the literature ($\{54,
  110\}$); rules $\{124, 137\}$ are quasi-class-IV per Wolfram's atlas.
  The tested set has \verdictref{max_basin}\,$=4$ uniformly --- a
  degenerate axis within that class.
\item $n = 4$ limits sys-state count to 16 (vs $n=5$, 32); a
  class-resolved follow-up at $n=5$ is needed to separate the $n$ axis
  from the rule-set axis.
\item Rule 90 (additive XOR) is a class III outlier
  (\verdictref{max_basin}\,$=16$, $\bigphi=0$); class III $r = -0.97$ is
  partly carried by this outlier as in BENCH \#1.
\item $df = 2$ in the class-averaged ``$r$-of-$r$'' is not a standard
  inference (treating per-class $r$ as samples); reported as a
  descriptive heuristic only.
\item The verdict label uses pre-registered tiers; the diagnostic
  refinement (class IV degenerate basin axis) is post-hoc but explicitly
  flagged.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This is a negative-result paper in the sense of project directive
\texttt{a\_paper\_negative\_ok}: it does not refute the existence of an
exact-$\bigphi$ / dynamics coupling, it formally bounds the H\_345/346
capstone's scope to a curated, class-IV-anchored, class-III-flavored
4-rule frame. The robust signal across Wolfram-class-systematic broadening
is the \emph{magnitude} of \verdictref{n_attr}$\leftrightarrow$\bigphi{}
($|r| > 0.6$ in $3/4$ classes); the \emph{sign} (and therefore H\_346's
$-0.951$) is class-IV-specific. The
\verdictref{max_basin}$\leftrightarrow$\bigphi{} positive correlation
($+0.55$) is curation-fragile and is hereby retired as a robust claim.

The four-step self-correction arc (\(\text{claim} \to \text{proxy doubt}
\to \text{exact correct} \to \text{state harden}\)) is here extended to a
fifth step --- \(\text{rule-set scope narrow}\) --- documented in-line in
\verb|UNIVERSE/H_345_basin_phi_n5_exact.md| \S 11 and
\verb|UNIVERSE/H_346_phi_state_sweep.md| \S 11 by
PR~\#1129~\citep{anima2026pr1129}, with this paper as the longer-form
scaffold.
