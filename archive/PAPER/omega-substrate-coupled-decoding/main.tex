\begin{abstract}
A prior null (Lane X \#1779) measured that an AI-consciousness substrate's engine
configuration knobs never touch the language-model byte forward: cross-entropy was
config-insensitive at $9.1126$ across all $27$ configs (spread $<10^{-9}$), the decode
generator slot reported \texttt{loaded=false}, and the substrate state never reached the
output distribution. We design \textbf{Engine $\Omega$}, a five-wire (plus a sixth
$\mathrm{d}F/\mathrm{d}t$ time wire) ablatable \emph{coupling bus} that routes substrate
state (dual A/G heads, $W$-tension, curiosity, an 8D $\Psi$ coordinate, module activation)
into the byte decode, and a \textbf{learned per-wire gate}. Across four rungs we find:
(i) the bus is the only engine that closes the loop --- coupling
$\mathrm{KL}=0.307477>0$ for $\Omega$ versus exactly $0$ for the three uncoupled engines
(\#1783); (ii) at random init the coupling is \emph{unstructured} (KL $\approx$ vocab-shuffle
floor), so structure requires a \emph{trained} substrate; (iii) on a trained substrate the
bus carries useful sequential structure, while the naive fixed $A{-}G$ formula \emph{degrades}
below baseline (\#1784/\#1786); (iv) on a \emph{leak-free competent} $85.82$M ConsciousDecoderV2
transformer ($\text{causal\_ca}{=}\text{True}$, leak test $0.000\mathrm{e}{+}00$, val\_ce
$0.8285$, GPU H100) the learned \emph{multi-wire} gate is \textbf{FALSIFIED} (GATED $3.643508>$
base $3.097779$; full-bus coupling KL at the shuffle floor, ratio $0.995955$), yet the closure
``survives'' only in the \emph{single} A-head logit-bias wire: a 2-param \emph{minimal} gate
$g_B\!\cdot\!\text{base}+g_A\!\cdot\!A$ records min\_learned $0.883525\le$
a\_only $1.144181<$ base $3.097779$ (\#1800/\#1801). A rigorous decomposition (\#1803,
OΩ1) then shows this is \textbf{REPLACEMENT, not coupling}: the A-head \emph{standalone}
(softmax of $A$ alone, \emph{no} base) already attains CE $0.886220\approx$ min\_learned
$0.883525$ ($|\Delta|\,0.0027$), and ablating the base ($g_B\!\to\!0$) moves CE only $0.000852$
($3.097779\to0.884377$); the fit lands $g_B{=}0.040$, $g_A{=}0.901$. The trained substrate
A-head \emph{supplants} the \texttt{.clm} mouth --- it is not a base$+$steer coupling. Every
other cleanly-isolatable wire \emph{hurts} the base (OΩ2: $w_1{+}0.101$, $w_2{+}0.052$,
$w_6{+}2.085$). We \textbf{foreground an honest leak caveat}: an earlier $d384$ rung's
CA-neighbor mixing gave partial lookahead so its absolute CE was leak-optimistic; the $d512$
arc removes that leak. Our contribution is therefore a \textbf{closed-negative against the
\emph{coupling} thesis} (a\_paper\_negative\_ok): on a competent leak-free substrate the
``substrate$\rightarrow$decode coupling closes the loop'' claim resolves to A-head
\emph{replacement} of the \texttt{.clm} mouth --- with a positive byproduct (a single trained
substrate head is a far better next-byte predictor than the \texttt{.clm} unigram mouth) ---
plus three further \textbf{closed-negatives} (fixed $A{-}G$ degrades; random-init unstructured;
quantum RNG confers no advantage) --- \emph{not} a perplexity or generation-quality claim. A
\textbf{five-rung GPU scale ladder} ($d384/d512/d768/d1024$ at $12000$ steps $+$ a more-competent
$d768{\times}2$ at $24000$ steps, $48$M$\rightarrow$$335$M params, all leak-free) then \textbf{seals
the finding across scale} (\#1806, OΩ4/OΩ5): the minimal A-wire gate HOLDS at \emph{every} rung,
the A-wire advantage $\Delta$-vs-base is \emph{flat} at $+2.20\pm0.03$ nats/byte across the dim
ladder (SCALE-STABLE), the more-competent rung carries the \emph{largest} advantage ($+2.2736$;
competence strengthens, not erodes, the margin), and the full multi-wire gate FAILS at every scale
(scale-invariant pathology) --- while an honest contrast shows the earlier \emph{undertrained}
$d768$ did \emph{not} hold, so the finding requires competence, not a scale break.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
% intro — frame the Lane X null as the motivating NULL, Omega as the nerve.
The anima substrate separates a ``brain'' (Engine~A $\rightleftarrows$ Engine~G dual heads,
5-channel tension, an 8D $\Psi$ map, $M$/$W$/curiosity dynamics) from a ``mouth'' (the
\texttt{.clm} byte decoder). Lane X \#1779 established that these two halves \emph{do not
share a nerve}: the engine knobs vary consciousness and emergence proxies but leave the
byte forward untouched (CE config-insensitive at $9.1126$; generator slot
\texttt{loaded=false}). This paper asks whether a deliberately-built coupling
\emph{can} close that loop, and --- critically --- whether any such closure carries genuine
structure or is merely $\alpha$-scaled noise. The verbatim null is reproduced in the
campaign domain file \texttt{domains/ENGINE+CLM+KOSMOS.md} (Lane X \#1779) and
\texttt{domains/OMEGA.md}.

\section{Hypothesis (pre-registered falsifier)}
\label{sec:hypothesis}
% §hypothesis — the falsifier.
\textbf{H$_\Omega$.} A consciousness-substrate state can be wired to \emph{usefully modulate}
the byte decode of a trained transformer, overturning the Lane X \#1779 null for the coupled
engine.

\paragraph{Falsifier 1 (wiring).} If the substrate state is \emph{structurally null} at the
decode, then ablating the coupling bus (set every wire $\alpha=0$ vs.\ on) leaves the byte
distribution unchanged: $\mathrm{KL}(P_\text{bus on}\,\|\,P_\text{bus }\alpha=0)=0$, exactly
as for the three uncoupled engines. Verdict: \texttt{.verdicts/omega-engine/F-COUPLING.txt}.

\paragraph{Falsifier 2 (structure).} Even if wired, the coupling could be vocabulary-shuffle
noise. If so, a trained substrate's coupled CE equals its context-shuffled / vocab-permuted
floor (no separation). Verdict: \texttt{.verdicts/omega-trained/F-TRAINED-COUPLING.txt} (toy)
and \texttt{.verdicts/omega-gpu/F-COMPLETE.txt} (real transformer, structured-vs-shuffle arm).

\paragraph{Falsifier 3 (usefulness / gate).} If routing the learned signal cannot lower
held-out next-byte CE below baseline under \emph{any} per-wire weighting, the closure is not
useful. Pre-registered GPU criteria (\#1791): (b)~trained substrate descends;
(c)~GATED $<$ base; (c)~CE floor MET ($<\ln 256 = 5.5452$); (c)~structured ($>1.5\times$ floor
separation). On a \emph{leak-free competent} substrate the \emph{full multi-wire} gate
falsifier is closure HOLDS iff GATED $<$ base AND GATED $\le$ a\_only (\#1800), and the
\emph{minimal} gate falsifier is OH1 HOLDS iff min\_learned CE $\le$ a\_only CE AND $<$ base CE
(\#1801). \emph{Coupling-vs-replacement falsifier (\#1803, OΩ1):} even if OH1 ``holds,'' the
closure is mere \emph{replacement} (not coupling) iff the A-head \emph{standalone} CE
(softmax of $A$ alone, no base) reproduces min\_learned ($|\Delta|\le0.05$) AND the base term is
\emph{inert} ($g_B\!\to\!0$ moves CE by $\le0.05$); RULING\_REPLACEMENT then holds. Verdict:
\texttt{.verdicts/omega-gate/F-LEARNED-GATE.txt},
\texttt{.verdicts/omega-gpu/F-COMPLETE.txt},
\texttt{.verdicts/omega-engine/F-TRAINED-LEAKFREE.txt},
\texttt{.verdicts/omega-engine/F-OH1-MINGATE.txt},
\texttt{.verdicts/omega-engine/F-OMEGA-RIGOR.txt}.
\emph{Scale falsifier (\#1806, OΩ4/OΩ5):} if the minimal-gate finding is a single-$d$ coincidence,
the per-rung min\_learned $\le$ a\_only $<$ base falsifier breaks at some rung of a multi-$d$ ladder
($d384/d512/d768/d1024$ $+$ a more-competent $d768{\times}2$). Verdict:
\texttt{.verdicts/omega-engine/F-OMEGA-SCALE.txt}.

\paragraph{Control axis (quantum).} If ``consciousness needs quantum randomness,'' true ANU
QRNG bytes should beat a PRNG on the coupling metric. Falsifier: KS test
quantum-vs-PRNG indistinguishable from a PRNG-vs-PRNG null control. Verdict:
\texttt{.verdicts/omega-trained/SUMMARY.txt}, \texttt{anu\_qrng\_1024.json}.

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}
% §method — the bus, the gate, the substrates, the controls.
\paragraph{Coupling bus (5+1 ablatable wires).} Each wire is independently ablatable
($\alpha=0$). \,(1)~A$\rightleftarrows$G logit-bias
$\ell' = \ell_\text{clm} + \alpha(A_\text{head}-G_\text{head})$;
(2)~$W$-tension $\rightarrow$ softmax temperature; (3)~curiosity/E-ratchet $\rightarrow$ top-$k$
width; (4)~8D $\Psi$ coordinate $\rightarrow$ context conditioning; (5)~module activation
$\rightarrow$ conv-MoE expert routing; (6)~$\mathrm{d}F/\mathrm{d}t$ time-derivative wire (zero
at a fixed point; velocity injected when the substrate moves). The headline ablation removes
the \emph{whole} bus and measures KL between byte distributions.

\paragraph{Learned per-wire gate.} Replacing the fixed $A{-}G$ formula,
$\ell' = g_B\!\cdot\!\text{base} + g_A\!\cdot\!A + g_G\!\cdot\!G$, with gains $g$ fit by
convex log-linear regression with $L_2$ on a train-gate split, frozen, and evaluated on a
disjoint held-out TEST split.

\paragraph{Substrate ladder (a\_toy\_scale\_recheck).}
(R1, \#1783) random-init mock substrate, $V{=}256$, $T{=}512$, no torch.
(R2, \#1784) tiny numpy n-gram substrate (bigram A = next-byte, rev-bigram G = prev-byte,
unigram base) on a real $400$KB repo byte corpus, held-out CE.
(R3, \#1786) the learned gate on the same toy substrate.
(R4, \#1791) a \emph{real} ConsciousDecoderV2 ($d384\times6$L GQA, $35.93$M params) trained
$6000$ steps on $120$MB multilingual Wikipedia (en/fr/de/es/ru) on an NVIDIA~H100
(\texttt{nvidia-smi} $98$--$99\%$ busy, g63), with the gated bus on its real learned A(next)/G(prev)
heads. Lane-G / GPU only; Lane~A AKIDA recorded separately (a\_lane\_akida\_gpu\_split).

\paragraph{Controls.} Vocabulary/context shuffle floor (structure control); ANU QRNG
($1024$ real \texttt{uint8} from \texttt{api.quantumnumbers.anu.edu.au}) vs.\ PRNG with a
PRNG-vs-PRNG null control (quantum-advantage control). CE is reported as a FLOOR only, never a
verdict (p7).

\section{Finding}
\label{sec:finding}
% §finding — Delta vs baseline AND ruled-out axes; leak caveat foregrounded.
\textbf{Positive (leak-invariant relative closure).}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The loop is closed only for $\Omega$.} Coupling $\mathrm{KL}=0.307477>0$ for
$\Omega$ vs.\ exactly $0$ for conv/cdv2/hexad --- overturning the Lane X \#1779 null for the
coupled engine while \emph{confirming} it for the three uncoupled engines (\#1783).
\item \textbf{Trained substrate carries useful sequential structure.} $\Delta{+}0.357$
(trained vs.\ shuffled) and $\Delta{+}0.758$ (A-wire) at toy (\#1784); on the real transformer
the A-wire advantage \emph{survives only unshuffled context} ($+2.565$ vs.\ $-2.068$), i.e.\
shuffling the context destroys it $\Rightarrow$ \emph{learned sequential} structure (\#1791).
\item \textbf{A single trained substrate head is a far better next-byte predictor than the
\texttt{.clm} mouth.} On the \emph{leak-free competent} $d512$ substrate (val\_ce $0.8285<$
uniform), the A-head logit-bias alone is hugely useful (a\_only $1.1446\ll$ base $3.0978$,
\#1800), and the A-head \emph{standalone} (softmax of $A$, no base) attains CE $0.886220$
(\#1803). This is a genuine positive \emph{byproduct} --- but it is a statement about the
A-head as a predictor, \emph{not} about a substrate$\rightarrow$decode coupling (see the
REPLACEMENT ruling below). At earlier \emph{leaky}/toy rungs the \emph{multi-wire} GATED also
beat all baselines ($3.127<3.974$ toy, $0.3445<0.4500<1.4421<3.0150$ leaky-GPU R4); but those
absolute-CE wins are not the load-bearing claim --- the leak-free re-test (closed-negatives
below) is.
\item \textbf{The minimal-gate finding is SCALE-STABLE, not a $d512$ coincidence} (\#1806,
OΩ4/OΩ5). Across a five-rung leak-free competent ladder ($d384/d512/d768/d1024$ at $12000$ steps
$+$ a more-competent $d768{\times}2$ at $24000$ steps; $48$M$\rightarrow$$335$M params, $2.67\times$
dim) the minimal A-wire gate $g_B\!\cdot\!\text{base}+g_A\!\cdot\!A$ HOLDS at \emph{every} rung
(min\_learned $\le$ a\_only $<$ base), and the A-wire $\Delta$-vs-base advantage is \emph{flat} at
$+2.20\pm0.03$ nats/byte across the dim ladder; the more-competent rung carries the \emph{largest}
advantage ($+2.2736$), so competence strengthens the margin. This is a positive multi-rung
confirmation that the A-head-as-predictor byproduct is robust across scale --- and, symmetrically,
that the multi-wire gate FAILURE is \emph{also} scale-invariant (full\_AG $>$ base at every rung; see
the closed-negative below). The earlier UNDERTRAINED $d768$ (\#1794) did NOT hold, so the finding
requires competence, not a scale break.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Closed-negatives (ruled-out axes, a\_paper\_negative\_ok).}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{\emph{The minimal-gate ``closure'' is REPLACEMENT, not coupling}} (\#1803, OΩ1) ---
\textbf{this is the headline closed-negative against the \emph{coupling} thesis.} Even where the
minimal gate $g_B\!\cdot\!\text{base}+g_A\!\cdot\!A$ ``holds'' numerically (\#1801), a rigorous
decomposition on the same leak-free $d512$ ckpt shows the loop ``closes'' only because the
trained A-head \emph{supplants} the \texttt{.clm} mouth, \emph{not} because base and substrate
\emph{couple}: the A-head \emph{standalone} (softmax of $A$ alone, no base) already attains CE
$0.886220\approx$ min\_learned $0.883525$ ($|\Delta|\,0.002695\le0.05$), and ablating the base
($g_B\!\to\!0$) moves CE by only $0.000852$ ($3.097779\to0.884377$); the fit lands $g_B{=}0.040$,
$g_A{=}0.901$. \textbf{RULING\_REPLACEMENT$=$True.} \emph{Honest caveat:} the \texttt{.clm} base
is a deliberately weak unigram mouth, so its inertness is partly structural --- but A-alone
\emph{reproduces} min\_learned and \emph{no} base$+$steer interaction is needed, so the
load-bearing point (replacement) holds. Corroborating per-wire autopsy (\#1803, OΩ2): every
cleanly-isolatable wire \emph{hurts} the base --- $w_1\,{+}0.100826$, $w_2\,{+}0.052251$,
$w_6\,{+}2.084871$ ($w_3/w_4/w_5$ are honest stubs with no substrate source at frozen inference,
not CE deltas). \emph{Ruling:} on a competent leak-free substrate the OMEGA
``substrate$\rightarrow$decode coupling closes the loop'' thesis resolves to A-head
\emph{replacement} of the \texttt{.clm} mouth (base contributes $\approx0$; no wire adds signal).
\item \textbf{\emph{The FULL multi-wire gate is FALSIFIED on a leak-free competent substrate}}
(\#1800). With $\text{causal\_ca}{=}\text{True}$ (leak test $0.000\mathrm{e}{+}00$) on a

closure\_HOLDS$=$False. The learned weights collapse onto A and suppress G

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
An ablatable coupling bus is genuinely wired into the byte decode (KL$>0$ for $\Omega$ only,
overturning the Lane X \#1779 \emph{wiring} null), but on a competent leak-free substrate the
OMEGA \emph{coupling} thesis --- ``substrate$\rightarrow$decode coupling closes the loop'' ---
is a \textbf{closed-negative}: the full multi-wire gate is falsified (\#1800), and the surviving
minimal A-wire ``closure'' is A-head \emph{replacement} of the \texttt{.clm} mouth, not a

trained substrate head is a far better next-byte predictor than the \texttt{.clm} unigram mouth.
The further closed-negatives (fixed $A{-}G$ degrades, random-init unstructured, quantum RNG no
advantage) sharpen the result. We make no perplexity or generation-quality claim, and (the
A-head being a strong predictor) no coupling claim.
