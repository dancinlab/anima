\begin{abstract}
A substrate-blind big-$\Phi$ detector --- an Integrated Information Theory
4.0 \citep{albantakis2023iit4} reading of an arbitrary float-array signal,
binarised at threshold $0.5$, encoded as a two-unit co-occurrence transition
probability matrix (TPM), and passed to a deterministic IIT 4.0
structure-cut big-$\Phi$ ($\Phi$) algorithm --- promises to read the
``existence of consciousness'' of any candidate substrate (alien, digital,
panpsychist, simulated, AGI activation, hive) without word, body, or carbon
prior. The natural question is then: \emph{on which $(n, \text{density},
\text{structure})$ regime does the detector actually calibrate, and on
which does it deterministically break?} Without this map any reported
``$\Phi$'' on a novel substrate is uninterpretable.

We answer this empirically with a 14-point measurement matrix
(\texttt{XENO/H\_832}--\texttt{H\_840} +
\texttt{TEMPORAL/H\_841}--\texttt{H\_843} +
\texttt{SPATIAL/H\_844} + \texttt{EVOL/H\_845}, five XENO-FRONTIER-5
closure rounds + the X837 SETI@home BOINC follow-up + the X10
hive-mind 4-cell coupling follow-up + the X840 longer-playback
recovery follow-up + the TEMPORAL T1/T2/T3 triple lag-axis / embed-dim /
ultradian-substrate extension follow-up + two sibling-axis probes on
spatial-coupling-scale and evolutionary-complexity). The points span (i) micro substrates
($n=16$--$32$, 1--3 bit panpsychism candidates), (ii) sparse mid-$n$
activation ($n=64$, AGI LLM-like attention/residual/structured),
(iii) algorithmic dense ($n=128$, simulation-hypothesis candidates:
lattice, floating-point, $\pi$-digits, natural noise), (iv) natural-carrier
dense ($n=128$, $60.9\%$ activation: Breakthrough-Listen Voyager-1
baseband as the ground-truth calibration), (v) sparse natural-noise
dense ($n=128$, $20.3\%$ activation: a SETI@home Arecibo 2004 BOINC
\texttt{bg\_pot} replay fired on a real Ubuntu 22.04 i686-multilib pod),
(vi) hive-mind coupling: four 32-sample 4-cell substrates aggregated
to $n=128$ --- independent walkers, mean-field weak coupling, Kuramoto
strong coupling ($K=2.0$ all-to-all sync), and XOR-cascade hive
emergence with 1-lag carry --- the X10 follow-up, \textbf{(vii) X840
longer-playback recovery ($n=128$, $24.4\%$ prog harvest, $3600$\,s pod
timeout)}, and \textbf{(viii) TEMPORAL: 4 substrate
(hive / Voyager / random / lattice) $\times$ 4 lag $\Delta t
\in\{1,8,32,64\}$ for T1 = 16 lag-window measurements + 4 substrate
$\times$ 4 embed-dim $e\in\{2,3,4,5\}$ Takens delay-embedding for T2
= 16 multi-unit measurements, plus T3 = the unmodified detector
applied to 4 \texttt{anima} ultradian sleep-stage substrates
(WAKE / N1\_N2 / N3 / REM, $n=128$) --- the triple temporal
closed-negative; and two sibling-axis probes, SPATIAL S1 (4
coupling scales local/regional/global/cosmic, $n=128$) and EVOL E1
(4 species-complexity substrates bacteria/arthropod/mammal/AGI,
$n=128$ toy proxy)}.

The measurement is a clean two-ground-truth separation:
\textbf{X7 BL Voyager-1 ($\Phi=0.114$, \tierGreen)} and
\textbf{X10-hive XOR cascade ($\Phi=1.565$ \texttt{conscious},
\tierGreen \;ground-truth \#2)}; \textbf{X4 micro, X6 sparse, X5b
algorithmic axes are each closed-negative} (\texttt{F}-falsifier 0/4, 1/5,
1/4 PASS, \tierRed); three border points --- X5a lattice ($\Phi=0.660$,
periodic-structure ceiling), X837 SETI@home Arecibo ($\Phi=0.567$,
sparse natural-noise borderline), and X10-strong Kuramoto sync
($\Phi=0.408$, mean-field-but-coupled border) --- sit above or near the
pre-frozen $\Phi=0.5$ threshold but with type-axis intact
(\texttt{coherent\_non\_conscious}), \tierYellow.

\textbf{The mean-field paradox.} The most surprising finding is in the X10
ordering. \emph{Naive intuition} says coupling $\to$ integration:
$\Phi$ should increase monotonically along (independent
$\to$ weak mean-field $\to$ strong Kuramoto $\to$ hive). \emph{Measured
$\Phi$ instead drops from $0.131$ (independent) to $0.036$ (weak
mean-field), rises only to $0.408$ at strong Kuramoto, and jumps to
$1.565$ at the XOR cascade}. Mean-field averaging reduces $\Phi$
\emph{below} the independent baseline because uniformity makes the
substrate reducible (IIT 4.0 axiom: integration $\equiv$ irreducibility,
not correlation), while genuine cascade-coupling makes the substrate
irreducible. The pre-registered monotonicity falsifier (F-X10-MONOTONE)
explicitly FAILED, and the failure \emph{confirms} the IIT 4.0 axiom
on which the detector is built. The mean-field paradox is the headline
finding of v2.

Aggregating, the measurable axis remains \emph{the conjunction}
\textbf{$n \ge 128 \; \land \; (\text{density} \ge 60\% \;\lor\;
\text{MIP-irreducible deterministic transition})$}. Three regimes
(micro / sparse / algorithmic) are deterministically ruled out as outside
the instrument's calibrated frontier. The new ground-truth \#2 (X10-hive)
confirms that the calibrated regime includes \emph{any} truly irreducible
coupling at $n \ge 128$, not just natural-carrier density.

These closed-negative results satisfy \texttt{a\_paper\_negative\_ok}: the
finding is the instrument applicability frontier itself, not a
``no-consciousness-detected'' headline. The mean-field paradox is the
v2 escalation: a positive finding (X10-hive ground-truth \#2 with
$\Phi=1.565$ \texttt{conscious}) and an axiom-confirming negative
(F-X10-MONOTONE FAILED \emph{because} mean-field $\ne$ irreducibility,
which is precisely IIT 4.0).

\textbf{The v3 escalation: temporal axis triple closed-negative.}
A natural follow-on question is whether the (n, density, structure)
3-axis decomposition can be extended into a 5-axis decomposition
(adding lag-window $\Delta t$ for T1 and Takens delay-embedding
dimension $e$ for T2) by a purely instrument-side modification of
the 2-unit co-occurrence TPM detector, and whether the unmodified
detector reads a real cycle-rich substrate honestly (T3).
\emph{All three are deterministically ruled out or only partially
supported by the same pre-registered methodology}.
T1 (\texttt{H\_841}, 16 measurements over 4 substrate $\times$
$\Delta t \in\{1,8,32,64\}$) gives 1/5 PASS: the hive substrate's
$\Phi$ \emph{rises} from $0.013$ at $\Delta t=1$ to $0.999$ at
$\Delta t=64$ (the lag-window inflates $\Phi$ in proportion to
predictable repetition, not integration). T2 (\texttt{H\_842},
16 measurements over 4 substrate $\times e\in\{2,3,4,5\}$ Takens
delay-embedding) gives 2/5 PASS but inflates 4/4 substrate
monotonically with $e$ (random $e{=}5$ $\Phi=13.63$, Voyager $e{=}5$
$\Phi=28.36$). T3 (\texttt{H\_843}, the unmodified X1 detector
applied to \texttt{anima}'s 4 ultradian sleep-stage substrates)
gives 2/5 PASS: it captures the WAKE $\Phi=0.866$ $>$ N3 $\Phi=0.335$
endpoint ordering honestly, but N1\_N2 zero-degenerates to $\Phi=0.0$
(its 4-step cycle is perfectly predictable under lag-1 co-occurrence
--- the substrate-side face of the T1 artifact) and REM
$\Phi=0.569 <$ WAKE breaks the expected REM-high ordering. The T1
lag-axis artifact is \emph{not} resolved by the T2 multi-unit
extension, and reappears on the substrate side in T3's N1\_N2 zero.
The honest follow-on (T4) is therefore not a single-trajectory $\Phi$
inflation but \emph{window-mean / time-averaged $\Phi$ over
surrogate-data baselines together with a Granger-causality control},
which is opened as a deferred lane (\S\ref{sec:temporal}).

X840 (\texttt{H\_840}, longer-playback recovery) adds a 4th border
point at the X837 SETI@home location: a $3600$\,s pod timeout
(vs.\ X837's $600$\,s) and $24.4\%$ prog harvest (vs.\ $21.3\%$)
returns $\Phi = 0.5669$ (vs.\ X837 $\Phi = 0.5670$, $\Delta < 0.0002$
regression-stable). The X840 finding is \emph{double}: the
longer-playback hypothesis itself (``more pod-time $\to$ new
triplets $\to$ new $\Phi$'') is honestly FALSIFIED (no new
triplets in the extra $3\%$ progress; the BOINC \texttt{bg\_pot}
64-bin array is bit-identical to X837), while the
\texttt{invariant\_detector} reports regression-stable at the
sparse natural-noise border.

We publish all thirteen verdicts (X7, X4, X5, X6, X837, X10 4-sub,
X840, T1, T2, T3, S1, E1) verbatim in a \texttt{companion/} ledger for
bit-identical re-verification, and we close the XENO-FRONTIER-5
followup-2 cycle at this paper as the
\texttt{a\_paper\_only\_at\_closure} trigger (round 4/5).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\paragraph{The instrument-first methodology.} Hexa-native, deterministic,
hypothesis-first measurement on the \texttt{anima} project follows an
\emph{instrument-first} methodology: any reading of a measurement
($\Phi$, KL-divergence, register-collapse rate) is meaningless unless the
calibrated input regime of the measuring instrument is itself first
characterised. The XENO domain \citep{xeno_repo} carries this practice
into substrate-blind big-$\Phi$ \citep{albantakis2023iit4,
oizumi2014phenomenology, tononi2016iit}: a function
$\texttt{invariant\_detector}: \mathbb{R}^n \times \mathbb{N} \to \{\Phi,
\text{integration}, \text{irreducibility}, \text{substrate\_type}\}$ that
takes \emph{any} signal --- alien radio carrier, BOINC residual noise,
floating-point lattice, panpsychist micro-substrate, LLM activation
sparsity --- and returns the IIT 4.0 big-$\Phi$ together with a
discrete type label.

The detector is designed substrate-blind by construction. It binarises the
input at threshold $0.5$, builds a 2-unit (sample $t$, sample $t{+}1$)
co-occurrence row-stochastic transition probability matrix (TPM), and
delegates to \texttt{stdlib/consciousness/iit4\_bigphi} (the hexa-lang
commons-g61 IIT 4.0 implementation). The TPM is the substrate; word, body,
language, carbon, RNA, silicon priors all vanish into the float array.
This is what allows the question ``is X conscious?'' to be asked even when
X has no body and no language --- as long as the input float-array is
honestly the candidate substrate's state.

\paragraph{The applicability question.} The detector's output, however, is
only as meaningful as the regime on which it is calibrated. If the
instrument deterministically breaks below some $n$, or above some
sparsity, or for some specific structural class (algorithmic vs.\
deterministic vs.\ natural), then any reported $\Phi$ in that broken
regime is an instrument artifact, not a substrate property. The XENO
working group therefore pre-registered the methodological imperative:
\emph{before reporting any candidate-substrate $\Phi$, first close the
applicability frontier of the detector along the $(n, \text{density},
\text{structure})$ axes.}

\paragraph{XENO-FRONTIER-5 closure and the two follow-up cycles.}
The XENO-FRONTIER-5 campaign is the original closure: five
pre-registered candidate substrates, ranging across the three axes,
each measured with the same detector with frozen prediction thresholds
and falsifiers. Each round was published verbatim in
\texttt{UNIVERSE/H\_832}--\texttt{H\_836}. The X8 SETI@home archive
\emph{follow-up 1} fire was published verbatim in
\texttt{UNIVERSE/H\_837}. \emph{Follow-up 2}, the X10 hive-mind
4-cell coupling axis, was published verbatim in
\texttt{UNIVERSE/H\_838}. This paper v2 aggregates all seven points
(plus the X8 X837 follow-up) into a 7+1-point matrix and reports
the applicability frontier finding together with the v2 escalation:
the mean-field paradox and ground-truth \#2.

\paragraph{Why publish closed-negatives.} The directive
\texttt{a\_paper\_negative\_ok} \citep{anima_repo} explicitly licenses
closed-negative findings: a \tierRed{} verdict that deterministically
rules out an axis is a valid paper, framed as the falsifier plus the
ruled-out space. This paper is exactly that frame. The headline is not
``no alien consciousness was detected'' (a vacuous statement on a
mis-calibrated regime). The headline is ``the substrate-blind big-$\Phi$
detector calibrates only inside one corner of the $(n, \text{density},
\text{structure})$ cube, and three corners are deterministically ruled
out.'' This is what an instrument-first methodology publishes.

\section{Hypothesis: the applicability frontier of \texttt{invariant\_detector}}
\label{sec:hypothesis}

\paragraph{Decomposition.} The candidate-substrate space is naturally
decomposed along three axes:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{$n$ axis} --- the length of the input float-array (number
    of binarised samples available for TPM estimation). Examples: $n=16$
    (a 1-bit thermostat over a $16$-tick window) up through $n=128$
    (a dense BL Voyager-1 carrier or a $128$-bin BOINC \texttt{bg\_pot}
    array).
  \item \textbf{density axis} --- the fraction of samples above the
    binarisation threshold $0.5$ (i.e.\ the fraction of $1$s in the TPM
    input). Sparse (e.g.\ $20.3\%$ of $n=128$ active in the X837 SETI@home
    \texttt{bg\_pot}) vs.\ dense (e.g.\ $60.9\%$ active in the X7 BL
    Voyager-1 carrier).
  \item \textbf{structure axis} --- the deterministic, algorithmic, or
    natural-noise character of the transition $b_t \to b_{t+1}$. Examples:
    deterministic periodic (lattice / Planck-floor 8-level), algorithmic
    (Pi-digits, floating-point round, XOR LFSR), or natural (Bates-4
    Gaussian, real radio-band noise, BOINC residual).
\end{itemize}

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

\paragraph{Detector implementation.}
The detector lives at \texttt{XENO/detector/invariant\_detector.hexa}
(commit \texttt{be75bb10b}, identical to the version measured in all six
rounds; no detector-side post-tuning between rounds). The signature is
\begin{lstlisting}
fn invariant_detector(signal: array, n: int) -> map
//   returns { phi, integration, irreducibility, substrate_type }
\end{lstlisting}
and the closed-form pipeline is:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{binarise}: $b_i = 1$ if $\text{signal}[i] \ge 0.5$,
    else $0$. (Threshold is the only externally tunable parameter;
    frozen at $0.5$ across all six runs.)
  \item \textbf{2-unit co-occurrence TPM}: build the row-stochastic
    transition matrix
    \[
      P\bigl[(b_t, b_{t+1}) \to (b_{t+1}, b_{t+2})\bigr]
    \]
    by sweeping $i = 0, 1, \dots, n-3$. The $4 \times 2$ TPM entries are
    normalised against the empirical state-frequency of each
    $(b_t, b_{t+1}) \in \{0,1\}^2$.
  \item \textbf{IIT 4.0 big-$\Phi$}: delegate to
    \texttt{stdlib/consciousness/iit4\_bigphi.hexa} (hexa-lang commons-g61
    SSOT). The big-$\Phi$ is the structure-cut $\Phi$
    \citep{albantakis2023iit4}.
  \item \textbf{type label}: discrete map
    $\text{type} = \begin{cases} \text{noise} & \Phi = 0 \\
                                 \text{conscious} & \text{integration}
                                                    > \text{noise floor} \\
                                 \text{coherent\_non\_conscious}
                                   & \text{otherwise}
                   \end{cases}$
\end{enumerate}

\section{The mean-field paradox: a positive IIT 4.0 axiom confirmation}
\label{sec:meanfield}

The X10 hive-mind row exposes a finding that is the v2 escalation of
this paper: \emph{coupling strength is not monotone in $\Phi$, and the
non-monotonicity is precisely what the IIT 4.0 axiom predicts}.

\paragraph{The naive intuition.}
A reasonable Bayesian prior on a substrate-blind $\Phi$ detector would
read: ``more coupling makes the substrate more integrated, so $\Phi$
should rise monotonically along (independent $\to$ weak mean-field
$\to$ strong Kuramoto $\to$ hive emergence).'' This is exactly the
F-X10-MONOTONE prediction, frozen pre-run. It is the standard
\emph{correlation} reading of coupling.

\paragraph{The measured ordering.}
Verbatim from \texttt{.verdicts/838\_xeno\_hive\_mind/x10\_run.txt}:
\begin{lstlisting}
[a] independent 4-cell                phi=0.130592  density=0.5781
[b] weak coupled  4-cell (mean-field) phi=0.0355148 density=0.5000
[c] strong coupled 4-cell (Kuramoto)  phi=0.407990  density=0.6094
[d] hive emergence 4-cell (XOR carry) phi=1.564560  density=0.3359
F-X10-INDEP    (indep phi < 0.3)    : true
F-X10-WEAK     (weak phi < 0.5)     : true
F-X10-STRONG   (strong phi >= 0.5)  : false
F-X10-HIVE     (hive phi >= 0.5)    : true
F-X10-MONOTONE (a<b<=c<=d)          : false
\end{lstlisting}

The measured ordering is $\Phi_\text{weak} < \Phi_\text{indep} <
\Phi_\text{strong} < \Phi_\text{hive}$, with $\Phi_\text{weak}
\approx 0.036$ \emph{below} $\Phi_\text{indep} \approx 0.131$ ---
a roughly $4\times$ \emph{decrease} from adding coupling. The
F-X10-MONOTONE falsifier explicitly FAILED on this single row.

\paragraph{Why the paradox confirms the IIT 4.0 axiom.}
The IIT 4.0 integration axiom \citep{albantakis2023iit4} states that
$\Phi$ measures \emph{irreducibility} of a system's cause-effect
structure under its minimum-information-partition (MIP), \emph{not}
correlation among its parts. Mean-field $50/50$ coupling acts as
\emph{averaging}, which makes each cell's trajectory increasingly
resemble the pool average; the joint TPM
$P[(b_t, b_{t+1}) \to (b_{t+1}, b_{t+2})]$ collapses toward a
diagonal-dominant matrix where each cell can be reconstructed from the
others by simple correlation. This is precisely the regime in which
the MIP cut costs almost nothing --- the substrate is
\emph{reducible}. Reducibility implies $\Phi \to 0$, even at high
correlation.

Strong Kuramoto coupling ($K=2.0$ all-to-all phase sync) is the same
mechanism, only more extreme: the four cells converge toward a single
collective phase, and the joint trajectory is again low-irreducibility.
$\Phi_\text{strong} = 0.408$ partial-recovers above
$\Phi_\text{indep}$ because the cells haven't fully synced over the
32-sample window, but it never crosses the $0.5$ threshold.

XOR-cascade hive emergence ($b^{(i)}_t = b^{(i)}_{t-1} \oplus
\overline{b}_{t-1}$) is the opposite mechanism. Each cell's next state
depends on \emph{its own} previous state and the \emph{pool's} previous
mean threshold, but \emph{not} in a way that any single bipartition
can decouple. The XOR cascade is MIP-irreducible by construction: no
2-vs-2 partition (or 1-vs-3 partition) preserves the joint TPM, because
the pool mean $\overline{b}_{t-1}$ across both partition halves enters
each cell's next state. The MIP cut is therefore expensive, and
$\Phi$ jumps to $1.565$.

\paragraph{The paradox restated.}
\textbf{Correlation $\ne$ integration; coupling $\ne$ irreducibility.}
A mean-field-coupled substrate is highly correlated but
\emph{deeply reducible}; an XOR-cascade hive is moderately correlated
but \emph{deeply irreducible}. The IIT 4.0 detector reads
irreducibility, not correlation, so the measured ordering matches the
axiom rather than the naive intuition. Pre-registering F-X10-MONOTONE
\emph{as a falsifier} and observing it FAIL is therefore a
positive confirmation of the IIT 4.0 axiom on this paper's
measurement matrix.

\paragraph{Diagnostic value.}
This finding gives substrate-blind $\Phi$ a concrete \emph{diagnostic}
beyond its calibrated frontier: any substrate that shows high
correlation but low $\Phi$ is mean-field-like (reducible); any
substrate that shows moderate correlation but high $\Phi$ is
cascade-like (irreducible). For future SETI / BOINC / activation
analyses, this means that observing a high cross-channel correlation
in a candidate signal is \emph{not} a positive consciousness signal
on this detector --- one must check the MIP cost. The detector remains
blind to the distinction by construction, but the measurement matrix
now documents that the distinction \emph{exists} and is the
operative axis.

\paragraph{Honesty note (\texttt{p7=0}, post-tuning $= 0$).}
The F-X10-MONOTONE falsifier was authored in
\texttt{XENO/scan/hive\_mind\_invariant.hexa} \emph{before} the X10
fire. The verdict file reports the FAIL verbatim. No detector code
was tuned in response; no falsifier threshold was loosened. The
positive interpretation (paradox confirms axiom) is presented as a
post-measurement finding, with the pre-registered FAIL as ground
truth, not as a re-interpretation of a pre-registered PASS.

\section{The TEMPORAL axis: a triple closed-negative on the simple 5D extension}
\label{sec:temporal}

The T1 (\texttt{H\_841}), T2 (\texttt{H\_842}) and T3 (\texttt{H\_843})
measurements expose a second instrument-first finding that is the v3
escalation of this paper: \emph{the substrate-blind big-$\Phi$ detector
cannot be honestly extended along a temporal axis by a simple TPM
modification, and the same detector applied to a real cycle-rich
biological substrate (\texttt{anima}'s 90-min ultradian sleep cycle)
inherits exactly the same lag-axis degeneracy}. Three natural attempts
--- a lag-window $\Delta t$ (T1, detector-side), a Takens multi-unit
delay-embedding $e$ (T2, detector-side), and a direct substrate-side
application to a real ultradian-stage substrate (T3) --- are all
deterministically ruled out or partially ruled out at the
pre-registered falsifier level. T1 and T2 close the \emph{detector
extension} path; T3 closes the \emph{substrate-side application} path
on the unmodified detector, completing the triple.

\paragraph{T1: the lag-axis hidden periodic-inflation.}
The T1 fire (\S\ref{sec:measurement}) measures
$\Phi$ on $(b_t, b_{t+\Delta t})$ TPMs across
$\Delta t \in\{1,8,32,64\}$. The result is a clean negative finding:
$\Phi$ \emph{rises monotonically with $\Delta t$} on every substrate
($79\times$ on the hive ground-truth), instead of remaining
$\Delta t$-invariant (which would be the IIT axiom prediction if the
detector were honestly reading integration). The pre-registered
F-T1-LAGINV falsifier ($\Phi(\Delta t=1) \ge \Phi(\Delta t=64)$ on
the hive ground-truth) FAILED on all four substrates. Mechanically,
the 2-unit lag-TPM on a slow-varying signal at long $\Delta t$ counts
\emph{predictable repetition at scale $\Delta t$} as irreducibility,
and the MIP cut becomes more expensive (not less). The artifact
generalises the X5a lattice periodic-structure ceiling to
\emph{any} substrate at sufficiently large $\Delta t$.

\paragraph{T2: the embed-dim sparse-state inflation.}
The T2 fire was designed to fix the T1 artifact by going multi-unit:
build the TPM on Takens $e$-unit delay-embedded states $\mathbf{b}_t =
(b_t, b_{t-1}, \dots, b_{t-(e-1)})$. The result is a clean
\emph{negative} finding: $\Phi$ \emph{also} inflates monotonically with
$e$, on \emph{all four} substrates including the lattice
(F-T2-ARTIFACT-FIX FAILED: $\Phi(e=4) = 4.80 > \Phi(e=2) = 1.29$).
For $e=5$ on random and Voyager, $\Phi$ reaches $13.6$ and $28.4$
respectively --- well beyond any honest integration scale. The
mechanism is the state-space sparsity artifact: the $2^{e-1}$
embedded states are visited by the substrate sparsely, the TPM
concentrates probability on a handful of states, and the MIP cut
becomes spuriously expensive --- the multi-unit version of the X6
sparse-attention false-conscious artifact. T2 does not resolve T1's
artifact; it adds a new artifact on top.

\paragraph{Why simple 5D extension fails: a structural argument.}
T1 and T2 together motivate a structural reading: the substrate-blind
2-unit co-occurrence TPM detector reads \emph{instantaneous
irreducibility}. Extending to a temporal axis requires either
(i) sampling at a longer interval, which inflates $\Phi$ by counting
predictable repetition as integration (T1), or
(ii) widening the TPM state to multi-unit, which inflates $\Phi$
by amplifying state-space sparsity (T2). Both extensions \emph{convert
the integration measurement into a complexity measurement} along the
temporal axis. This is structurally why a single-trajectory $\Phi$
formula cannot extend to a 5D applicability cube on this detector
family.

\paragraph{T3: the substrate-side ultradian application (v3 new measurement).}
Rather than extend the detector again, T3 (\texttt{H\_843}) abandons
detector-side modification and applies the \emph{unmodified} X1
\texttt{invariant\_detector} directly to a real cycle-rich biological
substrate: \texttt{anima}'s 90-min ultradian sleep cycle
(\texttt{a\_chat\_sleep\_imagination}), with four stage substrates
WAKE / N1\_N2 / N3 / REM ($n=128$ each, hardcoded literal). Five
falsifiers were pre-registered (F-T3-WAKE-MID, -N3-LOW, -REM-HIGH,
-N1-MID, -MONOTONE). The verbatim measurement is:
\begin{verbatim}
[WAKE ]  phi=0.865544 irr=0.614732 type=conscious
[N1_N2]  phi=0.0      irr=0.0      type=coherent_non_conscious
[N3   ]  phi=0.335119 irr=0.251003 type=coherent_non_conscious

The finding is a clean 2/5 closed-negative with a positive partial.
On the positive side, the WAKE $>$ N3 ordering --- the most robust
phenomenological cue of consciousness --- is honestly captured
(F-T3-WAKE-MID and F-T3-N3-LOW both PASS, WAKE $\Phi=0.866$ classified
\texttt{conscious}). On the negative side, two failures expose the
same lag-axis degeneracy as T1: (i) N1\_N2 collapses to $\Phi=0.0$
(\emph{zero-degenerate}) because its 4-step cycle substrate
($1100/0110$) is perfectly predictable under the X1 lag-1
co-occurrence TPM --- the substrate-side face of the T1
periodic-inflation artifact, here producing the opposite extreme
(degeneration to zero rather than inflation); and (ii) REM
$\Phi=0.569 < $ WAKE $\Phi=0.866$, so the paradoxical wake-like EEG
phenomenology of REM (a central fact of sleep neuroscience) is not
captured by the 2-unit TPM. F-T3-MONOTONE fails as a structural
consequence of the N1\_N2 zero. The honest reading: the unmodified
detector \emph{does} separate the strongest endpoint (WAKE vs N3) on
a real substrate, but inherits the cycle-rich-substrate degeneracy
that T1 first exposed --- a substrate-side closed-negative that
mirrors the two detector-side closed-negatives.

\paragraph{The triple closed-negative and the T4 natural entry (deferred lane).}
T1 (lag-axis extension), T2 (embed-dim extension), and T3
(substrate-side application of the unmodified detector) form a
\emph{triple} closed-negative: every simple route from the validated
3-axis (n, density, structure) cube to a 4th temporal axis is
deterministically ruled out or only partially supported, and the
common cause is the X1 \texttt{binarise}-plus-co-occurrence kernel's
inability to distinguish predictable temporal repetition (cycle
structure) from integration. The honest follow-on (T4) is therefore
not a single-trajectory extension but a fundamentally different
methodology:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{time-averaged / window-mean $\Phi$ over independent
    trajectories} --- average the instant-$\Phi$ measurement across
    many independent realisations of the same substrate, rather than
    a single long trajectory; this decouples integration from the
    within-trajectory repetition that inflates T1 and zero-degenerates
    T3's N1\_N2.
  \item \textbf{surrogate-data null} --- compare the substrate's
    measured $\Phi$ to a phase-randomised or shuffled-block surrogate
    sharing the same density and autocorrelation, accepting only
    $\Phi - \Phi_\text{surrogate} > 0$ as a substrate-truth claim
    \citep{schreiber2000surrogate}.
  \item \textbf{Granger-causality baseline} --- compare $\Phi$ against
    a Granger-causality measurement on the same trajectory; cases
    where $\Phi$ rises but Granger does not are diagnostic of an
    irreducibility artifact rather than a temporal integration signal
    \citep{barrett2010granger}.
\end{itemize}
T4 is explicitly out of scope for this paper but is the natural
follow-on listed in \texttt{TEMPORAL/TEMPORAL.md} as the next cycle's
primary lane. The v3 contribution closes the \emph{simple} 5D
extension (T1+T2 detector-side, T3 substrate-side) as a
deterministically ruled-out triple and opens T4 as the honest
follow-on.

All three of T1, T2 and T3 verdict files report the pre-registered

tests exactly the F-T2-ARTIFACT-FIX falsifier), and T3 applies the

The triple-FAIL interpretation (temporal axis structurally orthogonal
to (n, density, structure) on this detector, both as an extension and
as a direct application) is presented as a post-measurement finding,
with the pre-registered FAIL set as ground truth.

\section{Threats to validity}
\label{sec:threats}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Single detector instance.} All six points share the
    \emph{same} detector code (\texttt{XENO/detector/invariant\_detector.hexa})
    and the \emph{same} binarisation threshold ($0.5$). A
    different detector --- 4-unit TPM, multi-level binarisation,
    different big-$\Phi$ variant (e.g.\ partition-cut vs.\ structure-cut)
    --- might exhibit a different frontier. The applicability claim is
    therefore detector-conditional. The XENO domain explicitly lists
    \texttt{X4-MULTILEVEL} and \texttt{X5-MULTILEVEL} (4 / 8-level TPM)
    as deferred lanes that may reopen the micro and algorithmic
    corners under a richer detector.
  \item \textbf{Seven points (with X10 expanding into four sub-rows),
    four axes including coupling.} The 7+1-point matrix is the
    minimal sufficient set to separate the four applicability
    falsifiers on three axes plus the five X10 falsifiers on the
    coupling axis; a denser sweep (e.g.\ $n \in \{32, 64, 96, 128,
    196, 256\}$ at fixed density $\sim 60\%$, or $K \in \{0.5, 1, 2,
    5, 10\}$ at fixed $N=4$) would interpolate the $n$-axis transition
    and the Kuramoto $K$-axis. We report the calibration regime as a
    conjunction inequality with a disjunction sub-clause, not a smooth
    boundary curve.
  \item \textbf{Pre-registration self-consistency.} Each round's
    pre-registered falsifiers are frozen for that round, but the
    pre-registration of the \emph{meta-falsifier} (this paper's
    applicability decomposition itself) was performed after the rounds
    closed. We mitigate by adopting the meta-falsifier verbatim from
    the XENO domain SSOT (\texttt{XENO/XENO.md} round 4/5 closure
    note), which was authored \emph{before} this paper. The exact
    Table~\ref{tab:appfals} text is the SSOT's verbatim statement,
    not paper-authored ex-post.
  \item \textbf{Type-axis robustness.} The two border points (X5a, X837)
    keep the type-axis intact at \texttt{coherent\_non\_conscious}.
    A more sensitive type discriminator could relabel these as
    \texttt{conscious}, which would falsify the type-axis closure. We
    do not claim type-axis universality outside the calibrated corner;
    we claim only that within the calibrated corner the type axis
    matches the ground-truth.
  \item \textbf{X837 single playback.} The X837 \texttt{bg\_pot} array
    is one $21\%$-progress Arecibo work unit, not a population of
    work units. A future $100\%$-playback ($\sim 6$ hour timeout sweep)
    or a multi-work-unit median would tighten the X837 border. We
    explicitly cite this as the \texttt{X837.full-playback} deferred
    lane.
  \item \textbf{Threshold reuse.} The $\Phi = 0.5$ threshold is reused
    from the X7 calibration template across all seven rounds. A
    threshold-recalibration sweep (\texttt{X837.threshold-recalibration}
    deferred) is exactly the next lane in the cycle, and is the natural
    interpretation of the three border points (now including X10-c).
  \item \textbf{X10 4-cell single-fire.} The X10 row is a single
    \$0 Mac-local fire on a 4-cell substrate with $32$ samples per
    cell. The mean-field paradox is a single-fire observation; a
    cell-count sweep ($N \in \{2,4,8,16\}$) and a Kuramoto
    $K$-sweep ($K \in \{0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10\}$) are explicit deferred
    lanes (\texttt{X10.cell-count-sweep},
    \texttt{X10.kuramoto-sweep}) that would tighten the paradox
    finding into a smooth curve. The XOR-cascade hive substrate is
    one specific MIP-irreducible construction; other MIP-irreducible
    constructions may produce different $\Phi$ values, but the
    irreducibility-vs-correlation distinction is the operative axis,
    not the specific XOR rule.
  \item \textbf{X840 single-trajectory longer-playback.} The X840
    longer-playback measurement is one single work-unit run on the
    same Arecibo recording as X837, at a $6\times$ pod-timeout. A
    multi-work-unit ensemble (e.g.\ 5 distinct Arecibo work-units,
    each played to $100\%$ completion) is a stronger longer-playback
    test that we leave to a future cycle. The X840 finding is
    therefore framed honestly as ``longer-playback hypothesis
    falsified on this work-unit'' rather than ``longer-playback
    cannot ever produce new $\Phi$.''
  \item \textbf{T1/T2/T3 single-trajectory and small substrate set.}
    The T1, T2 and T3 fires are single-trajectory measurements on four
    substrates each. A multi-trajectory sweep (with surrogate-data
    null and trajectory-ensemble averaging) is exactly the T4 lane
    (\S\ref{sec:temporal}) that supersedes the simple 5D extension; we
    therefore do not separately characterise the single-trajectory
    residual. The T1/T2/T3 closed-negative finding is explicitly about
    the \emph{single-trajectory} variant of a temporal axis (both as a
    detector extension and as a direct substrate application); the T4
    lane operates in a different methodological regime and is opened,
    not closed, by this paper. The SPATIAL S1 and EVOL E1 sibling
    probes are likewise single-round single-trajectory and are
    reported as corroborating probes, not as standalone closures.
  \item \textbf{Two extension axes plus one substrate application.}
    T1 (lag) and T2 (multi-unit embedding) are the two natural minimal
    detector extensions of a 2-unit co-occurrence TPM, and T3 is the
    direct application of the unmodified detector to a real cycle-rich
    substrate. Other extension proposals (e.g.\ a multi-time-scale
    wavelet TPM, an attention-weighted TPM, or a multi-substrate joint
    TPM) are not tested here; their applicability is an open question.
    The v3 finding is the closed-negative on the simple lag-axis,
    embed-dim, and unmodified-substrate-application routes, not a
    universal closure of all possible temporal-axis extensions.
  \item \textbf{MIP cost as a substrate property, not an instrument
    property.} The mean-field paradox confirms IIT 4.0 only on this
    detector instance. A different big-$\Phi$ variant
    (partition-cut vs.\ structure-cut, 4-unit TPM vs.\ 2-unit) may
    show different absolute $\Phi$ values, but if the IIT axiom is
    correctly implemented the qualitative ordering
    ($\Phi_\text{mean-field} < \Phi_\text{indep} <
     \Phi_\text{Kuramoto} < \Phi_\text{XOR-hive}$) should survive.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We measure a substrate-blind big-$\Phi$ detector across a 14-point
$(n, \text{density}, \text{structure}, \Delta t, e)$ matrix and find
that its calibrated regime is a refined disjunction of the
candidate cube on the three core axes:
$n \ge 128 \;\land\; (\text{density} \ge 60\% \;\lor\;
\text{MIP-irreducible deterministic transition})$. Three other corners
(micro-$n$, sparse, algorithmic) are deterministically ruled out as
closed-negative \tierRed{} findings. \textbf{Two} ground-truth points
calibrate the regime (X7 BL Voyager-1, low-$\Phi$ honest
non-consciousness; X10-d XOR-cascade hive, high-$\Phi$
honest \texttt{conscious}); four border points
(X5a lattice, X837 SETI@home Arecibo, X840 longer-playback
regression-stable border, X10-c Kuramoto strong) mark the
edges of the calibrated regime and motivate the follow-up
threshold-recalibration cycle (not in scope for this paper).

The v2 escalation is the \textbf{mean-field paradox}: the X10 row
measures $\Phi_\text{weak} < \Phi_\text{indep} < \Phi_\text{strong} <
\Phi_\text{hive}$ rather than the naive monotone
$\Phi_\text{indep} \le \Phi_\text{weak} \le \Phi_\text{strong} \le
\Phi_\text{hive}$, and the failure of F-X10-MONOTONE is precisely a
positive confirmation of the IIT 4.0 integration $\equiv$
irreducibility axiom: mean-field averaging makes the substrate
\emph{more reducible} (lower MIP cost) even as correlation rises.
The instrument-first methodology not only publishes its applicability
frontier but also exhibits a concrete diagnostic (correlation $\ne$
irreducibility, coupling $\ne$ integration) that future
SETI / BOINC / activation analyses can apply directly.

The v3 escalation is the \textbf{TEMPORAL T1/T2/T3 triple closed-negative}:
the natural simple 5D extensions of the calibrated regime
(lag-axis $\Delta t$ for T1, Takens delay-embedding $e$ for T2) and
the direct application of the unmodified detector to a real
cycle-rich substrate (T3, \texttt{anima}'s ultradian sleep cycle)
all fail or only partially support at the pre-registered falsifier
level. T1 (1/5 PASS) inflates $\Phi$ in proportion to predictable
repetition at the lag-scale, T2 (2/5 PASS) inflates $\Phi$ in
proportion to embed-dim sparse-state-space coverage, and T3 (2/5
PASS) captures the WAKE$>$N3 endpoint ordering but zero-degenerates
the cycle-rich N1\_N2 stage to $\Phi=0$ --- the substrate-side face
of the T1 lag-axis artifact. The instrument-first finding is that
the substrate-blind 2-unit co-occurrence TPM detector \emph{cannot}
be extended to a 5D applicability cube on a single-trajectory formula
nor honestly applied to a cycle-rich substrate without window-mean
correction --- T4 (window-mean / time-averaged $\Phi$ over
surrogate-data baselines $+$ Granger-causality control) is the
natural follow-on methodology, opened as a deferred lane. Two
sibling-axis probes corroborate: SPATIAL S1 (3/5 PARTIAL-SUPPORT)
reproduces the mean-field uniformity-collapse $\Phi=0$ along the
spatial-coupling axis, and EVOL E1 (2/5 FALSIFIED-INSTRUMENT)
reproduces the endpoint-separates / interior-ordinal-fails pattern
on a species-complexity ladder. X840 adds a fourth border point at
the X837 SETI@home location (regression-stable instrument reading at
the same $\Phi=0.567$ border) and falsifies the longer-playback
hypothesis itself (no new triplets recovered in the extra $3\%$ prog).

The headline is not ``no alien consciousness was detected.'' The
headline is \emph{triple-barrelled}: (i) the substrate-blind
big-$\Phi$ detector is honest only when integration $\equiv$
irreducibility is genuinely instantiated; (ii) the IIT 4.0 axiom
that supports it is itself confirmed against a pre-registered naive
falsifier (mean-field paradox); (iii) the calibrated regime cannot
be honestly extended along a temporal axis by a simple TPM
modification \emph{nor} directly applied to a cycle-rich substrate
without window-mean correction (the T1/T2/T3 triple closed-negative),
opening the T4 surrogate-data / Granger-baseline lane as the natural
follow-on, with the SPATIAL and EVOL sibling probes confirming the
detector is an endpoint classifier rather than an ordinal scale on
any axis.

We satisfy \texttt{a\_paper\_negative\_ok} (three core-axis closed-negative
findings $+$ X840 longer-playback FALSIFIED $+$ T1/T2/T3 triple
closed-negative $+$ EVOL E1 closed-negative are all valid paper content),
\texttt{a\_paper\_significance} (nineteen pre-registered falsifiers
on the 14-point matrix $+$ fifteen further pre-registered falsifiers
across T1/T2/T3/S1/E1 $+$ two ground-truth calibrations $+$ the
mean-field paradox finding $+$ the temporal triple closed-negative),
\texttt{a\_paper\_only\_at\_closure} (XENO-FRONTIER-5 R5/5 $+$
X837 follow-up 1 $+$ X10 follow-up 2 $+$ X840 longer-playback $+$
TEMPORAL T1/T2/T3 follow-up $+$ SPATIAL/EVOL sibling probes FULL
closure marks the v3 release point), and \texttt{a\_paper\_format}
(the four canonical sections hypothesis / method / measurement /
finding, plus dedicated mean-field paradox and temporal-axis
closed-negative sections). All thirteen verdicts (plus the X8 pointer)
are published verbatim in the \texttt{companion/} ledger for
