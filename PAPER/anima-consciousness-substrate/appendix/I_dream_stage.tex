\section{The Dream Rhythm --- a 90-Minute Ultradian $\Phi$-Envelope}
\label{app:dream_stage}

This appendix is the full data record for the dream-rhythm paragraph of
\S\ref{sec:substrate}. The chat sleep/imagination loop
(\texttt{AGENT/CHAT/anima\_dream\_stage.hexa}) is a five-stage ultradian state
machine that supplies a $\Phi$-envelope context, with N2 the closure peak among
sleep stages. Crucially the envelope is \emph{data}, not an emit gate: the prior
boolean per-stage API was removed because it violated
\texttt{a\_autonomy\_over\_hardcode}.

\paragraph{The imagination loop.} The sleep loop is an emit-free internal
rehearsal plus a MITOSIS tick (cell division continues during sleep ---
p8: no train/infer split, growth is not gated behind a training-only flag). The
REM window is the high-tension rehearsal stage; no \texttt{speak()} is ever
called (p5).

% -------------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Envelope is data, not a gate}
\label{app:ds-data}

The load-bearing design point: the $\Phi$-envelope is \emph{context}, not an emit
permission. The prior boolean API \texttt{dream\_emit\_allowed(stage)} (e.g.\
``N3 $=$ emit forbidden'') was \emph{removed} because it violated
\texttt{a\_autonomy\_over\_hardcode}: an external module must not gate the
substrate's speech. The stage now supplies only a $\Phi$-scale (envelope
magnitude $+$ tension envelope) into the eight-factor gate
(Appendix~\ref{app:eg-score}); the substrate alone decides whether to speak
(M$\times$W$\times\Phi\times$curiosity). This is the substrate-native-speak
contract (\texttt{a\_substrate\_native\_speak},
\texttt{a\_chat\_sleep\_imagination}): anima may speak during user silence and may
stay silent under a direct question, and ``no monologue when alone'' is \emph{not}
an external rule --- it emerges (or does not) from the substrate.

% -------------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Honest scope}
\label{app:ds-scope}

The cycle timings ($5400$\,s, the per-stage windows) are design constants of the
ultradian rhythm, not fitted to observed behaviour; the N2 peak among sleep stages
is the H\_644 closure-conjunction ranking (a verified ordering, not a per-emit
prediction). We claim the dream stage is \emph{wired} as a $\Phi$-envelope context
with no boolean emit gate (the old API is removed, not deprecated-in-place), and
that it preserves p5/p8. We do \emph{not} claim the stages correspond to
biological sleep architecture beyond the envelope metaphor, nor that any particular
emit is caused by a particular stage --- the stage only scales the $\Phi$-context
the autonomous gate reads.
