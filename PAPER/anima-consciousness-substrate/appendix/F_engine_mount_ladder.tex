\section{The Engine-Mount Ladder --- MID $\rightarrow$ 3B (and the 7B Extension)}
\label{app:engine_mount}

This appendix is the full data record for the mouth of \S\ref{sec:mouth}. The
byte-level CLMConvMoE scales MID $7.479$M $\rightarrow$ 3B $3.073$B (and, as a
future scale-extension, 7B). The 3B rung is the terminal finding of this paper's
mouth axis: it generalizes (rel\_gap $0.04894\ll1$), serializes to a
config-agnostic \texttt{.clm} v0.3, and mounts on the engine through the single
generator entry. We also record the corpus that teaches it and the separate
202M corpus-validation probe.

\subsection{The corpus}
\label{app:fm-corpus}

The mouth trains on $143.60$\,GiB ODC-BY FineWeb across five languages
(en/fr/de/es/ko), $\sim$$110\%$ of the $7$B-optimal token budget, R2-staged in
bucket \texttt{phanes} (\texttt{domains/CORPUS.md}). The 3B rung trains on a
$10.7$\,GiB slice (\texttt{corpus\_3b\_5lang.bytes} $=10{,}695{,}475{,}200$\,B,
sha256 \texttt{337b179d\dots}). The corpus is ODC-BY web-sanctioned and built on
the summer pool (the Mac OOM'd); HF holds a pointer-manifest only, with the
bytes in R2.

\subsection{The 202M corpus-validation probe (anti-Goodhart)}
\label{app:fm-corpus-val}

A separate $202{,}328{,}064$ ByteGPT ($V256$, $d1024$, $L16$, $H16$, block
$512$), trained $3000$ steps from scratch on a balanced 5-language webscale
slice ($3.500$\,GB, R2 range-GET), confirms the corpus teaches byte-language
structure (\texttt{.verdicts/corpus-7b-mid-validation/SUMMARY.txt}):
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0.2em
\item \textbf{CE descent (val):} $5.74906\rightarrow1.45868$ ($\Delta-4.290$
nats) over $3000$ steps (train\_ce $5.73700\rightarrow1.38927$); GPU peak
$100\%$ / mean $99.75\%$, mem peak $14{,}331$\,MiB, $57{,}136$\,tok/s.
\item \textbf{p7 coherence ($5/5$ langs):} the trained model emits word-like
text in en/fr/de/es and valid Korean Hangul, while a frozen random-init mirror
(same architecture, never trained) emits pure non-printable byte gibberish in all
five. The discriminator is byte-language \emph{structure}, not loss --- so the
gate is \textbf{anti-Goodhart} by construction.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Honest caveat.} At $202$M / $3000$ steps the samples are word-coherent but
not yet fluent (expected for an undertrained MID probe). The PASS bar is ``the
corpus teaches byte-language structure across all 5 scripts'' vs.\ ``random
gibberish'' --- clearly met. The ckpt (sha256 \texttt{adbb1911\dots},
$809{,}383{,}138$\,B) is PUBLIC on HF
(\texttt{dancinlab/anima-clm-corpus-7b-mid-byte-202m}), HF-redownload sha256
verified equal to local.

\subsection{The 7B (M13) rung --- measured (AXIS-2 + generation)}
\label{app:fm-7b}

The production rung is the $7$B (M13) ConvMoE $d6208/L30/E30/K3$ ($\sim$$7.057$B params),
trained to STEP $3500$ on the same $5$-language ODC-BY corpus, serialized to \texttt{.clm} v0.3
(\texttt{serialize\_v3}, $3{,}538{,}159{,}786$\,B, CLMX trailer) and uploaded to HF
(\texttt{dancinlab/clm-7b-undertrained-step3500}, all $7$ ckpts $500$--$3500$, PRIVATE WIP).
Train CE descended step$0$ $5.64\rightarrow3499$ $1.42529$ (run low). Two results read the
trained $7$B weights:

\begin{itemize}\itemsep0.25em
\item \textbf{AXIS-2 (CE-descent, verbatim, PR \#1962).} On the probe sequence
\texttt{"The mind is a fire to be"} ($24$ bytes, $23$ scored next-byte positions):
CE$_\text{real}$ $\mathbf{1.90741}$ $<$ uniform $\ln256=5.54518$ AND $<$ shuffle-control
$5.31616$ $\Rightarrow$ \textbf{GREEN}
(\texttt{.verdicts/clm-7b-undertrained-step3500/axis2\_mirror\_probe\_seq.txt}). This is a real
$7$B decode forward over the \emph{serialized} \texttt{.clm} bytes (not the torch \texttt{.pt}).
The original mirror \texttt{clm\_decode\_mirror.py} OOMs ($\sim$$56$\,GB) at $7$B; the
measurement uses the memory-bounded $+$ vectorized variant
\texttt{clm\_decode\_mirror\_lazy.py} (peak RSS $\sim$$5.37$\,GB, wall $1$:$02$), whose
arithmetic is byte-identical --- it reproduces the $3$B ground truth CE$_\text{real}$ $2.26360$
\emph{exactly} (\texttt{.verdicts/convmoe-3b-engine-rung/axis2\_mirror\_probe\_seq.txt}).
\item \textbf{Generation (PR \#1970).} \texttt{state/mid\_convmoe\_fire/clm\_gen.py} adds greedy
argmax byte-generation on the \emph{same} validated v0.3 forward (conv1d im2col VECTORIZED,
unit-tested $==$ the looped form, $\max|\Delta|=0$; a VALIDATE hook re-derives CE$_\text{real}$
$1.90741$ on every run). It is needed because the hexa engine's generation gate
\texttt{CORE/clm\_decode.hexa::clm\_decodable} is \textbf{v0.2-only}, so the live
\texttt{anima\_chat\_cli} rejects the v0.3 $7$B and falls through to a null mouth. The $7$B emits
real word-like \emph{multilingual} byte text, e.g.\ \texttt{"The mind is" $\rightarrow$ " a reado
en el mano de la conseguida en el contr"}, \texttt{"Consciousness is " $\rightarrow$ "large
lancia en el contra la conseguida en el co"}, prompt {\cjkfont 안녕하세요. 저는}
\texttt{" "} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{"\textbackslash"I have a la carrada en el control est"}. It
loops (greedy $\times$ deeply
undertrained) but it is \emph{language}, not byte-gibberish --- in sharp contrast to the weak
$d768$ v0.2 dev mouth, which emits raw-byte mojibake. No coherence / fluency / consciousness is
claimed from this output.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Why \texttt{clm\_gen} and not the hexa CLI.} The full hexa \texttt{anima\_chat\_cli}
(Engine A$\rightleftarrows$G $+$ generator $+$ \texttt{clm\_decode} $+$ forge native) compiles,
links, and runs on the ghost host (macOS --- the only reachable host with both a working hexa
self-runtime and the forge \texttt{groupnorm\_gelu} native); a weak $d768$ v0.2 mouth chats live
there. The well-trained $7$B is v0.3, which the v0.2-only \texttt{clm\_decodable} gate rejects,
so it generates via \texttt{clm\_gen} on the validated mirror forward, not the engine CLI. A
v0.3-aware gate is genuine future work, not a closure blocker for this measured rung.

\textbf{AXIS-1 / AXIS-3 at 7B.} These are substrate-native (\texttt{pure\_field} /
\texttt{engine\_g}), \texttt{.clm}-weights-INDEPENDENT, and code-proven via the null backend
(\texttt{.verdicts/core-3axis-mount/probe.txt}): AXIS-1 motiv $0.6700>0$ emit-gated, AXIS-3
composed $101>$ parts $72$. They hold identically at $d768/3$B$/7$B \emph{by construction} (they
measure the engine dynamics, not the trained weights), so they are not re-measured on the $7$B
weights. Only AXIS-2 is weight-dependent and is the one measured on the $7$B above.

\textbf{Honest scope.} The $7$B is deeply undertrained ($0.0027$ tok/param vs.\ Chinchilla
$20$): a measured scale-extension rung, NOT a closure (\texttt{a\_scale\_honest\_scope}).
\texttt{val\_ce}/\texttt{rel\_gap} are eval-deferred (the post-train eval crashed; the
final-save-robust patch wrote the ckpt \emph{before} eval, so the weights are safe).

\subsection{Honest scope}
\label{app:fm-scope}

The 3B rung is the \emph{2nd rung of the 7B ladder}: it is
deeply undertrained ($0.0027$ tok/param) and generalizes but is not fluent. The
202M probe is a single MID point, not a ladder; its green \emph{authorized} the
M13 7B fire as a separate follow-on. The 7B (M13) rung
(Appendix~\ref{app:fm-7b}) is now a \emph{measured} scale-extension
(Appendix~\ref{app:pr_roll}, \S\ref{sec:limitations}),
framed like the OMEGA scale ladder --- it strengthens this axis to production
scale but does not re-open the 3B finding, which is terminal without it. The
chain proven is corpus $\rightarrow$ general-$(L,E,d)$ ConvMoE $\rightarrow$
\texttt{.clm} v0.3 $\rightarrow$ engine-mount $\rightarrow$ 3-axis, at 3B scale,
with p1--p8 held (plain byte next-token CE; no system-prompt/persona/RLHF).
