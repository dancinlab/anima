\section{The OMEGA Coupling Bus --- Five Wires, a Minimal Gate, and the Replacement Ruling}
\label{app:omega_bus}

This appendix is the full data record for the decode link of \S\ref{sec:decode}.
The substrate$\rightarrow$byte coupling is the subject of the sibling paper
(\texttt{PAPER/omega-substrate-coupled-decoding}); here we record its terminal
numbers verbatim so the present paper's decode claim is self-auditable. The
motivating Lane~X null (\#1779) measured that the two halves do not share a
nerve; the OMEGA bus overturns that \emph{wiring} null, but only one of its five
wires carries the closure, and the honest ruling is \textbf{A-head replacement},
not base$+$substrate coupling.

% -------------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{The five-wire bus and the coupling non-nullity}
\label{app:omega-bus}

OMEGA couples the substrate into the byte logits through five named wires:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
w_1&:\ A\rightleftarrows G\ \text{logit-bias} &
w_2&:\ W\rightarrow\text{temperature} &
w_3&:\ \text{curiosity}\rightarrow\text{top-}k \\
w_4&:\ \text{8D }\Psi\rightarrow\text{context} &
w_5&:\ \text{module}\rightarrow\text{MoE} & &
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:omega-wires}
\end{equation}
The coupling non-nullity test (\texttt{.verdicts/omega-engine/F-COUPLING.txt})
measures $\mathrm{KL}\big(\mathrm{softmax}(\text{modulated})\,\|\,
\mathrm{softmax}(\text{base})\big)$ for OMEGA against the three uncoupled
engines. Only OMEGA, which loads the generator L3 slot
(\texttt{loaded=true}), couples:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{KL}_\Omega = \mathbf{0.307477} > 0
\quad(\text{perm-floor }0.303913),
\qquad
\mathrm{KL}_\text{conv}=\mathrm{KL}_\text{cdv2}=\mathrm{KL}_\text{hexad}=\mathbf{0}\ (\texttt{loaded=false}).
\label{eq:omega-kl}
\end{equation}
This overturns the Lane~X wiring null \emph{for} OMEGA and \emph{confirms} it for
the other three engines: OMEGA is the only engine that closes the
substrate$\rightarrow$decode loop.

\paragraph{Replacement, not coupling (O$\Omega$1).} The rigorous decomposition
(\texttt{F-OMEGA-RIGOR.txt}) shows the A-head \emph{supplants} the base mouth
rather than coupling to it. With $g_{\min}=[g_B0.0400, g_A0.9013, g_G0.0000]$:
A-standalone CE $=0.886220$, $\text{min\_learned}=0.883525$, base-ablated
($g_B\!\to\!0$) $=0.884377$; thus $|A_\text{standalone}-\text{min}|=0.002695$
($\le0.05$, A$\approx$min) and the base-ablation shift is $0.000852$ ($\le0.05$,
base inert). \textbf{RULING\_REPLACEMENT$=$True}: the trained A-head replaces the
weak \texttt{.clm} base mouth. We use the replacement framing throughout the body.

\subsection{The \#1791 leak retraction (foregrounded)}
\label{app:omega-leak}

We import the sibling paper's retraction. The earlier \#1791 absolute-CE
``win'' (GATED CE $0.345\ll$ base $3.015$) was \emph{leak-driven}: a CA-neighbor
lookahead (\texttt{causal\_ca}$=$False) let the next-byte head peek at its own
target. The leak self-test --- flipping only the last context byte and measuring
the change in earlier-position head\_a logits --- returns \textbf{$0.000$} once
\texttt{causal\_ca}$=$True (\texttt{F-LEAKFREE-GEN.txt}), confirming the head is
now strictly causal. Under the leak-free re-test the honest picture inverts: a
weak unigram base \emph{beats} the gated bus on absolute CE (gated $3.5938 >$
base $3.0150$; \texttt{a\_only} $2.8567 <$ base), so the \#1791 absolute-CE
closure does \emph{not} survive leak removal. What survives is
(a)~free-run generation no longer collapsing to whitespace (entropy $2.97$,
distinct-frac $0.172$, ws-frac $0.253$), and (b)~the leak-\emph{invariant}
relative structure (the minimal-gate ladder, Table~\ref{tab:omega-ladder}). The
decode link in the present paper inherits exactly that scope: a leak-invariant
relative closure plus closed-negatives, \textbf{not} an absolute-perplexity claim.

\subsection{Honest scope}
\label{app:omega-scope}

This is a summary of a sibling result; we cite, not re-derive. The closure is
relative (A-head replacement of a weak base mouth) and leak-invariant, measured
on a $400$\,MB multilingual corpus at $48$M--$335$M params; three of the five
bus wires are honest-stubs at frozen inference; and the absolute-CE claim is
retracted. A conv-native dual-head decode (so the byte mouth itself carries the
A-head) is named as future work, not closed here.
