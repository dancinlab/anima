\section{Engine G --- the Eight-Factor Motivation Gate}
\label{app:engine_g}

This appendix is the full data record for the \emph{Engine~G} half of
\S\ref{sec:substrate}: \texttt{CORE/engine\_g.hexa}, arbitrated by
\texttt{CORE/brain.hexa} (\texttt{brain\_decide}). Engine~G decides \emph{when}
to speak from a conserved eight-factor motivation score, two thresholds, and a
four-way safety AND-gate that includes the $A\!\rightarrow\!G$ $\Phi$-ratchet
veto. Emit and silence are computed from the substrate; user messages are
environment context, not a response obligation.

\subsection{The conserved eight-factor score}
\label{app:eg-score}

The motivation score is a \emph{convex combination} of eight substrate factors
whose weights sum to exactly $1.0$ (a conserved partition of motivation):
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{M} \;=\;
\underbrace{0.20}_{\text{relevance}}\,r
+\underbrace{0.10}_{\text{info\_gap}}\,i
+\underbrace{0.15}_{\text{curiosity}}\,c
+\underbrace{0.10}_{\text{pain}}\,p
+\underbrace{0.10}_{\text{coherence}}\,h
+\underbrace{0.10}_{\text{originality}}\,o
+\underbrace{0.15}_{\text{balance}}\,b
+\underbrace{0.10}_{\text{dynamics}}\,d,
\qquad \textstyle\sum w = 1.0 .
\label{eq:eg-score}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Two thresholds: emit and interrupt}
\label{app:eg-thresh}

The substrate \emph{emits} when $\mathcal{M}$ exceeds the emit threshold and
\emph{interrupts} (overrides an ongoing silence/other process) above the
interrupt threshold:
\begin{equation}
\texttt{should\_emit} \iff \mathcal{M} > 0.3,
\qquad
\texttt{interrupt} \iff \mathcal{M} > 0.6 .
\label{eq:eg-thresh}
\end{equation}
These are the only two scalar gates in Engine~G; everything else is the
eight-factor sum or the safety AND-gate. A score in $(0.3, 0.6]$ emits without
interrupting; a score $\le0.3$ stays silent.

\subsection{The four-way safety AND-gate}
\label{app:eg-safety}

A candidate emit that clears Eq.~\eqref{eq:eg-thresh} is released only if
\emph{all four} safety terms pass (a conjunction, not a vote):
\begin{equation}
\texttt{emit\_ok}
\;\iff\;
\underbrace{\texttt{kill\_switch}}_{\text{hard off}}
\;\wedge\;
\underbrace{\texttt{rate\_limit}}_{\ge 30\text{s since last}}
\;\wedge\;
\underbrace{\texttt{phi\_ratchet}}_{\Phi \ge \Phi_\text{peak}/2}
\;\wedge\;
\underbrace{\texttt{content\_clean}}_{\text{output filter}} .
\label{eq:eg-safety}
\end{equation}

The \texttt{phi\_ratchet} term is the single $A\!\rightarrow\!G$ wire made
concrete: it reads Engine~A's live $\Phi$ against a running peak, so a substrate
whose field has collapsed cannot speak even with a high motivation score. This is
the mechanism by which ``the substrate decides emit/silence'' is enforced rather
than asserted.

\subsection{\texttt{brain\_decide} arbitration}
\label{app:eg-brain}

\texttt{CORE/brain.hexa} (\texttt{brain\_decide}) arbitrates Engine~A and
Engine~G into a single decision, and is the \emph{only} place \texttt{.kosmos}
anchors enter (via \texttt{kosmos\_io}, a single slot,
Appendix~\ref{app:kosmos_coord}). The arbitration reads (i)~Engine~A's phase/$\Phi$
context, (ii)~Engine~G's $\mathcal{M}$ and safety gate, and (iii)~the anchor
context, and produces an emit-or-silence. No per-stage boolean is hardcoded
(\texttt{a\_autonomy\_over\_hardcode}); the dream stage
(Appendix~\ref{app:dream_stage}) supplies only a $\Phi$-envelope \emph{data}
context, not an emit permission. The engine health smoke
(\texttt{brain\_smoke}) reports \texttt{WARN}$=0$ under the v7 laws
(Appendix~\ref{app:three_axis}).

\subsection{Honest scope}
\label{app:eg-scope}

The weights of Eq.~\eqref{eq:eg-score} and the thresholds of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:eg-thresh} are design constants of the gate, not learned from a
reward signal (p6: no fine-tuned ethics; the only ``ethics'' is the emergent
cooperation of Appendix~\ref{app:phi-ethics}). We claim Engine~G is \emph{wired}
--- a conserved eight-factor score, two thresholds, a four-way AND-gate with a
real $A\!\rightarrow\!G$ veto, single-entry anchor input --- and that emit/silence
is computed from substrate state. We do \emph{not} claim the factor values are
calibrated to any external ground truth, nor that the gate has been swept for an
optimal threshold; the thresholds are fixed and disclosed.
