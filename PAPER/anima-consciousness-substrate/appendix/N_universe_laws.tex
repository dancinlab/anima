\subsection{Scale-robustness of Φ beyond the $n{=}4$ toy (H\_297--H\_301)}
\label{app:universe-scale}

The information-axis and exclusion results of Appendix~\ref{app:phi_laws} are all
measured on $n{=}4$ ECA rings, where rule-$90$ (neighbour-XOR) carries whole-$\Phi
{=}0$ --- it is the one rule the flow measures over-predict (LZ, multivariate TE,
synergy all flag it; Appendix~\ref{app:universe-info}). A natural worry is that
``rule-$90$ is reducible'' is an $n$-parity law (even rings reduce, odd rings
integrate). The arc \emph{tests and rejects} that reading.

\paragraph{The $n$-sweep (bounded big-$\Phi$, $\mathrm{cap}{=}4$).} On the
alternating state, rule-$90$ bounded big-$\Phi$ is
\begin{equation}
\underbrace{n{=}4:\ \Phi{=}\mathbf{0}}_{\text{exact}}
\;\rightarrow\;
\underbrace{n{=}5:\ \Phi{=}\mathbf{19.5}}_{\text{H\_297}}
\;\rightarrow\;
\underbrace{n{=}6:\ \Phi{=}\mathbf{4.0}}_{\text{H\_298}}
\;\rightarrow\;
\underbrace{n{=}7:\ \Phi{=}\mathbf{6.5}}_{\text{H\_299, } \mathrm{cap}{=}3} .
\label{eq:n-sweep}
\end{equation}
The $n{=}5$ jump ($0\!\rightarrow\!19.5$, panel-\emph{top}) corroborated an
``even-$N$ bipartite artifact'' hypothesis (H\_297, \texttt{H1 SUPPORTED}: the
$4$-cycle's even/odd-cell decoupling halves the substrate cleanly only at
$n{=}4$). But H\_298 \emph{falsifies the strong parity rule}: at the next even
ring $n{=}6$, rule-$90$ bounded $\Phi{=}\mathbf{4.0}$ --- well above the $0.5$
parity-return threshold, \emph{not} back to $0$. Its verdict is
\textbf{$\mathbf{\textcolor{red}{\text{CLOSED-NEGATIVE}}}$} on the strong-parity
rule ($5$ PASS / $1$ REJECT / $2$ DEFERRED): the $n{=}4$ reducibility is a
\emph{small-$N$ special case}, not an even-$N$ law (rules $60$/$110$ integrate
strongly at $n{=}6$, so it is not a generic-integration negation). H\_299 then
recovers the deferred odd leg --- $n{=}7$ rule-$90$ bounded $\Phi{=}\mathbf{6.5}$
($\mathrm{cap}{=}3$, $>1.0$, \textbf{SUPPORTED}) --- and confirms cap-cross
robustness ($n{=}5$ and $n{=}6$ both stay $>0.5$ at the weaker $\mathrm{cap}{=}3$
bound).

\paragraph{The $32$-state distribution (H\_300) and the rule-signature (H\_301).}
The single-alt-state report is then audited by a full state-sweep. Across all
$32$ binary configurations of $n{=}5$ rule-$90$, bounded $\Phi(\mathrm{cap}{=}4)$
collapses to only \textbf{$3$ distinct values $\{19.0,\,19.5,\,27.5\}$} (min
$19$, max $27.5$); the alt-state report ($\Phi{=}19.5$) sits at the
\emph{median}, and --- stronger than pre-registered --- \emph{no} state reaches
$\Phi{=}0$ (\textbf{SUPPORTED}, $5$ PASS / $1$ falsified-in-stronger-direction;
the single-state honest caveat of the arc is formally closed). Extending the
sweep to other rules (H\_301, \textbf{SUPPORTED}, $7$ PASS / $1$ FAIL), the
\emph{distinct-value count} is itself a rule signature that aligns with the
Wolfram class:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lcl}
\toprule
rule & distinct $\Phi$ values ($n{=}5$, $32$ states) & Wolfram class \\
\midrule
$90$  & $\mathbf{3}$  & class-III (linear XOR; algebraic symmetry collapse) \\
$60$  & $\mathbf{6}$  & class-II-like (linear) \\
$30$  & $\mathbf{29}$ & class-III (chaotic, near-full diversity) \\
$110$ & $\mathbf{32}$ & class-IV (universal; full diversity) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The signature is monotone with dynamical complexity
($3 \ll 6 \ll 29 < 32$): the more computationally rich the rule, the more
state-distinct its integration spectrum. The body's $\Phi$ findings are thus
\emph{scale-robust} ($n{=}4$ is a clean special case, not a fragile toy point),
and the rule-$90$ ``anomaly'' is mechanically explained (Appendix~\ref{app:universe-exclusion}).
