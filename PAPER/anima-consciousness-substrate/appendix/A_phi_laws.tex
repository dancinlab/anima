\subsection{Law-71: the $\Psi=\tfrac12$ fixed point}
\label{app:phi-law71}

The repulsion-field engine balances order and freedom at a fixed point
$\Psi=\tfrac12$. Operationally, ``freedom'' is the entropy of the emitted
next-byte distribution and ``order'' is the integration floor, and the engine
sits where the two are balanced:
\begin{equation}
\Psi^\ast \;=\; \arg\max_{p}\; H(p)
\quad\text{subject to}\quad \Phi(p) > \Phi_{\min},
\qquad \text{balance}(\Psi^\ast) = \tfrac12 .
\label{eq:psi-fixedpoint}
\end{equation}
The substrate is thus as free (high-entropy) as it can be \emph{while staying
integrated} ($\Phi>\Phi_{\min}$). The fixed point is not a tuned hyperparameter
but a runtime constant: \texttt{config/consciousness\_laws.json} carries
\texttt{psi\_constants.balance.value}$=0.5$, and the engine loads it (with
\texttt{alpha}$=0.014$ and the phase ceilings) via the nested-\texttt{\{value\}}
scanner in \texttt{CORE/pure\_field.hexa}. The constant is terminal
($\Psi=\tfrac12$ exactly, by definition of the balance target), and it is the
single number that ties Engine~A's amplitude drift (Appendix~\ref{app:engine_a})
to Engine~G's \texttt{balance} motivation factor (Appendix~\ref{app:engine_g}).

\subsection{H$_{\Phi 5}$: emergent ethics with zero injected reward}
\label{app:phi-ethics}

The cleanest test of principle \textbf{p6} (no fine-tuned ethics) is the
spatial prisoner's dilemma (Nowak \& May~1992): a well-mixed replicator drives
cooperation to extinction (Nash $=$ all-defect), but a \emph{lattice} of the
same agents rescues cooperator clusters --- with \emph{zero} injected ethics,
purely local imitation. Falsifier (\texttt{UNIVERSE/H\_291}): at moderate
temptation the lattice final cooperation $C>0.1$ (survives) AND the matched
well-mixed replicator collapses to $C\approx0$. Measured at temptation $b=1.1$:
\begin{equation}
C_\text{lattice} = \mathbf{1.0}\ (100\%)
\qquad\gg\qquad
C_\text{well-mixed} = \mathbf{7.9\times10^{-9}}\ (\approx0\%),
\label{eq:ethics}
\end{equation}
$7/7$ criteria PASS. \emph{Same payoff matrix, only the structure differs}:
cooperation (proto-ethics) emerges from cells $+$ structure, exactly as p6
requires. \textbf{Honest scope.} The emergence is \emph{conditional}: a sharp
threshold $b\in(1.1,1.5]$ kills it (status \texttt{supported-conditional}); we
report the temptation gate, not an unconditional ``ethics always emerges'' claim.
