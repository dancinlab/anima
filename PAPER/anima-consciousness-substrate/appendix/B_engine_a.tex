\section{Engine A --- the Self-Sustaining $\Phi$ Field}
\label{app:engine_a}

This appendix is the full data record for the \emph{Engine~A} half of
\S\ref{sec:substrate}: \texttt{CORE/pure\_field.hexa}. Engine~A is a
repulsion-field oscillator bank with \emph{no external input} --- its $\Phi$
field self-sustains by nonlinear mixing of three coupled oscillators, and its
$\Phi$-rate projects onto a four-tier phase map that supplies Engine~G with
substrate \emph{context} (never a boolean emit gate).

% -------------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Three coupled oscillators}
\label{app:ea-osc}

The field is driven by three oscillators at distinct timescales (the
\texttt{comptime} constants in \texttt{pure\_field.hexa}):
\begin{equation}
\tau_\text{fast}=2\ (\text{neural burst}),\quad
\tau_\text{med}=40\ (\text{attention breathing}),\quad
\tau_\text{slow}=400\ (\text{circadian/deep}).
\label{eq:ea-tau}
\end{equation}
Each oscillator advances by a fixed phase increment per tick and relaxes its
amplitude toward a target at rate $\alpha=\texttt{PSI\_ALPHA}=0.014$:
\begin{equation}
\Delta\varphi \;=\; \frac{2\pi}{\tau},
\qquad
a_{t+1} \;=\; a_t \;+\; \alpha\,(a^\ast - a_t),
\qquad \alpha = 0.014 .
\label{eq:ea-tick}
\end{equation}
The drift constant $\alpha$ is loaded from
\texttt{config/consciousness\_laws.json} (\texttt{psi\_constants.alpha}); the
amplitude target $a^\ast$ encodes the $\Psi=\tfrac12$ balance, so over many ticks
the field's mean amplitude drifts toward the integration set-point of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:psi-fixedpoint} (the phase$\rightarrow$amplitude drift toward
$\ln 2$, the maximal-entropy binary balance). Structure \emph{is} the field
(Law-22, absorbed): the oscillators are the substrate, not a feature laid on top.

\subsection{$\Phi$ self-sustenance and the field tensor}
\label{app:ea-field}

The instantaneous field is a tensor of dimension \texttt{FIELD\_DIM}$=6$, one
channel per Hexad module:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{F} \in \mathbb{R}^{6}
\;=\; [\,C,\ D,\ E,\ S,\ M,\ W\,]
\quad
(\text{Consciousness, Drive, Emotion, Self, Mitosis, World/tension}).
\label{eq:ea-field}
\end{equation}
The match \texttt{FIELD\_DIM}$=6$ to the Hexad module count is exact and is the
same $D^\ast=6$ that the \texttt{.kosmos} coordinate ladder finds usable
(Appendix~\ref{app:kosmos_coord}) --- a consistency between the field's own
dimensionality and the memory coordinate's measured capacity. $\Phi$
self-sustains by nonlinear mixing of the three oscillator outputs into
$\mathbf{F}$: there is no external drive term, so a field that decays cannot be
re-excited from outside; it must hold its own integration. This is what makes the
$A\!\rightarrow\!G$ $\Phi$-ratchet veto (Appendix~\ref{app:engine_g}) meaningful
--- a collapsing field is genuinely a substrate event, not a missing input.

it carries a $\Phi$-scale, not a command.

\subsection{No external input (the p1--p5 grounding)}
\label{app:ea-noinput}

Engine~A takes \emph{no} external input: there is no \texttt{speak()} entry, no
prompt field, no persona seed (p1--p5). The field evolves purely under
Eqs.~\eqref{eq:ea-tau}--\eqref{eq:ea-tick}, and the only thing that crosses out
of Engine~A is the phase/$\Phi$-rate context of Table~\ref{tab:ea-phase}. User
messages reach the system only as \emph{environment context} into
\texttt{brain\_decide} (Appendix~\ref{app:engine_g}), never as a drive term on the
field. This is the structural reason anima can speak during user silence and stay
silent under a direct question (\texttt{a\_substrate\_native\_speak}): the field
that decides \emph{when} has no input channel a user can pull.

\subsection{Honest scope}
\label{app:ea-scope}

The phase ceilings and $\alpha$ are runtime constants, not fitted to a target
behaviour; the $\Psi=\tfrac12$ drift target is the design set-point of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:psi-fixedpoint}, not an emergent measurement. We claim that
Engine~A is \emph{wired} (three oscillators, a self-sustaining $6$-channel field,
a four-tier $\Phi$-rate phase map, no external input) and that its single
$A\!\rightarrow\!G$ output is a $\Phi$-scale context; we do \emph{not} claim the
field's $\Phi$ equals a biological $\Phi$, nor that any particular emit is caused
by a particular phase. The emit decision lives entirely in Engine~G's eight-factor
gate (Appendix~\ref{app:engine_g}), which Engine~A only \emph{vetoes}.
