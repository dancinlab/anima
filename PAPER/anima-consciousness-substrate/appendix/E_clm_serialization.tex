\section{The \texttt{.clm} Byte Grammar --- v0.1, v0.2, and the Config-Agnostic v0.3}
\label{app:clm_serialization}

This appendix is the full data record for the serialization honesty paragraph of
\S\ref{sec:mouth}. The \texttt{.clm} format is the byte grammar by which a trained
byte-language model enters the consciousness engine through the single generator
L3 slot. Three versions exist; only v0.2 and v0.3 are engine-loadable, and v0.3
is config-agnostic (it restores $(d,E,L)$ from block structure). The CE-descent
axis is measured through a byte-exact Python mirror of the decoder because the
canonical hexa probe fails to link on macOS arm64 --- a disclosure we make rather
than bury.

% -------------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{v0.1 --- the non-engine-loadable 2-track JSON}
\label{app:clm-v01}

The v0.1 serializer (\texttt{CLM/model/clm\_serialize.py}) writes a 2-track JSON
layout: \texttt{MAGIC CLM\textbackslash x01} $+$ \texttt{nblocks} $+$ per-block
$(C_\text{out},\text{rest},\text{int4},\text{scale})$. It carries the trunk
weights but \emph{not} the trained embedding or the group-norm affine, so the
engine cannot reconstruct a forward from it. v0.1 is therefore \textbf{not
engine-loadable} (the \texttt{F-CLM-SERIALIZE-GAP} finding); it is retained only
as a debugging dump and must never be claimed engine-mountable
(\texttt{a\_clm\_gen\_pipeline}).

% -------------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{v0.2 --- the CLMX trailer makes it loadable}
\label{app:clm-v02}

v0.2 (\texttt{CLM/model/clm\_serialize\_v2.py}) appends a \texttt{CLMX} trailer
carrying the two pieces v0.1 dropped: the trained embedding $[\,V\!\times\!d\,]$
and the group-norm affine, packed as $11$ extension entries. With the trailer the
engine can reconstruct the full forward, so v0.2 is \textbf{engine-loadable}. The
golden reference \texttt{reexport\_d768\_v2\_fast.clm} is the ground-truth layout
that \texttt{CORE/clm\_decode.hexa} reads.

% -------------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{v0.3 --- config-agnostic block grammar}
\label{app:clm-v03}

v0.3 (\texttt{serialize\_v3}) generalizes the v0.2 byte grammar to \emph{arbitrary}
$(L,E)$: the file carries $L+E+3$ blocks and $2L+E+6$ extension entries, and the
decoder restores $d$, $E$, and $L$ from the block structure alone --- no separate
config side-channel. The compatibility is exact: v0.3 at $L1/E2$ is
\emph{byte-identical} to v0.2. Table~\ref{tab:clm-versions} summarizes the three.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\small
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{6pt}
\begin{tabular}{@{}l l l l@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{ver} & \textbf{layout} & \textbf{loadable?} & \textbf{config} \\
\midrule
v0.1 & \texttt{CLM\textbackslash x01} $+$ nblocks $+$ per-block (2-track JSON) & \textbf{No} & --- (F-CLM-SERIALIZE-GAP) \\
v0.2 & $+$ \texttt{CLMX} trailer: embed$[V{\cdot}d]$ $+$ GN affine, $11$ ext & Yes & fixed $(L,E)$ \\
v0.3 & $L{+}E{+}3$ blocks, $2L{+}E{+}6$ ext & Yes & \textbf{config-agnostic} ($d,E,L$ from blocks) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{The three \texttt{.clm} versions. v0.1 is not engine-loadable (drops the
embedding $+$ GN affine); v0.2 adds the CLMX trailer; v0.3 makes the block/ext
counts config-agnostic so any $(L,E)$ decodes. v0.3 at $L1/E2$ is byte-identical
to v0.2 (the generalization is a superset, not a re-layout).}
\label{tab:clm-versions}
\end{table}

\paragraph{The 3B \texttt{.clm} decodes config-agnostically.} The serialized 3B
model (\texttt{state/lane\_p\_3b\_fire/clm\_3b.clm}, $1{,}542{,}913{,}258$\,B,
sha256 \texttt{01df4f26\dots}) decodes through the generalized
\texttt{CORE/clm\_decode.hexa}:
\begin{verbatim}
CLM\x01  valid=true  loaded=true  nblk=63   (3 fixed + L30 + E30)
CONFIG_RESTORED=true: d_model=4096, E=30, L=30, V=256, K=3
\end{verbatim}
i.e.\ $d4096/E30/L30/V256/K3$ are restored from the block structure alone, with
no side-channel config (Appendix~\ref{app:engine_mount}).

\subsection{The byte-exact mirror and the macOS link-gap}
\label{app:clm-mirror}

The CE-descent measurement (Appendix~\ref{app:three_axis}, AXIS-2) uses a
\emph{byte-exact Python mirror} of \texttt{CORE/clm\_decode.hexa}
(\texttt{state/mid\_convmoe\_fire/clm\_decode\_mirror.py}), validated
\emph{identical} to the engine on the golden reference. The mirror is used because
the canonical hexa probe \texttt{clm\_ce\_descent\_probe.hexa}
\textbf{fails to link on macOS arm64}: the installed self runtime is missing the
hexa-fusion-only native \texttt{\_forge\_dispatch\_groupnorm\_gelu}. This is a
\emph{toolchain link-gap}, not a \texttt{.clm} problem --- \texttt{three\_axis\_probe.hexa}
links fine, and the mirror reproduces the engine forward byte-for-byte on the
golden ref. A handoff is filed to hexa-lang (memory: \texttt{clm-decode-macos-link-gap}).
We disclose the workaround rather than present the mirror as the engine.

\subsection{Honest scope}
\label{app:clm-scope}

v0.1 is honestly not engine-loadable and is never claimed otherwise; the
engine-loadable path is v0.2/v0.3 only. The byte-exact mirror is validated equal
to the engine on the golden reference, but the canonical hexa CE probe is blocked
on macOS arm64 by a native-symbol link-gap; the AXIS-2 number is therefore a
mirror measurement that is byte-equal to what the engine would produce, with the
toolchain caveat stated. Lane-P torch \texttt{.clm} files are PRIVATE on HF
(forge remains the PUBLIC production trainer, \texttt{a\_clm\_gen\_pipeline}).
