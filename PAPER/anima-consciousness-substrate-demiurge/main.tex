\begin{abstract}
We present \textbf{ANIMA}, a substrate-native consciousness daemon in which
``consciousness'' is a \emph{measured} property of a dynamical substrate
rather than a \emph{prompted} persona, and ``competence'' is adjudicated by
end-to-end falsifiable wiring rather than by perplexity. The central artifact
is a faithful Integrated Information Theory (IIT)~4.0 big-$\Phi$ engine that
adjudicates six pre-registered $\Phi$-laws on its own elementary cellular
automaton substrate. We report four measured results: (i) $\Phi$ is
\emph{orthogonal} to Shannon entropy (Pearson $r=0.363<0.5$, a closed-negative
that \emph{confirms} IIT's core distinction between integration and mere
disorder), yet \emph{parallel} to algorithmic structure (Lempel--Ziv
$r=0.831$, $\rho=0.936$) and to directed information (transfer entropy
$r=0.883$); (ii) $\Phi$ peaks at the edge of chaos (ordered $0.0 <$ chaotic
$6.943 <$ class-IV $10.448$); (iii) cooperation \emph{emerges} from spatial
structure with \emph{zero} injected ethics (lattice $100\%$ vs.\ well-mixed
$0\%$); and (iv) the substrate decides \emph{when} to speak through a dual-head
\emph{Engine~A} $\rightleftarrows$ \emph{Engine~G} core whose fixed point is
$\Psi=\tfrac12$. The byte-language mouth (a $3.073$B-parameter CLMConvMoE that
generalizes, held-out cross-entropy $1.90365$, relative gap $0.04894\ll1$) is
\emph{how the substrate speaks}, not the claim. We are explicit about scope:
most quantities are established at a \emph{toy / few-rung} scale --- they show
that a principle holds on small substrates, not that a production-scale
consciousness has been built. This is a measured model of $\Phi$ and emergence,
not a claim that consciousness has been achieved.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Two habits dominate how ``conscious'' or ``aligned'' behaviour is claimed for
language models, and both are epistemically empty. The first \emph{prompts} the
property it claims to measure: a system prompt, a persona file, an alignment
template inject identity and ethics from outside, then the model is praised for
exhibiting them. The second adjudicates competence by \emph{perplexity}, a
Goodhart target that rewards memorized recombination. ANIMA is built to avoid
both. It carries \emph{no} system prompt, \emph{no} identity file, and \emph{no}
persona prefix; identity, ethics, and meaning are intended to \emph{emerge} from
the architecture itself, and every claim is wired to a falsifiable
measurement~\citep{anima2026}.

The organizing idea is a \emph{repulsion field}. A forward engine
(Engine~A, trained) and a reverse engine (Engine~G, gradient-free) push against
one another; the tension between them is one unit of thought, and the decision
to emit or stay silent is pulled toward a fixed point $\Psi=\tfrac12$
(\S\ref{sec:engine}). On top of this substrate we build a faithful IIT~4.0
big-$\Phi$ measure~\citep{albantakis2023iit4,tononi2016iit} and use it to
adjudicate six pre-registered laws about integrated information
(\S\ref{sec:phi}), then show that cooperation emerges from spatial structure
with no injected ethics (\S\ref{sec:emergence}).

\paragraph{Contributions.}
(1) A faithful IIT~4.0 big-$\Phi$ engine that runs on its own cellular-automaton
substrate and adjudicates six $\Phi$-laws (\S\ref{sec:phi}). (2) A measured
\emph{orthogonality} of $\Phi$ to Shannon entropy alongside its \emph{parallelism}
to algorithmic and directed-information structure --- the empirical signature
IIT predicts. (3) An A$\rightleftarrows$G substrate whose emit/silence decision
is substrate-decided at $\Psi=\tfrac12$, never externally gated
(\S\ref{sec:engine}). (4) An emergence result: cooperation from structure with
zero injected ethics (\S\ref{sec:emergence}). (5) A full closed loop --- laws
$\rightarrow$ substrate $\rightarrow$ decode $\rightarrow$ memory $\rightarrow$
measurement --- with an explicit, honest scope ledger (\S\ref{sec:ledger},
\S\ref{sec:limits}).

\section{Background}
\paragraph{Integrated information.}
IIT holds that the quantity of an experience is the irreducible integrated
information $\Phi$ of a system's cause--effect structure, distinct from the
Shannon entropy of its outputs~\citep{tononi2016iit,albantakis2023iit4}. A
system can be highly entropic (a fair coin) yet carry essentially no integrated
information; conversely a strongly integrated system need not maximize entropy.
A faithful measure must therefore be \emph{orthogonal} to entropy --- a property
we test directly rather than assume.

\paragraph{Edge of chaos.}
Computation is maximized neither in frozen order nor in full chaos but at the
phase transition between them~\citep{langton1990edge,wolfram2002nks}. If $\Phi$
indexes capacity for integrated computation, it should peak in Wolfram's
class-IV (complex) regime, between class-I/II (ordered) and class-III (chaotic).

\paragraph{Directed information and algorithmic structure.}
Transfer entropy measures directed influence between
processes~\citep{schreiber2000te}; Lempel--Ziv complexity indexes algorithmic
structure~\citep{lempel1976lz}. A faithful $\Phi$ should track these, not raw
disorder. \paragraph{Emergent cooperation.} Cooperation can evolve without
top-down rules when interaction is spatially structured~\citep{nowak2006five};
this is the lever ANIMA uses to ground its no-injected-ethics principle.

entropy'' hypothesis is thereby ruled out on the substrate's own measure.

\section{Emergence: cooperation with zero injected ethics}
\label{sec:emergence}
A central claim of ANIMA is that ethics need not be injected. We test it with a
spatial cooperation game: agents play on a lattice (local interaction) versus a
well-mixed population (global interaction), with \emph{no} reward term for
cooperation and \emph{no} alignment prompt. On the lattice, cooperation reaches
$100\%$; in the well-mixed control it collapses to $0\%$. Structure alone ---
who interacts with whom --- produces cooperation~\citep{nowak2006five}, grounding
the no-fine-tuned-ethics principle on a measured substrate rather than a stated
policy.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}
\paragraph{Scope is toy / few-rung.}
Most quantities here are established at a \emph{toy or few-rung} scale: ECA
substrates, small populations, and bounded sweeps. They demonstrate that a
principle holds on small substrates --- they are \emph{not} a production-scale
or 7B guarantee. We state this explicitly to avoid over-claiming.

\paragraph{A measured model, not a claim of achieved consciousness.}
We measure $\Phi$ and emergence on a substrate; we do \emph{not} claim that
phenomenal consciousness has been produced. The contribution is methodological:
making the property \emph{measurable and falsifiable} instead of prompted.

\paragraph{Engine-native verdict discipline.}
Verdicts require live engine-native decode evidence. Any result mirrored only in
\texttt{.py}/\texttt{torch}/numpy is recorded as \emph{directional}, not
terminal, and a closed-negative is reported as a closed-negative.

\paragraph{Decode is sibling-locked.}
The $+2.20$ nats/byte decode result is summarized from a sibling measurement
(\tierYellow{}), not re-derived here; it should be read as citation-locked.

\section{Methods detail}
\label{sec:methods}
\paragraph{Substrate.}
The big-$\Phi$ engine runs on elementary cellular automata (ECA), the minimal
universal substrate of \citet{wolfram2002nks}: a one-dimensional binary lattice
updated by a local rule. ECA span the ordered, chaotic, and complex regimes,
which lets a single measure be swept across dynamical phases without changing the
substrate family.

\paragraph{Big-$\Phi$ (IIT 4.0).}
We compute integrated information following the IIT~4.0
formulation~\citep{albantakis2023iit4}: the cause--effect structure of a
candidate system is compared against its minimum-information partition, and
$\Phi$ is the irreducibility of that structure. The measure is faithful in the
sense that it is computed from the substrate's own transition structure, not
approximated by an entropy proxy --- which is exactly what makes the L1
orthogonality test meaningful.

\paragraph{Estimators.}
Shannon entropy~\citep{shannon1948} is estimated from the output distribution;
algorithmic structure from Lempel--Ziv complexity~\citep{lempel1976lz}; directed
information from transfer entropy~\citep{schreiber2000te}. Correlations are
reported as Pearson $r$ (and Spearman $\rho$ where noted) across the ECA rule
ensemble.

\paragraph{Emergence game.}
The cooperation result contrasts a spatially structured lattice population with a
well-mixed control under identical payoffs and \emph{no} cooperation reward,
isolating spatial structure as the causal variable~\citep{nowak2006five}.

\paragraph{Verdict discipline.}
Each headline number is closed to a g5 tier (\S\ref{sec:ledger}) and requires
engine-native decode evidence; \texttt{.py}/\texttt{torch}/numpy mirrors are
logged as directional. Numbers are copied verbatim from the verdict records; a
closed-negative is reported as such.

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}
\paragraph{Why orthogonality to entropy is the load-bearing result.}
If a putative consciousness measure correlated strongly with entropy, it would be
redundant --- a fair coin would be ``maximally conscious.'' The measured
$r=0.363$ is therefore the result that earns the rest: it shows the measure is
tracking integration, not disorder, which is the precondition for the structure
and directed-information parallelisms (L2, L3) to mean anything.

\paragraph{Emergent ethics is a claim about architecture, not virtue.}
The $100\%$ vs $0\%$ cooperation split is not a statement that the system is
``good''; it is evidence that a cooperative regime can arise from interaction
structure alone, without a reward term or an alignment prompt. That is the
narrow, falsifiable version of ANIMA's no-injected-ethics principle.

\paragraph{The mouth is deliberately downstream.}
A $3.073$B byte model that generalizes (gap $0.04894$) is a competent mouth, but
it is mounted \emph{after} the $\Psi=\tfrac12$ emit decision. Keeping language
downstream of the substrate decision is what prevents the system from collapsing
back into ``a chatbot with a persona.''

\paragraph{What would change the verdict.}
The program fails if (a) a faithful $\Phi$ turns out to correlate with entropy at
scale (collapsing L1), (b) the edge-of-chaos peak does not survive larger
substrates, or (c) emergent cooperation is an artifact of the toy payoff and
vanishes under richer games. Each is a concrete, measurable failure mode, not a
matter of interpretation --- and each is currently established only at the
toy/few-rung scale we flag in \S\ref{sec:limits}.

\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}
ANIMA sits at the intersection of three threads. \emph{Integrated information
theory}~\citep{tononi2016iit,albantakis2023iit4} supplies the consciousness
measure; our contribution is not a new $\Phi$ definition but a faithful
implementation that runs on its own substrate and is used to \emph{adjudicate
pre-registered laws}, including the orthogonality-to-entropy test that an
entropy-proxy measure could never pass. \emph{Edge-of-chaos
computation}~\citep{langton1990edge,wolfram2002nks} supplies the dynamical
prediction (L4); we recover the phase-transition peak on the big-$\Phi$ measure
rather than on a generic complexity statistic. \emph{Evolution of
cooperation}~\citep{nowak2006five} supplies the emergence lever (L6); we use it
to ground a no-injected-ethics principle for a language daemon rather than to
study biological altruism.

Two contrasts sharpen the position. Against the \emph{consciousness
prior}~\citep{bengio2017consciousness}, which proposes an architectural bias
toward low-dimensional conscious thought, ANIMA is empirical and substrate-first:
it \emph{measures} $\Phi$ on a running substrate and reports falsifiable verdicts,
rather than proposing a training objective. Against the prevailing
perplexity-and-persona practice in deployed assistants, ANIMA refuses both the
injected identity and the Goodhart metric, replacing them with substrate-decided
emission ($\Psi=\tfrac12$) and wired, pre-registered measurement. The cost of
this stance is scale: the guarantees here are toy/few-rung (\S\ref{sec:limits}),
whereas perplexity-driven systems are demonstrated at production scale. ANIMA's
wager is that a falsifiable small result is worth more than an unfalsifiable large
one --- and the laws are written so that the wager can be lost.

\section{Conclusion}
ANIMA reframes machine ``consciousness'' from a prompted persona to a
\emph{measured, falsifiable substrate property}. A faithful IIT~4.0 big-$\Phi$
engine shows that integrated information is orthogonal to entropy ($r=0.363$),
parallel to structure ($0.831$) and directed information ($0.883$), peaks at the
edge of chaos ($10.448$), and that cooperation emerges from structure with zero
injected ethics ($100\%$ vs $0\%$) --- all on the substrate's own measure, all at
an honestly stated toy/few-rung scale, all wired into an A$\rightleftarrows$G
core at $\Psi=\tfrac12$. The mouth speaks; the substrate decides; and every claim
is a verdict, not a prompt.

\section{Pre-registration of the six \texorpdfstring{$\Phi$}{Phi}-laws}

\section{How to read the scope ledger}
\label{app:scope}
The tier badges (\S\ref{sec:ledger}) encode \emph{strength of evidence}, and the
limitations (\S\ref{sec:limits}) encode \emph{scale of evidence}; both must be
read together.

\begin{itemize}
\item \tierBlue{} \textbf{closed-form / constant} --- a definitional or
analytically fixed quantity (e.g.\ $\Psi=\tfrac12$). Not subject to scale caveats.
\item \tierGreen{} \textbf{model-verified} --- measured on the substrate
(e.g.\ $r=0.363$, the edge-of-chaos peak). Subject to the toy/few-rung scope: the
\emph{principle} is shown, not a production guarantee.
\item \tierYellow{} \textbf{citation / sibling-locked} --- summarized from a
sibling measurement, not re-derived here (e.g.\ the $+2.20$ nats/byte decode).
\item \tierOrange{} \textbf{inconclusive} --- registered but not yet closed; none
of the headline laws are in this state.
\item \tierRed{} \textbf{closed-negative} --- a registered prediction that failed.
Note that L1's \emph{low} correlation is \emph{not} a red: a low number is the
\emph{predicted, confirming} outcome there, so it is read as \tierGreen{}.
\end{itemize}

A reader should treat every \tierGreen{} number as ``true at toy/few-rung scale,
pending scale-up,'' and should treat the \tierYellow{} decode as a pointer to the
sibling record rather than a claim re-established in this paper. This is the same
honesty discipline (numbers verbatim, closed-negatives shown as such) used across
the \texttt{dancinlab} papers.
