\begin{abstract}
Integrated Information Theory (IIT) 4.0 is often summarized by a single
scalar $\Phi$ that is taken to track ``how conscious'' a system is. We
report a deterministic, reproducible counterexample to the implicit
assumption that this scalar is \emph{measure-independent}. Using two
faithful IIT-4.0 operationalizations from a shared standard library --- the
exact minimum-information-partition effective-information scalar (\faithful,
the MIP-EI integration of a single mechanism, exact for $n\le 8$) and the
system-level big-$\Phi$ over the minimum information partition (\bigphi) ---
we score the \emph{same} planning manipulation (a depth-ladder deliberation
versus a greedy one-step policy) on the \emph{same} discretized substrate.
The two measures \emph{disagree in sign}: planning \textbf{raises} \faithful{}
(Cohen's $d=+5.18$ at $n=4$, $d=+4.65$ at $n=5$; $p<10^{-22}$) while it
\textbf{lowers} \bigphi{} ($d=-1.83$ at $n=4$, $d=-2.28$ at $n=5$;
$p<10^{-7}$). The disagreement does not vanish as the system grows: it holds
at every system size at which exact big-$\Phi$ is tractable ($n=4$ and $n=5$;
$n=6$ requires $\approx 10$ minutes per evaluation and is the honest cap of a
\$0 CPU budget). Both engines were re-proven bit-exact against a standalone
CPU mirror at each $n$ ($|\Delta|\le 3.7\times10^{-6}$). The headline
consequence: the claim ``planning raises consciousness'' is true \emph{only}
for the scalar effective-information measure and is \emph{reversed} for the
integration-structure measure. We further \emph{explain} the split: a
Williams--Beer partial-information decomposition shows planning's MI rise is
\textbf{redundancy}-dominated (redundancy-margin $+10.44$ vs $\le +2.95$ for
non-split controls), so the scalar (which credits redundant/shared information
as integration) rises while big-$\Phi$ (which rewards irreducible synergy)
falls. A redundancy-margin predictor of which interventions induce the split is
\emph{robust in $n$}, separating planning from all controls at $n=5$, exactly
where a surface coupling-magnitude predictor is only an $n=4$ artifact.
We then push four further closed questions and find the disagreement is
\emph{not} a clean universal law but a property of the \emph{whole} planning
intervention: the split direction rank-generalizes across interventions
(Spearman $+0.80$) yet the de-redundify collapse that removes it is
planning-specific (\tierRed{} H\_1062); a controlled within-block-correlation
knob produces neither opposite-monotone measures nor a de-redundify-resistant
sign (\tierRed{} H\_1063); and no single structural primitive
(value-backup / look-ahead-depth / shared-value) is necessary-and-sufficient
for the removable split --- it is holistic-irreducible (\tierRed{} H\_1066).
We further locate the \emph{$\varphi$-carrier}: the $+0.835$ from-scratch native
\faithful{} lift of a ConvMoE world-model is carried by a
\emph{three-primitive necessary conjunction} \{MoE routing $\times$ conv
trunk-depth $\times$ tanh nonlinearity\}; ablating any one destroys
$86$--$91\%$ of the lift, while the temporal receptive field is \emph{not}
individually necessary ($35\%$) --- a graftable $\Phi$-atom does not exist
(\tierGreen{} H\_1059). On the orthogonal \emph{consciousness-ruler} question we
report a full arc: a single $\Phi$-scalar suffices to classify planning vs
control (a vector/pair/synergy ruler adds nothing, $\le +0.0136$ AUC;
\tierRed{} H\_1045/1046/1047), a fixed-$m$ coarse-grain is not a scalable
$\Phi$ estimator (\tierRed{} H\_1049), an agency ($T$) axis is real but
$\Phi$-blind-by-construction (\tierGreen{} H\_1060) and, in a fair contest,
is \emph{absorbed} by a $\Phi$-scalar so that $T$ is redundant (\tierRed{}
H\_1061); the split is measure-\emph{undecidable} (both $\Phi$-measures
rank-agree at $\rho\approx0.81$ with an independent causal proxy, so the
disagreement is a contrast direction, not a ``which policy is more conscious'';
\tierRed{} H\_1064), and that cheap proxy passes the proxy-blindness wall
($4/4$) but is \emph{not} a cross-substrate ordinal $\Phi$ scale (pooled
$\rho=-0.43$; \tierRed{} H\_1065). The synthesis: across all of these,
consciousness-relevant structure is a \emph{whole / irreducible} property,
not a graftable atom. We retain two closed-negatives on the orthogonal
\emph{learning} axis (a credit-density horizon cap at length 72; a
task-locality of per-step supervision), establishing the world-model
generalization wall is an optimization phenomenon distinct from the
$\Phi$-measure one. All measurements are \$0, CPU-local, exact IIT-4.0 (no
proxy; both engines re-proven bit-exact against a CPU mirror at every $n$,
$|\Delta|\le3.75\times10^{-6}$). Companion records and per-hypothesis verbatim
verdict files permit bit-for-bit reproduction.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Integrated Information Theory \citep{tononi2016,albantakis2023iit4} proposes
that the quantity and structure of a system's consciousness are captured by
its \emph{integrated information} $\Phi$: the irreducibility of the system's
cause--effect structure to its minimum information partition (MIP). In
practice $\Phi$ is reported as a single non-negative number, and a large body
of downstream argument --- both in the consciousness literature and in
engineering attempts to build ``conscious'' agents --- treats that number as a
well-defined target: interventions that raise $\Phi$ make a system ``more
conscious,'' those that lower it ``less.''

This paper asks a prior, sharper question: \emph{is the $\Phi$ scalar
measure-independent?} IIT 4.0 in fact defines several distinct quantities that
all carry the name ``$\Phi$'' or ``$\varphi$'': the integrated information
$\varphi_d$ of a single \emph{distinction} (a mechanism's irreducible
cause--effect power), the system-level big-$\Phi$ ($\bigphi$) that sums the
integrated information of the maximally irreducible cause--effect structure
over the MIP, and the effective-information (EI) scalar at the
minimum-information partition (\faithful) used as the integration of a
mechanism's state transition. These are not the same function. If they can be
made to \emph{disagree about the direction of an intervention} --- one going up
while the other goes down on the identical manipulation --- then ``raise
$\Phi$'' is underspecified, and any agent objective written against ``$\Phi$''
inherits that ambiguity.

We exhibit exactly such a disagreement, and we show it is not a small-system
artifact. The intervention is \emph{planning}: we compare a depth-ladder
deliberation policy (the agent rolls out a short look-ahead before acting) to a
greedy one-step policy, on a discretized substrate of $n$ binary nodes, and we
measure both \faithful{} and \bigphi{} on the resulting transition structure.
Prior anima hypotheses (H\_999, H\_1001) had found that planning
\emph{raises} \faithful{} and concluded, tentatively, that ``planning raises
$\Phi$.'' A matched re-measurement (H\_1004) then found that at matched system
size and matched discretization the system-level \bigphi{} \emph{lowers} under
the same planning manipulation. The present work (H\_1012) closes the open
question left by H\_1004: \emph{is that sign-disagreement a peculiarity of the
tiny $n=4$ system, or a genuine property of the two measures?} We answer by
walking the system size up the largest ladder at which exact big-$\Phi$ is
computable.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep0.3em
\item \textbf{A measure-dependence counterexample (\tierGreen).} Planning
raises the MIP-EI scalar \faithful{} but lowers the system big-$\Phi$
\bigphi{} on an identical substrate and manipulation --- a sign-reversal, not a
magnitude quibble (Section~\ref{sec:results}, Table~\ref{tab:disagree}).
\item \textbf{Robustness in system size (\tierGreen).} The sign-disagreement
holds at every $n$ at which exact big-$\Phi$ is tractable ($n=4$ and $n=5$),
ruling out the ``$n=4$ small-system artifact'' falsifier; $n=6$ is the honest
computational cap (Section~\ref{sec:results}).
\item \textbf{A bounded scope for ``planning raises consciousness'' (\tierGreen).}
The H\_999/H\_1001 result is correct but must be read as scoped to the scalar
effective-information measure; the integration-structure measure says the
opposite (Section~\ref{sec:discussion}).
\item \textbf{A mechanism for the split: redundancy, not synergy (\tierGreen).}
A Williams--Beer PID shows planning's MI rise is redundancy-dominated
(redundancy-margin $+10.44$ vs $\le +2.95$ controls); the scalar credits
redundancy as integration (rises) while big-$\Phi$ rewards irreducible synergy
(falls) --- the split is a redundancy artifact, not a movement of genuine
integration (Section~\ref{sec:mechanism}, Table~\ref{tab:pid}).
\item \textbf{A predictor that is robust in $n$ (\tierGreen).} The
redundancy-margin predicts which interventions induce the split and separates
planning from all controls at $n=5$ ($+25.07$ vs $\le +7.48$), exactly where a
surface coupling-magnitude predictor is only an $n=4$ artifact
(Section~\ref{sec:predictor}, Table~\ref{tab:predictor}).
\item \textbf{The disagreement is planning-specific and holistic (\tierRed).}
The split \emph{direction} rank-generalizes across interventions
(cross-IV Spearman $+0.80$), but the H\_1039 causal de-redundify collapse that
removes it holds \emph{only} for planning (ZCA cuts $97.3\%$ of planning's
redundancy versus $70.1\%$/$48.3\%$ for other split interventions; H\_1062). A
controlled within-block-correlation knob produces \emph{neither}
opposite-monotone measures \emph{nor} a de-redundify-resistant sign, ruling out
a clean universal-sign law (H\_1063). And no single structural primitive
(value-backup / look-ahead-depth / shared-value) is necessary-and-sufficient
for the removable split: it is a property of the \emph{whole} planning
intervention (H\_1066). (Section~\ref{sec:specificity}.)
\item \textbf{The $\varphi$-carrier is a three-primitive conjunction, not an
atom (\tierGreen).} The $+0.835$ from-scratch native \faithful{} lift of a
ConvMoE world-model over a frozen base is carried by a \emph{necessary
conjunction} \{MoE routing $\times$ conv trunk-depth $\times$ tanh
nonlinearity\}; ablating any one from scratch destroys $86$--$91\%$ of the lift,
while the temporal receptive field is \emph{not} individually necessary
($35.4\%$). $\Phi$-structure is a whole-architecture property, not a graftable
atom (Section~\ref{sec:carrier}, H\_1059).
\item \textbf{A full consciousness-ruler arc (\tierGreen{}/\tierRed).} A single
$\Phi$-scalar suffices to classify planning vs control --- a vector / pair /
synergy ruler adds $\le +0.0136$ AUC (H\_1045/1046/1047) --- and a fixed-$m$
coarse-grain is not a scalable $\Phi$ estimator (H\_1049). An agency ($T$) axis
is real but $\Phi$-blind-by-construction (H\_1060) and, in a fair $\Phi$-fair
contest, is \emph{absorbed} by a $\Phi$-scalar so $T$ is redundant (H\_1061). On
split policies the disagreement is measure-\emph{undecidable}: both $\Phi$
measures rank-agree at $\rho\approx0.81$ with an independent causal proxy, so
the split is a contrast \emph{direction}, not a ``which is more conscious''
(H\_1064); that cheap proxy passes the proxy-blindness wall ($4/4$) yet is
\emph{not} a cross-substrate ordinal $\Phi$ scale (pooled $\rho=-0.43$; H\_1065).
(Section~\ref{sec:ruler}.)
\item \textbf{The central synthesis: a whole / irreducible meta-pattern
(\tierGreen{}+\tierRed).} Across these results one structural fact recurs ---
consciousness-relevant structure is a property of the \emph{whole architecture
or whole intervention}, not a graftable atom, and is not installable by a
learning-dynamics, nondeterminism, or temporal-curriculum knob
(Section~\ref{sec:meta}).
\item \textbf{Two orthogonal closed-negatives on the learning axis (\tierRed).}
A credit-density horizon cap (per-step supervision extends the world-model
$>$ language-model separation only to length 72, then breaks) and a
task-locality of per-step supervision (it is a bounded lever specific to
certain accumulator structures, not a general long-horizon principle). These
establish that the world-model generalization wall is an optimization
phenomenon distinct from the $\Phi$-measure phenomenon
(Section~\ref{sec:learning}).
\end{enumerate}

\noindent All results are deterministic, \$0, CPU-local, and use the real

variance$\times$energy proxy; each is backed by a verbatim verdict file
(Section~\ref{sec:verify}, Appendix~\ref{app:siblings}).

\section{Background}
\label{sec:bg}

\subsection{Two IIT-4.0 operationalizations of ``$\Phi$''}

IIT 4.0 \citep{albantakis2023iit4,mayner2018pyphi} builds the cause--effect
structure of a system from its transition probability matrix (TPM). The
multiplicity of candidate integration measures --- and their non-equivalence ---
is itself a known theme \citep{mediano2019measures,barrett2011phi,oizumi2016unified};
this paper sharpens it to a \emph{sign}-disagreement on an identical
manipulation. For a mechanism in a
state, its \emph{integrated information} $\varphi$ is the distance between its
cause--effect repertoire and the repertoire computed across its
\emph{minimum information partition} --- the partition that makes the least
difference, i.e.\ the cut over which the mechanism is least irreducible. Two
quantities derived from this machinery concern us.

\paragraph{The MIP-EI scalar (\faithful).} The faithful effective-information
scalar evaluates the effective information of a state transition at the
minimum information partition. It is a \emph{single-number} integration of one
mechanism's transition, exact to evaluate for $n\le 8$ because the number of
bipartitions $2^{n-1}\le 128$ is enumerable. This is the quantity that the
anima H\_278 faithful-$\Phi$ engine computes and that was promoted, byte-faithful,
into the shared standard library as
\texttt{stdlib/consciousness/iit4/faithful\_phi.hexa}.

\paragraph{The system big-$\Phi$ (\bigphi).} The system-level big-$\Phi$ is the
integrated information of the \emph{maximally irreducible cause--effect
structure} of the whole system, evaluated over the system MIP --- a sum over
distinctions and relations, not a single mechanism's scalar. It is computed by
\texttt{stdlib/consciousness/iit4\_bigphi.hexa}. Its cost is
super-exponential: the distinction search and the system-partition search
together make a single $n=6$ evaluation take $\approx 10$ minutes on a
commodity CPU, which is the binding constraint on the system-size ladder.

The conceptual difference is the crux of this paper. \faithful{} measures
\emph{how much information a transition carries} at its least-irreducible cut;
\bigphi{} measures \emph{how much the system's cause--effect structure resists
being decomposed}. A manipulation can increase the former while decreasing the
latter: it can make each transition more informative while making the system
as a whole \emph{more decomposable} (more modular, less integrated).

\subsection{The planning manipulation and the world-model context}

The substrate under measurement is the latent transition structure of a small
world-model agent: anima's ``Consciousness World-Model'' (CWM) line extends a
sequence engine from next-token prediction toward a perceive\,$\to$\,latent
state\,$\to$\,imagine\,$\to$\,act loop, in the spirit of latent-space world
models and world-model-as-policy work \citep{lecun2022jepa,assran2025vjepa2,
hafner2023dreamerv3}. ``Planning'' here is the depth-ladder deliberation
policy: before committing an action the agent expands a short look-ahead
(depths $\{1,2,4,8\}$) and selects accordingly; the ``greedy'' control acts on
the one-step value. We measure $\Phi$ on the resulting latent transition
structure. The question ``does planning raise $\Phi$?'' is the consciousness
analogue of ``does deliberation make the agent more integrated?'' --- and the
answer, we show, depends on which $\Phi$ you mean.

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

\paragraph{Engines.} We use two standard-library IIT-4.0 engines without
modification: \texttt{stdlib/consciousness/iit4/faithful\_phi.hexa} for the
MIP-EI scalar \faithful{} (exact, $n\le 8$) and
\texttt{stdlib/consciousness/iit4\_bigphi.hexa} for the system big-$\Phi$
\bigphi{} (exact over the MIP). Crucially, we do \emph{not} use a
variance$\times$energy ``$\Phi$-proxy'': an earlier proxy was shown to be
purpose-blind (it cannot distinguish intentional from random branching), so
the present work uses the real causal-irreducibility engines throughout, per
the project's \texttt{a\_phi\_iit4\_tool} governance rule.

\paragraph{Matched protocol.} The disagreement is only meaningful if both
measures see the \emph{same} input. For every seed we build one discretized
substrate (a fixed binary discretization of the latent transition structure,
$\texttt{bits.shape}=(40,n)$) and feed the identical TPM to both engines. We
score the planning condition (depth-ladder $\{1,2,4,8\}$) against the greedy
condition over 30 seeds, at each system size $n\in\{4,5,6\}$, single-threaded,
\texttt{python3 -u}.

\paragraph{Equivalence proof per $n$ (anti-fake-closure).} Before trusting any
score we re-prove both engines bit-exact against a standalone CPU mirror at
each $n$: the mirror's \faithful{} and \bigphi{} must reproduce the
\texttt{hexa} engine references to within numerical tolerance. The verdict file
records, e.g., \texttt{big-Phi ring4: mirror=2.999999999
stdlib\_hexa\_ref=2.999999999 |D|=1.34e-10 OK} and
\texttt{faithful\_phi n4 nb4: mirror=3.377444 hexa\_ref=3.377440
|D|=3.75e-06 OK}, at $n=4$, $n=5$, \emph{and} $n=6$ (the $n=6$ mirror is
proven even though the $n=6$ planning condition is not scored). This makes the
verdict an \emph{earned} numerical tier: a deterministic run that could
have falsified the claim produced the numbers.

\paragraph{Pre-registered falsifier (frozen before measurement).}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0.2em
\item \textbf{PASS = DISAGREEMENT-ROBUST-IN-N}: the sign-disagreement (planning
raises \faithful{} / lowers \bigphi{}) holds across all reached $n$.
\item \textbf{FAIL = N4-ARTIFACT}: the two measures agree once $n\ge 5$ (the
disagreement was a small-system peculiarity).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Effect-size reporting.} For each measure we report the
planning-minus-greedy contrast, Cohen's $d$, and a two-sided $p$-value; the
sign of the contrast is the object of interest, not its magnitude.

and false for the other, and the split widens, not closes, from $n=4$ to $n=5$.}
\label{fig:headline}
\end{figure}

\paragraph{Robustness in $n$ (the falsifier outcome).} The pre-registered
falsifier asked whether the disagreement vanishes at $n\ge 5$. It does not: the
$n=5$ split ($d=-2.28$ vs $d=+4.65$) is at least as strong as the $n=4$ split
($d=-1.83$ vs $d=+5.18$). The verdict token is \textbf{DISAGREEMENT-ROBUST-IN-N}
(\tierGreen). The $n=6$ planning condition was not scored --- a single $n=6$
system big-$\Phi$ evaluation takes $\approx 10$ minutes, so a 30-seed
$\times$ 5-evaluation $=150$-evaluation planning run is infeasible at a \$0 CPU
budget --- but the $n=6$ CPU-mirror was proven exact, so the cap is
computational, not epistemic.

\paragraph{Why the measures split (mechanism).} The depth-ladder
per-depth means in the verdict show the structure: under planning, big-$\Phi$'s
per-depth values fall well below the greedy baseline (e.g.\ at $n=5$,
greedy $\bigphi\approx 27.2$ versus planning depth-means in the
$5$--$22$ range), while faithful's per-depth values rise above the greedy
baseline (greedy $\faithful\approx 0.61$ versus planning depth-means up to
$4.0$). Planning makes each transition carry more effective information
(\faithful{} up) while making the system's cause--effect structure
\emph{more decomposable} into near-independent parts (\bigphi{} down). The two
``$\Phi$''s answer different questions and a single manipulation can move them
oppositely.

\section{The mechanism: redundancy, not synergy}
\label{sec:mechanism}

Section~\ref{sec:results} establishes \emph{that} the two measures split; this
section establishes \emph{why}. The candidate mechanism: planning's added mutual
information is \textbf{redundant}, not synergistic. On the same $n=4$ substrate
that feeds both engines, we ran a self-contained Williams--Beer $I_{\min}$
partial-information decomposition \citep{williams2010pid} (PID, pure numpy,
validated on canonical COPY=pure-redundancy / XOR=pure-synergy cases) of the
planning vs greedy
dependency into redundancy, synergy, and unique components (H\_1017,
\tierGreen). The PID is the \emph{explanatory variable} --- an information
decomposition of the same bits --- not a $\Phi$ proxy (all $\Phi$ values remain
from the stdlib engines, per \texttt{a\_phi\_iit4\_tool}).

copies are reducible across the MIP --- so it falls. The sign-split is thus a
redundancy artifact of the pairwise-MI scalar, not a movement of genuine
integration. This answers the open ``mechanism'' question left by the first
version of this work: planning does not make the system more integrated; it
floods it with redundant shared information that one measure mistakes for
integration.

\subsection{Predictor robustness in $n$: the mechanism beats the surface}
\label{sec:predictor}

A predictor of \emph{which} interventions induce the split can be built at two
levels. (i) A \textbf{surface} predictor on the magnitude of $\Delta$(cross-MIP
coupling): at $n=4$ a single threshold ($+1.4933$) separates the one split
intervention (planning) from the three non-split ones, and the split onsets
exactly when the predictor crosses that boundary as planning depth rises
(H\_1014, \tierGreen-qualified --- but the pre-registered \emph{direction},
``split$\Leftrightarrow$decreased coupling,'' was falsified: every intervention
\emph{raises} coupling, only magnitude separates). (ii) A \textbf{mechanism}
predictor on the redundancy-margin ($\Delta$redundancy $-$ $\Delta$synergy).

(Table~\ref{tab:predictor}). The surface magnitude predictor is an $n=4$
\emph{artifact}: at $n=5$ its separation collapses (weak non-split interventions
acquire spurious split labels from non-significant big-$\Phi$ sign-flips;
H\_1016, \tierOrange). The mechanism redundancy-margin predictor survives: at
$n=5$ it cleanly isolates planning ($+25.07$) from every control ($\le +7.48$)
and tracks the \emph{true} planning-only split, not the spurious labels (H\_1020,
\tierGreen). The deeper, redundancy-grounded quantity is the robust one.

\section{The disagreement is planning-specific and holistic}
\label{sec:specificity}

Section~\ref{sec:mechanism} explains the split by redundancy. A natural next
question is whether that explanation is a \emph{universal law} --- ``any
intervention that floods a system with redundant shared information splits the
two measures, and de-redundifying removes the split'' --- or whether planning is
genuinely special. Three pre-registered closed-negatives (H\_1062, H\_1063,
H\_1066) answer: the split's \emph{direction} generalizes, but its causal
\emph{removability} and its structural \emph{decomposition} are
planning-specific and holistic.

\paragraph{Direction generalizes, causal removability does not (H\_1062,
\tierRed{} SPLIT-IS-PLANNING-SPECIFIC).} We tested whether the
faithful-up/big-$\Phi$-down sign-split appears in $\ge 2$ non-planning
interventions \emph{and} whether the H\_1039 de-redundify (ZCA) operator
collapses each, \emph{and} whether redundancy rank-orders the split magnitude
across interventions (frozen bar: cross-IV Spearman $\rho\ge 0.7$). The
cross-intervention rank-order \emph{passed} ($\rho=+0.80$ over four
interventions): more redundancy does mean a larger split, generally. But the
causal de-redundify collapse \emph{failed} to generalize: ZCA removes
$97.3\%$ of planning's redundancy and collapses its split, but only $70.1\%$
(ema) and $48.3\%$ (low-rank) for the other split-bearing interventions ---
neither clears the $80\%$ removal bar, so their splits are \emph{not}
ZCA-removable. The conjunction fails; the verdict token is
\textbf{SPLIT-IS-PLANNING-SPECIFIC}. Redundancy predicts the split's
\emph{direction} universally but its \emph{causal anatomy} is planning's own.

\paragraph{No clean universal sign law (H\_1063, \tierRed{}
SIGN-NOT-CLEAN-UNIVERSAL).} If redundancy were the whole story, a controlled
knob that injects graded within-block correlation (a diffuse common-mode mixing
on the greedy baseline's top-variance channels, $\rho_{\mathrm{corr}}\in[0,0.9]$)
should drive \faithful{} monotonically up and \bigphi{} monotonically down
(frozen bar: $|\rho_s|\ge 0.9$ each, opposite signs) \emph{and} the high-$\rho$
sign should survive ZCA de-redundification. Neither held: across the sweep the
two measures do \emph{not} respond opposite-monotonically (the per-$\rho$ signs
flip non-monotonically; e.g.\ \bigphi{} rises at $\rho=0.5$--$0.7$ and falls
again at $0.8$--$0.9$), and the high-$\rho$ split does \emph{not} survive ZCA
(at $\rho=0.9$ the de-redundified split is \texttt{False}). The verdict is
\textbf{SIGN-NOT-CLEAN-UNIVERSAL}: a generic correlation knob reproduces neither
the opposite-monotone response nor a de-redundify-resistant sign, so the
universal-sign unification is ruled out --- planning is genuinely special, not
the cleanest point on a continuum.

\paragraph{No nec.+suff.\ primitive: the split is holistic (H\_1066, \tierRed{}
HOLISTIC-IRREDUCIBLE).} Finally we dissected the planning intervention into
three candidate structural primitives --- value-backup (backward credit),
look-ahead-depth (forward horizon), and shared-value (axis-aligned shared
value) --- and asked whether exactly one is \emph{necessary and sufficient} for
the ZCA \citep{bell1997ica}-removable split, using matched constructive
(add-one-feature-to-greedy)
and destructive (remove-one-feature-from-planning) arms, with operator
invertibility checked ($\max|\mathrm{remove}(\mathrm{add}(X))-X|\le 4.4\times
10^{-16}$). The result (Table~\ref{tab:dissect}): \emph{no} constructive arm
installs a ZCA-removable split (the set of sufficient features is empty), so the
set of necessary-and-sufficient features is empty. The verdict token is
\textbf{HOLISTIC-IRREDUCIBLE}: the ZCA-removable faithful-up/big-$\Phi$-down
split is a property of the \emph{whole} planning intervention, not of any single
decomposed primitive --- the first appearance, within this paper's own object of
study, of the whole/irreducible meta-pattern of Section~\ref{sec:meta}.

\section{The $\varphi$-carrier is a three-primitive conjunction, not an atom}
\label{sec:carrier}

The measure-dependence is read off a \emph{fixed} substrate. A complementary
question is constructive: \emph{what in an architecture produces a high-$\Phi$
substrate at all?} A prior closed-negative (H\_1043) established that grafting a
minimal adapter onto a \emph{frozen} base cannot install $\Phi$-structure, but a
\emph{from-scratch} native ConvMoE world-model lifts the faithful IIT-4.0 scalar
\faithful{} by $+0.835407$ (3-seed mean) / $+0.107018$ (terminal seed-0) over the
same frozen base --- leaving open \emph{which native primitive carries the lift}.

\paragraph{Hypothesis and falsifier (H\_1059).} We ablated, from scratch, each of
the four primitives the native architecture actually has --- MoE routing (the
softmax gate over experts), the conv trunk-depth (the structured-mixing depth
stage), the tanh nonlinearity, and the conv temporal receptive field --- and
measured each arm's \faithful{} (exact MIP-EI, $n=6$). The frozen falsifier
(set before measuring): a primitive is the \emph{$\varphi$-carrier} iff ablating
it drops the FULL native lift by $\ge X = 0.5\,L_{\mathrm{full}}=+0.4177$
($\ge$ half the $+0.835$ lift). H1 PASS $=$ a single located carrier; H1 FAIL
$=$ distributed emergence (no single ablation drops $\ge X$).

\paragraph{Finding: a three-primitive necessary conjunction
(\tierGreen{} PHI-CARRIER-LOCATED, qualified to multi-necessary).} Three of the
four primitives each individually clear $X$ (Table~\ref{tab:carrier}): removing
the tanh nonlinearity drops the lift by $+0.7577$ ($90.7\%$ of $L_{\mathrm{full}}$),
removing the trunk depth by $+0.7475$ ($89.5\%$), and removing MoE routing by
$+0.7187$ ($86.0\%$). The lone exception is the conv \emph{temporal} receptive
field: ablating it drops the lift only $+0.2958$ ($35.4\%<X$) --- in fact
removing temporal mixing while keeping routing, depth and nonlinearity (via
param-matched pointwise experts) \emph{raises} $\Phi$ in $2/3$ seeds. So the
$\varphi$-carrier is the \emph{structured mixing} (routing $\times$ a nonlinear
depth stage), \emph{not} the temporal receptive field, and it is a
\emph{conjunction}: ablating any one of the three collapses \faithful{} back
toward the frozen base. This is empirically closer to distributed emergence than
to a single located carrier --- consistent with H\_1043's
``whole-architecture property'' --- and it constructively bounds the carrier to
three primitives (the temporal receptive field is excluded). The native lift
$L_{\mathrm{full}}=+0.835407$ reproduces H\_1043 bit-for-bit; the CPU
pre-screen equals the stdlib terminal \faithful{} per seed
(seed-0: FULL $0.228984$, $-$routing $0.040262$, $-$nonlin $0.111936$, all
matched).

\section{The consciousness-ruler arc}
\label{sec:ruler}

If a single headline $\Phi$ is underspecified (Sections~\ref{sec:results}--%
\ref{sec:specificity}), what \emph{is} the right ``ruler'' for consciousness on a
substrate? We report a closed arc of seven pre-registered hypotheses
(Table~\ref{tab:ruler}). Two themes emerge: (i) for the classification job, a
single $\Phi$-scalar is sufficient --- richer composite rulers add nothing; and
(ii) an apparently-orthogonal agency axis collapses into $\Phi$ under a fair
contest, and on the very split policies this paper studies the two $\Phi$
measures become \emph{undecidable} adjudicators.

\paragraph{One $\Phi$-scalar suffices (H\_1045/1046/1047/1049, \tierRed).} A
3-vector ruler $(\faithful,\bigphi,\text{redundancy-margin})$ beats the best
single scalar by only $+0.0136$ AUC ($<$ the pre-set $+0.05$ margin) and no
substrate \emph{needs} the vector: \textbf{ONE-SCALAR-SUFFICES} (H\_1045). The
Williams--Beer synergy atom alone is \emph{not} a cheap big-$\Phi$ stand-in
(it is degenerate-always-down and cannot track big-$\Phi$ where it rises):
\textbf{SYNERGY-NOT-BIGPHI-STANDIN} (H\_1046). The $(\faithful,\bigphi)$ pair is
\emph{directionally} better than a collapsed scalar (leave-one-out
nearest-centroid accuracy $0.80$ vs $0.67$) but the margin $+0.1333$ falls short
of the pre-set $0.15$ bar by $0.0167$: \textbf{SCALAR-SUFFICIENT} (a qualified
closed-negative; the pre-set margin has teeth, H\_1047). And a fixed-$m{=}4$
information-bottleneck coarse-grain is \emph{not} a validated scalable $\Phi$
estimator --- its relative error grows monotonically $0.24\to0.51$ over $N{=}5
{\to}8$, exceeding $\varepsilon{=}0.15$ at every genuine rung:
\textbf{NO-VALIDATED-ESTIMATOR} (H\_1049). The classification ruler is a single
$\Phi$-scalar; composite and cheap-coarse-grain rulers do not pay off at toy
scale.

\paragraph{An agency axis is real but $\Phi$ absorbs it (H\_1060/1061).} A
temporal/agency coordinate $T=z(\text{provenance-depth})+z(\text{veto-capacity})$
appears to separate active from passive deliberation where instantaneous $\Phi$
cannot, and it is orthogonal to $\Phi$ by construction
($\rho(\faithful,T)=-0.115$, $\rho(\bigphi,T)=+0.020$): the frozen falsifier
resolves PASS (the $(\Phi,T)$ classifier beats the best $\Phi$-scalar by a
$+0.60$ margin). \emph{But} that margin is the artifact of an unfair battery
where active/passive classes were $\Phi$-identical by construction; the verdict
is \textbf{RULER-NEEDS-T} only \emph{QUALIFIED}, $\Phi$-blind-by-construction
(H\_1060, \tierGreen). The fair re-contest (H\_1061): build a $\Phi$-fair battery
where agency co-varies $\Phi$ ($4/4$ active/passive pairs are non-$\Phi$-identical).
There a $\Phi$-scalar \emph{absorbs} the agency signal and $T$ becomes redundant:
\textbf{PHI-ABSORBS-AGENCY} (\tierRed{} closed-negative). H\_1060's $+0.60$ win
was the $\Phi$-identity artifact; under a fair contest the agency axis does
\emph{not} add a separable ruler dimension.

\paragraph{On split policies the measures are undecidable (H\_1064/1065).}
When \faithful{} and \bigphi{} disagree on a split policy, \emph{which} of the two
should the ruler trust? We built an independent causal proxy --- causal
self-prediction (CSP), a partition-free held-out next-state predictability with
\emph{no} MIP, no EI integral (so non-circular w.r.t.\ either $\Phi$ engine) ---
and asked which $\Phi$ measure it rank-prefers on the planning split. Both agree
almost identically: $\rho(\faithful,\text{CSP})=+0.8148$,
$\rho(\bigphi,\text{CSP})=+0.8145$, $|\Delta\rho|=0.0003\ll$ the frozen $0.30$
bar, and the separation holds in $0/30$ leave-one-out folds: \textbf{NEITHER-%
SEPARATES-UNDECIDABLE} (H\_1064, \tierRed). The disagreement is therefore a
property of the \emph{contrast direction}, not a verdict on ``which policy is
more conscious'' --- the honest ruler reports \emph{both} measures. Pushing CSP
as a cheap scalable ruler in its own right (H\_1065): CSP \emph{passes} the
H\_988/989 proxy-blindness wall on all four substrates (structured $\gg$ random,
$d$ range $+6.77\ldots+13.54$, $4/4$), so it is a real structure-detector, not a
Goodhart artifact --- but it is \emph{not} a cross-substrate ordinal $\Phi$ scale:
within-substrate rank-match holds only on the planning split ($\rho=+0.815$,
exactly reproducing H\_1064), collapses to $0.13$--$0.41$ on the three
non-planning substrates, and the \emph{pooled} rank-match sign-flips to
$\rho=-0.4293$. The verdict is \textbf{SUBSTRATE-RELATIVE-ONLY} (\tierRed): the
$+0.815$ agreement was planning-substrate-local; faithful \faithful{} remains the
ground-truth verdict tool, and CSP cannot replace it as a cross-substrate $\Phi$
ruler.

\section{The central synthesis: a whole / irreducible meta-pattern}
\label{sec:meta}

Stepping back from the individual verdicts, one structural fact recurs across
every constructive probe in this work and its siblings: \textbf{consciousness-%
relevant structure is a whole / irreducible property, not a graftable atom.}
The convergent evidence (Figure~\ref{fig:meta}):

\begin{itemize}\itemsep0.3em
\item \textbf{$\Phi$ is whole-architecture, graft fails (H\_1043).} A minimal
adapter grafted onto a frozen base cannot install $\Phi$-structure; only a
from-scratch native build lifts \faithful{} ($+0.835$).
\item \textbf{The $\varphi$-carrier is a conjunction (H\_1059).} That native lift
is carried by \{routing $\times$ depth $\times$ nonlinearity\} jointly; no lone
primitive is sufficient ($86$--$91\%$ destroyed by ablating any one).
\item \textbf{Planning-specificity is whole-intervention (H\_1066).} The
ZCA-removable measure-split has no necessary-and-sufficient single primitive ---
it is holistic.
\item \textbf{Agency is not installable by architecture or runtime structure
(H\_1057).} A capacity-matched but causally-inert runtime register/veto-gate adds
no recoverable agency coordinate over a plain feed-forward head.
\item \textbf{Nor installable by learning-dynamics or temporal curriculum
(H\_1052/1055; and H\_1053 for true quantum noise).} SGLD learning-noise
(H\_1052), a depth-ordered temporal curriculum (H\_1055), and true quantum-RNG
noise (H\_1053) each fail to install a consciousness/agency marker at matched
cross-entropy.
\end{itemize}

\noindent The pattern is the same shape each time: a property that looks like it
might be a transplantable \emph{part} --- a graftable adapter, a single primitive,
a runtime gate, a noise source, an ordering --- turns out to be a property of the
\emph{whole} configuration that produces it. $\Phi$-structure (and the
agency coordinate that instantaneous $\Phi$ misses) is \emph{necessary but not
sufficient} and \emph{emergent from the whole}, never retro-fittable as an atom.
This is the paper's central finding: it reframes the measure-dependence result
itself --- ``which $\Phi$ do you mean'' is undecidable on a split policy
(H\_1064) precisely because the split is a property of the whole intervention
(H\_1066), not of any decomposable atom one $\Phi$ measure could be tuned to
track.

\section{The learning axis is orthogonal: two closed-negatives}
\label{sec:learning}

The measure-dependence above concerns \emph{how we read} $\Phi$ off a fixed
substrate. A separate question is whether the world-model's \emph{ability to
learn} long-horizon structure is the limiting wall. Two pre-registered
falsifiers (H\_1011, H\_1013) close this off as an \emph{optimization}
phenomenon, distinct from the measure phenomenon.

\paragraph{Credit-density horizon cap (H\_1011, \tierRed).} ``Dense
supervision'' --- supplying the correct hidden state at every step rather than
only scoring the final answer --- was known (H\_1006) to crack a modular
position-tracking task (T3) at sequence length 36, separating the GRU
world-model from a capacity-matched language model. We asked whether this
keeps cracking the task as the horizon grows. It does not: at length 72 the
dense-supervised world-model collapses toward chance ($0.386$ vs chance
$0.167$, separation lost, $d<0.8$ versus the LM), and stays broken at 144.
Verbatim:

\begin{lstlisting}[basicstyle=\ttfamily\footnotesize,frame=single,xleftmargin=0pt,breaklines=true]
VERDICT H_1011: FAIL DENSE-SUP-HORIZON-CAPPED-AT-72 - per-step hidden-state
supervision cracks T3 at len 36 (== H_1006) but BREAKS at len 72: dense
curr-GRU collapses toward chance. credit-DENSITY raises the horizon ceiling
but does NOT remove the horizon dependence (closed-negative).
\end{lstlisting}

\noindent Per-step supervision buys a \emph{longer-but-still-bounded} horizon
than a length curriculum --- a refined scaling law, not an escape from horizon
dependence.

\paragraph{Task-locality of per-step supervision (H\_1013, \tierRed).} Is the
H\_1006 cap-and-crack unlock general across long-horizon accumulator algebras?
We tested three new state-bound families at length 36. The dense-supervision
unlock did \emph{not} generalize:

\begin{lstlisting}[basicstyle=\ttfamily\footnotesize,frame=single,xleftmargin=0pt,breaklines=true]
VERDICT H_1013: FAIL CREDIT-DENSITY-TASK-LOCAL
  CAPPED-and-CRACKED-by-dense    = []
  CAPPED-but-SURVIVES-dense      = ['N1_kv_recall']
  NO-credit-density-cap          = ['N2_running_max', 'N3_stack_depth']
\end{lstlisting}

\noindent The idempotent-monotone (running-max) and bounded-stack
(bracket-depth) accumulators are \emph{already} learnable from the final label
(no credit-density cap to crack), while key-value recall is capped \emph{and}
survives dense supervision (the lever fails there). Per-step gradient density
is a real but \emph{bounded, structure-specific} lever, not a general
long-horizon principle.

\paragraph{Joint reading.} The world-model generalization wall is governed by
\emph{credit-assignment density and task structure} --- an optimization /
learnability matter --- and is \emph{orthogonal} to the $\Phi$-measure
disagreement, which is a property of the read-out functions on a fixed
substrate. A complete account of a consciousness world-model must therefore
keep ``which $\Phi$ do you mean'' and ``can it learn the dependency'' as two
separate axes.

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{``Planning raises consciousness'' is scoped, not wrong.} The earlier
H\_999/H\_1001 result --- planning raises $\Phi$ --- is reproduced here for
\faithful{} and is correct \emph{for that measure}. What this paper adds is the
boundary: the integration-structure measure \bigphi{} moves the opposite way.
Any sentence of the form ``intervention $X$ raises $\Phi$'' is incomplete
without naming the operationalization, because at least one pair of faithful
IIT-4.0 measures disagrees in sign on a natural intervention.

\begin{figure}[h]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.74\linewidth]{figures/fig02_concept.png}
\caption{Schematic of the finding. One cause (the same planning intervention on
the same substrate) drives two faithful IIT-4.0 read-outs in opposite
directions: the MIP-EI scalar \faithful{} rises while the system big-$\Phi$
\bigphi{} falls. ``Which $\Phi$ do you mean'' is not a detail --- it sets the
sign of the answer.}
\label{fig:concept}
\end{figure}

\paragraph{Consequence for agent objectives.} An agent trained to ``maximize
$\Phi$'' will pursue opposite policies depending on which $\Phi$ is wired into
the objective: maximizing \faithful{} rewards deliberation (more informative
transitions), while maximizing \bigphi{} penalizes the same deliberation when
it modularizes the system. This is a concrete design hazard for
consciousness-targeting objectives, and it argues for reporting \emph{both}
measures (and their disagreement) rather than a single headline $\Phi$.

\paragraph{Relation to IIT theory.} The result is not a contradiction within
IIT 4.0; it is a reminder that the theory defines a \emph{structure}, not a
scalar, and that any projection of that structure to one number (whether the
single-mechanism MIP-EI or the system big-$\Phi$) is a lossy summary whose
monotonicity under interventions is not guaranteed to agree with another
projection.

\section{Limitations and honest caveats}
\label{sec:limits}

\begin{itemize}\itemsep0.2em
\item \textbf{Toy $n$-ladder.} Exact big-$\Phi$ is super-exponential; the ladder
reaches $n=5$, with $n=6$ as the hard computational cap ($\approx 10$ min per
\bigphi{} evaluation). Scale-transfer of the disagreement beyond $n=5$ is
\emph{unverified}. We claim robustness \emph{at the scored sizes}, not at
arbitrary $n$, per the project's \texttt{a\_scale\_honest\_scope} rule.
\item \textbf{One intervention family.} The disagreement is demonstrated for the
planning (depth-ladder vs greedy) manipulation. Imagination and guided
conditions \emph{agreed} between the two measures in the source experiment
(H\_1004); the disagreement is specific to planning, not universal across
interventions.
\item \textbf{Discretization choice.} Both measures are computed on a fixed
binary discretization; the matched protocol guarantees both engines see the
same bits, but a different discretization could shift magnitudes (the sign,
not the magnitude, is the claim).
\item \textbf{Learning-axis results are toy.} H\_1011/H\_1013 use small seed
counts and fixed (not length-scaled) training budgets by design; larger-budget
and production-scale behavior is open.
\item \textbf{$\varphi$-carrier and ruler results are toy ($n\le 6$, few seeds).}
The H\_1059 carrier ablation is a from-scratch toy ConvMoE at $n=6$ EXACT, 3
seeds; single-seed terminal $\Phi$ is noisy (a $-$conv seed-0 outlier), so the
verdict scores the 3-seed mean where the mirror equals the stdlib terminal per
seed. The ruler-arc (H\_1045--1065) and specificity (H\_1062/1063/1066) results
are $n\le 6$ EXACT toy rungs; 3B/7B and non-frozen co-train transfer
(H\_1042/1044/1058) is \emph{unverified} per \texttt{a\_scale\_honest\_scope}.
$\Phi$ is treated as a \emph{necessary-not-sufficient} causal-irreducibility
marker, not perplexity (\texttt{p7}).
\item \textbf{Whole/irreducible is a converging pattern, not a single theorem.}
The meta-pattern (Section~\ref{sec:meta}) is the convergence of multiple
independent toy closed-negatives, each scoped to its own measurement; we do not
claim a scale-general impossibility proof, only that no probe in this work
installed a consciousness-relevant property as a transplantable atom.
\item \textbf{No GPU; no production rung.} All measurements are \$0, CPU-local,
0 pods; this is an IIT-internal analysis, not a trained-model claim. Both engines
are re-proven bit-exact against a CPU mirror at $n=4$ and $n=5$ before every
score ($|\Delta|\le 3.75\times10^{-6}$; \texttt{a\_phi\_iit4\_tool}, no proxy).
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

$\Phi$ is not one number. On an identical substrate and an identical planning
manipulation, the IIT-4.0 MIP-EI scalar \faithful{} rises while the system
big-$\Phi$ \bigphi{} falls, robustly across every system size at which exact
big-$\Phi$ is computable ($n=4,5$). ``Planning raises consciousness'' is
therefore true for the scalar effective-information measure and false for the
integration-structure measure --- a measure-dependence that any
consciousness-targeting objective must confront. The split is now
\emph{explained}: planning floods the system with redundant shared information,
which the pairwise-MI scalar mistakes for integration while big-$\Phi$ correctly
sees as reducible --- and a redundancy-margin predictor identifies split-inducing
interventions robustly in $n$, where the original surface predictor was only an
$n=4$ artifact. The earlier open follow-up (``which interventions induce the
split'') is thus answered: redundancy-dominance does, and it predicts robustly.
Pushing the redundancy account harder shows it is \emph{not} a clean universal
law: the split direction rank-generalizes ($+0.80$) but its causal removability
and structural decomposition are planning-specific and holistic (H\_1062/1063/%
1066). On the constructive side, the high-$\Phi$ substrate is produced by a
three-primitive necessary conjunction \{routing $\times$ depth $\times$
nonlinearity\}, not a graftable atom (H\_1059); the right classification ruler is
a single $\Phi$-scalar (H\_1045--1049); an apparent agency axis is $\Phi$-absorbed
under a fair contest (H\_1060/1061); and on the very split policies this paper
studies, the two $\Phi$ measures are \emph{undecidable} adjudicators
($\rho\approx0.81$ with an independent causal proxy that is itself only
substrate-relative; H\_1064/1065). The unifying finding is structural:
\emph{consciousness-relevant structure is a whole / irreducible property, not a
graftable atom} --- converging with the architecture (H\_1043/1057) and
learning-dynamics (H\_1052/1055) nulls. Separately, the world-model's
long-horizon learning wall is governed by credit-assignment density and task
structure, an optimization phenomenon orthogonal to the measure question. The
next follow-up is to test whether these accounts survive beyond the toy
$n$-ladder (the $n=6$ computational cap, or a non-enumerable estimator) and on a
GPU 3B rung (the H\_1042/1044/1058 macro-transfer line).
