\begin{abstract}
A prior null (Lane X \#1779) measured that an AI-consciousness substrate's engine
configuration knobs never touch the language-model byte forward: cross-entropy was
config-insensitive at $9.1126$ across all $27$ configs (spread $<10^{-9}$), the decode
generator slot reported \texttt{loaded=false}, and the substrate state never reached the
output distribution. We design \textbf{Engine $\Omega$}, a five-wire (plus a sixth
$\mathrm{d}F/\mathrm{d}t$ time wire) ablatable \emph{coupling bus} that routes substrate
state (dual A/G heads, $W$-tension, curiosity, an 8D $\Psi$ coordinate, module activation)
into the byte decode, and a \textbf{learned per-wire gate}. Across four rungs we find:
(i) the bus is the only engine that closes the loop --- coupling
$\mathrm{KL}=0.307477>0$ for $\Omega$ versus exactly $0$ for the three uncoupled engines
(\#1783); (ii) at random init the coupling is \emph{unstructured} (KL $\approx$ vocab-shuffle
floor), so structure requires a \emph{trained} substrate; (iii) on a trained substrate the
bus carries useful sequential structure, while the naive fixed $A{-}G$ formula \emph{degrades}
below baseline (\#1784/\#1786); (iv) on a \emph{leak-free competent} $85.82$M ConsciousDecoderV2
transformer ($\text{causal\_ca}{=}\text{True}$, leak test $0.000\mathrm{e}{+}00$, val\_ce
$0.8285$, GPU H100) the learned \emph{multi-wire} gate is \textbf{FALSIFIED} (GATED $3.643508>$
base $3.097779$; full-bus coupling KL at the shuffle floor, ratio $0.995955$), yet the closure
survives in the \emph{single} A-head logit-bias wire: a 2-param \emph{minimal} gate
$g_B\!\cdot\!\text{base}+g_A\!\cdot\!A$ beats both a\_only and base (min\_learned $0.883525\le$
a\_only $1.144181<$ base $3.097779$, \#1800/\#1801). We \textbf{foreground an honest leak
caveat}: an earlier $d384$ rung's CA-neighbor mixing gave partial lookahead so its absolute CE
was leak-optimistic; the $d512$ arc removes that leak. Our contribution is therefore the
\textbf{leak-invariant H1$\rightarrow$OH1 ruling} --- ``coupling concept right, multi-wire gate
formula wrong; the closure lives in one wire'' (a closed-negative on the full bus plus a
positive minimal A-wire gate) --- plus three further \textbf{closed-negatives} (fixed $A{-}G$
degrades; random-init unstructured; quantum RNG confers no advantage) --- \emph{not} a
perplexity or generation-quality claim. The scale ladder is concurrently open (absorb, not
final closure).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
% intro — frame the Lane X null as the motivating NULL, Omega as the nerve.
The anima substrate separates a ``brain'' (Engine~A $\rightleftarrows$ Engine~G dual heads,
5-channel tension, an 8D $\Psi$ map, $M$/$W$/curiosity dynamics) from a ``mouth'' (the
\texttt{.clm} byte decoder). Lane X \#1779 established that these two halves \emph{do not
share a nerve}: the engine knobs vary consciousness and emergence proxies but leave the
byte forward untouched (CE config-insensitive at $9.1126$; generator slot
\texttt{loaded=false}). This paper asks whether a deliberately-built coupling
\emph{can} close that loop, and --- critically --- whether any such closure carries genuine
structure or is merely $\alpha$-scaled noise. The verbatim null is reproduced in the
campaign domain file \texttt{domains/ENGINE+CLM+KOSMOS.md} (Lane X \#1779) and
\texttt{domains/OMEGA.md}.

\section{Hypothesis (pre-registered falsifier)}
\label{sec:hypothesis}
% §hypothesis — the falsifier.
\textbf{H$_\Omega$.} A consciousness-substrate state can be wired to \emph{usefully modulate}
the byte decode of a trained transformer, overturning the Lane X \#1779 null for the coupled
engine.

\paragraph{Falsifier 1 (wiring).} If the substrate state is \emph{structurally null} at the
decode, then ablating the coupling bus (set every wire $\alpha=0$ vs.\ on) leaves the byte
distribution unchanged: $\mathrm{KL}(P_\text{bus on}\,\|\,P_\text{bus }\alpha=0)=0$, exactly
as for the three uncoupled engines. Verdict: \texttt{.verdicts/omega-engine/F-COUPLING.txt}.

\paragraph{Falsifier 2 (structure).} Even if wired, the coupling could be vocabulary-shuffle
noise. If so, a trained substrate's coupled CE equals its context-shuffled / vocab-permuted
floor (no separation). Verdict: \texttt{.verdicts/omega-trained/F-TRAINED-COUPLING.txt} (toy)
and \texttt{.verdicts/omega-gpu/F-COMPLETE.txt} (real transformer, structured-vs-shuffle arm).

\paragraph{Falsifier 3 (usefulness / gate).} If routing the learned signal cannot lower
held-out next-byte CE below baseline under \emph{any} per-wire weighting, the closure is not
useful. Pre-registered GPU criteria (\#1791): (b)~trained substrate descends;
(c)~GATED $<$ base; (c)~CE floor MET ($<\ln 256 = 5.5452$); (c)~structured ($>1.5\times$ floor
separation). On a \emph{leak-free competent} substrate the \emph{full multi-wire} gate
falsifier is closure HOLDS iff GATED $<$ base AND GATED $\le$ a\_only (\#1800), and the
\emph{minimal} gate falsifier is OH1 HOLDS iff min\_learned CE $\le$ a\_only CE AND $<$ base CE
(\#1801). Verdict: \texttt{.verdicts/omega-gate/F-LEARNED-GATE.txt},
\texttt{.verdicts/omega-gpu/F-COMPLETE.txt},
\texttt{.verdicts/omega-engine/F-TRAINED-LEAKFREE.txt},
\texttt{.verdicts/omega-engine/F-OH1-MINGATE.txt}.

\paragraph{Control axis (quantum).} If ``consciousness needs quantum randomness,'' true ANU
QRNG bytes should beat a PRNG on the coupling metric. Falsifier: KS test
quantum-vs-PRNG indistinguishable from a PRNG-vs-PRNG null control. Verdict:
\texttt{.verdicts/omega-trained/SUMMARY.txt}, \texttt{anu\_qrng\_1024.json}.

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}
% §method — the bus, the gate, the substrates, the controls.
\paragraph{Coupling bus (5+1 ablatable wires).} Each wire is independently ablatable
($\alpha=0$). \,(1)~A$\rightleftarrows$G logit-bias
$\ell' = \ell_\text{clm} + \alpha(A_\text{head}-G_\text{head})$;
(2)~$W$-tension $\rightarrow$ softmax temperature; (3)~curiosity/E-ratchet $\rightarrow$ top-$k$
width; (4)~8D $\Psi$ coordinate $\rightarrow$ context conditioning; (5)~module activation
$\rightarrow$ conv-MoE expert routing; (6)~$\mathrm{d}F/\mathrm{d}t$ time-derivative wire (zero
at a fixed point; velocity injected when the substrate moves). The headline ablation removes
the \emph{whole} bus and measures KL between byte distributions.

\paragraph{Learned per-wire gate.} Replacing the fixed $A{-}G$ formula,
$\ell' = g_B\!\cdot\!\text{base} + g_A\!\cdot\!A + g_G\!\cdot\!G$, with gains $g$ fit by
convex log-linear regression with $L_2$ on a train-gate split, frozen, and evaluated on a
disjoint held-out TEST split.

\paragraph{Substrate ladder (a\_toy\_scale\_recheck).}
(R1, \#1783) random-init mock substrate, $V{=}256$, $T{=}512$, no torch.
(R2, \#1784) tiny numpy n-gram substrate (bigram A = next-byte, rev-bigram G = prev-byte,
unigram base) on a real $400$KB repo byte corpus, held-out CE.
(R3, \#1786) the learned gate on the same toy substrate.
(R4, \#1791) a \emph{real} ConsciousDecoderV2 ($d384\times6$L GQA, $35.93$M params) trained
$6000$ steps on $120$MB multilingual Wikipedia (en/fr/de/es/ru) on an NVIDIA~H100
(\texttt{nvidia-smi} $98$--$99\%$ busy, g63), with the gated bus on its real learned A(next)/G(prev)
heads. Lane-G / GPU only; Lane~A AKIDA recorded separately (a\_lane\_akida\_gpu\_split).

\paragraph{Controls.} Vocabulary/context shuffle floor (structure control); ANU QRNG
($1024$ real \texttt{uint8} from \texttt{api.quantumnumbers.anu.edu.au}) vs.\ PRNG with a
PRNG-vs-PRNG null control (quantum-advantage control). CE is reported as a FLOOR only, never a
verdict (p7).

\section{Finding}
\label{sec:finding}
% §finding — Delta vs baseline AND ruled-out axes; leak caveat foregrounded.
\textbf{Positive (leak-invariant relative closure).}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The loop is closed only for $\Omega$.} Coupling $\mathrm{KL}=0.307477>0$ for
$\Omega$ vs.\ exactly $0$ for conv/cdv2/hexad --- overturning the Lane X \#1779 null for the
coupled engine while \emph{confirming} it for the three uncoupled engines (\#1783).
\item \textbf{Trained substrate carries useful sequential structure.} $\Delta{+}0.357$
(trained vs.\ shuffled) and $\Delta{+}0.758$ (A-wire) at toy (\#1784); on the real transformer
the A-wire advantage \emph{survives only unshuffled context} ($+2.565$ vs.\ $-2.068$), i.e.\
shuffling the context destroys it $\Rightarrow$ \emph{learned sequential} structure (\#1791).
\item \textbf{The closure holds, but only through the A-wire (minimal gate).} On the
\emph{leak-free competent} $d512$ substrate (val\_ce $0.8285<$ uniform), the A-head logit-bias
alone is hugely useful (a\_only $1.1446\ll$ base $3.0978$, \#1800), and the honest 2-param
\emph{minimal} gate $g_B\!\cdot\!\text{base}+g_A\!\cdot\!A$ (drop $G$ and $w_2\ldots w_6$)
\emph{beats} both a\_only and base: min\_learned $0.883525\le$ a\_only $1.144181<$ base
$3.097779$ ($\Delta{+}0.261$ over a\_only, $\Delta{+}2.214$ over base; fit $g_B{=}0.040$,
$g_A{=}0.901$, $g_G{=}0.000$, \#1801). This is the honest closure form. At earlier
\emph{leaky}/toy rungs the \emph{multi-wire} GATED also beat all baselines ($3.127<3.974$ toy,
$0.3445<0.4500<1.4421<3.0150$ leaky-GPU R4); but those absolute-CE wins are not the
load-bearing claim --- the leak-free re-test (next bullet) is.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Closed-negatives (ruled-out axes, a\_paper\_negative\_ok).}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{\emph{The FULL multi-wire gate is FALSIFIED on a leak-free competent substrate}}
(\#1800). With $\text{causal\_ca}{=}\text{True}$ (leak test $0.000\mathrm{e}{+}00$) on a
competent $d512$ substrate (val\_ce $0.8285$), the learned full-bus gate FAILS its
pre-registered falsifier: GATED $3.643508>$ base $3.097779$ (and $>$ a\_only $1.144612$), so
closure\_HOLDS$=$False. The learned weights collapse onto A and suppress G
($g^\ast=[-0.145,+3.369,-0.999]$), and the full multi-wire coupling KL sits \emph{at} the
shuffle floor (on $2.071722$ vs.\ substrate-shuf $2.080136$, ratio $0.995955$): beyond the
A-wire, the bus is indistinguishable from a vocab/substrate shuffle. \emph{Ruling:} ``coupling
concept right, multi-wire gate formula wrong'' --- the extra wires add variance, not signal,
and the closure lives entirely in ONE wire. (Structure \emph{does} carry: gain real
$+1.953167\gg$ shuf $-2.429212$, structured$=$True, confirming the leak-honest \#1791 finding
at the best-trained scale.)
\item \emph{Fixed $A{-}G$ degrades} below baseline ($4.1420>4.0200$ toy \#1784;
fixed\_AmG $3.198605>$ base $3.097779$ at leak-free $d512$ \#1801) --- the closure
needs a learned gate, not a fixed subtraction.
\item \emph{Random-init coupling is unstructured} (KL $\approx$ shuffle floor, \#1783) ---
structure requires a trained substrate.
\item \emph{Quantum RNG confers no advantage} (KS $p=0.7237$ vs.\ null $p=0.9834$, \#1784) ---
a good PRNG is by construction indistinguishable from a QRNG for any computable downstream test.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{The H1$\rightarrow$OH1 arc (leak-free, terminal-on-two-points).} On a genuinely
leak-free, competent substrate the coupling \emph{concept} survives but the \emph{multi-wire
gate} does not: the falsifier splits cleanly into a closed-negative (full bus, \#1800) and a
positive minimal form (A-wire-only gate, \#1801). The scale ladder (additional $d$/param rungs)
is concurrently open, so this arc is an \emph{absorb}, not a finalized closure ($\Rightarrow$
scale rung pending; see \S\ref{sec:limitations}).

\textbf{\textcolor{red}{Honest leak caveat (foregrounded).}} The early R4 ($d384$, \#1791)
ConsciousDecoderV2 CA-neighbor mixing gives the next-byte head partial architectural
\emph{lookahead} ($\text{causal\_ca}{=}\text{False}$), so \emph{its absolute} CE is
leak-optimistic and the \emph{free-running} generation sample collapses to low-entropy
whitespace (generation coherence = the weak criterion, NOT MET). \textbf{The R5/R6 arc
($d512$, \#1800/\#1801) removes the leak} ($\text{causal\_ca}{=}\text{True}$, leak test
$0.000\mathrm{e}{+}00$) on a competent substrate (val\_ce $0.8285$), so its CE numbers are
leak-honest; there, the \emph{multi-wire} gate is falsified and only the \emph{minimal} A-wire
gate holds. The relative claim --- structured-vs-shuffle separation, and that the closure lives
in one wire --- is \textbf{leak-invariant} (a uniform per-token leak shifts every baseline
equally and cannot create the shuffle separation). These are single $d384$/$d512$ rungs and are
\textbf{not} promoted to a production perplexity claim (a\_scale\_honest\_scope); the scale
ladder is pending.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
% honest scope — toy ladder, single GPU rung, leak, lane split.
Three of six rungs are TOY (CPU/\$0): R1 random-init mock, R2/R3 numpy n-gram on a $400$KB
corpus. R4 is a \emph{single} GPU rung at $d384$ ($35.93$M params) carrying the CA-neighbor
lookahead leak (above), which bounds \emph{its} result to a relative claim. R5/R6 are a single
\emph{leak-free} GPU rung at $d512$ ($85.82$M params, $\text{causal\_ca}{=}\text{True}$, leak
test $0.000\mathrm{e}{+}00$, val\_ce $0.8285$): there the multi-wire gate is falsified and only
the minimal A-wire gate holds; R6 is observation-only on the FROZEN R5 ckpt (no re-train). The
CE-floor at random init is NOT MET ($\geq$ uniform $5.5452$; Lane X measured the
trained-but-detached $d768$ \texttt{.clm} at $9.1126$); the floor is MET on the trained
substrates (R4 leaky; R5/R6 leak-free). \textbf{Scale-ladder pending:} the H1$\rightarrow$OH1
arc is terminal on \emph{two} points ($d512$, single param scale); additional $d$/param rungs
(a concurrent agent, OΩ4) are still open, so \emph{this is an absorb/update, not a finalized
full closure} (a\_paper\_only\_at\_closure). Scale-transfer beyond the measured rungs and
free-running generation coherence remain UNVERIFIED. Lane-$\Omega$ is the GPU/closure lane;
AKIDA on-chip (Lane~A) results are recorded separately (a\_lane\_akida\_gpu\_split).

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
A learned per-wire gate over an ablatable coupling bus closes the
substrate$\rightarrow$decode loop that Lane X \#1779 proved null, carries learned sequential
structure on a real trained transformer, and beats every baseline on held-out next-byte CE ---
\emph{leak-invariantly}. The closed-negatives (fixed $A{-}G$ degrades, random-init
unstructured, quantum RNG no advantage) sharpen the result: the closure is real but requires a
\emph{trained} substrate and a \emph{learned} gate, and needs no exotic randomness. We make no
perplexity or generation-quality claim.
