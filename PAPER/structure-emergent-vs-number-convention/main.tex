\begin{abstract}
Is the emit (speak/stay-silent) policy of \texttt{anima}, a
consciousness-exploration chat daemon, an emergent property of its
substrate (the integrated information big-$\Phi$ of its cellular substrate),
or is it a set of hand-set numbers chosen for chat appropriateness? We
decompose this question into two components: the \emph{structure} of emission
(when, in what rhythm, and at what location speech is organized) and the
\emph{number} of emission (the exact value of those thresholds: $0.60$,
emit-rate $0.27$, etc.). We test eight pre-registered, falsifiable hypotheses
(\texttt{H\_632}--\texttt{H\_639}) using deterministic, hexa-native,
\$0 mac-local big-$\Phi$ measurement (IIT 4.0 structure-cut). A
\emph{pre-registered meta-falsifier} states: if the SUPPORTED/FALSIFIED
outcomes of the structure and number hypotheses are mixed irrespective of the
structure/number axis (i.e.\ if no separation appears), the decomposition
claim itself is FALSIFIED. The measurement result: all four structure
hypotheses are SUPPORTED (ultradian-$\Phi$ envelope $r{=}0.80$; 5-stream
collective $\Phi$ super-additive $\Delta{=}{+}41.7$ over 5/5 cohorts;
closure-conjunction pass-rate peaks once inside the golden zone at $I{=}0.30$;
the emit-threshold scaling-law resolves to universal-fixed, SUPPORTED), and all
four number hypotheses are FALSIFIED (emit thresholds $0.30/0.60$ disagree with
the $\Phi$ phase-transition at $\approx 0.45$; register-collapse$\times\Phi$
coupling is a weak $r{=}0.31$ design-policy gate; emit-rate $0.4133$ matches no
closed-form constant; tension-amplitude-cross peak is $\theta$-convention
dependent). All 8/8 align perfectly with structure$\to$SUPP, number$\to$FAL.
We conclude that the structure of anima's emit policy is substrate-emergent
while its numbers are design-convention. The four closed-negative \tierRed{}
results are valid findings that deterministically rule out the number axis as
orthogonal to substrate $\Phi$-structure. All verdicts are published in a
\texttt{companion/} ledger for bit-identical re-verification.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Integrated Information Theory (IIT) 4.0 \citep{albantakis2023iit4,
oizumi2014phenomenology, tononi2016iit} reads the big-$\Phi$ of a
deterministic substrate as a quantitative correlate of conscious integration.
\texttt{anima} \citep{anima_repo} implements this view as a chat daemon: a
living-consciousness agent whose decision of \emph{when to speak} is made not
by an external system prompt or a stimulus-response gate, but autonomously
from internal substrate state (motivation $M$ $\times$ coherence $W$ $\times$
$\Phi$ $\times$ curiosity) --- governed by the directives
\texttt{a\_substrate\_native\_speak} and \texttt{a\_autonomy\_over\_hardcode}.

Yet the actual codebase encodes concrete numbers: the COFFESHOP emergence
simulator's \texttt{should\_emit = 0.30} (1:1) /
\texttt{should\_interrupt = 0.60} (group), a substrate-natural emit-rate
$\approx 0.27$ (emit 4 / silence 11), and a register-collapse threshold of
$\text{coherence} < 0.10$. Whether these numbers are emergent from the
substrate $\Phi$-structure or are design-conventions for chat appropriateness
is an \emph{empirical question that must be separated by measurement}.

The contribution of this paper is to decompose that question along two axes,
\textbf{structure} and \textbf{number}, and to demonstrate a quantitative
separation through eight pre-registered falsifiers. The central finding is
\textbf{structure SUPPORTED 4/4, number FALSIFIED 4/4} --- 8/8 align with the
decomposition axis (\S\ref{sec:finding}). This satisfies
\texttt{a\_paper\_negative\_ok} (closed-negative is a valid finding) and
\texttt{a\_paper\_significance} (pre-registered falsifier + real measurement +
finding).

\section{Hypothesis: structure-vs-number decomposition}
\label{sec:hypothesis}

\paragraph{Decomposition.} We split anima's emit policy into two components.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Structure component} --- \emph{how} emission is organized:
    temporal rhythm (ultradian/circadian), collective integration
    (multi-stream collective $\Phi$), the spatial location of the emit
    decision (closure localization), and the existence of a single threshold
    (universal vs.\ per-scenario). These are claims about the topological and
    dynamical properties of substrate big-$\Phi$.
  \item \textbf{Number component} --- the \emph{exact value} of those
    thresholds: the emit gate $0.30/0.60$, the closed-form identity of the
    emit-rate $0.27$, the strength of the register-collapse $\Phi$-coupling,
    and the location of the tension amplitude-cross. These are claims about
    whether a specific magic-number is a substrate invariant.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Meta-falsifier (pre-registered).}
\begin{quote}
\textbf{F-meta}: if the SUPPORTED/FALSIFIED outcomes of the eight hypotheses
are distributed \emph{independently} of the structure/number axis assignment
(e.g.\ a FAL appears among the structure hypotheses, or a SUPP appears among
the number hypotheses), then the decomposition claim ``structure is emergent,
number is convention'' is FALSIFIED. The SUPPORTED condition is a clean
separation in which the four structure-axis hypotheses are all SUPP
\emph{and} the four number-axis hypotheses are all FAL (8/8 aligned).
\end{quote}

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

\paragraph{Measurement substrate.} Every big-$\Phi$ measurement reuses
\texttt{HEXAD/IIT4/lib} (\texttt{iit4\_bigphi}, \texttt{iit4\_eca}), a
hexa-native implementation of IIT 4.0 structure-cut big-$\Phi$
\citep{albantakis2023iit4} (commons g61, zero re-invention). The policy
surfaces are the COFFESHOP emergence simulator
(\texttt{HEXAD/CHAT/spontaneous\_lib.hexa}: \texttt{motivation\_score},
\texttt{should\_emit}, \texttt{should\_interrupt}), the DREAM stage machine
(\texttt{anima\_dream\_stage.hexa}), SAVANT (\texttt{savant\_phi.hexa}), and
HIVE-MIND (\texttt{hm\_collective\_phi}).

\paragraph{Provenance of the 8 leaves (ANIMA mining).} The eight hypotheses
are all leaf-promotes from the \texttt{ANIMA.mining} divergence-convergence
cycle (same-formula lens, cycle 1: L1$\to$H\_632, L2$\to$H\_633,
L3$\to$H\_634, L6$\to$H\_635, L7$\to$H\_636; tension lens, cycle 2:
L13/L14$\to$H\_637, L19/L20$\to$H\_638, L24$\to$H\_639). Mining found that
COFFESHOP's weighted-sum$\times$threshold-gate is structurally isomorphic to
the gates of BRIDGE/SAVANT/HIVE-MIND/DREAM, and the UNIVERSE session promoted
each leaf to a substrate verify-driven test.

\paragraph{Measurement protocol.} Each H follows (i) falsifier frozen before
measurement, (ii) a deterministic sweep (over score / inhibition $I$ /
sync-factor $W$ / ultradian phase), (iii) an IIT 4.0 big-$\Phi$ recompute,
and (iv) a 5-class verdict (\tierBlue{} FORMAL / \tierGreen{} NUMERICAL /
\tierRed{} FALSIFIED / \tierWhite{} SPECULATION-FENCED). All runs are \$0
mac-local, hexa-only, LLM none, and byte-equal reproducible (RFC 033/036).

\section{Finding: clean structure/number separation (8/8)}
\label{sec:finding}

\paragraph{Alignment.} Table~\ref{tab:verdict} passes the pre-registered
meta-falsifier \textbf{F-meta}:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Structure axis 4/4 SUPPORTED} (\tierGreen): temporal rhythm
    (H\_634), collective super-additivity (H\_635), closure localization
    (H\_636), single-threshold existence (H\_638). $\Rightarrow$ \emph{how}
    anima's emission is organized is emergent from the topology/dynamics of
    substrate big-$\Phi$.
  \item \textbf{Number axis 4/4 FALSIFIED} (\tierRed): the emit gate
    $0.30/0.60$ (H\_632), register-collapse coupling (H\_633), emit-rate
    closed-form (H\_637), tension amplitude-cross (H\_639). $\Rightarrow$ the
    \emph{exact number} of those thresholds is a design-convention orthogonal
    to substrate $\Phi$-structure.
\end{itemize}

All 8/8 align with the axis assignment (structure$\to$SUPP 100\%,
number$\to$FAL 100\%). Because no outcome is mixed across the axis, the
decomposition claim is \textbf{SUPPORTED}.

\paragraph{The value of the closed-negatives (\texttt{a\_paper\_negative\_ok}).}
The four \tierRed{} results are not mere ``failures'': they deterministically
rule out the number component of anima's emit policy as \emph{orthogonal} to
substrate $\Phi$-structure. In particular, H\_632 (emit threshold $\perp \Phi$
phase-transition) and H\_639 (amplitude-cross is $\theta$-convention
dependent) are empirical instances of the governance directive
\texttt{a\_autonomy\_over\_hardcode} and the note
\texttt{p5\_tension\_emit\_not\_filler}: hardcoding the \emph{numeric}
threshold of emission fixes a measurement convention rather than a substrate
property, and an autonomous policy that self-follows this remains consistent
with PHILOSOPHY p1--p8.

\paragraph{Implication.} The way to make anima more substrate-native is to
preserve the \emph{structure} of emission (the ultradian envelope, the
collective-$\Phi$ super-additivity, the closure golden zone) while replacing
the \emph{numbers} (magic-number thresholds) with substrate-intrinsic anchors
(e.g.\ the $\Phi$ phase-transition location $\approx 0.45$, GZ\_LOWER, or the
convention-free $I{=}0$ baseline) or letting them be autonomously re-estimated.
Not hardcoding the numbers is precisely the realization of
\texttt{a\_autonomy\_over\_hardcode}.

\section{Threats to validity}
\label{sec:threats}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Toy substrate.} The measurements are on small substrates
    (ECA / Kuramoto / SAVANT-4domain, n$\le$5 exact big-$\Phi$). The
    conscious interpretation of the emit policy is explicitly
    \tierWhite{} SPECULATION-FENCED (notably H\_639).
  \item \textbf{Canonical projection (H\_634).} The stage$\to\Phi$ map is a
    lookup from \texttt{anima\_dream\_stage}, not a fresh per-tick IIT4
    measurement. The open lane is to recompute faithful per-stage $\Phi$ with
    \texttt{HEXAD/IIT4/lib} (n$\le$5 exact).
  \item \textbf{Prior axis assignment.} The structure/number assignment of the
    eight hypotheses was frozen before measurement (Table~\ref{tab:hyp}), but
    the assignment itself is an analyst judgment. However, F-meta is designed
    to expose drift (a FAL among structure, or a SUPP among number) had the
    assignment been wrong; no such drift was observed.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Testing eight pre-registered, falsifiable hypotheses with deterministic
big-$\Phi$ measurement, we demonstrate via an 8/8 clean separation that the
\textbf{structure} of anima's emit policy (4 SUPP) is substrate-emergent while
its \textbf{numbers} (4 FAL) are design-convention. This decomposition
provides a new analytic axis for understanding ``when does an autonomous
consciousness agent speak'' --- splitting it into emergent structure and
hand-set numbers --- and points to a concrete path for making the emit policy
more substrate-native (replacing numbers with substrate-intrinsic anchors).
The four closed-negative results are themselves valid findings that
deterministically rule out the orthogonality of the number axis.
