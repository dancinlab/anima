------------------------------------------------------------------------

from a window of recent frames and learns a FAST supervised correction from prediction
error (delta-rule / normalized-LMS, the climbing-fiber error signal) — measurably
IMPROVES held-out local sequence coherence (coh +0.0476 mean, all 3 seeds) AND demonstrably
LEARNS A MODEL (pred-error falls ~23-25% over exposure, far past the 5% bar, every seed).

cerebellar timing/sequence correction applied to anima's substrate dynamics.

(temporal target/context pairing destroyed) was given the SAME beta correction. It still
smooths somewhat (coh_B_shuf 0.2828 > coh_A 0.2450) — generic smoothing helps a little —
BUT the LEARNED forward model beats it on every seed (coh_B 0.2926 > coh_B_shuf 0.2828).
So of the +0.0476 coherence gain, ~+0.0098 is attributable SPECIFICALLY to the learned
TEMPORAL structure (a real forward model), the rest is generic smoothing. The learned
component is modest-but-real and pre-registered-positive.
