F-DFDT-TIME — the #1763 d/dt time-encoding test on REAL dolphin FM contours
verdict: HOLDS
=============================================================================
TEST (#1763 analogue on natural data): a dolphin whistle is a time-ORDERED
frequency contour F0(t). Its |dF0/dt| is the frame-to-frame FM slope. We
TIME-SCRAMBLE the F0 contour (permute frame order) so the SET of frequencies
is preserved but the TEMPORAL ORDER (the FM trajectory) is destroyed, then
recompute |dF0/dt|. A smooth real FM sweep has small adjacent steps; random
frame order makes large jumps. If temporal order carries real d/dt structure,
scrambling INFLATES |dF0/dt|. We also require the dF/dt-aware fingerprint to
separate species better than a static-only (centroid/bandwidth) spectrum.
