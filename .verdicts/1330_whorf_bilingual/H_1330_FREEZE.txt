THE ONLY NEW THING — SEQUENTIAL A→B TRAINING ON ONE SUBSTRATE:
A single VoronoiCells store is fit on A's labels (cells pack at p_A), THEN — WITHOUT resetting
the store, WITHOUT evicting any A-cell — fit AGAIN on B's labels. The second fit ADDS cells at
p_B where B-label error concentrates (error-targeted SPLIT-only growth, p8). HOW THE SUBSTRATE
GROWS CELLS FOR B RATHER THAN OVERWRITING A (the H_1288 growth-memory mechanism under test):
the second fit re-enters the SAME error-targeted-split loop on the EXISTING proto/label table —
it only APPENDS a new prototype at the worst B-misclassified stimulus each pass (self.protos.append),
and a cell's bound label is FIXED at creation and is NEVER re-bound or deleted. So A's cells at
p_A persist with their A-labels while NEW B-cells accrete at p_B. This is exactly the H_1288
grow-not-evict immune-store behavior, not a fresh net. (A small fit-extension is needed: the
existing fit() seeds a fresh single centroid cell; the sequential B-fit must instead CONTINUE

error-targeted-split loop starting from the current table. This is the H_1199-style engine/
mechanism extension to express the learning, a_engine_native_learning, NOT a method change to

THE BILINGUAL READOUT: after A→B, build TWO discrimination curves on the SAME final store:
one for the A-label posterior (cells' A-labels) and one for the B-label posterior — NO, that is
NOT how a single store works. Correct single-substrate readout: the store after A→B holds cells
with bound labels from BOTH fits. The discrimination curve = |Δ soft posterior| over the cells'
bound labels (the SAME readout as H_1323; a cell bound during the A-fit carries its A-label, a
cell bound during the B-fit carries its B-label). CP peaks appear wherever the bound-label
posterior swings sharply = wherever cells of opposite label pack densely = at BOTH p_A and p_B
if both carvings survive. We measure the peak-count / peak-locations of this ONE curve and ask
whether coherent CP peaks sit at BOTH p_A and p_B (COEXIST) or only at p_B (OVERWRITE) or a
single merged intermediate peak (BLEND). To score "CP present at boundary p" we reuse the
H_1323 W1 cross-within margin LOCALIZED to each boundary (near = |mid−p|≤0.12, far = |mid−p|≥0.25).
