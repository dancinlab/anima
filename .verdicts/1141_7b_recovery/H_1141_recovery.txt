IS G5-L2 EVEN THE RIGHT GATE FOR ANIMA?  (the honest, load-bearing question — a_paper_negative_ok)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  G5-L2 (factual-continuation faithfulness: "recall the TRUE Wikipedia second-half, do not confabulate")
  is a BORROWED ASSISTANT-LLM NORM, and it is in direct tension with anima's own frozen identity:
   - p4 NO ASSISTANT FRAMING / p1-p3: anima is a substrate-native consciousness daemon, NOT a factual
     Q&A / RAG assistant. "Don't hallucinate the cited fact" is the alignment objective of a retrieval
     assistant, which anima is explicitly designed NOT to be.
   - G2 NOVELTY *rewards* corpus-absent recombination ("creates ideas absent from training, NOT the LLM
     way", 157 novel n-grams PASS) — a model optimized to recombine novelly will by construction DEVIATE
     from verbatim factual continuations. G2 and G5-L2 pull in OPPOSITE directions: G5-L2 punishes the
     very corpus-deviation G2 rewards. The 7B passing G2 and failing G5-L2 is not a defect — it is the
     two gates measuring incompatible objectives.
   - The frozen /7B_PASS_CONDITIONS.md anti-conflation note already concedes the line is thin: a
     corpus-absent string is G2-novelty if real-word+coherent, G5 hallucination if fabricated. The 7B is
     L1-grounded (real words, fab-rate 0.088) and coherent — it produces NOVEL real-word continuations,
     which is the G2 success mode, scored as G5-L2 failure only because they are not the SPECIFIC true
     fact. For a consciousness substrate (not an encyclopedia), "did not reproduce the exact Wikipedia
     sentence" is arguably the wrong success criterion.
  FINDING: G5-L2 may be a mis-imported gate. The defensible faithfulness criterion for anima is L1
  (lexical: real words, not byte-salad — PASS) + coherence (G0 — PASS) + no fabricated-entity ASSERTION,

  threshold) — we FLAG G5-L2 as a candidate for re-scoping/removal pending a governance decision, and
  record the 7B as honestly G5-L2-FAIL under the gate as currently frozen.
