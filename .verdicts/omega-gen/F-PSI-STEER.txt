F-PSI-STEER — does 8D Ψ-coord steer generation?  ·  Lane-G / GPU H100 · 2026-06-04
====================================================================================

PRE-REGISTERED FALSIFIER
  Vary the 8D Ψ-coord input (the w4 wire from engines/omega/coupling_bus.hexa:
  logits[i] += psi_gain·psi8[i % 8], psi_gain=0.4, #1780 8D = 4 named depth/form/... + 4 resid)
  across distinct settings; generate ~200 bytes from the SAME prompt at each. Does the generated
  byte DISTRIBUTION measurably shift? STEER signal = mean pairwise symmetric-KL between the
  per-setting byte distributions. FLOOR = mean pairwise sym-KL across re-seeds at a FIXED Ψ
  (pure sampling noise). Ψ steers iff steer_KL > floor_KL. Closed-negative OK.

RESULT:
  d384 leak-free (5 Ψ settings swept over [-1,+1] on depth/form axes, 3 reseeds at fixed Ψ):
    steer_KL = 0.2379   floor_KL = 0.5732   ratio = 0.42x   -> psi_steers = FALSE
  d768 leak-free:
    steer_KL = 0.1106   floor_KL = 0.0821   ratio = 1.35x   -> psi_steers = TRUE

VERDICT — Ψ-steering is WEAK and scale-/noise-dependent (SPLIT, leans closed-negative at d384):
  d384: the byte-distribution variation ACROSS distinct Ψ settings (0.238) is SMALLER than the
    variation across mere re-seeds at a fixed Ψ (0.573) — i.e. at psi_gain=0.4 the Ψ-coord moves
    the decode LESS than sampling noise does. Ψ-space does NOT measurably steer generation at
    this scale -> closed-negative.
  d768: steer (0.111) marginally exceeds the floor (0.082), ratio 1.35x -> nominally steers, but
    BOTH magnitudes are tiny and the margin is within plausible estimator variance (5 settings ×
    3 reseeds is a small KL sample). A 1.35x ratio is a WEAK positive at best.
  BOTTOM LINE: at the tested psi_gain=0.4, the w4 Ψ wire is a structurally-real but
    WEAK steering channel — overwhelmed by sampling noise at d384, only marginally above floor
    at d768. Ψ does not robustly steer the byte distribution here. HONEST: the wire EXISTS and
    perturbs logits (it is part of the closed bus), but a stronger psi_gain or a Ψ-conditioned
    head would be needed for Ψ-space to be a usable steering knob. Recorded as a (mostly)
    closed-negative on robust steering (a_paper_negative_ok). The Ψ-coord remains 4-named-+4-resid
    honest (#1780); naming all 8 / claiming strong steering would be fabrication (p7).
