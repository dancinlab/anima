THE GENUINELY-NEW ANGLE (R5) — DENSER distributed coupling, not a re-run
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
R3/R4's re-entrant relay was SPARSE: each module reciprocally coupled to its OWN
private relay channel only (a topographic 1:1 thalamo-cortical loop, N_MOD distinct
edges). The seed-8 failure mode R4 named is precisely a SPARSITY/structure
shortage: with too few reciprocal edges, a near-orthogonal substrate gives the MIP
a cheap cut to find. The distributed-multi-edge diagnosis taken to its conclusion
predicts the cure is DENSITY: a COMPLETE recurrent coupling graph — every relay
channel reciprocally and recurrently coupled to EVERY other channel via the relay
stage — so that EVERY bipartition crosses O(N^2) recurrent loops and NO single cut
is cheap, EVEN when the modules start near-orthogonal (seed 8).

This is a DIFFERENT mechanism, NOT the R3/R4 loop re-run:
  R3/R4 'reentrant'  : ring + per-module 1:1 relay, relay cross-coupled only to
                       ring NEIGHBOURS (sparse, mean over ring nbrs)
  R5    'dense'      : ring + per-module relay, relay cross-coupled to ALL OTHER
                       relay channels (complete intra-thalamic graph, mean over
                       ALL j≠i — N(N-1) directed reciprocal edges at the relay)
The ONLY structural change vs R3/R4: the intra-thalamic relay cross-coupling goes
from RING-NEIGHBOUR (mean over ring[i], 2 of 3 others) to ALL-PAIRS (mean over all
j≠i, the complete graph). This directly attacks the seed-8 mechanism R4 isolated:
density supplies the shared structure that orthogonal init lacks, distributed
across a complete edge set the MIP cannot cut cheaply along any one channel.

GEOMETRY-CONDITIONED (per R4's mandate): the hypothesis is explicitly that DENSITY
clears the bar on the NEAR-ORTHOGONAL substrate (seed 8) where the SPARSE relay
failed — so the gate below requires per-seed clearance on ALL THREE seeds
INCLUDING seed 8, the one R4 missed. This is the falsifier: if dense coupling
ALSO misses seed 8, the wall is confirmed beyond the relay-density axis.
