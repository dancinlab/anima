INTERPRETATION

  to a SUSTAINED every-checkpoint guarantee and, more strongly, neutralizes H_872's single
  non-monotone failure depth when that depth is traversed transiently. The deployment freeze

  losing the RETAIN∧GAIN band — provided the H_865 adapter edge is present. @L1 honored:
  boundary moves are requires_grad flips + optimizer rebuild over the new trainable set with
  learned values retained — edge-only piecewise, no deterministic full-retrain (H_679).
