THE MEASURE — O-INFORMATION Ω(X) (Rosas, Mediano, Rassouli, Barrett 2019)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
For an n-variate system X = (X_1,...,X_n):
   Ω(X) = TC(X) − DTC(X)
where
   TC(X)  = Σ_i H(X_i) − H(X)                         (total correlation)
   DTC(X) = H(X) − Σ_i H(X_i | X_{-i})                (dual total correlation)
          = H(X) − Σ_i [ H(X) − H(X_{-i}) ]
          = Σ_i H(X_{-i}) − (n−1) H(X)
Equivalent closed form used by the probe (exact, plug-in):
   Ω(X) = (n−2) H(X) + Σ_i [ H(X_i) − H(X_{-i}) ]
SIGN CONVENTION (Rosas 2019): Ω < 0 ⇒ SYNERGY-dominated (the whole carries info absent in
parts) ; Ω > 0 ⇒ REDUNDANCY-dominated (parts share info) ; Ω = 0 ⇒ balanced.

joint-entropy synergy/redundancy sign over the 4 module marginals + joint. n=4 → entropies
computed EXACTLY by empirical plug-in over the 2^4=16 joint binary states (T=64 ticks).
Binarization = variance-free median split (H_1328 lesson, BYTE-IDENTICAL to H_1348): unit i

RELATIONSHIP, not the amplitude-variance confound.
