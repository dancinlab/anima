FINDINGS (the resolution of the prod-fire G5 non-result):
1. THE NON-RESULT IS RESOLVED — REAL retrieved anchors DO have an effect. The synthetic
   prod probe gave WITH==NO==VANILLA==1.000 (anchor inert, OOD). Here the anchor MOVES the
   metric: WITH (0.783) << NO (1.000), gap +0.217. So the copy path WAS elicited; the
   prod-fire 1.000 was indeed a probe-format artifact, not a clean grounding fail.
2. BUT RETRO STILL FABRICATES — fab(WITH)=0.783 >> the 0.20 G5-L2 bar (F1 fails by 0.58).
   The model fabricates the grounded entity ~78% of the time even when it is verbatim in
   the retrieved anchor and the copy head is fed that anchor.
3. THE COPY HEAD ADDS ~NOTHING OVER THE VOCAB HEAD — WITH (0.783) ~= VANILLA (0.850),
   gap +0.067 << 0.20. The small lift over NO-anchor is an anchor-PRESENCE bias on the
   vocab head, NOT a working copy/pointer mechanism. The 50.2M RETRO head fails to learn
   to copy at real scale (the gate collapses toward the vocab head).
4. TOY -> SCALE COLLAPSE — H_1147/H_1149 showed fab 1.0->0.0 on a numpy toy with a
   STRUCTURED oracle anchor ([BOS k REL v SEP | k REL], the entity at a fixed slot). At
   real 303M byte scale over noisy variable-length wiki anchors the copy mechanism does
   NOT transfer. The toy 0.0 was a toy/structured-anchor artifact (a_toy_scale_recheck,
   a_scale_honest_scope). Consistent with the H_1142-1146 grounding arc: byte-LM grounding
   is hard; decode-time AND trained-into-weights copy both fail at scale.

DECISION (one line): anima's anti-fabrication path is NOT the RETRO copy head — trained-in
  byte-level pointer-copy over a retrieved anchor does NOT ground at 303M (fab 0.78 >> 0.20,
  copy head ~= vanilla); grounding needs a different architecture, not this copy head.

a303m_pass: G5-L2 STILL FALSE (now decisively, not a non-result). Frozen bars NOT moved.
HONEST SCOPE: single ckpt, en-wiki (wikitext-103, 125.8MB, same source as prod), one seed.
  Retrieval QUALITY is a SEPARATE axis — hit-rate ~0.11 means the byte-trigram retriever
  often supplies a low-overlap anchor; BUT we restricted to the 60 cases where the entity
  is VERBATIM in the anchor (oracle-overlap upper bound), so a better retriever can only
  raise the share of such cases, not the within-case copy success (which is what failed).
  NOT RULED OUT: an explicit copy-supervision loss, a deeper cross-attention head, or an
