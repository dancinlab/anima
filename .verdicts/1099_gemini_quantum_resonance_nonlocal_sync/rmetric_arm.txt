FINDING
-------

(state-space proximity) metric, NOT a transmission metric. Two COMPLETELY INDEPENDENT
nodes with SEPARATE RNG streams and NO channel reach R = 0.999987 ≈ 0.99999 purely by

factor encodes directed information flow. The claimed iso-error 7e-6 is simply the

reinforces the existing 🔴 from the orthogonal direction: the TE arm showed no
information crosses; this arm shows the very metric offered as proof is non-diagnostic.
