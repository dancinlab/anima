   "capacity is capacity"). The salience rule merely RE-DISTRIBUTES which facts win,
   it does not raise the total — and the redistribution it achieves is the same one
   recurrence already achieves without any tag.

⇒ The amygdala-as-eviction-lever is FALSIFIED at this scale: a substrate-derived
salience tag added to the immune store's eviction priority does not protect
