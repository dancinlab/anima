THE QUESTION
  As feature-space dimension D grows with a CONSTANT Monte-Carlo sample size N, does the move-the-cells
  RELOCATION (drift residual phase-1 cells along the boundary NORMAL toward a shifted (D−1)-dim
  hyperplane cat=⟨w,x⟩>c) survive? At which D, if any, does it break? Constant-N in growing-D is the
  curse-of-dimensionality stressor — that IS the point.

THE MOVE (only D is varied; w orientation HELD CONSTANT across D, per-seed fixed unit normal)
  • TRUE partition: cat = int(⟨w,x⟩ > c), w = fixed per-seed unit normal in R^D, x ∈ [0,1]^D.
  • boundary shift along the normal: c_A = m·(1/3) → c_A' = m·(2/3), where m = ⟨w, 1·(1,..,1)⟩ scale
    chosen so the cut sits at the 1/3 and 2/3 quantile of the projection ⟨w,x⟩ over [0,1]^D (so both
    phases see a balanced split regardless of D). c_A, c_A' computed from the projection quantiles of
    a fixed reference sample (frozen, NOT from the scored sample) — stated honestly.
  • move-the-cells: drift residual phase-1 prototype cells' SOURCE position along +w toward the moved
    hyperplane (project source onto w, drift the projection s_i toward c_A', re-embed, re-read label).
    eta = 0.15 FROZEN (same as H_1369). v-axes (orthogonal-to-normal) are the irrelevant axes — not
    drifted, not scored (a coherent ridge legitimately spans the orthogonal complement).

SAMPLING (honest — full lattice infeasible past D≈4)
  • CONSTANT N = 169 Monte-Carlo sample points per D, drawn uniform in [0,1]^D (matches H_1369's 13×13
    =169 grid cardinality so the sample budget is identical across the ladder — the stressor). Same
    sample for all arms within a (D,seed). RBF basis = K_CTR=64 centers drawn uniform in [0,1]^D
    (DIM=64 fixed across D, as in H_1369). 3 seeds [4333,4334,4335].
  • DIRECTIONAL: $0 CPU numpy mirror, gradient-free. engine-transfer UNVERIFIED.

METRICS (per D)
  • RELOCATION = |ridge_s − c_A'| where ridge_s = the projection-onto-w centroid of the discrimination
    ridge (the N-D analog of H_1369 peak_u; measured ALONG THE NORMAL).
  • COH_D (bounded, generalizes COH2D) = S_CONC · (1 − RIDGE_FRAC), where:
       RIDGE_FRAC = (#ridge nodes)/N,
       S_CONC     = 1 − min(1, s_std / S_STD_REF), s_std = std of the ridge nodes' projection s=⟨w,x⟩.
    High COH_D = a THIN ridge concentrated at one projection value (a single coherent hyperplane);
    low = a sample-filling smear. Only the NORMAL-projection spread is scored (the orthogonal complement
    spread is NOT scored — same principle as COH2D not scoring v-spread). Bounded in [0,1] → no NCOMP
    saturation (the H_1369 R1 lesson carried in by design).
  Discrimination FIELD: D(node) = max |Δ posterior| to its k_NN=4 NEAREST sample neighbors (the N-D
    analog of H_1369's 4-grid-neighbor field; kNN because a lattice has no neighbors off-grid).
  ridge mask = D ≥ 0.5·D.max() (same 0.5-of-peak rule as H_1369).

ARMS (per D, per seed)
  RE-PACK   : eta=0.15, phase-2 trained on moved labels (the move-the-cells lever).
  SPLIT-ONLY: eta=0.00, phase-2 trained on moved labels (H_1364 ablation — isolates the geometric drift).
  NO-RETRAIN: eta=0.00, NO phase-2 (holds the OLD boundary c_A).
  SHUFFLE   : eta=0.15, phase-2 on permuted random labels (anti-Goodhart — must NOT fabricate a ridge).

FROZEN BARS (per D; verbatim mirror of H_1369 R2; NO threshold moved)
  LOC_TOL    = 0.12     (RELOCATION reach tolerance, along the normal; = H_1360/H_1369)
  COH_MIN    = 0.50     (COH_D presence)
  COH_SEP    = 0.10     (COH_D earned-vs-split separation)
  SHUF_COH_MAX = 0.20   (shuffle COH_D must collapse)
  S_STD_REF  = 0.20     (projection-spread reference, frozen = H_1369 U_STD_REF)

  Per-D pass iff ALL of:
    (1) RELOCATION : |ridge_s − c_A'| ≤ LOC_TOL for all 3 seeds.
    (2) COH_D      : mean COH_D ≥ COH_MIN  AND  mean COH_D ≥ split-only mean COH_D + COH_SEP.
    (3) EARNED     : (3a) no-retrain holds c_A: |ridge_s − c_A| ≤ LOC_TOL all 3 seeds
                     AND (3b) shuffle mean COH_D ≤ SHUF_COH_MAX.
    (4) DISTINCT-FROM-SPLIT : split-only stays SHORT — mean |ridge_s − c_A'| > LOC_TOL
                     (the geometric drift, not the re-growth, lands the ridge).
