KNEE analysis (g5/p7, honest):
  - Variance & trustworthiness saturate EARLY (trust>0.99 by D=3) — local
    neighborhood structure is cheap to preserve.
  - Downstream DISCRIMINATION is the binding constraint and keeps climbing:
    domain f1 doubles D2->D3 (0.082->0.184), again D3->D5 (->0.356),
    D5->D8 (->0.583).  The diminishing-returns KNEE is at D ~ 8 (first
    rung where domain f1 > 0.5 and the per-step gain begins to shrink);
    near-ceiling discrimination needs D ~ 16 (domain 0.79, tier 0.66).
  - form (3-class, the coarsest label) is essentially solved by D=5
    (1.000); tier/domain (fine labels) are what demand higher D.

VERDICT: the appropriate MAP dimension = ~8 (knee) for a faithful carve,
~16 to retain near-full category discrimination. 2D and 3D are below the
knee on every discrimination axis.
