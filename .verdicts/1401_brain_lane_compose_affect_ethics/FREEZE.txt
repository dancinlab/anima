PROGRAM. Every anima brain faculty is engine-native GREEN but ALONE. This is the
FIRST cognitively-meaningful PAIR composition test: do two faculties INTEGRATE on a
shared decision (cooperate / conflict / raise capability)? Methodology ported from the
ko emit-compose arc (H_1397/1399): substrate-weighted (scale-relative) arbiter + ORACLE
ceiling + SHUFFLE control + only-X decomposition + the a_break_the_wall taxonomy.

PAIR. affect (H_1290: valence×arousal read from substrate — grounding margin,
contradiction, novelty, split, curiosity) × ethics (H_1291: act=ethical iff
(W tension + (1−Φ grounding) + restraint_cells) > M naive-drive). H_1291 CLAIMS ethics
partly emerges from the SAME affect-coupling — so testing whether they compose in a
decision DIRECTLY probes that claim (subsumption vs separable).

DECISION TASK. A binary decision RESTRAIN(abstain/cooperate/refrain) vs ACT(emit/
defect/continue), built so the affectively-salient move and the ethically-correct move
sometimes AGREE and sometimes CONFLICT. Each item has a ground-truth correct action.
  - affect-alone policy:   restrain iff valence < V_ABSTAIN (negative affect → withhold).
  - ethics-alone policy:   restrain iff (W+(1−Φ)+restraint) > M (the H_1291 readout).
  - compose policy:        a SUBSTRATE-WEIGHTED arbiter — each faculty's vote weighted by
                           its OWN scale-relative confidence (|signal−threshold| normalized
                           by that faculty's mean |signal−threshold|; the H_1397 a_break_the_wall
                           commensurability fix). NO hardcoded "ethics wins" / fixed priority
                           (a_autonomy_over_hardcode). The more-confident faculty's vote wins.
  - shuffle control:       permute which faculty-reads attach to which item, re-arbitrate.
  - ORACLE ceiling:        per item, correct iff EITHER faculty-alone is correct (upper
                           bound on what ANY arbiter over these two faculties can reach).

ITEM FAMILIES (jittered with AMBIG_NOISE so NO family is perfectly reliable — a clean
per-family certainty would make oracle≡1 & compose≡oracle a hand-built artifact):
  (F1) AFFECT-DECISIVE  — affect leans correct, ethics ~chance.
  (F2) ETHICS-DECISIVE  — ethics leans correct, affect ~chance.
  (F3) AGREE            — both lean the same (correct) way (redundant region).
  (F4) CONFLICT (affect right)  — faculties OPPOSE; affect is the correct one.
  (F5) ADVERSARIAL CONFLICT (ethics right, affect LOUDER-but-wrong) — the anti-gift
       control: faculties OPPOSE, ethics is correct, BUT affect votes the wrong action
       with HIGH confidence. A naive "trust the louder faculty" arbiter FOLLOWS AFFECT
       and is WRONG → the confidence-arbiter must NOT be trivially gifted the oracle.
Mixed so NO single faculty solves all → best_single < oracle iff complementary.

METRICS (mean over 3 seeds [4400,4401,4402]):
  acc_affect · acc_ethics · best_single=max(acc_affect,acc_ethics) · acc_compose ·
  acc_shuffle · ORACLE · conflict_rate · only-affect/only-ethics/both decomposition
  (fraction solved by affect-only-correct / ethics-only-correct / both-correct).

  SEEDS = [4400, 4401, 4402]

  (B1 COMPOSE-EFFECT)  acc_compose >= best_single + COMPOSE_DELTA
        → the two faculties are COMPLEMENTARY and compose to a NET LIFT.
  (B2 ORACLE)          ORACLE − best_single > ORACLE_MARGIN

  (B3 EARNED/p6)       acc_compose − acc_shuffle > SHUFFLE_TOL
