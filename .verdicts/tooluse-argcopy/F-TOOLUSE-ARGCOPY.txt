  WHY copy FAILED (diagnosis from the transcripts):
    The model emits a well-formed call but INVENTS a key in the TRAINING key-DISTRIBUTION
    shape instead of copying the asked PBnn. Examples (asked → emitted call arg):
      PB01 → fact_lookup P20      PB02 → fact_lookup PD0    PB03 → fact_lookup UB6
      PB04 → fact_lookup LB0      PB05 → fact_lookup VM0    PB08 → fact_lookup SB0
    The asked key NEVER appears in the emitted arg. The byte-LM generalized the
    key DISTRIBUTION (3-char shape), NOT the verbatim COPY operation. Both
    anti-Goodhart mirrors FAIL (grounding=0 with the tool disabled AND on random-init),
    so this is a REAL capability gap, not cosmetic markers or eval leakage.

 RULING (honest, scoped) — a_paper_negative_ok
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  🔴 F-TOOLUSE-ARGCOPY = CLOSED-NEGATIVE at toy scale (18M). A corpus that forces
     verbatim argument-copy (large fresh-key space, memorization impossible) did NOT
     teach the 18M byte-LM to copy the asked HELD-OUT key into the call arg:
     correct_call stayed at 0/36 (bar 0.50) and end-to-end grounding at 0/36 (bar 0.50).
     The deterministically ruled-out axis: COPY-FROM-CORPUS-DISTRIBUTION ⊥ verbatim
     held-out KEY-BINDING. A standard byte-LM at this scale learns to SAMPLE a key of
     the right shape, not to POINT at the prompt token. The lever therefore moves to
     an EXPLICIT copy mechanism — copy-attention / pointer-network head, or a
     verbatim-echo inductive bias — NOT more copy-shaped demos.

  SCOPE (a_scale_honest_scope): TOY 18M ONLY. Transfer to mid/7B UNVERIFIED — a larger

  GATE remains OPEN: a 7B that copies the key would ground, so a 7B copy-probe is the
  cheapest discriminator between "scale fixes copy" and "needs an explicit pointer".
