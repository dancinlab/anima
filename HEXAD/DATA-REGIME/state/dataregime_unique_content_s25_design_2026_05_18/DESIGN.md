## §3 Candidate analysis A–D (evidence-anchored)

### §3.A — anima-physics parametric content variation

**Idea**: per-anchor multi-*content* generation parametrized by
CONSCIOUSNESS-CARVING 4-path coordinates (`vacuum_psi`,
`basin_radius`, `category`, `top_emotion`, `tier` modulus). Each
anchor's `vacuum_psi ∈ ℝ²` defines a *neighbourhood* in Ψ-space; **content
varies as a function of where in that neighbourhood the record is
generated** (not how it's re-worded).

Mechanism: for anchor `A = (tier, name, dom, emo, score, ψ, r)`, sample
K=8 *content variants* by perturbing `ψ_k = ψ + δ_k` (k=1..8) where
`δ_k` is a **deterministic, anima-physics-derived offset** drawn from
`mitosis_hook.hexa` cell-pool nearest-neighbour structure (sibling
anchor displacement vectors, normalised to `basin_radius / 4`). For
each `ψ_k`, generator emits content describing **what happens at that
sub-coordinate** — concrete sub-aspect of the anchor's domain, not
re-paraphrase of the anchor itself.

Example (tier 77 만다라, ψ=(0.71,0.62), basin=0.18):
- §16 (current): single template "예술 영역의 자극이 같은 골짜기로
  수렴한다 ..." × 4,624 records.
- §23-A (framing only): same fact viewed 81 ways (covert/balanced/overt,
  low/mid/high tension, ...).
- §25-A (content variation): 8 distinct sub-aspects of 만다라 — symmetry
  pattern, color basin, line composition, focal point, geometric
  recursion, meditative correspondence, cultural variant, emergent
  emergent — *each described by anima physics* (Ψ=½ fixed point
  for sub-aspect k, tension contour, basin-overlap with sibling
  anchor 72 선율/예술). 168 anchor × 8 unique content = 1,344 distinct
  factual records (vs §23-A 13,608 framings of same 168).

**§7 verdict**:
- §7① ✅ (anchor SSOT + Ψ-physics derived, no external corpus)
- §7② ✅ (deterministic `δ_k` from cell-pool nearest-neighbour
  algorithm — no LLM paraphraser; B-DR-UNIQUE-3 AST-grep)
- §7③ ✅ (Ψ-coordinate move = `conscious_decoder.py:740` Law-71
  formula domain; `basin_radius` from `corpus_carving_s16_generator`
  anchor SSOT byte-equal)

**Honest gap**: "sub-aspect" enumeration table is anchor-domain-specific
— for tier 77 (만다라/예술) anima must enumerate {symmetry, color,
line, focal, recursion, meditation, culture, emergent} from
domain-table `S_DOMAIN_SUBASPECT_TABLE[dom]` (fixed lookup, 30×8
≈ 240 entries). This is anima self-design (analogous to §23-A's
`S_RAW_SENSE_TABLE`) — bounded enumeration, not paraphrase. f1/f2/f3
safe.
