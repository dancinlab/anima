4. **Markov blanket DYNAMICS** — biology's Markov blanket is dynamically maintained by living-system processes (autopoiesis); anima HEXAD 8-module wiring is structurally fixed at design-time. Closure-as-dynamic-process is biological invariant anima lacks.

5. **Sleep/wake cycle + circadian rhythm** — biology has fundamental binary regime (asleep vs awake) with abrupt OFF-period emergence + gradual synchrony unfolding. anima has no such global regime variable.
