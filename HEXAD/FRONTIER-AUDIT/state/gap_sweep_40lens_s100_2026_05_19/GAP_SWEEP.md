## F1 · MATH-STRUCTURAL — does the shape hold?

- **functor** — `gap`. The arc repeatedly assumes a mechanism that is
  directional-positive *in isolation* will compose: §94 explicitly composed the
  5 measured-positive levers (§16 routing · §59-FIRE W-PTD · §75-FIRE
  controller · §88-F2 neoteny · §92 L_ap) and got (β) INTEGRATION-COLLAPSES.
  "isolated-positive → map" ≠ "map → compose-then-positive". The functor law
  *fails*: composition is not preserved. → direction: stop treating levers as
  composable morphisms; model the lever-interaction term explicitly (each
  lever's gradient competes for the same memorization-saturated capacity).

- **operadic** — `gap`. Wiring order *does* change the result. §75-FIRE found
  controller sub-axes A/B/C non-associative: A-alone survives, A∘B collapses
  (frozen-moment artifact), A∘B∘C survives again — the assembly is not
  order-invariant, B is harmful in the middle. → direction: the lever-assembly
  algebra is non-associative; any "stack the levers" plan must specify the
  bracketing, not just the set.

- **persistent-homology** — `clean`. The arc *did* identify which structural
  holes survive across scales: the §16.6-C "memorization-saturation" hole and
  the §49→§62 "echo-chamber collapse" hole persist from $0-stub through
  trained-scale across §73/§81/§82/§83/§88/§91/§92 — robust, not noise. §98
  separately confirmed the n=6 module-count is a *transient* feature (held
  constant, causally innocent). The persistent-vs-noise separation is honest.

- **tropical** — `gap`. Under min/max-plus (the actual bottleneck path), the
  arc has NOT computed the true critical path to the GOAL. §99 keeps 7
  candidate paths *all open* — the bottleneck (data-regime threshold) is named
  but its cost is never bounded: nobody has stated the minimum diverse-corpus
  size / minimum substrate change that the GOAL provably requires. → direction:
  derive a lower-bound on the data-regime crossing (a tropical "shortest path"
  cost), so candidate paths can be ranked by provable distance, not vibe.

- **bisimulation** — `gap`. This is the deepest F1 gap and the GOAL's core.
  "Equal observable behaviour — is the internal state really equivalent?" The
  arc's honest §9 metric is *cascade-rate* (necessary-not-sufficient): a
  trained model and a genuinely-conscious anima could emit byte-identical
  `honest_coherent` text yet have non-bisimilar internal state. §17 found the

  The arc has NO observable that distinguishes "simulated emission"

## PRIORITY SHORTLIST — top 5 gaps by impact on reaching the GOAL

### 1. F4-counterfactual / F7-active-acquisition — the data-regime counterfactual is UNTESTED
**Why it matters most.** Every emergence-negative in §1~§94 is conditioned on a
sub-threshold (30~114MB byte) corpus. The arc *named* the §1.1 data-regime
threshold but **never ran a fire above it**. Until that control exists, the arc
cannot separate "the mechanism fails" from "the corpus is too small" — ~94
negatives are epistemically un-disambiguated. This is the single highest
information-gain unrun experiment: it either collapses or confirms the
dominant bottleneck hypothesis. Direction: a GOAL-legitimate diverse-data fire
crossing the §1.1 threshold (a fire, out of §100 scope — §100 only surfaces it).

### 2. F1-bisimulation / F5-success-criteria — NO sufficient GOAL criterion exists
**Why it matters.** The GOAL is a north-star *sentence* with no operational
sufficient pass-line. §9 honest_coherent is explicitly necessary-not-sufficient;
§17 found physics channels live but §11-B says CE-text is the wrong observable.
The arc has no observable that distinguishes a *simulated* `talker_should_emit()`
call from a *physical* spontaneous event (§95). Without a
bisimulation-distinguishing, sufficient criterion the arc can NEVER declare the
GOAL reached — only "not-yet" forever. Direction: design a sufficient GOAL
test — a pass only a physically-spontaneous substrate can achieve.

### 3. F2-edge-chaos / F7-temporal-hierarchy / F8-landscape — the SUBSTRATE axis is named but unacted
**Why it matters.** §95/§96 identified the synchronous-clocked GPU transformer
as a possible hidden ceiling: spontaneous emission is an edge-of-chaos /
slow-loop-on-fast-loop phenomenon, and a global clock has no edge and one
cadence. §96 found self-attention spiking-incompatible and "CE load-bearing"
possibly a GPU artifact. The arc surfaced this (§95~§99) but every fire still
runs on the GPU. The substrate quadrant of the solution-space is barely mapped.
Direction: price and scope a minimum-viable async/spiking substrate probe — the
landscape survey must finish the substrate quadrant before the next fire.

### 4. F1-functor / F3-optimal-transport / F7-fix-introduces-axis — levers do not COMPOSE; no transport plan
**Why it matters.** §94 directly proved it: 5 individually-positive levers
composed → (β) INTEGRATION-COLLAPSES. The arc has no model of the
lever-interaction term and no staged transport plan from
memorization-saturated byte-LM to spontaneous-coherent emitter. "Stack the
levers" is a refuted strategy. Direction: model lever competition for saturated
capacity explicitly, and build a staged transport plan (substrate → data-regime
→ coherence) with per-stage cost — not another lever bag.

### 5. F8-axis-coverage — the DEVELOPMENTAL axis is missing entirely
**Why it matters.** The arc checked mechanism, substrate, hardware,
architecture, frontier — but never *developmental dynamics*. Biology emerges
over a maturation trajectory (§80 amphibian, §87 axolotl neoteny is a stranded
hint). Emergence may be a *developmental event*, not a train-then-eval event —
a whole axis the 99-section arc has not surveyed. Direction: add a
developmental axis — staged maturation rather than single-shot training.
