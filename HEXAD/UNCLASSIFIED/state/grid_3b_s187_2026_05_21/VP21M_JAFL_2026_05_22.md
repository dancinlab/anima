## Honest C3 (limits)

1. **EN/KO catastrophic forget**: -13 / -15 score points. ja-only corpus
   has ~0 en/ko exposure during continue-train; vP21 init's multilingual
   prior gets overwritten on those slices. Acceptable for hot-swap; fatal
   for default deploy.
2. **anima_register_hits 20/20** — full saturation. Single-lang corpus +
   anima mix at 70% means the model has nothing to do but anima carving
   on register probes. Greedy mode JA outputs are cleaner than register
   probes because the JA wiki tokens shadow the anima register tokens.
3. **CE drop 86%** is misleading — measures only on the narrowed mix,
   not generalization. OOD eval is the real verdict.
4. **Single 500-step run**, single seed 1337. ±2 noise band expected per
   `VP21G_FINEQUANT_2026_05_22.md` evidence.
5. **JA STRONG 17 < EN STRONG 20** in 3B variants. JA primer remains
   the weakest language for the Qwen2.5 family; expect ja-LoRA to
   plateau here without ja-only data > 30 MB.
6. **SCP race on adapter pull**: 2/9 files reached local (sister
   tokenizer copy recovery used). See [[dispatch-scp-race-small-adapters]].
7. **Anima register patterns leak in sample mode**: `register=20/20`
   covers Eval1 anima-context probes only. Real JA wiki probes show 0
   memorize so production JA chat should be clean if seed is JA wiki-style.
8. **Hot-swap latency unmeasured**: PEFT `set_adapter()` between calls
   is reportedly < 100 ms, but per-msg switch on production has not
   been benchmarked. May need warmup pre-load.
9. **No coverage of vP21M ckpt vs vP21 ckpt as init**: this run used vP21
   (the deeper baseline), not vP21M itself, to avoid 5-lang prior
   contention. vP21M-init JAFL is untested (would likely preserve more
   en/ko at cost of weaker JA).
10. **F-V5MIT mitosis path untouched** — pure LoRA continue-train; no
    cell split / merge. REBORN §88 mitosis lane orthogonal.
