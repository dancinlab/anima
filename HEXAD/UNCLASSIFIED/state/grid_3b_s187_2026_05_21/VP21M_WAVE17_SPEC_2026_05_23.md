## § Hypothesis

True minimum lies in the open interval `(0.20, 0.40)`; v11=0.30 may not be
the precise vertex. Density-side smoothness from v9 (74) → v11 (34) → v12 (91)
suggests vertex is bracketed but not yet localized.

- **Strict** — ≥1 sweep point has `continuous_total < 34` **AND** `n_strong ≥ 4`
  → swap mini production `lora_adapter/` to that corpus.
- **Weak** — minimum localized (vertex visible) but no point meets both swap
  criteria → finer 0.25/0.30/0.35 sub-sweep next (Wave-18).
- **Null** — all 4 points monotone with v11 still global min → original 0.30
  sweet spot confirmed, close Wave-17 as "v11 single-point confirmed".
