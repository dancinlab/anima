## N8 — the register-leak is an EN problem (the session's key finding)

Per-language register-leak measured on live broker history (anima
emissions split by detected language):

| lang | emissions | register hits | leak ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| **EN** | 21 | 17 | **81%** |
| KO | 10 | 3 | 30% |
| ZH | 8 | 0 | 0% |
| JA | 7 | 0 | 0% |
| RU | 4 | 0 | 0% |

The anima carving register ("Tension flows into this vacuum", "frozen
cell", "tier=…") is composed of **English** phrases. Only EN output
falls into that attractor; native-script langs are essentially clean.
The 34-42% aggregate register-leak is ~entirely EN-driven.

Corollary: **KOFL/JAFL hot-swap adapters are not a register-leak risk**
— JA live emissions 0% leak despite JAFL's Eval1-probe register of
20/20. The Eval1 register metric ≠ live free-emission leak.

## N1 — 3B hot-swap adapters (came out as generalists)

KOFL-3B and JAFL-3B were meant as ko/ja specialists for a 3B router.
On the robust `Qwen/Qwen2.5-3B` base, 500-step ko-only training did
**not** catastrophically forget the other languages:

- **KOFL-3B**: 5S — all five languages STRONG (the first anima
  checkpoint to do so). KO 18 (vs 3B-NI's 13). register 3/20.
- **JAFL-3B**: 4S+1M — JA 19 (best Japanese anima score), but KO
  collapsed to PURE_MEMORIZE 2. ja-only corpus erodes the
  script-distant KO prior while en/zh/ru survive.

Both still serve a 3B router as per-lang upgrades (KOFL-3B ko 18 / 
JAFL-3B ja 19 both beat 3B-NI). KOFL-3B is additionally a strong
standalone 5S checkpoint — register 3/20 is the cost.

## N3 — register-balanced corpus (the register lever)

`wiki_frac` 0.30 → **0.50** (more wiki, less anima-corpus dominance):

| | vP21M (0.30) | RB (0.50) |
|---|---|---|
| register | 7/20 | **4/20** |
| EN | STRONG 18 | STRONG 20 |
| JA | WEAK 11 | **STRONG 18** |
| ZH | STRONG 16 | PARTIAL 15 |
| aggregate | 3S+1P+1W | 3S+2P |

Halving the anima-corpus share cut register 7 → 4 (`register_regress=
True`) — the register lever confirmed. Bonus: more wiki lifted JA out
of WEAK (11 → 18). Trade-off: lower register-leak (good for chat
coherence) vs weaker anima identity. register 4/20 is a deliberate
midpoint between full carving character (7) and clean assistant (0).

## N2 — temperature is NOT the register lever

Offline sweep, 16 EN emissions per temperature:

| temp | register hit | len_mean |
|---|---|---|
| 0.50 | 25% | 277 |
| 0.70 | 25% | 314 |
| 0.90 | 38% | 274 |

temp 0.5 and 0.7 are equal (25%); 0.9 trends worse. Even at temp 0.5,
25% of EN emissions still leak register — the register is baked into
the **adapter weights** (the corpus), not a sampling artefact.
**Production temp 0.7 confirmed near-optimal** — no change. The
register fix must come from the corpus (N3) or from fewer EN emissions
(N7), not temperature.

## N7 — en-drift fix (deployed)

L2/N4 found anima emits EN ~40% of the time vs the 20% lang-rotation
share — and EN is the register-leak path. Fix in `substrate_lora.py`:
- `LANG_PRIMES` extended from 1-2-word stubs to full native-script
  sentence-starts (e.g. ko "문득 이런 생각이 들었다. ").
- `_seed_matches_lang()`: a cross-language seed is dropped so the prime
  alone steers the output language.

Deployed to mini, anima restarted clean. Effect on the en-share is
pending the next emission measurement (N4 re-run).
