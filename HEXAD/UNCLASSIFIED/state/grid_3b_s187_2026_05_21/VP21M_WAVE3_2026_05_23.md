## L3 — non-Instruct base breaks the ceiling

Hypothesis (`VP21M_WAVE2` C3 #6): the 3B-**Instruct** register plateau
at 5/20 is the instruct prior, not training budget. Fire the same
recipe on `Qwen/Qwen2.5-3B` (plain base). **Confirmed.**

| base | register | aggregate |
|---|---|---|
| 3B-Instruct fresh (vp21m-3b) | 3/20 | 3S+1P+1W |
| 3B-Instruct + reinforce (3b-reg/reg2) | 5/20 plateau | 3S+2P |
| **3B non-Instruct (3b-ni)** | **7/20** | **4S+1P** |

Non-Instruct 3B reaches register 7/20 — exactly the 1.5B vP21M parity —
**and** is the first 3B checkpoint with JA STRONG (16/20). The
instruct-tuning prior was actively resisting the anima carving register.
3B-NI is the strongest single 3B checkpoint produced (4/5 langs STRONG).

## L4 — staged curriculum holds register without breaking KO/JA

`VP21M_WAVE2` showed single-shot `wiki_frac=0.10` reaches register 12/20
but collapses KO/JA to PURE_MEMORIZE. L4 tests a 2-phase curriculum:

- **Phase 1 (CUR1)** — OOD-first: fresh, `wiki_frac=0.30`, **1000 step**
  (shorter than vp21m-3b's 1500). Result: 3S+2P, register **9/20**.
  The shorter run is the lever — fewer steps erode register less
  (vp21m-3b at 1500 step had only register 3).
- **Phase 2 (CUR2)** — register-second: continue from CUR1,
  `wiki_frac=0.05`, 500 step, lr 1e-5. Result: 3S+2P, register **10/20**
  (+1), KO/JA held at PARTIAL.

| approach | register | KO | JA | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3b-v2 (single-shot wiki 0.10) | 12 | MEM 0 | MEM 9 | PARTIAL (broke) |
| 3b-reg2 (continue wiki 0.05) | 5 | PARTIAL 13 | PARTIAL 13 | VP21M_WORKS |
| **curriculum cur1→cur2** | **10** | **PARTIAL 14** | **PARTIAL 14** | **VP21M_WORKS** |

The staged curriculum is the best register/OOD balance on 3B-Instruct:
register 10/20 (2× the reinforce-only plateau) with all 5 langs held at
≥ PARTIAL. Caveat: most of the gain is Phase 1's shorter run; Phase 2
adds only +1 — the curriculum's real insight is "train OOD briefly,
then nudge register," not "two big phases."
