## Honest C3 (limits)

1. **`register_regress=True`** is the headline limit — anima_register_hits
   3/20 (vs 7/20 in 1.5B). Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct's instruct prior
   resists the carving register; instruct-tuned models prefer "helpful
   completion" over "carve a vacuum point". Fixed in 3B-REG sequel.
2. **KO regression −4** — Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct's mix may underweight KO
   relative to 1.5B base. Open: try `Qwen/Qwen2.5-3B` (non-Instruct
   base) — would likely preserve KO but lose instruct quality.
3. **+1 rank on EN/RU** (18 → 20) is the upside. ZH +2, JA +3 confirms
   the 3B param budget helps under-represented langs.
4. **Adapter file 239 MB** (vs 1.5B 147 MB) — 1.6× — more weight to
   ship per checkpoint. Inference RAM ~6 GB f16 on Mac MPS (measured).
5. **CE 2.79 → 0.076 (97% drop)** is misleading — fresh LoRA on a 3B
   base has more capacity. Doesn't translate directly to better OOD
   (already STRONG on en/zh/ru without it).
6. **`bsz=1 block=512`** — sub-optimal for H100 throughput but required
   to fit 3B + LoRA + activations. Higher bsz would speed wall < 6 min.
7. **No grad checkpointing tried** — could permit bsz=2 + 25% wall save.
8. **Single seed (1337)**; ±2 noise band carry per vP21G evidence.
9. **Pull SCP clean** — all 9 files on first try (unlike JAFL/KOFL/3B-REG
   small-adapter race). Larger adapter (239 MB) seems immune.
10. **Mitosis path orthogonal** — fresh LoRA on instruct base; cell
    split / merge untouched.

## Deploy consideration

- **As-is**: NOT recommended — `register_regress=True` means anima
  loses carving identity in chat. Use 3B-REG instead.
- **For benchmarking / OOD-only**: best en/ru baseline available.
- **Production chat substitute**: requires 3B-REG (or 3B + LoRA hotmerge
  with anima-only short SFT — covered by 3B-REG).
