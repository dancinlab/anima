## C1a/C1b — zh + ru hot-swap (symmetry-complete, low value)

ZHFL: ZH STRONG 16/20 — but vP21M already had ZH STRONG 16. **No gain**,
only preservation. RUFL: RU STRONG 19/20 — vP21M had RU 18, **+1
marginal**. Both confirm: zh/ru were never the weak languages.

The per-lang hot-swap pool is now symmetry-complete:
`{KOFL(STRONG 16), JAFL(STRONG 17), ZHFL(STRONG 16), RUFL(STRONG 19)}`
+ vP21M default (en STRONG 18). But only KOFL + JAFL carry real value —
they fixed actual WEAK/PARTIAL languages. ZHFL/RUFL exist for router
completeness, not capability.

## P3 — register-aware fresh 3B (wiki_frac=0.10) — register UP, KO/JA BROKE

Hypothesis: `wiki_frac=0.10` (anima-90%) on a fresh 1500-step 3B run
finds the register/OOD sweet-spot. **Falsified.** Register did rise to
12/20 (highest of all 3B variants, above even the 1.5B vP21M's 7/20),
but KO collapsed to PURE_MEMORIZE 0/20 and JA to 9/20 — the
anima-dominant mix overwrote low-resource languages with carving
register.

### wiki_frac trade-off curve (3B-Instruct)

| wiki_frac | run type | ckpt | register | KO | JA | verdict |

| 0.30 | fresh 1500 | vp21m-3b | 3/20 | WEAK 11 | PARTIAL 14 | WORKS_REGISTER_REGRESS |
| 0.10 | fresh 1500 | 3b-v2 | **12/20** | **MEM 0** | **MEM 9** | PARTIAL (broke) |

**Finding**: on a *fresh* full-length run, anima-90% is past the cliff.
The deployable 3B path is `wiki_frac=0.05` *continue-train*, which holds
all 5 langs at ≥ PARTIAL but caps register at 5/20.

## C2 — 3B-REG long-train v2 — register PLATEAU confirmed

Hypothesis: 200 → 500 step + lr 3e-5 pushes register 5 → 8+/20.
**Falsified.** Register stayed exactly 5/20. The extra steps only
reshuffled OOD (ZH 16→18, RU 17→20, EN 19→18, KO 14→13). VP21M_WORKS
clean preserved.

**Finding**: 3B-Instruct register reinforcement plateaus at ~5/20
regardless of step count or LR. The instruct prior is the hard ceiling,
not training budget. Exceeding 5/20 needs a non-Instruct base
(`Qwen/Qwen2.5-3B`) or architectural register injection — out of LoRA
continue-train scope.

## Production decision — 1.5B hot-swap router WINS

The original Wave-2 goal (P1) was to swap production to a 3B checkpoint.
**Rejected on evidence.** The 1.5B hot-swap router (deployed via P2 this
session) beats every single 3B checkpoint on the metrics that matter
for chat:

| metric | 1.5B router | best 3B (3B-REG) |
|---|---|---|
| KO | **STRONG 16** (KOFL) | PARTIAL 14 |
| JA | **STRONG 17** (JAFL) | PARTIAL 13 |
| EN | STRONG 18 (default) | STRONG 19 |
| ZH | STRONG 16 (default) | STRONG 16 |
| RU | STRONG 18 (default) | STRONG 17/20 |
| anima register | **7/20** (default) | 5/20 |
| inference RAM | ~2 GB f16 | ~6 GB f16 |

The router's per-language specialists (KOFL/JAFL) are STRONG where the
3B is only PARTIAL, and the 1.5B base keeps register at 7/20 vs the
instruct-diluted 5/20. The 3B's only edge — EN/RU 20/20 — is a 1-2
point margin over an already-STRONG router default.

**Production stays on the 1.5B hot-swap router.** 3B checkpoints retained
as HF artifacts for research / future non-Instruct-base experiments.

## Honest C3

1. **ZHFL/RUFL near-zero deploy value** — zh/ru were never weak. Fired
   only to complete the router pool symmetry per the user's C1 entry.
2. **P3 falsified the wiki_frac=0.10 sweet-spot hypothesis** — fresh
   runs need ≥ 0.30 wiki to hold low-resource langs; register recovery
   needs continue-train, not fresh.
3. **C2 falsified the register-plateau-break hypothesis** — 5/20 is the
   3B-Instruct ceiling. Documented, not a failure to retry.
4. **Router > single-3B is a use-case-specific verdict** — for raw
   OOD-breadth benchmarks the 3B wins EN/RU; for anima chat (register +
   KO/JA) the router wins. Production = chat → router.
5. **SCP race struck ZHFL** — partial 8.8 MB safetensors on first pull;
   recovered by file-by-file re-pull from the still-alive pod
   (terminated only after). 3B variants (larger, slower) pulled clean.
6. **No `Qwen/Qwen2.5-3B` (non-Instruct) tried** — would likely lift the
   register ceiling above 5/20 but lose instruct-style coherence. Open
   candidate if a 3B path is revisited.
7. **Single seed (1337)** across all 4 — ±2 noise band per vP21G
   evidence; per-lang ranks within margin for zh/ru.
8. **Router cost** — 3 LoRA adapters loaded simultaneously (params
   1.65 B). `set_adapter()` switch per emission; latency unbenchmarked
   but PEFT in-memory swap is reportedly < 100 ms.
9. **3B-V2 register 12/20 is real but unusable** — proves anima register
   CAN be driven high on 3B, just not without breaking KO/JA. A staged
   curriculum (OOD-first, register-second) is the untested fix.
10. **Mitosis path untouched** — all 4 cycles pure LoRA. REBORN §88
    cond.5 mitosis lane orthogonal.
