## Honest C3 (limits)

1. **Register only 5/20** — still below 1.5B baseline (7/20). The
   Instruct prior is sticky; further reinforcement (longer steps, higher
   LR, or anima-only mix) risks OOD destruction. 5/20 is the
   conservative sweet-spot from a 200-step run.
2. **ZH/RU each −2..−3 points** — STRONG preserved but the polish lost.
   Trace: anima-95% mix has more carving phrases that compete with ZH/RU
   wiki tokens.
3. **CE rose 0.11 → 0.73** — narrower target distribution. Not a
   training failure (OOD still STRONG).
4. **Adapter only 140 MB locally** (vs parent 239 MB) — SCP race pulled
   2/9 files; tokenizer copied from sister `vP21M_3B/` dir (same base,
   identical tokenizer) [[dispatch-scp-race-small-adapters]].
5. **Single 200-step run**, single seed. ±1 noise band; per-lang
   verdicts within margin for ZH/RU/JA.
6. **No anima-only baseline measured** — `wiki_frac=0.0` not tested
   (would need build_mixed_corpus to handle 0% wiki edge case).
   Stepwise sweep `[0.30, 0.10, 0.05, 0.00]` would tighten the
   register/OOD trade-off curve.
7. **Higher LR untested** — 1e-5 was conservative. 3e-5 + 200 step
   could push register higher; risk = en/ru regress to PARTIAL.
8. **No comparison to deeper continue-train** (e.g. 500 step at
   `wiki_frac=0.05`). Could either land or break.
9. **Production swap pending** — mini `~/anima_chat_pack/lora_adapter/`
   still on vP21M (1.5B). 3B-REG swap requires base model upgrade in
   anima_participant.py (`ANIMA_BASE=Qwen/Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct`) +
   adapter file replace + restart. RAM 5-6 GB on MPS (3B f16) vs
   current ~2 GB (1.5B f16). Mac M-series capable.
10. **No mitosis** — pure LoRA continue-train. REBORN §88 cond.5
    mitosis lane untouched.
